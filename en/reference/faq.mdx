This page is the way in when something doesn't add up. Each answer takes three
lines and ends in a link to the page that tells the whole story. If you are
looking for what a word means, it is in the
[glossary](/en/reference/glossary).

## My agent didn't reply to a customer

Before letting your agent speak, the system checks five things, each with its
own screen. **Go through them in this order**, which is the same one it checks
them in.

<Steps>
  <Step title="1. Someone turned off Autopilot in that chat">
    The conversation header has an **Autopilot** switch. Turned off, your agent
    goes quiet **in that specific chat** and you reply instead. It takes one
    click from the header to turn it off, so it is the first thing to look at.
    Turn it back on right there.
  </Step>
  <Step title="2. The agent is paused">
    A deactivated agent doesn't reply to anyone. You can see it and turn it on
    from the dashboard **Home**, on the agent's card, and also in **Channels**.
    → [Your first look](/en/getting-started/first-look)
  </Step>
  <Step title="3. The free trial expired">
    That very moment your agents stop replying, even if you have credit to
    spare. The dashboard says so when you try to turn one on: *"Your trial has
    ended: choose a plan to turn your agents back on."* **Topping up credits
    does not unlock it**: the trial date is checked before the balance and does
    not look at it. You need to subscribe.
    → [Plans and pricing](/en/getting-started/pricing)
  </Step>
  <Step title="4. You ran out of credit">
    The cutoff doesn't wait for exactly zero: it trips when what is left is
    less than the cost of answering a message (around **US\$ 0.10**), so you can
    see a remainder on screen with the agent already mute. The **Balance** chip
    in the top bar turns red when you get there.
    → [Credits and top-ups](/en/getting-started/credits-and-top-ups)
  </Step>
  <Step title="5. That same chat already had a turn in progress">
    The system doesn't open two replies at once for the same customer. If they
    wrote to you three times in a row, you are not going to see three replies.
    There is nothing to change on any screen: it is deliberate.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
**If all five are fine, look at the conversation in the inbox.** Your agent may
have replied and the message not gone out: in that case the bubble carries a
**red exclamation mark** and the text **"Not sent (…)"**. Also check that
**that** agent's channel is connected: each agent has its own number, there is
no WhatsApp "for the account".
</Note>

<Warning>
**Autopilot is not the way to get "don't reply to anyone yet".** It is a
**per-conversation** switch, and it only exists in chats that already exist.
Anyone writing to you for the first time comes in with the agent in charge. If
you really don't want it replying to anyone, **deactivate the agent**.
</Warning>

## Does the copilot spend my credit?

It depends on what you ask it for, and since it is your money it all goes in a
table. **The copilot** is the AI inside the dashboard, the one in the drawer on
the right — don't confuse it with Claude or ChatGPT connected from outside,
which on these pages are **your assistant**.

| What you do with the copilot | Does it spend your credit? |
| --- | --- |
| The messages of the **guided setup**: telling it how you sell so it builds your first agent | **No.** The platform pays for these on purpose |
| The messages of the **"Guide me"** helper, the one that shows you where everything is | **No.** Same as the previous one |
| **Generating the script** for the playbook (every time you ask it to write it or redo it) | **Yes** (with one exception at the start: see the box) |
| Any other conversation with the copilot | **Yes**, and with the balance at zero it stops |

The reason for the first two rows is simple: whoever needs that conversation
most is exactly the person who still has US\$ 0 because they have not connected
their WhatsApp yet. Everything else goes through the same balance check as the
rest of the AI.

<Warning>
**"You have already used your complimentary generations."** Generating the
script costs money before you connect too. So that you can build your agent
with the balance at zero, the system **fronts you up to four generations**
during the trial (the normal path spends three; the fourth is in case you were
not happy), with a cap of **US\$ 2**. It is not a gift: the spend is recorded
and your account goes into debt, which settles itself with the welcome credits
the day you connect your WhatsApp. When they run out — by count or because you
reached the US\$ 2 — the dashboard tells you: *"You have already used your
complimentary generations: connect your WhatsApp to activate your trial credits
and keep generating."*
→ [Plans and pricing](/en/getting-started/pricing)
</Warning>

## Credit and charges

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="My balance says US\$ 0 and I just signed up">
    That is expected, not a half-finished account. The welcome credits are
    granted **when you connect your WhatsApp**, not when you sign up, and the
    trial clock starts at that same moment. In the meantime you can build your
    agent all the same.
    → [Connect WhatsApp](/en/getting-started/connect-whatsapp)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I was credited US\$ 9.50 instead of US\$ 10">
    You tested your agent in the simulator before connecting. So that you could
    watch it reply without having connected anything, the system fronted you
    **US\$ 0.50** and that now comes off the welcome credit. The advance is one
    per account only and only kicks in if you had no balance at that moment.
    → [Plans and pricing](/en/getting-started/pricing)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I have credit on screen and the agent doesn't reply">
    The cutoff trips before zero: when what is left is less than the cost of a
    turn, around **US\$ 0.10**. That is why the dashboard uses three different
    notices — **Insufficient balance**, **Balance exhausted** and **Balance in
    debt** — and each one tells the truth about its own number.
    → [Credits and top-ups](/en/getting-started/credits-and-top-ups)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I topped up and my agent didn't answer what arrived while it was stopped">
    Correct, and it is worth knowing beforehand: the messages **were saved and
    you can see them in the inbox**, but they were not left in a queue. When you
    top up, your agent picks up with the **next** message that customer writes.
    If someone was left hanging, write to them yourself from the inbox.
    → [Credits and top-ups](/en/getting-started/credits-and-top-ups)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="If I run out of credit, does any charge keep running?">
    Yes, and the dashboard warns you in the balance-exhausted banner: the apps
    you have contracted and the jobs that run in the background stay active, so
    the debt can grow with the agent already silent. The concrete example is the
    Library's **audio transcription**, which does not stop for lack of credit.
    → [Credits and top-ups](/en/getting-started/credits-and-top-ups)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My trial is over: do I top up or subscribe?">
    You subscribe. Topping up credit does **not** turn your agents back on once
    the trial has expired: they are two different things and the trial date is
    checked first.
    → [Plans and pricing](/en/getting-started/pricing)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I hit my limit of agents turned on">
    During the trial you can have several at once — today it is **5**, and the
    dashboard always tells you how many you get. When you try to turn on one
    more it offers you two ways out: subscribe, or turn another one off to
    relieve it without paying. Careful with the second: **the agent you turn off
    stops replying instantly** and any messages it had queued are cancelled.
    → [Plans and pricing](/en/getting-started/pricing)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I got a WhatsApp from DarkFunnels, is it real?">
    If it says your account ran out of credit or that the trial expired, yes:
    that notice goes out to the numbers you have in the agent's **Error
    alerts**, and it **never reaches your customer**, who never sees the state
    of your account. The intent is to send you one a day per agent, but it is
    not a guarantee: with several agents, or if the system restarts, another one
    can reach you the same day.
    → [Credits and top-ups](/en/getting-started/credits-and-top-ups)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I want to cancel, change my card or request an invoice">
    Those three are resolved by writing to us, not from a dashboard screen. It
    is better to say so before you find out hunting for the button. **Plans and
    pricing** has the full list of what you cannot do on your own today and of
    which receipt you get for each charge.
    → [Plans and pricing](/en/getting-started/pricing)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is there a referral code?">
    Yes. If you signed up with one, you are credited **US\$ 25 extra** alongside
    the welcome credit, the day you connect your WhatsApp. And your own business
    has its code for referring others.
    → [Plans and pricing](/en/getting-started/pricing)
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## WhatsApp and the QR code

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The QR expires before I have time to scan it">
    That is normal: it expires in seconds and renews itself while that screen is
    open. Have WhatsApp on your phone already at **Linked devices** before you
    look at the dashboard screen, and if the code got away from you, press
    **Refresh QR** and scan the new one.
    → [Connect WhatsApp](/en/getting-started/connect-whatsapp)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="It says “Connecting…” and there is no button">
    For the first few seconds you will only see a spinner and **"Preparing the
    QR code…"**: there is nothing to press yet. The buttons appear around the
    20-second mark, once the badge has changed to **Action required**. Wait
    before reloading the page.
    → [Connect WhatsApp](/en/getting-started/connect-whatsapp)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="It says “Action required” and no code appears">
    The session was parked after a dozen or so codes that nobody scanned. The
    button says **Connect** and restarts the QR cycle from scratch.
    → [Connect WhatsApp](/en/getting-started/connect-whatsapp)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="It stays on “Connecting…” and doesn't move">
    There are two ways out, in this order: **Retry**, which asks for the
    connection again, and **Restart**, which tears the session down and brings
    it up from scratch. The dashboard says it in its own words: *"The connection
    is taking longer than usual. Retry or restart."*
    → [Connect WhatsApp](/en/getting-started/connect-whatsapp)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My WhatsApp was connected and dropped: do I have to rescan?">
    Wait a bit before redoing anything. With the number already linked, a
    network drop recovers on its own when the connection heals. If the dashboard
    asks you for a code again, scan it calmly: your conversations, your playbook
    and your catalog are untouched.
    → [Connect WhatsApp](/en/getting-started/connect-whatsapp)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I connected the wrong number">
    Disconnect it and connect the right one. The welcome credits are granted
    **once per account only**, so reconnecting does not duplicate them and
    disconnecting does not take them away from you.
    → [Connect WhatsApp](/en/getting-started/connect-whatsapp)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I connected WhatsApp but my second agent doesn't reply">
    Each agent connects **its own number**: there is no WhatsApp for the whole
    account. In **Channels** you will see one card per agent with its status. If
    you try to write from the inbox, the notice distinguishes the two cases: the
    agent not having a number linked yet, or having one and being disconnected.
    → [Connect WhatsApp](/en/getting-started/connect-whatsapp)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I don't want the agent to reply yet">
    Connect anyway — it is what credits your credits and starts the trial — and
    **then** deactivate the agent. The order matters: **a deactivated agent
    cannot connect its WhatsApp**. And remember that Autopilot only silences the
    agent in chats that already exist, not with someone writing to you for the
    first time.
    → [Connect WhatsApp](/en/getting-started/connect-whatsapp)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I already connected, but Home keeps asking me to">
    Go back to the **Home** tab: it refreshes when you return to it. If it is
    still there, look at the channel status: if it stayed on **Connecting…** or
    on **Action required**, the connection never finished.
    → [Your first look](/en/getting-started/first-look)
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## The agent and its script

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="My agent made up a price or a condition">
    It means you did not give them to it. Prices live in the **catalog** and the
    hard rules — shipping, warranties, what cannot be promised — in the
    **playbook**. Whatever is not written down, the agent fills in its own way.
    → [The catalog](/en/guides/catalog) · [The sales playbook](/en/guides/sales-playbook)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I approved a funnel and it replaced my whole playbook">
    Yes: approving a funnel replaces the complete playbook, it does not merge
    it. But you have a way out: the Playbook keeps a **History** with the latest
    versions and the previous one can be restored from there, instead of fixing
    it chapter by chapter.
    → [The sales playbook](/en/guides/sales-playbook)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I closed the copilot mid-conversation and lost the thread">
    From **the same machine**, the thread reopens where you left it: Home →
    **Create agent**. From another device it starts from scratch, although it
    does recognize the agent you left without a script.
    → [Your first agent](/en/getting-started/your-first-agent)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I approved the agent but did not approve the script">
    The agent already exists, with an empty playbook. Go into its **Playbook**
    and press **Build it by chatting**: you pick the script back up without
    taking up another agent slot.
    → [Your first agent](/en/getting-started/your-first-agent)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What is the red exclamation mark on a message from my agent?">
    That the message **did not go out**. The bubble shows **"Not sent (…)"**
    with a technical label for the reason that today is not translated
    anywhere: do not try to decipher it, go into the conversation and, if it
    repeats, write to us with the agent's name and the time.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My agent replied halfway and the rest doesn't appear">
    That is expected, not a failure, and that is why it carries no mark: when
    the customer writes while the agent still had messages queued, the rest of
    that batch is cancelled so as not to talk over them. The agent carries on
    with the last thing the customer said.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I got a WhatsApp saying my funnel had an error">
    It is a real DarkFunnels notice to your **Error alerts** numbers. It names
    the funnel and includes, in parentheses, a technical label for the reason,
    untranslated. The actionable part is to go into the conversation in the
    dashboard; so as not to flood you, at most one notice arrives every 5
    minutes per funnel and reason.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A customer asked not to be written to again">
    Do it by hand: no text the customer sends you ("BAJA", "STOP") unsubscribes
    them on its own. The contact's record has **Unsubscribe contact**; from then
    on they stop receiving messages from the agent and from you too, on every
    channel, and it is reversed from the same place.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Files

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How large can a file be and how many fit?">
    There is no limit on quantity. The limit is **20 MB per file** from the
    dashboard, with the notice *"The file … exceeds the 20MB limit."* If your
    catalog weighs more, split it into two PDFs or export it at a lower
    resolution. There is a second cap that causes confusion: when the file is
    uploaded by **your assistant** connected from outside, the maximum drops to
    **2.5 MB**.
    → [The file Library](/en/guides/library)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does sending files cost me separate credits?">
    Sending them has no charge of its own separate from the conversation's. Two
    things that happen on their own when you upload do consume credit:
    **processing the file for the agent's memory** and **transcribing an
    audio** — the latter happens even if you never send it.
    → [Credits and top-ups](/en/getting-started/credits-and-top-ups)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A file says “Something went wrong” and I don't know why">
    The reason is not in plain sight: put the mouse over the label and it
    appears. The two most common cases are a **scanned PDF**, which has no text
    to read, and a **CSV with no data rows**; it also shows up when you have run
    out of credit. Next to it you have **Retry**, but with a scanned PDF
    retrying changes nothing: a different file is needed.
    → [The file Library](/en/guides/library)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does the customer see my file's name?">
    On documents, yes: WhatsApp shows the name as it is. On photos and videos,
    no. Give a presentable name to whatever you are going to send as a document.
    → [The file Library](/en/guides/library)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The customer says the audio doesn't play for them">
    Almost always it is in the source file, not in the send: the Library
    re-encodes the audio into a voice note when you upload it, and if the file
    will not let itself be processed, the upload fails with a notice. Try
    uploading it again recorded from your phone.
    → [The file Library](/en/guides/library)
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Signing in, signing out and your account

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="“An account with that email already exists”">
    Sign-up stops and sends you to the login. If you do not remember the
    password, use **Forgot your password?**: you get a link to set it again.
    → [Create your account](/en/getting-started/create-your-account)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="It won't accept the password I want to set">
    The rule is short: **at least 8 characters, with at least one letter and at
    least one number**. No symbols or capitals are asked for.
    → [Create your account](/en/getting-started/create-your-account)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="It rejected my email and it is spelled correctly">
    Look at it again calmly: sign-up rejects impossible endings — `.con`
    instead of `.com` is the classic one. It is done on purpose, because
    nothing reaches an account with a misspelled email: not the welcome, not
    the password recovery.
    → [Create your account](/en/getting-started/create-your-account)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The anti-bot check won't pass">
    Press **Retry** in the box. It tends to fail inside the Instagram or
    Facebook browser: open the page in Chrome or Safari and it goes through
    first time. Depending on the configuration, it may not even appear for you.
    → [Create your account](/en/getting-started/create-your-account)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="“We are receiving too many sign-ups right now”">
    It is not a problem with your email or your account: it is a ceiling for the
    whole platform that was reached in that hour. Try again in a few minutes,
    with the same details.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I came back from Google and it asks me for details again">
    That is the normal step. Google does not supply your WhatsApp number or your
    business name, so those two are asked for all the same.
    → [Create your account](/en/getting-started/create-your-account)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I entered my WhatsApp number wrong at sign-up">
    You do not have to redo the sign-up, but you do have to correct it **agent
    by agent**: that number is copied into the notification lists of every agent
    you create — error alerts, sale notices and daily summary — and **Profile**
    does not let you change it. You correct it in the agent's **Settings**, in
    those lists.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="No WhatsApp notification reaches me">
    Two reasons, both silent. One: the number is written without the country
    code — at least 8 digits are needed with it — and then the send is counted
    as good and the message does not arrive. Two: the **Error alerts** list is
    empty, and then those notices reach nobody in your business. Check that your
    number is there, complete.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I want to change my password, my login email or delete my account">
    All three are done from **Profile**. When you change the email you get a
    confirmation link and the old email keeps working until you confirm. What is
    not changed there is the WhatsApp number from the sign-up.
    → [Security](/en/security)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The steps card disappeared from Home">
    It is only hidden **in that browser**: your progress still counts and the
    card reappears from another browser or from the phone. The guided tour opens
    once per browser and reopens whenever you want with **How it works**, in the
    Home header.
    → [Your first look](/en/getting-started/first-look)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The “Have your first conversation” step doesn't tick for me">
    The dashboard's test conversations do not tick it: that step waits for a
    **real** conversation, from someone who writes to the WhatsApp number you
    connected.
    → [Your first look](/en/getting-started/first-look)
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## If you connected Claude, ChatGPT or Codex

This part is **optional** and only applies if you connected **your assistant**
to your business from outside the dashboard. If you work only in the dashboard,
you are done.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="My assistant doesn't see my data, or sees it empty">
    Almost always you authorized with **a different account**. Since there is no
    waiting list, the wrong account does not error out: it connects you to an
    empty space. Ask it *"which DarkFunnels account are you connected to?"* — it
    has a tool for answering that. If it is not yours, remove the connector,
    sign in to `darkfunnels.ai` with the right account and connect again.
    → [Security](/en/security)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The new tools don't appear for me">
    Open a **new chat**: each conversation keeps the list of features that
    existed when it was opened. If with a new chat it still does not see them —
    or it is missing some detail a feature ought to be asking you for — remove
    the connector and connect it again: that also refreshes the descriptions.
    Changing the connection address also forces a reconnect.
    → [The connection URL](/en/reference/connection-url)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My assistant says “out of credits”">
    It is the same balance as the dashboard: the AI features stay stopped until
    you top up, and the assistant itself gives you the link. You top up in
    **Balance and billing**.
    → [Credits and top-ups](/en/getting-started/credits-and-top-ups)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I ask it to review my conversations?">
    Yes, it is the bridge's flagship job: reading the real chats and pointing
    out where the sale falls apart. Note that those guides are written for the
    connected assistant, not for the dashboard inbox.
    → [Reviewing conversations](/en/guides/conversations) · [Orders and metrics](/en/guides/orders-and-metrics)
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
