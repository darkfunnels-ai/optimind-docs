## My agent did not reply to a customer

Before letting your agent reply, the system runs several checks. **Go through
them in this order.**

<Steps>
  <Step title="Autopilot is off in that chat">
    The conversation header has a **«Piloto automático»** (*Autopilot*) switch.
    When it is off, your agent goes quiet **in that chat** and you reply
    instead. Turn it back on right there.

    You don't have to be the one who turned it off. **The agent turns it off
    itself** when the customer asks to talk to a person, when they get
    genuinely angry, or when your playbook tells it to (for example, when
    closing an order). And if someone writes to the customer from the line's
    phone **repeating the first word** of the message (*«ok ok, mañana te
    confirmo»*, "ok ok, I'll confirm tomorrow"; *«ya ya»*, "sure, sure"),
    Autopilot in that chat switches state.
    → [Taking over the conversation yourself](/en/guides/conversations)
  </Step>
  <Step title="The agent is paused">
    A paused agent doesn't reply to anyone. On **Inicio** (*Home*), its card
    says **«Activo»** (*Active*) or **«Pausado»** (*Paused*) and has its own
    switch: turn it on there.
    → [Your first look](/en/getting-started/first-look)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your free trial has ended">
    From that moment your agents stop replying, **even if you still have
    credit**. If you try to turn one on, the dashboard tells you that your
    trial has ended and that you need a plan. **Topping up credits does not
    unlock it**: you need to subscribe.
    → [Plans and pricing](/en/getting-started/pricing)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your balance doesn't cover one more message">
    The cutoff kicks in before your balance reaches 0: when you have less than
    **US\$ 0.10** left, the minimum to reply to a message. That is why you can
    see a remainder on screen with the agent already silent. The **Saldo**
    (*Balance*) chip in the top bar turns red, and **Saldo y facturación**
    (*Balance and billing*) tells you what happened.
    → [Credits and top-ups](/en/getting-started/credits-and-top-ups)
  </Step>
  <Step title="They wrote several times in a row">
    It isn't that it doesn't reply: your agent waits a few seconds, gathers
    what the customer sent in a row and answers it all together. If the
    customer writes while your agent is still sending them a reply, what was
    left of that reply is cancelled and it answers the latest message. It works
    this way on purpose: there is nothing to change.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The message came from another line of your business">
    Your agent doesn't reply to another WhatsApp line of your own business:
    that way, two of your agents don't answer each other in a loop. To test,
    write to it from a personal phone.
    → [Test numbers](/en/guides/agent-settings#test-numbers)
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
**If all of that is fine, open the conversation in the inbox.** Your agent may
have replied and the message not gone out. That bubble carries a **red «!»**
and, when you hover over it, the notice gives the reason in words: *«No
enviado: el cliente pidió no recibir mensajes»* (*Not sent: the customer asked
not to receive messages*), *«No enviado: el canal llegó a su tope de mensajes
por minuto; se reintenta solo»* (*Not sent: the channel reached its
per-minute message cap; it retries on its own*)… If the reason shows up as a
code in parentheses, send it to us exactly as it is.

Also check that **that agent's WhatsApp** is still connected in **Personalizar
→ Canales** (*Customize → Channels*): each agent has its own line. And bear in
mind that your agent doesn't reply in groups or in statuses.
→ [When a message does not arrive](/en/guides/conversations)
</Note>

<Warning>
**To keep your agent from replying to anyone yet, pause it: Autopilot is not
the way to do that.** Autopilot works **per conversation**, and every new
conversation starts with Autopilot on: anyone who writes to you for the first
time comes in with the agent in charge.
</Warning>

## Does the copilot spend my credit?

Yes: the copilot is AI and spends your balance, just like your agent when it
holds a conversation. **The copilot** is the AI inside the dashboard, the one
in the drawer on the right. Don't confuse it with Claude or ChatGPT connected
from outside, which on these pages are **your assistant**.

| What you do with the copilot | Does it spend your balance? |
| --- | --- |
| The **setup** (*puesta en marcha*): telling it what you sell so it builds your first agent | **Yes.** That is why your account is born with a US\$ 10 advance |
| **«Guíame»** (*Guide me*), the helper that points out where to click (**Ayuda → Guíame paso a paso**, *Help → Guide me step by step*) | **Yes** |
| Any other conversation with the copilot | **Yes**, and with your balance used up it stops |

For the copilot there is a single budget, your balance, and a single gate: if
it covers it, the copilot works; if not, it stops and tells you. Your welcome
credits are in your balance from the very first minute.

<Note>
**When the copilot can't reply, it leaves you a card with the way out.**

- **No balance:** *«Tu cuenta no tiene saldo disponible. Recarga créditos para
  seguir usando el copiloto.»* (*Your account has no balance available. Top up
  credits to keep using the copilot.*), with the **«Recargar créditos»** (*Top
  up credits*) button if you can see billing. If you haven't connected
  WhatsApp yet, the notice tells you that your starting US\$ 10 were spent on
  building your agent.
- **Your trial has ended:** *«Tu prueba terminó: el copiloto y tus agentes
  quedan en pausa hasta que elijas un plan…»* (*Your trial has ended: the
  copilot and your agents stay paused until you choose a plan…*), with **«Ver
  planes»** (*See plans*) if you can see billing. Topping up doesn't help
  here.
- **A temporary glitch:** **«Reintentar»** (*Retry*) sends your last message
  again.
- **A proposal that didn't pass the playbook checks:** the card shows you the
  rule that failed and the **«Reintentar con eso corregido»** (*Retry with that
  fixed*) button.

→ [Credits and top-ups](/en/getting-started/credits-and-top-ups)
</Note>

## Balance and charges

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I just signed up and I already have a balance">
    That is expected: your account is born with your full **US\$ 10 welcome
    credit**, advanced so that you can build and test your agent before
    connecting anything. **No more balance arrives when you connect your
    WhatsApp**: what starts that day is your trial clock.
    → [Connect WhatsApp](/en/getting-started/connect-whatsapp)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I connected my WhatsApp and nothing was credited">
    That is correct: your welcome credits were already in your balance from
    sign-up. When you connect, your trial starts and your agent begins handling
    that number. The welcome credit is **US\$ 10**, once per business. If you
    signed up with a referral code, your **US\$ 25** arrive separately, on the
    day you activate your subscription.
    → [Plans and pricing](/en/getting-started/pricing)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I have a balance on screen and the agent doesn't reply">
    The cutoff kicks in before reaching 0: when you have less than
    **US\$ 0.10** left. That is why **Saldo y facturación** uses three red
    notices: **«Saldo insuficiente»** (*Insufficient balance*: you have
    something left, but not enough to reply to a message), **«Saldo agotado»**
    (*Balance used up*) and **«Saldo en deuda»** (*Balance in debt*: your
    charges went past what you had left, for example a reply that was already
    under way or the transcription of an audio; your next top-up pays off the
    debt).
    → [Credits and top-ups](/en/getting-started/credits-and-top-ups)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I topped up and my agent didn't answer what arrived while it was stopped">
    It works this way on purpose: the messages **were saved and you can see
    them in the inbox**, but they were not left in a queue. Your agent picks up
    again with each customer's **next** message.

    To catch up, turn on **«Esperando respuesta»** (*Waiting for a reply*) in
    the inbox: only the customers nobody answered are left. Reply to them
    yourself or, if you see **«Seleccionar»** (*Select*) above the list, pick
    several and press **«Responder con IA»** (*Reply with AI*): the AI answers
    them one per minute. Before that, it asks you to accept a notice about the
    risk of WhatsApp banning your number.
    → [Reply with AI](/en/guides/conversations)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="If I run out of balance, does any charge keep running?">
    Yes, and the balance notice tells you so: *«Tus apps y los trabajos en
    segundo plano siguen activos, y su cargo estimado sigue corriendo.»* (*Your
    apps and background jobs stay active, and their estimated charge keeps
    running.*) The debt can grow with the agent already silent. The concrete
    example is the **transcription of the audio files** you upload to the
    Library, which doesn't stop for lack of balance.
    → [Credits and top-ups](/en/getting-started/credits-and-top-ups)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My trial is over: do I top up or subscribe?">
    You subscribe. Topping up your balance does **not** turn your agents back
    on once the trial has ended: they are two different things. Your balance
    isn't lost: it is still there when you subscribe.
    → [Plans and pricing](/en/getting-started/pricing)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I hit my limit of agents turned on">
    During the trial you can have up to **5** agents active at once; with the
    subscription, it depends on your slots. When you try to turn on one more,
    the dashboard offers you to subscribe (or to add one more slot) or to turn
    another one off to make room. Careful with the latter: **the agent you turn
    off stops replying instantly** and the messages it had queued are
    cancelled.
    → [Plans and pricing](/en/getting-started/pricing)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I got a WhatsApp from optiMind about my balance or my trial">
    It is real. It starts with *«⚠️ optiMind:»* and goes out when a customer
    writes to you and your agent can't reply, because your balance isn't enough
    or because your trial ended. It goes to that agent's **«Alertas de error»**
    (*Error alerts*) numbers, from the agent's own WhatsApp line, with the
    funnel's name at the bottom. **It doesn't reach your customer.**

    The intent is one notice a day per agent and reason, but it isn't a
    guarantee: with several agents, or if the system restarts, you may get
    another one the same day.
    → [Error alerts](/en/guides/agent-settings#error-alerts)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I want to change my card, cancel or request an invoice">
    It depends on what:

    - **You change the card yourself**, in **Saldo y facturación → Métodos de
      pago → Cambiar tarjeta** (*Payment methods → Change card*), with no
      charge. That is also where you add a **backup card**, which is charged if
      the bank declines the main one on a renewal or an auto top-up.
    - **An app you no longer use** is deactivated in the apps table on that
      same screen, with the **⋯** menu → **«Desactivar»** (*Deactivate*) (if
      you manage the apps). Its charge stops; what you have already paid is not
      refunded.
    - **An agent slot** is removed with **−** on the **«Suscripción»**
      (*Subscription*) card. The removal takes effect on your next billing
      date.
    - **Cancelling the whole subscription** has no button: write to us.
    - **An invoice or SUNAT receipt for what we charge you** is not issued by
      the dashboard: your proof of payment is the receipt you get by email with
      each charge. (The **«Facturación electrónica»** (*Electronic invoicing*)
      app is for you to issue receipts to your customers, not for this.)

    → [Plans and pricing](/en/getting-started/pricing#how-it-is-charged)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is there a referral code?">
    Yes, and they are two different things:

    - **If you signed up with a code**, you get **US\$ 25** in credit on the
      day you activate your subscription, on top of your US\$ 10 welcome
      credit.
    - **Your business also has its own code**, in **Administración →
      Plataforma → Referidos** (*Administration → Platform → Referrals*) (if
      you can see billing). For each business that subscribes with it you earn
      **US\$ 15 of credit for each of their charges**, up to 6 charges.

    Don't confuse it with the **«Red de referidos»** (*Referral network*) in
    Ventas (*Sales*): that one is for your customers to bring you customers.
    → [Invite other businesses](/en/getting-started/credits-and-top-ups#invite-other-businesses-and-earn-credits) · [Referral network](/en/guides/referral-network)
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## WhatsApp and the QR code

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need the WhatsApp Business API or a Meta account?">
    No. Your number is connected by scanning a QR from the dashboard, as a
    linked device, just like WhatsApp Web. It doesn't go through the official
    WhatsApp Business API (Meta's): there is no application to get approved,
    no message templates and no per-conversation fee from Meta. Regular
    WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business both work, with the number your customers
    already write to.

    DarkFunnels is not a Meta product and is not affiliated with Meta. The
    optional **pixel** and **Ads** features do connect to Meta, with your
    permission.
    → [Connect WhatsApp](/en/getting-started/connect-whatsapp)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can WhatsApp block my number?">
    It can happen. Since the connection is not the official API, WhatsApp can
    ban a number that writes to many people in a row or out of the blue. To
    lower that risk, the platform caps what your agent sends through your line
    (per minute, per hour, per day and distinct contacts per day) and doesn't
    write at night to a number it has no previous conversation with.

    And there is something that depends on you: **don't use the line to
    cold-message lists of numbers.** When you ask the AI to reply in a batch
    (**«Responder con IA»**), the dashboard asks you to accept that risk every
    time.
    → [Security](/en/security)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The QR expires before I have time to scan it">
    That is normal: each code expires in seconds and renews itself while that
    screen is open. Have WhatsApp on your phone already at **Linked devices**
    (*Dispositivos vinculados*) before you look at the dashboard and, if the
    code got away from you, press **«Actualizar QR»** (*Refresh QR*) and scan
    the new one.
    → [Connect WhatsApp](/en/getting-started/connect-whatsapp)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I only have my phone with me and can't scan my own screen">
    The simplest way: open the dashboard on a computer, or on another phone,
    and scan from the line's phone.

    If you see the **«Vincular con código»** (*Link with a code*) button on the
    card, you can do it from that same phone with an 8-character code: in
    WhatsApp, **Linked devices → Link a device → Link with phone number
    instead** (*Vincular con el número de teléfono*), and type in the code. It
    works with Peruvian mobile numbers. If you don't see the button, it isn't
    available for your account or for that line.
    → [Common problems when connecting](/en/getting-started/connect-whatsapp#common-problems)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My phone says «Couldn't link device»">
    You scanned and WhatsApp rejected the link at the last step. The dashboard
    detects it and tells you so under the code: close WhatsApp, open **Linked
    devices** again and scan the code that is on screen. If it fails again,
    **«Actualizar QR»** and once more; if that fails too, **«Escribir al
    equipo»** (*Write to the team*) from that same card.
    → [Common problems when connecting](/en/getting-started/connect-whatsapp#common-problems)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="It stays on «Conectando…» (Connecting…) and I see no buttons">
    For the first few seconds you will only see *«Preparando el código QR…»*
    (*Preparing the QR code…*): there is nothing to press yet. If it is still
    the same after 20 seconds, the badge switches to **«Acción requerida»**
    (*Action required*) with *«La conexión está tardando más de lo normal.
    Reintenta o reinicia.»* (*The connection is taking longer than usual. Retry
    or restart.*) Try in this order: **«Reintentar»** (*Retry*), which asks for
    the connection again, and **«Reiniciar»** (*Restart*), which starts it up
    again.
    → [Common problems when connecting](/en/getting-started/connect-whatsapp#common-problems)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="It says «El código caducó» (The code expired) and the QR no longer appears">
    You let about ten codes go by without scanning and the line stopped asking
    for them, so that a forgotten tab doesn't spend the night generating them.
    Press **«Conectar»** (*Connect*) and the cycle starts again. If it is the
    first time you connect that line, the card goes back to **«Desconectado»**
    (*Disconnected*): press **«Conectar / Escanear»** (*Connect / Scan*).
    → [Common problems when connecting](/en/getting-started/connect-whatsapp#common-problems)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My WhatsApp was connected and dropped">
    **We email you** within a few minutes and, if you turned on browser
    notifications, you get one there too. If the card says **«Conectando…»**,
    it is reconnecting on its own: wait a little. If it asks you for a new
    code, scan it calmly: your conversations, your playbook and your catalog
    are untouched.

    **What people wrote to you during the outage** may show up in the inbox and
    be left unanswered. Find it with **«Esperando respuesta»** and reply
    yourself or use **«Responder con IA»**, about 10 minutes after
    reconnecting.
    → [Common problems when connecting](/en/getting-started/connect-whatsapp#common-problems)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I connected the wrong number">
    If the number you linked is not the one from sign-up, the card asks you
    whether it is the right one, with **«Sí, es este»** (*Yes, it's this one*)
    and **«Desconectar y usar otro»** (*Disconnect and use another one*). If
    you noticed it yourself, press **«Desconectar»** (*Disconnect*) and connect
    the right one. Reconnecting doesn't give you more credits or take any
    away.
    → [Connect WhatsApp](/en/getting-started/connect-whatsapp)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I connected WhatsApp but my second agent doesn't reply">
    Each agent works through **its own line**: there is no WhatsApp for the
    whole account. In **Personalizar → Canales** there is one card per agent
    with its status: connect the missing one. In the inbox, the message box
    tells you too: *«Conecta WhatsApp para enviar mensajes»* (*Connect WhatsApp
    to send messages*) or *«Este embudo no tiene línea de WhatsApp»* (*This
    funnel has no WhatsApp line*).
    → [Connect WhatsApp](/en/getting-started/connect-whatsapp)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I don't want the agent to reply yet">
    Connect anyway and **then** pause your agent: on **Inicio**, its card goes
    from **«Activo»** to **«Pausado»**. The order matters: **a paused agent
    can't connect its WhatsApp**. Bear in mind that your trial clock keeps
    running while the agent is paused.
    → [Connect WhatsApp](/en/getting-started/connect-whatsapp)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I wrote to my agent from my phone and it didn't reply">
    Your own WhatsApp, the one from sign-up, already counts as a test number:
    the agent replies to you as it would to a customer. If it didn't, go over
    [the checks above](#my-agent-did-not-reply-to-a-customer). Two typical
    cases when testing:

    - **You wrote from another WhatsApp line of your business**, for example
      another of your agents' line. The agent doesn't reply to it: test from a
      personal phone.
    - **You want to test with the phone of someone on your team.** Add it
      under **«Números de prueba»** (*Test numbers*), in the agent's
      **Configuración** (*Settings*).

    If you only want to see how the script responds, without going through
    WhatsApp, press **«Probar»** (*Test*) in that agent's
    [Manual](/en/guides/sales-playbook) (*Playbook*): it is a test chat, with
    no WhatsApp involved.
    → [Test numbers](/en/guides/agent-settings#test-numbers)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I already connected, but Home keeps asking me to">
    Go back to the **Inicio** tab and wait a few seconds: it checks again when
    you return to it. If it is still there, look at the status in **Canales**:
    if it stayed on **«Conectando…»** or on **«Acción requerida»**, the
    connection didn't get to finish.
    → [Your first look](/en/getting-started/first-look)
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## The agent and its script

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="My agent made up a price or a condition">
    Most often, you didn't give them to it. Prices live in the **catalog** and
    the hard rules — shipping, warranties, what cannot be promised — in the
    **playbook**. Whatever is not written down, the agent fills in its own way.
    If the information was written down, write to us with the agent's name and
    the time of the conversation.
    → [The catalog](/en/guides/catalog) · [The sales playbook](/en/guides/sales-playbook)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I approved a funnel and it replaced my whole playbook">
    Yes: approving the whole funnel the copilot proposes replaces the complete
    playbook, it doesn't merge it. You have a way out: in the Manual,
    **«Historial»** (*History*) keeps the previous versions. Pick the earlier
    one, accept its changes (all of them or chapter by chapter) and press
    **«Guardar cambios»** (*Save changes*).
    → [The History](/en/guides/sales-playbook)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I closed the copilot mid-conversation and lost the thread">
    You didn't lose it: the setup is saved in your account. Press **«Crear
    agente»** (*Create agent*), from this device or from another one, and it
    reopens where you left it. It is also in the copilot's **«Sesiones»**
    (*Sessions*) tab: at the top, **«Tu puesta en marcha»** (*Your setup*) →
    **«Reanudar»** (*Resume*).
    → [If you stop halfway](/en/getting-started/your-first-agent#if-you-stop-halfway)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I approved the agent but didn't approve the script">
    It depends on how your agent was born:

    - **Selling over WhatsApp (Ecommerce template):** when you approve, it is
      born with its shared block and the template's chapters, with placeholders
      you fill in in the Manual.
    - **Appointments or applicants:** it is born with its chapter 1 written.
      You write the missing ones in its **Manual**, or you ask the copilot for
      the whole funnel in your setup, without taking up another agent slot.

    → [Your first agent](/en/getting-started/your-first-agent)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What is the red exclamation mark on a message from my agent?">
    That the message **did not go out**. Hover over the **«!»**: the notice
    gives the reason in words, for example *«No enviado: pasaron más de 24 h
    desde el último mensaje del cliente»* (*Not sent: more than 24 h have
    passed since the customer's last message*). Some fix themselves (the ones
    that end in *«se reintenta solo»*, "it retries on its own"). If you see a
    code in parentheses, write to us with the agent's name, the time and that
    code.
    → [When a message does not arrive](/en/guides/conversations)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My agent replied halfway and the rest doesn't appear">
    That is expected, not a failure, and that is why it carries no mark: when
    the customer writes while the agent still had messages queued, the rest of
    that batch is cancelled so as not to talk over them. The agent carries on
    with the last thing the customer said.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My agent stopped replying in a chat and I didn't turn anything off">
    Look at that chat's **«Piloto automático»**. The agent turns it off on its
    own when the customer asks for a person, gets genuinely angry or the
    playbook tells it to; and writing from the line's phone repeating the first
    word also switches it. In **Clientes** (*Customers*), the **«Por cerrar»**
    (*To close*) queue gathers the chats where the customer wrote last and
    Autopilot is off.

    To hand the conversation back to the agent, turn Autopilot on. It doesn't
    reply to what arrived while it was off: it waits for the customer to write
    again.
    → [Taking over the conversation yourself](/en/guides/conversations)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I got a WhatsApp saying my funnel had an error">
    It is a real notice to your **«Alertas de error»** numbers. It names the
    funnel, says what happened — *«no pudo responder a un mensaje del cliente,
    ni al reintentar»* (*it couldn't reply to a customer's message, not even on
    retry*), or *«no pudo entregar una respuesta»* (*it couldn't deliver a
    reply*) if what failed was the sending —, adds **«Motivo:»** (*Reason:*)
    with the detail and asks you to check the conversation.

    When the agent can't reply, it first retries on its own. If you have
    browser notifications turned on, on that first failure you get *«⚠️ Tu
    agente no pudo responder a…»* (*⚠️ Your agent couldn't reply to…*); the
    WhatsApp goes out if the retry fails too. So as not to flood you, the
    intent is one notice every 5 minutes per funnel and reason, although if the
    system restarts you may get another one sooner.
    → [Error alerts](/en/guides/agent-settings#error-alerts)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A customer asked me to stop messaging them">
    Unsubscribe them yourself: writing «BAJA» or «STOP» doesn't unsubscribe
    them on its own. In their record, press **«Dar de baja al contacto»**
    (*Unsubscribe contact*). From then on they stop receiving messages from
    the agent and from you, across all your agents. It is reversed from the
    same record with **«Reactivar contacto»** (*Reactivate contact*), if your
    user manages the business's WhatsApp lines.
    → [The customer's record](/en/guides/conversations)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The same customer appears in two of my agents">
    That is correct: each agent has its own record of each customer, with
    their details, tags, notes and sales. If the same number writes to two of
    your agents, it shows up in both lists. What applies to the whole business
    is the unsubscribe.
    → [Customers](/en/guides/orders-and-metrics)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I want to copy a funnel, give it to another account or delete it">
    It is on **Inicio**, in the **⋯** menu on the agent's card: **«Duplicar
    embudo»** (*Duplicate funnel*), **«Compartir embudo…»** (*Share funnel…*)
    and **«Eliminar agente…»** (*Delete agent…*). **«Duplicar embudo»** only
    shows up for whoever manages the account or can see billing. A duplicated
    funnel starts with no customers.
    → [Your funnels](/en/guides/your-funnels)
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Files

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How large can a file be and how many fit?">
    There is no limit on quantity. The limit is **20 MB per file** from the
    dashboard, with the notice *«El archivo … excede el límite de 20MB»* (*The
    file … exceeds the 20MB limit*). If your catalog is bigger, split it into
    two PDFs or export it at a lower resolution. When the file is uploaded by
    **your assistant** connected from outside, the maximum drops to **2.5 MB**.
    → [The file Library](/en/guides/library)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does sending files cost me extra credits?">
    Sending them has no charge of its own apart from the conversation's. Two
    automatic processes do use balance: **processing the file for the agent's
    memory**, if the agent has **«memoria del agente»** (*agent memory*)
    turned on, and **transcribing the audio files** you have in an agent's
    Library, even if you don't send them.
    → [Credits and top-ups](/en/getting-started/credits-and-top-ups)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A file says «Hubo un problema» (There was a problem) and I don't know why">
    Put the mouse over the label and the reason appears. The most common ones:
    a **scanned PDF**, which has no text to read; a **CSV with no data rows**;
    or *«sin créditos»* (*no credits*), if you ran out of balance. Next to it
    you have **«Reintentar»**, but with a scanned PDF retrying changes nothing:
    you need a different file.
    → [The file Library](/en/guides/library)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does the customer see my file's name?">
    On documents, yes: WhatsApp shows the name as it is. On photos and videos,
    no. Give a presentable name to whatever you are going to send as a
    document.
    → [The file Library](/en/guides/library)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The customer says the audio doesn't play">
    Usually the problem is in the source file, not in the sending: the Library
    converts the audio into a voice note when you upload it, and if the file
    can't be processed, the upload fails with *«No se pudo procesar el archivo
    de audio»* (*The audio file could not be processed*). Try uploading it
    again, recorded from your phone.
    → [The file Library](/en/guides/library)
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Your team and your businesses

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I invite someone from my team?">
    In **Administración → Personas y accesos** (*Administration → People and
    access*), with the **«Invitar»** (*Invite*) button. You decide, app by app,
    what each person can use. Invitations are sent by whoever manages the team:
    if you don't see the button, ask that person.
    → [Invite your team](/en/getting-started/invite-your-team)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Someone on my team sees «No tienes acceso a…» (You don't have access to…)">
    They are missing access to that app. Whoever manages the team turns it on
    in **Personas y accesos**, on that person's record.
    → [If something goes wrong](/en/getting-started/invite-your-team#if-something-goes-wrong)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I was invited from another business and I already have an account">
    Don't create another one. Open the link in the invitation email and join
    with the account you already have, signing in with your password or with
    Google, as usual. When you enter the dashboard you will also see the notice
    *«… te invitó a su equipo»* (*… invited you to their team*), with
    **«Reenviarme el enlace»** (*Resend me the link*) in case you can't find
    the email.
    → [One account in several businesses](/en/getting-started/invite-your-team#one-account-in-several-businesses)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I have two businesses: how do I switch between them?">
    In your account menu, the circle with your initials at the top right. The
    current business says **«Estás aquí»** (*You're here*) and the others,
    **«Cambiar a esta empresa»** (*Switch to this business*). The active
    business belongs to your account, not to the tab: if you switch on your
    phone, you switch on your computer too. Each business has its own
    permissions, its own balance and its own subscription.
    → [One account in several businesses](/en/getting-started/invite-your-team#one-account-in-several-businesses)
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Signing in, signing out and your account

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="«Ya existe una cuenta con ese correo» (An account with that email already exists)">
    Sign-up stops and sends you to sign in. If you don't remember your
    password, press **«¿Olvidaste tu contraseña?»** (*Forgot your password?*):
    you get a link to set a new one. If what you wanted was to join another
    business's team, sign in with your account and open the invitation link.
    → [Create your account](/en/getting-started/create-your-account)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="It won't accept the password I want to set">
    The rule is short: *«Mínimo 8 caracteres, con letras y números.»* (*At
    least 8 characters, with letters and numbers.*) No symbols or capitals are
    required.
    → [Create your account](/en/getting-started/create-your-account)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="It rejected my email and it is spelled correctly">
    Look at it again calmly: sign-up rejects impossible endings — `.con`
    instead of `.com` is the classic one — and tells you so. It is done on
    purpose: nothing reaches an account with a misspelled email, neither the
    welcome nor the password recovery.
    → [Create your account](/en/getting-started/create-your-account)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The anti-bot check won't pass">
    If you see *«No pudimos verificar tu navegador.»* (*We couldn't verify
    your browser.*), press **«Reintentar»**. It tends to fail inside the
    Instagram or Facebook browser: open the page in Chrome or Safari and try
    again.
    → [Create your account](/en/getting-started/create-your-account)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="«Estamos recibiendo demasiados registros ahora mismo» (We're receiving too many sign-ups right now)">
    It is not a problem with your email or your account: it is a sign-up
    ceiling for the whole platform that was reached in that hour. Try again in
    a few minutes, with the same details.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I came back from Google and it asks me for details again">
    That is the normal step. Google doesn't supply your WhatsApp number or your
    business name, so those two are asked for all the same.
    → [Create your account](/en/getting-started/create-your-account)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I entered my WhatsApp number wrong at sign-up">
    You don't have to redo the sign-up. That number was copied into **«Informar
    de las ventas»** (*Report sales*), **«Reportes»** (*Reports*) and
    **«Alertas de error»** of every agent you created, and **Perfil**
    (*Profile*) doesn't let you change it. Correct it in each agent's
    **Configuración** and, so that new agents are born with the right number,
    write to us.
    → [If you entered your WhatsApp number wrong](/en/getting-started/create-your-account#if-you-entered-your-whatsapp-number-wrong)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="WhatsApp notifications don't reach me">
    Check three things on the agent that should be notifying you:

    - **That your number is on the right list** in its Configuración:
      **«Alertas de error»**, **«Informar de las ventas»** or **«Reportes»**.
      With the list empty, that notice reaches nobody in your business.
    - **That its WhatsApp line is connected.** The notices go out from it.
    - **For the morning summary**, that **«Enviar el resumen cada mañana»**
      (*Send the summary every morning*) is turned on.

    → [Agent settings](/en/guides/agent-settings)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I want to change my password, my login email or delete my account">
    All three are in **Perfil** (your account menu → **Perfil**). When you
    change the email you get a confirmation link at the new one, and the
    current one keeps working until you confirm. **«Eliminar cuenta»**
    (*Delete account*) deletes your entire business and is done by the owner;
    if you sign in with Google or your account is also in another business,
    the dashboard asks you to write to us. What is not changed there is the
    WhatsApp number from sign-up.
    → [Security](/en/security)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The steps card disappeared from Home">
    There are two possible reasons. You hid it with **«Ocultar»** (*Hide*),
    which only hides it in that browser: your progress still counts and it
    shows up again in another browser. Or you registered your first sale, and
    with it the card is retired in every browser. What you can repeat whenever
    you want is the **«Cómo funciona»** (*How it works*) tour, in the Inicio
    header, which goes over those same steps.
    → [The start-up list](/en/getting-started/first-look#the-start-up-list-pon-tu-empresa-a-operar)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The «Ten tu primera conversación» (Have your first conversation) step doesn't tick">
    The dashboard's test conversations don't tick it: that step waits for a
    **real** conversation, from someone who writes to the WhatsApp number you
    connected.
    → [Your first look](/en/getting-started/first-look)
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## If you connected Claude, ChatGPT or Codex

This part is **optional** and only applies if you connected **your assistant**
to your business from outside the dashboard. If you work in the dashboard,
you are done.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="My assistant doesn't see my data, or sees it empty">
    The most common cause: you authorized with **a different account**. The
    connection doesn't throw an error, it just shows you that account's data.
    Ask it *"which DarkFunnels account are you connected to?"*: it has a tool
    that tells you the connection's user and business. If it is not yours,
    remove the connector in your assistant, sign in to the dashboard at
    `optimind.darkfunnels.ai` with the right account and connect again.
    → [Security](/en/security)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My assistant says my account switched business">
    Your account is in several businesses, and each connection stays pinned to
    the one you had active on its first call. If you later switched business in
    the dashboard, that connection doesn't save changes, so as not to write to
    the wrong business. Go back to that business in the dashboard, or remove
    the connector and connect it again to work in the other one.
    → [One account in several businesses](/en/getting-started/invite-your-team#one-account-in-several-businesses)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The new tools don't appear for me">
    Open a **new chat**: each conversation keeps the list of features that
    existed when it was opened. If it still doesn't see them with a new chat —
    or it is missing some detail a feature ought to be asking you for — remove
    the connector and connect it again: that also refreshes the descriptions.
    Changing the connection address also forces a reconnect.
    → [The connection URL](/en/reference/connection-url)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My assistant says «sin créditos» (out of credits)">
    It is the same balance as the dashboard's: the AI features stay stopped
    until you top up, and the assistant itself gives you the link. You top up
    in **Saldo y facturación**.
    → [Credits and top-ups](/en/getting-started/credits-and-top-ups)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I want to take away my assistant's access">
    In the dashboard, go to **Ventas → Personalizar → Asistentes IA** (*Sales →
    Customize → AI assistants*). On the **«Seguridad y acceso»** (*Security and
    access*) card, press **«Revocar»** (*Revoke*) next to the assistant and
    confirm: it loses access within a few seconds. If you also want to remove
    it from your assistant, delete the connector there.
    → [Connect Claude](/en/quickstarts/claude-ai)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I ask it to review my conversations?">
    Yes: reading your chats and pointing out where the sale falls apart is one
    of the most useful things it can do. And you don't need to connect
    anything for that: the dashboard's **copilot** also reads, searches and
    summarizes your conversations. When it summarizes, it works on a sample
    and tells you how many it looked at.
    → [The same thing, from your assistant](/en/guides/conversations)
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
