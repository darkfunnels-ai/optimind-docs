<Note>
This page is **optional and only applies if you connect your assistant**
(Claude, ChatGPT or Codex) to your business from outside the dashboard. If you
work only in the dashboard, you don't need it.
</Note>

The connection URL is the address you paste into your assistant to connect it
to your business. **With it you choose what you offer it**: just looking,
editing the catalog, answering chats, testing the agent…

What your assistant can actually do doesn't depend on the URL alone. **Your
permissions**, **the business** the connection works in and **some usage
limits** count too. You'll see this in
[What the URL does not decide](#what-the-url-does-not-decide).

The dashboard interface is in Spanish, so its buttons and menus are named here
in Spanish with the English meaning in parentheses.

## The URL, bare and complete

The base is this one:

```
https://mcp.darkfunnels.ai/mcp
```

Like that, with nothing else, it already works. It brings your business's
**reads** — your agents, the playbook, the catalog, the conversations and the
customers — and three minor writes: creating customer tags, changing a
customer's tags and logging a request your assistant couldn't fulfill, which
reaches us so we can build what's missing.

The one the dashboard gives you is the complete one. It's in **Personalizar →
Asistentes IA** ("Customize → AI assistants"), with the **Copiar link** ("Copy
link") button:

```
https://mcp.darkfunnels.ai/mcp?features=all
```

  <img src="/images/asistentes/01-conecta-tu-asistente.png" alt="Conecta tu asistente (Connect your assistant) card in Personalizar, Asistentes IA, with the connection link, the Copiar link button, three numbered steps and a note about read-only mode" />
</Frame>

If you don't see **Personalizar** in your menu, copy the URL from here: it's the
same one.

### How to build it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start from the base">
    `https://mcp.darkfunnels.ai/mcp`, or the dashboard's, which already comes
    with `?features=all`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose which groups travel">
    Add `?features=` with the groups separated by commas, or `all` for all of
    them. The groups are in the next section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the other parameters">
    The first one goes after `?` and the following ones after `&`. For example:
    `?features=all&read_only=true`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect">
    Paste it as a new connector in your assistant and authorize with your
    account. If you already had it connected, see
    [Changing the URL afterwards](#changing-the-url-afterwards).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
If you add a parameter, **keep the ones you already had**: it's the most common
mistake when reconnecting. And write the values **in lowercase and exactly as**
they appear here: with `ALL`, `True` or `FULL`, the parameter has no effect.
</Note>

## `?features=` — which tool groups travel

The bridge's tools come in **groups**. Without this parameter, the default set
travels: `core`, `manual`, `catalog` and `conversations`, plus the reads of
some apps that only appear in certain accounts. Everything else has to be asked
for.

If your account has one of those apps and you use a list, name its group too:
you'll find it in the [Tools reference](/en/reference/tools).

The last column says which access you need in your business. They are the names
of the switches in **Personas y accesos** ("People and access").

**Included by default**

| Group | What it brings | Access it requires |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `core` | The basics: who you are, which business the connection works in, your agents with their settings, their summary and the status of their WhatsApp, your account's setup, your balance and quick product help (a few basic topics, each with its link to the dashboard). One write: logging a request it couldn't fulfill. | Your agents' data, **Ventas** ("Sales"). The balance, **Ver saldo y facturación** ("See balance and billing"). Who you are, the business, the quick help and logging requests don't require any app permission. |
| `manual` | Reading your agents' playbook, its version history and reviewing it without saving. | **Ventas** |
| `catalog` | Reading your products and each one's detail sheet. | **Ventas** |
| `conversations` | Reading conversations, the customer list and each customer's profile, and explaining what the agent did in a turn. Two writes: creating tags and changing a customer's tags. | **Ventas** |

**You have to ask for these**

| Group | What it adds | Access it requires |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `manual_write` | Saving chapters, writing a new one, editing the block shared by all chapters, restoring a version and applying a complete funnel. **Destructive.** | **Ventas** |
| `catalog_write` | Creating, editing and deleting products. **Deleting is permanent.** | **Ventas** |
| `agents_write` | Creating or duplicating a funnel — **it uses up a slot in your subscription** —, pausing or resuming it (resuming takes up a slot again), changing its settings, editing the persona and switching its personality on or off. | **Ventas** |
| `library` | Viewing, uploading and reading Library files, linking them to an agent (to send them or for its memory) or unlinking them, and changing their send trigger. Reading the text in an image spends credits. | **Ventas** |
| `operations` | Replying as the operator — **it really goes out over WhatsApp** —, changing who replies in a chat, viewing, scheduling and cancelling reminders, and deleting a tag from all customers. | **Ventas** |
| `manual_ai` | Optimizing the playbook with AI. It doesn't save: it returns suggestions. Spends credits. | **Ventas** |
| `testing` | Testing the agent. In the dashboard's test chat nothing goes out over WhatsApp; simulating a message in a real chat, or opening a new one with a number, **does really go out over WhatsApp**. Each reply from the agent spends credits. | **Ventas** |
| `orders` | Reading orders and stock. Read-only. | Orders, **Órdenes** ("Orders"). Stock, **Inventario** ("Inventory"). |
| `metrics` | AI usage per day and the agent's results per week or month. | Usage, **Ver saldo y facturación**. Results, **Ventas**. |
| `copilot` | Asking the dashboard's copilot. Spends credits. | **Copiloto** ("Copilot") |

<Warning>
**An explicit list REPLACES the default set, it does not extend it.**
Connecting with `?features=orders` leaves you with `orders` and `core`
**only**: you lose playbook, catalog and conversations.

To add without losing anything, the simplest option is `?features=all`. If you
prefer a list, also name the ones that came by default:
`?features=manual,catalog,conversations,orders`.
</Warning>

Three more details:

- **`core` travels with any URL**, even if you don't name it.
- **Writing brings reading.** `manual_write` also brings `manual`, and
  `catalog_write` brings `catalog`: to rewrite something, your assistant has to
  be able to read it first.
- **A misspelled name is ignored without warning.** If no valid one is left in
  your list, you go back to the default set.

**`?features=all`** turns on all the groups, including the ones we publish
later on. It's the URL the dashboard gives you and the one in the quickstarts:
the one you don't have to touch again.

**`all` doesn't give you more permissions.** Out of all those groups, your
assistant gets only what your account can use in that business. In exchange,
it also connects the destructive writes. Those actions travel marked as
destructive, and assistants usually use that mark to ask you for confirmation.
If you want it only to look, add `&read_only=true`.

## `?read_only=true` — the handbrake

It disables **every** write and overrides any other option, `all` included.
It's for letting your assistant look without being able to touch.

```
https://mcp.darkfunnels.ai/mcp?features=all&read_only=true
```

These count as writes, so they don't travel either: asking the copilot,
optimizing the playbook with AI, opening the test chat and logging a request.
If your URL brings `library` (or `all`), reading the text of a Library file is
still available, and with an image it spends credits.

<Warning>
Write it exactly like this: `read_only=true`. With `True`, `TRUE` or `1` **it
doesn't turn on**, and the connection keeps its writes.
</Warning>

## `?pii=full` — full phone numbers

By default, your customers' phone number arrives masked: `51•••••4321`. It
keeps the country hint and the last four digits, enough to recognize a number
you already know. With `pii=full` it arrives whole:

```
https://mcp.darkfunnels.ai/mcp?features=all&pii=full
```

The masking applies to the conversation list, the customer list, a customer's
profile and orders. **What gets masked is the phone number.** The name, the ID
document and the address on an order, and what your customers write in their
messages, arrive as they are.

Having it masked hardly gets in the way. The tools that act on a chat find it by
the conversation, not by the number. The ones that do ask for a number —
simulating a new chat or changing the number lists in the agent's settings —
ask you for it, and you type it yourself. What you lose is convenience, in two
ways: your assistant can't give you the full number (the panel does show it),
and although customer search accepts a phone number, a masked number can't be
used to search.

Any misspelled variant, like `pii=FULL`, leaves the phone masked. The details
and the why, in [Security](/en/security).

## `?agent=<uuid>` — pinning an agent

If you have several sales agents, almost every tool needs to know which one you
mean. This parameter sets one as the connection's **default agent**, and your
assistant stops asking you. With a single agent you don't need it: that one is
used.

```
https://mcp.darkfunnels.ai/mcp?features=all&agent=8f14e45f-ceea-467a-9c1d-2d4a6bcbb3f5
```

Your own assistant gives you the identifier: ask it *"give me my agents'
identifiers"*. It has to be complete, with its hyphens. If it doesn't have that
shape, it's ignored without warning and your assistant asks you about the agent
again.

Pinning it doesn't lock you in: if you expressly ask for another agent, it works
with that one.

## What the URL does not decide

### Your permissions and your business's apps

Your assistant gets in **with your account and with your permissions**. Of
what the URL asks for, it gets the tools of the apps that you can use in that
business and that the business has active. And if another one ever appeared to
it, it couldn't use it. It's the rule of your dashboard menu: whatever your
account can't open there, your assistant can't use either.

That's why two people with the same URL can see different lists. For example,
someone on your team who only has **Órdenes**, connected with the bare URL,
sees only the basics: who they are, which business they work in, the quick
product help and logging requests. To read orders they need `?features=orders`
or `all`. For stock, they also need access to **Inventario**.

- **If your assistant tries a tool without permission, or from an app that's
  switched off**, it gets a notice that it doesn't have that access and does
  nothing. The notice may name the app differently from the switch: for orders
  it says «Pedidos», while the switch is called **Órdenes** (both mean
  "Orders"); for the balance it says «Saldo y consumo» ("Balance and usage"),
  while the switch is **Ver saldo y facturación**.
- **Permissions are given by whoever manages your team**, in
  [Personas y accesos](/en/getting-started/invite-your-team#what-each-person-can-see-and-do).
  Apps are switched on in **Administración → Apps** ("Administration → Apps").
- **If you were just given a permission**, wait a minute and open a new chat in
  your assistant.

### The business the connection works in

If your account is in
[several businesses](/en/getting-started/invite-your-team#one-account-in-several-businesses),
**each connection stays pinned to the business you had active when you
connected it**, and it works with your permissions in that business. If your
account is in only one, this changes nothing for you.

If your active business changes later on, because you chose another one in
your account menu or because you accepted another business's invitation (when
you accept it, that business becomes the active one):

- **Its writes are rejected without running**, so that nothing gets saved in
  the wrong business. The notice says which business the connection works in
  and which one you have active now.
- **Its reads keep working**, but they show the data of the business you have
  active now.

<Warning>
**After switching business, what your assistant reads is already from the
other one.** If you were comparing figures, first go back to the connection's
business.
</Warning>

To make it save changes again, do one of two things:

1. **Choose that business again** in your account menu, with **Cambiar a esta
   empresa** ("Switch to this business"). The connection carries on as before,
   without reconnecting.
2. **Disconnect the connector and connect it again.** The new connection stays
   pinned to the business you have active when you connect it: if you want to
   work in another one, switch first in your account menu.

If your access to the connection's business is removed, its writes are rejected
too. To work in another business, disconnect and connect again.

Not sure where it's working? Ask it *"which business are you working in?"*. It
has a tool for that, which also says which permissions it works with and
whether the active business is still the connection's.

### Usage limits

- **Per account.** If your assistant goes over 60 requests in a minute or 300
  in an hour, the bridge holds it back and replies *«rate limit; reintenta en
  N s»* ("rate limit; retry in N s"). It counts what you do with all your
  assistants together. It happens above all with batch jobs, like reviewing
  hundreds of chats in a row. **The wait is the one N says**: a few seconds if
  it went over the per-minute cap, and up to an hour if it went over the hourly
  one. For big jobs, ask it to go in parts.
- **Per business.** There's a daily quota of tool calls — by default, 20,000 —
  that resets at midnight, Lima time. If it runs out, your assistant tells you.
  If your business needs more, [write to us](/en/security#how-to-reach-us).

## Changing the URL afterwards

Changing any of these parameters requires **reconnecting**: in your assistant,
delete the connector you had and add it with the new URL. Careful if you're in
several businesses: the new connection stays pinned to the business you have
active when you connect it. Choose the business in your account menu first.

Don't confuse that with new tools. **The tool list is frozen per
conversation**: if we add one, or you were just given a permission, and it
doesn't show up, opening a new chat is normally enough. If it still doesn't
show up in a new chat, delete the connector and connect it again.

Reconnecting is needed when the URL changes, when you want the connection to
work in another business and after revoking access.

### Cutting off access

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Asistentes IA">
    In the dashboard, go to **Personalizar → Asistentes IA** and scroll down to
    the **Seguridad y acceso** ("Security and access") card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Revocar">
    Under **Asistentes con acceso a tu cuenta** ("Assistants with access to
    your account") you see each assistant you authorized, with the date. Click
    **Revocar** ("Revoke") next to the one you want to cut off and confirm with
    **Revocar**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Done">
    That assistant loses access to your account within a few seconds. To use it
    again, you'll have to connect it again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Personalizar is part of **Ventas**: if you don't see it in your menu, ask
whoever manages your team for access to Ventas so you can revoke from here. In
the meantime, remove the connector in your assistant.

<Frame caption="«Seguridad y acceso» (Security and access): how the connection is protected and the «Asistentes con acceso a tu cuenta» (Assistants with access to your account) list. In this screenshot it is empty; each assistant you authorize shows up there with its «Revocar» (Revoke) button.">
  <img src="/images/asistentes/03-seguridad-y-acceso.png" alt="Seguridad y acceso (Security and access) card with three bullet points about the connection and the Asistentes con acceso a tu cuenta (Assistants with access to your account) list, which reads Ningún asistente tiene acceso a tu cuenta ahora (No assistant has access to your account right now)" />
</Frame>

Revoking cuts off the access, but the connector stays in your assistant. To
remove it from there too: in claude.ai, **Customize → Connectors**; in Codex,
**Settings → MCP servers**.

## If something goes wrong

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I added a parameter and my assistant lost features">
    It's usually one of two things. Either you put a list in `?features=` and
    it replaced the default set, or when reconnecting you left out what you had
    before. Rebuild the URL with everything together, for example
    `?features=all&pii=full`, and reconnect.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My assistant doesn't see a tool it should">
    Check in this order:

    1. **The group isn't in your URL, or your URL has `read_only=true`**, which
       removes all writes. Look for it in the tables above.
    2. **You don't have access to that app, or it's switched off in your
       business.** Ask it *"which business are you working in, and with what
       permissions?"*.
    3. **It's an old chat.** Open a new one.
    4. **It still doesn't show up.** Delete the connector and connect it again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="It won't save: it says the account changed business">
    Your active business changed after you connected: you switched it in your
    account menu or accepted an invitation from another business. Go back to
    that business from your account menu, or disconnect and reconnect the
    connector to work in the one you have active.
    → [The business the connection works in](#the-business-the-connection-works-in)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I changed the URL and nothing changed">
    First, did you reconnect? Changing the URL requires deleting the connector
    and adding it again. If you did, check how it's written. A misspelled group
    is ignored, and `read_only=True` or `pii=FULL` have no effect: they go in
    lowercase. An incomplete `agent=` is ignored too. Fix it and reconnect.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="It can't see your data, or sees it empty">
    The most common cause is that you authorized with another account. Ask it
    *"which DarkFunnels account are you connected to?"*. If it isn't yours,
    delete the connector, go into the dashboard at `optimind.darkfunnels.ai`
    with the right account (sign out of the other session first if needed) and
    connect it again. When you authorize, the screen says «Autorizas como»
    ("You are authorizing as") and your email: check that it's yours. If the
    account is the right one, ask it which business it's working in.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="It says «rate limit; reintenta en N s» (rate limit; retry in N s)">
    It made too many requests in a row. Wait the time it says: a few seconds if
    it went over the per-minute cap, or up to an hour if it went over the hourly
    one. If it's working in batch, ask it to go in parts.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="It says the business has reached its daily quota">
    It resets at midnight, Lima time. If your business needs more,
    [write to us](/en/security#how-to-reach-us).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
