The DarkFunnels words this documentation uses as if you already knew them, in a
few lines each. If you run into one mid-page, come back here.

The dashboard is in Spanish, so menus, buttons and labels are named here the
way you'll see them on screen, with the English translation in parentheses.

## They look alike, but they're not the same

These pairs confuse almost everyone in their first month. **If you're torn
between two words, start with this table.**

| This… | …is not this | The difference |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Copilot** (*Copiloto*) | **Your assistant** | The copilot lives inside the dashboard. Your assistant is Claude, ChatGPT or Codex connected from outside, and it's optional. |
| **Agent memory** (*Memoria del agente*) | **Long-Term Memory** (*Memoria a Largo Plazo*) | The first is a per-file permission, in the Library. The second is a knowledge document you edit in the Playbook. |
| **Agent memory** | **Copilot memory** | The agent's is used by your WhatsApp salesperson. The copilot's is notes for the copilot, in its **Memoria** (*Memory*) tab. |
| **Test chat** (*Chat de prueba*) | **Test numbers** (*Números de prueba*) | The test chat doesn't go out over WhatsApp. Test numbers are real phones your agent answers as if they were a customer. |
| **Autopilot** (*Piloto automático*) | **Activo / Pausado** (*Active / Paused*) | Autopilot is switched off in **one** conversation. Activo / Pausado switches off the whole agent, on its card on Inicio (*Home*). |
| **Red de referidos** (*Referral network*) | **Referidos** (*Referrals*) | The network is **your customers'** referral program. Referidos is your code for inviting other businesses to DarkFunnels. |
| **Agent tool** | **Bridge tool** | The agent's is used by your salesperson on WhatsApp. The bridge's is used by your assistant to work on your business. |
| **Dar de baja** (*Unsubscribe*) | **Borrar datos de usuario** (*Delete user data*) | Unsubscribing can be reversed and applies to your whole business. Deleting data can't be undone and affects that customer with a single agent. |

## The basics

</Frame>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Dashboard">
    The web app you sign in to with your email:
    [optimind.darkfunnels.ai](https://optimind.darkfunnels.ai). Almost
    everything this documentation covers happens there. The exceptions are
    some steps in the pixel and ads guides, which are done on Meta's screens,
    and whatever is marked "from your assistant".
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Inicio (Home)">
    The first screen in the menu. It's the day's summary, and it's built from
    your apps: your agents and their numbers, the customers waiting for a
    reply, your appointments. Don't look for a "Dashboard" entry: it's called
    **Inicio** (*Home*).

    → [Your first look](/en/getting-started/first-look)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="App">
    A piece of the dashboard that's switched on or off **for your whole
    business**. The menu is organized by app. **Ventas** (*Sales*),
    **Calendario** (*Calendar*), **Inventario** (*Inventory*) and **Órdenes**
    (*Orders*) come switched on when you open the account; **Envíos**
    (*Shipping*) and **Facturación electrónica** (*Electronic invoicing*) you
    install yourself in **Administración → Apps** (*Administration → Apps*).

    That's why two businesses see different menus, and so do two people in
    the same business: each one sees the apps they have access to. Some apps
    only appear in certain accounts.

    → [The DarkFunnels apps](/en/getting-started/the-apps)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Agent">
    Your automatic salesperson. It has a name it introduces itself with (its
    **persona**), a script (its **playbook**) and **its own WhatsApp number**:
    there's no WhatsApp "for the account". That number is connected in
    **Ventas → Personalizar → Canales** (*Sales → Customize → Channels*). If
    you sell two very different things, that's two agents.

    → [Your first agent](/en/getting-started/your-first-agent)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Funnel">
    **Another name for the whole agent.** The dashboard uses both words
    (*agente* and *embudo*): the menu on the agent's card says **Duplicar
    embudo** (*Duplicate funnel*) and **Compartir embudo…** (*Share funnel…*),
    and when you delete it, it asks *«¿Eliminar el embudo…?»* (*Delete the
    funnel…?*). Sometimes «embudo» refers only to its script: the sales
    conversation written in chapters. When a screen says «embudo», think of
    the agent.

    → [Your funnels](/en/guides/your-funnels)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Agent selector">
    The dropdown under **Ventas** in the menu, with an agent's photo (or
    initial) and name. It decides which agent you're working with inside
    Ventas: the playbook, the conversations or the customers you see are that
    agent's. **If a list comes up empty, first check which agent is
    selected.**
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Copilot">
    The AI that lives **inside** the dashboard. It opens with its button in the
    top bar or with **⌘J** (**Ctrl+J** on Windows). It helps you build and
    correct your agent, and it can read, search and analyze your
    conversations.

    When it's going to change something in your business, it usually proposes
    it on a card with **Aprobar** (*Approve*) and **Rechazar** (*Reject*). It
    has three tabs: **Chat**, **Sesiones** (*Sessions*: your previous
    conversations with it) and **Memoria** (*Memory*: notes you want it to
    remember). It consumes balance.

    Don't confuse it with "your assistant" (Claude or ChatGPT connected from
    outside).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Setup (puesta en marcha)">
    The conversation with the copilot that creates your first agent. If you
    leave it halfway, your progress stays saved: you pick it up from the
    copilot's **Sesiones** tab (*«Tu puesta en marcha»*, Your setup) or with
    **Crear agente** (*Create agent*).

    → [Your first agent](/en/getting-started/your-first-agent)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Guíame (Guide me)">
    The copilot mode that points out on screen where to click. You open it
    from the **Ayuda** (*Help*) menu (the question mark in the top bar) →
    **Guíame paso a paso** (*Guide me step by step*). It consumes balance,
    like the rest of the copilot.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Pendientes (Pending)">
    A card on Inicio with what's waiting for **an action from you**: for
    example, a copilot proposal to approve. Tapping a row takes you straight
    to resolving it. If there's nothing pending, the card doesn't appear.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## The script

<Frame caption="The Manual (Playbook): on the left, the chapter index; at the top, the Historial (History), Recursos (Resources), Doctor and Probar (Test) buttons; on the right, the Recursos panel open.">
  <img src="/images/manual/01-editor.png" alt="Playbook editor with the six-chapter index on the left, chapters 1 and 2 in the middle, the button bar at the top and the agent's Recursos (Resources) panel open on the right" />
</Frame>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Playbook (Manual)">
    The agent's script, and the screen where it's edited (**Ventas → Manual**,
    *Sales → Playbook*). It's what you'll touch most. It keeps a
    **Historial** (*History*) of versions, so you can go back.

    → [The sales playbook](/en/guides/sales-playbook)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Chapter">
    Each stretch of the script: greeting and understanding what they're after,
    presenting the product, settling the price, closing and taking the
    details. The agent moves from chapter to chapter according to what
    happens in the conversation.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Shared section (§)">
    A block of the playbook marked with the **§** symbol that applies to
    several chapters at once: you write it once and it serves all the ones you
    choose. A chip tells you which ones it applies to (*«aplica a capítulos
    1–2, 4»*, applies to chapters 1–2, 4). In a playbook born from the
    Ecommerce template you'll see it as **the shared block**, with your
    business details.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Ecommerce template">
    The starting point for an agent that sells over WhatsApp: a shared block
    and three chapters (**Saludo**, **En venta** and **Pedido confirmado** —
    *Greeting*, *Selling* and *Order confirmed*) with your business's details
    left to fill in. It's created when you build a sales agent with the
    copilot. If your account has it and a sales agent wasn't born from it,
    with an empty playbook or a single chapter, you can load it from a
    computer with **Cargar plantilla de Ecommerce** (*Load the Ecommerce
    template*).

    → [Your first agent](/en/getting-started/your-first-agent)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Placeholder (hueco)">
    In a template playbook, each of your details left to fill in, written
    between « » (for example `«PRODUCTO_MARCA»`). The editor highlights them,
    and the Playbook's **Huecos** (*Placeholders*) button lists them so you
    can fill them in. **While any are left, you can't connect that agent's
    WhatsApp.**
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Turn">
    Each time your agent replies. **It doesn't answer message by message**: it
    gathers what the customer sends in a row and answers it all at once. If
    they send you three messages in a row, one after another, they normally
    get one reply, not three.

    There are also turns that don't come from a new customer message: a
    reminder or a scheduled follow-up, or **Responder con IA** (*Reply with
    AI*).

    A turn carries at most 12 messages, and files count among them. If the
    text doesn't fit, the ending is joined into the last message.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Marker">
    The mark that says **where** a file goes out inside a chapter. In the
    editor you insert it with the **@** menu → **Archivos** (*Files*) or with
    the **Recursos** (*Resources*) button; underneath, it's written like this:
    `###SEND_FILES: catalogo###`. Written correctly, the customer doesn't see
    it: it's replaced by the file.

    If it's written wrong, it may come out as-is in the chat, or the file may
    not go out. That's why it's best to insert it with **@** or with
    **Recursos** and not by hand. Note: the marker decides where the file goes
    out; the **permission** to send it is given in the Library.

    → [The file Library](/en/guides/library)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Tool (the agent's)">
    Each thing your agent can **do** besides writing: record the sale, hand
    the chat to a person, save a piece of data, schedule a follow-up, look up
    a price. Some you insert into the playbook with the **@** menu →
    **Herramientas** (*Tools*) (underneath, they stay as an exact sentence,
    along the lines of
    `Ejecuta la herramienta "Recordatorio Tool" con el query "…"`); others the
    agent uses on its own. Not to be confused with the **bridge** tools,
    below.

    → [The agent's tools](/en/guides/tools)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Doctor">
    The **Doctor** button in the Playbook's bar. It lists the problems it
    found in your playbook and explains each one. Many come with a button that
    fixes them in one click; the rest you correct yourself, and with **Ir al
    sitio** (*Go to the spot*) you jump to that part of the playbook.

    → [The sales playbook](/en/guides/sales-playbook)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Test chat">
    A conversation with your agent that **doesn't go out over WhatsApp**: you
    write as if you were the customer and it answers right there. In the
    Playbook, the **Probar** (*Test*) button opens it; with an empty inbox,
    **Pruébalo en el chat de prueba** (*Try it in the test chat*).

    The **Resultados** (*Results*) screen doesn't count it, but the agent's
    card on Inicio does: if the agent records a sale during the test, that
    sale adds to its numbers. And it consumes balance, because the agent
    thinks just as it does with a real customer.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## What the agent uses

<Frame caption="In the Library, each file has two independent switches: «enviar por WhatsApp» (send via WhatsApp) and «memoria del agente» (agent memory).">
  <img src="/images/libreria/05-tabla-recien-subido.png" alt="Library table with the enviar por WhatsApp (send via WhatsApp) and memoria del agente (agent memory) switches, the send trigger and the status" />
</Frame>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Catalog">
    Your products with their prices, their variants (sizes, colors…), their
    stock and their photos. It's where your agent gets exact prices from when
    the customer asks "how much is it?". It's in **Ventas**, under
    **Comercio** (*Commerce*).

    → [The catalog](/en/guides/catalog)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Library (Librería)">
    The business's files: photos, PDF catalogs, price lists, audio, videos. In
    the menu it's called **Librería** (*Library*) and the screen is titled
    **«Archivos»** (*Files*). Each file is uploaded **once for your whole
    business**, and each agent has its own permissions over it.

    The screen has three tabs: **Archivos**; **Ubicaciones** (*Locations*),
    your business's branches that the agent sends as a map pin; and
    **Papelera** (*Trash*), where you can restore whatever you sent there
    (after about 30 days, it's deleted). An image can be marked as a WhatsApp
    **sticker**.

    → [The file Library](/en/guides/library)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Send trigger (condición de envío)">
    A file's nickname: the word you call it by from the script. By default
    it's the file name without the extension. Despite its Spanish name
    («condición», *condition*), **it's not a sentence in prose**: to hit the
    right file by nickname, the marker has to say the nickname **exactly and
    in full**, with no extra or missing words (capitals, accents, hyphens and
    underscores make no difference). If it doesn't match anything, the system
    may fall back to a word-similarity match and **deliver a different
    file**.

    → [The file Library](/en/guides/library)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Agent memory (memoria del agente)">
    Permission for the agent to **read** a file from the Library in order to
    answer questions about it. It's given per file and per agent, in the
    **memoria del agente** (*agent memory*) column.

    **It's independent of «enviar por WhatsApp» (send via WhatsApp).** With
    memory and no sending, the agent learns what the file says but doesn't
    send it. If you switch both on, it does both. Having the agent read a file
    consumes a little balance.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Long-Term Memory (RAG)">
    A knowledge document separate from the playbook, of up to 200,000
    characters, that your agent consults when a customer asks something. It
    opens in the Playbook, with the **Memoria a Largo Plazo (RAG)**
    (*Long-Term Memory (RAG)*) button next to the persona (in older agents it
    may not appear). The difference in one line: **the playbook is
    instructions; the memory is knowledge.**

    → [The sales playbook](/en/guides/sales-playbook)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Persona">
    The identity your agent adopts: its name, its role, its personality and,
    if you set them up, its face and its voice. **It's not a flesh-and-blood
    person.** In the Playbook it's chosen in the **Persona activa** (*Active
    persona*) panel.

    Note: on Inicio, when there are no conversations to count yet, the
    headline says *«Personas que venden por ti»* (*People who sell for you*).
    There, "people" means your agents.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## The agent's settings

Each agent has its own settings screen: **Ventas → Configuración** (*Sales →
Settings*).

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Business details (Datos del negocio)">
    Your Yape, your account, the account holder, your social media… written
    once. In the Playbook you insert them with the **@** menu and the agent
    reads the value from Configuración: **if you change banks, you change it
    in one place.** When you share the funnel with another account, these
    values don't travel.

    → [Agent settings](/en/guides/agent-settings)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Frequent numbers (Números frecuentes)">
    Your business's contact book. You save them once and in the Playbook you
    insert them by name with **@**. From this list you choose who's notified
    of each sale (**Informar de las ventas**, *Report sales*), who gets the
    summary every morning (**Reportes**, *Reports*) and who receives the
    **Alertas de error** (*Error alerts*).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Test numbers (Números de prueba)">
    Phones your agent answers **as if they were a customer**, even if they're
    also in Reportes, Informar de las ventas or Alertas de error. They're for
    testing your funnel over real WhatsApp. Your business's WhatsApp lines
    don't work as test numbers: test from a personal phone. Not to be confused
    with the **test chat**, which doesn't go out over WhatsApp.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Deletion phrase (Frase de borrado)">
    The word that, if a customer writes it exactly, deletes their data with
    that agent; their next message starts a new conversation. By default it's
    «borrar» (*delete*; capitals make no difference). It's changed in
    Configuración. It's useful, for example, to repeat a test from the
    beginning.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Conversations

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Conversations (the inbox)">
    The screen with the selected agent's chats, one by one: **Ventas →
    Conversaciones** (*Sales → Conversations*; on the phone, **Chats**). Above
    the list it reads **Centro de mensajería** (*Messaging center*).

    → [Conversations](/en/guides/conversations)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Waiting for a reply (Esperando respuesta)">
    A customer who wrote and whom nobody has answered yet: the last message is
    theirs. In the inbox you filter them with **Esperando respuesta**
    (*Waiting for a reply*), and on Inicio they're counted by the **Esperan
    respuesta** card.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Autopilot (manual mode)">
    The switch for **each conversation**, in the chat header (**Piloto
    automático**, *Autopilot*). Off, the agent stops answering in that
    conversation and you handle it; in the list, the row carries the
    **Manual** mark. You switch it off, or the agent itself does when it hands
    the chat to a person.

    To switch off the whole agent, the switch is a different one: **Activo /
    Pausado** (*Active / Paused*), on its card on Inicio.

    → [Conversations](/en/guides/conversations)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Responder con IA (Reply with AI)">
    In Conversaciones, you pick customers who were left without a reply and
    the AI answers them one by one, roughly one per minute. Before it starts,
    it asks you to tick that you understand your number may be banned. Each
    reply consumes balance.

    If you don't see the **Seleccionar** (*Select*) button above the list,
    it isn't available in your account yet, or that agent is paused or has no
    WhatsApp line.

    → [Conversations](/en/guides/conversations)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Customer record (ficha)">
    What you know about a customer **with one agent**: their details, their
    internal notes, their conversions. You open it from Conversaciones or from
    **Clientes** (*Customers*). **It's per agent:** if the same number writes
    to two of your agents, it has two records, one in each.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Variables and captured data">
    The details your agent saves about each customer while it chats: the
    name, the city, the product they ordered, whatever you've defined
    yourself. You see and correct them in the record, under **Datos
    capturados** (*Captured data*), and they appear as columns in
    **Clientes**, **Tabla** (*Table*) view.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Dar de baja and Borrar datos de usuario">
    Two buttons on the record that look alike and do very different things:

    - **Dar de baja al contacto** (*Unsubscribe the contact*): that number
      stops receiving messages, from the agent and from you, on **all** your
      business's channels. It can be reactivated from the same record with
      **Reactivar contacto** (*Reactivate contact*), but only by whoever
      manages your business's apps; for everyone else, the dashboard refuses
      it.
    - **Borrar datos de usuario** (*Delete user data*): deletes that
      customer's data **with this agent**: their conversations, their
      messages, their record and the sales or appointments recorded with
      them, which stop counting in your results. Their orders stay in
      Órdenes, but without their name or address: they now say «Cliente
      borrado» (*Deleted customer*). It can't be undone, and whatever they
      have with your other agents isn't touched.

    → [Conversations](/en/guides/conversations)
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Money and results

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Credits / balance">
    What the AI consumes when it works, measured in dollars: each reply from
    your agent, the audio it transcribes, the photos it interprets, the
    documents it reads and the copilot's work. Having WhatsApp connected
    doesn't consume anything.

    The cut-off comes before your balance runs out: when it drops below
    **US\$ 0.10** (a small safety reserve), your agents stop answering new
    turns even if you see something left.

    → [Credits and top-ups](/en/getting-started/credits-and-top-ups)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Trial and subscription">
    **The trial** lasts 7 days. Its clock runs from sign-up, but **it starts
    over with the full 7 days when you connect your WhatsApp**. If you don't
    get to connect, it expires on the date set when you signed up. When it
    ends, your agents stop answering even if you have balance left, and
    topping up doesn't fix it: you need to **subscribe**.

    **The subscription** is the monthly payment that keeps your agents
    switched on, and its base comes back in full to your balance each month
    you pay it. Balance is consumed with use, and you top it up whenever you
    like.

    → [Pricing](/en/getting-started/pricing)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Conversion">
    A recorded result of a conversation: a **sale** (*venta*), a **service**
    (*servicio*), an **appointment** (*cita*) or a **lead**. Your agent
    records it while it chats, or your team does from the customer's record
    with **Registrar conversión** (*Record conversion*; the AI helps you fill
    it in). Some are recorded on their own, like the one for an appointment
    confirmed in Calendario (*Calendar*).

    It's the unit results are measured in, and a cancelled conversion stops
    counting. Note: if your funnel books appointments or captures leads,
    "conversions" isn't the same as "sales".

    → [Orders and metrics](/en/guides/orders-and-metrics)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Order (pedido)">
    What has to be collected and delivered for a sale. It lives in the
    **Órdenes** (*Orders*) app. With Órdenes active, when your agent records a
    sale with its products and their prices, its order opens. If the sale
    comes in without products, or one of them is missing its price, it's
    recorded anyway, but without an order.

    Cancelling that sale from the record also cancels its order, and for that
    you need access to Órdenes. If the order is already on its way to
    delivery (waybill issued or stock moved), the sale is cancelled but the
    order isn't: you review that one yourself in Órdenes.

    → [Orders and metrics](/en/guides/orders-and-metrics)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Red de referidos (Referral network)">
    **Your customers'** referral program, in **Ventas → Red de referidos**:
    who recommended whom, which deposits came in and which rewards you owe at
    each level of the network. It's per agent.

    → [Referral network](/en/guides/referral-network)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Referidos (DarkFunnels referrals)">
    In **Administración → Referidos** (*Administration → Referrals*): your
    code for inviting other businesses to DarkFunnels. When one signs up with
    it and activates its subscription, you earn **US\$ 15 in credit** for that
    payment and for each of its renewals, up to 6 charges. It has nothing to
    do with your customers' Red de referidos.

    → [Credits and top-ups](/en/getting-started/credits-and-top-ups)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Meta pixel">
    The connection that tells Meta when a customer meets one of your
    **rules**: reaches a chapter, buys, books an appointment… Each notice is
    an **event**, and it travels through the **Conversions API**, Meta's
    channel for notices that don't come from a web page. With that, Meta
    fine-tunes who it shows your ads to. It's connected in **Ventas →
    Personalizar → Píxeles** (*Sales → Customize → Pixels*).

    → [Connect your Meta pixel](/en/guides/meta-pixel)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Qualified chapter and «rinde» (yield)">
    Two words from the **Anuncios** (*Ads*) page, which isn't in every account
    yet: if you don't see it in your menu, it isn't available in yours yet.

    - **Capítulo calificado** (*Qualified chapter*): the chapter of each
      funnel from which a chat that came in through an ad counts as a serious
      customer. You choose it with the **Capítulo calificado** button.
    - **«rinde»** (*yield*): how much each ad brings you for what you spend on
      it, compared with your average. Depending on what your business already
      measures, it compares sales, qualified leads or chats.

    → [Connect your Meta ads](/en/guides/meta-ads)
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Your funnels

All three actions are on Inicio, in the menu on each agent's card.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Duplicar embudo (Duplicate funnel)">
    Creates a copy of the agent **in your same business**: its playbook, its
    persona, its settings and its Library permissions. It doesn't copy
    conversations or contacts. It needs a free agent slot, just like creating
    a new one. If you don't see this option, ask whoever manages your business
    for it.

    → [Your funnels](/en/guides/your-funnels)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Compartir embudo (Share funnel)">
    Sends a copy of the agent **to another DarkFunnels account**: you type
    that account's email and a request reaches them. Afterwards you can also
    copy the link and send it to them over WhatsApp; if they don't have an
    account yet, they create it from that same link. If the other person
    accepts, the funnel is copied into their account, ready to connect their
    WhatsApp. The playbook, the persona, the files, the catalog, the
    variables, the labels, the schedule and the settings travel; the
    conversations, the contacts, the orders, the connections and your
    notification numbers don't.

    **The playbook travels as-is**: if it has your Yape or your numbers
    written in it, the other person will see them.

    → [Your funnels](/en/guides/your-funnels)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Deleting an agent">
    In the menu it's called **Eliminar agente…** (*Delete agent…*), and the
    confirmation asks *«¿Eliminar el embudo…?»* (*Delete the funnel…?*). It's
    permanent: its conversations, its playbook, its contacts and the sales it
    recorded in its chats are deleted, and its WhatsApp session is closed.
    Its persona is deleted too, unless another of your agents uses it.

    **The files in your Library stay** for your other agents: only this
    agent's permission goes. The orders in Órdenes and the usage already
    charged to you also stay. If it has contacts, sales or notes, a copy is
    saved in Excel before deleting.

    → [Your funnels](/en/guides/your-funnels)
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Your team and your businesses

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Business (empresa)">
    Your business inside DarkFunnels: its apps, its team, its balance and its
    agents. A single account — the email you sign in with — can be in several
    businesses: yours and that of a client who invited you, for example. In
    each one you can have different access.

    → [Invite your team](/en/getting-started/invite-your-team)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Active business">
    The business you're working in right now: what you see — conversations,
    customers, balance — belongs to that business. If your account is in more
    than one, you switch from your account menu (the circle at the top
    right): the current one says **Estás aquí** (*You're here*) and the
    others, **Cambiar a esta empresa** (*Switch to this business*).

    **The active business belongs to your account, not to the tab**: if you
    switch on your phone, you also switch on your computer.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Per-person permissions (access)">
    What each person on your team can use. They're given **app by app**, with
    one switch per active app — also for the copilot and for seeing the
    balance and billing — in **Administración → Personas y accesos**
    (*Administration → People and access*). Whoever creates the account gets
    full access.

    Ventas is given whole: whoever has it sees the conversations and the
    customers of all your agents.

    → [Invite your team](/en/getting-started/invite-your-team)
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Only if you connect Claude or ChatGPT

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Your assistant">
    Claude, ChatGPT, Codex or another compatible client, connected to your
    business **from outside** the dashboard. It's optional. In this
    documentation, "your assistant" means that; the dashboard copilot is
    something else. It's connected in **Ventas → Personalizar → Asistentes
    IA** (*Sales → Customize → AI assistants*).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="MCP and “the bridge”">
    **MCP** is the standard Claude and ChatGPT use to connect to external
    tools. **The bridge** is our connection to that standard: what lets your
    assistant read and change things in your business, with your
    authorization.

    The same standard works the other way round: in **Ventas → Personalizar →
    Herramientas → Añadir servidor MCP** (*Sales → Customize → Tools → Add MCP
    server*) you give your agent tools from another service.

    → [The connection URL](/en/reference/connection-url) · [Security](/en/security) · [Your own tools (MCP)](/en/guides/tools#your-own-tools-mcp)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Tool (the bridge's)">
    Each specific action your assistant can run over the bridge: listing
    files, reading the playbook, recording something. You don't write them;
    you ask for them in your own language. They're a separate list from the
    **agent's** tools.

    → [Tool reference](/en/reference/tools)
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
