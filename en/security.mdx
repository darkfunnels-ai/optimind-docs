You are about to connect the WhatsApp number your customers buy from. Before
that you deserve concrete answers, not a brochure. This page gives them, the
uncomfortable ones included.

The dashboard is in Spanish, so buttons, menus and on-screen messages are named
here the way you'll see them, with the English meaning in parentheses the first
time they appear.

<Note>
**"Customer"** on this page always means **the person who writes to you on
WhatsApp**. When we mean the people in your business we will say **your team**.
And **your assistant** is Claude, ChatGPT or Codex connected from outside the
dashboard: it is optional and only shows up in the last section.
</Note>

## The short answers

| The question | The short answer |
| --- | --- |
| Can another business see my chats? | No. In the inbox and conversations the lock is set by the database itself; in **Órdenes** (*Orders*), **Envíos** (*Shipping*), **Inventario** (*Inventory*), **Facturación electrónica** (*Electronic invoicing*) and **Calendario** (*Calendar*), by a filter in the application (section 1). |
| And the people on my team? | **Only those who have the Ventas (*Sales*) app.** And whoever has it sees **every** conversation in your business: there is no permission per chat or per funnel. |
| And someone from DarkFunnels? | **Yes, to give support**, with a tool that reaches every business, yours included. And a WhatsApp number of our team receives copies of your customers' photos and documents, of every sale and of the morning summary (sections 1 and 2). |
| Do my chats leave DarkFunnels? | Yes, to the AI that answers them (OpenAI or Google, depending on the model). The table below goes item by item. |
| Do they train on my data? | What we can honestly state has a section of its own. We don't over-promise. |
| How long are my messages kept? | **Indefinitely**, until you or your customer delete them. |
| And my files? | In a private store. The dashboard opens them with links that expire after 24 hours (section 7). |
| Can I delete a customer? | Yes, with a button, funnel by funnel. And they can also ask for it over WhatsApp. |
| Is there two-step verification? | **Not today.** We cover it in section 1. |

## 1. Who can see my customers' chats?

### Between businesses: a lock and a filter

In the inbox and in conversations, the lock is set by the database itself on
every query: **it returns what belongs to the business you are working in, and
only if you have the Ventas app there** (the exception is grouped businesses,
which we cover further down). If the query does not arrive with your identity,
it returns nothing. There is no screen that has to "remember" to filter; the
filter sits underneath the screen.

In other areas — **Órdenes**, **Envíos**, **Inventario**, **Facturación
electrónica**, **Calendario** and the integrations in **Personalizar**
(*Customize*) — the server queries with a platform key. Before reading, it
checks that you have that app, and it is the application itself that adds your
business's filter to every read. The result you see is the same; the guarantee
is of a different kind, and it is only fair to say so.

<Check>
**There are automated tests that watch over the lock.** Every time a change to
the database is proposed, they check that each app's tables have their lock in
place and that the lock asks for that app's permission. An honest caveat: they
check **the shape** of the lock. Whether it separates businesses correctly is,
on top of that, reviewed by hand.
</Check>

### Inside your business: each person sees what you give them

Whoever administers your team decides, **person by person and app by app**,
what each one can use. It is done in **Personas y accesos** (*People and
access*), in the **Administración** (*Administration*) block of the menu. Each
person has one switch per app and a box, **Qué puede ver y hacer** (*What they
can see and do*), that sums it up in one sentence.

<Frame caption="Personas y accesos (People and access): a person's profile card with «Qué puede ver y hacer» (What they can see and do) and one switch per app.">
  <img src="/images/equipo/01-personas-y-accesos.png" alt="Personas y accesos (People and access) screen with the team pyramid and, on the right, a person's profile card with their switches per app" />
</Frame>

For chats, the one that rules is **Ventas**:

- **Without Ventas, that person does not see the chats**, nor the contacts, nor
  your funnels, nor their playbook. For example, someone you only gave
  **Órdenes** sees the orders and not the inbox.
- **With Ventas, they see every conversation in your business**, from all your
  funnels.

<Warning>
**Ventas is given whole.** There is no finer permission, neither per funnel nor
per conversation. **Assigning a chat to someone (*Asignar a…* — Assign to…) is
an on-screen notice, not a lock**: it does not stop someone else from opening
it. Give Ventas to whoever you would trust with your whole inbox.
</Warning>

Careful: other apps also show your customers' data. **Órdenes** brings the
name, phone and address of whoever bought; **Calendario**, the name and phone
of whoever booked. How to invite people and hand out access, step by step:
[Invite your team](/en/getting-started/invite-your-team).

Three more things worth knowing:

- **Each person's copilot is their own.** It works with that person's
  permissions: if they don't have Órdenes, their copilot doesn't read your
  orders either. And each person's conversation with their copilot is not seen
  by anyone else on the team, not even the owner. (DarkFunnels support can see
  it, as explained further down.) The change proposals and bulk jobs their
  copilot puts together are also seen by whoever has the copilot and the app
  that change affects.
- **A message that already went out can be edited or deleted.** Whoever has
  Ventas does it from the bubble's menu, on the messages that went out from
  your side (from your agent or your team). **Modificar mensaje** (*Edit
  message*) only works for text messages and within 15 minutes, the window
  WhatsApp gives. Both actions are first requested from WhatsApp, and **only if
  WhatsApp accepts them (and your line is connected) does anything change**; if
  it rejects them, everything stays as it was. When you edit, the stored text
  is replaced with the new one. When you delete, the message disappears from
  your customer's phone, your inbox keeps the mark *«Eliminaste este mensaje»*
  (*You deleted this message*) and your agent stops taking it into account,
  **but its text stays stored in DarkFunnels**: it is only hidden. It is truly
  deleted when you delete that customer's data, their funnel or your account
  (section 6). Your customer's messages cannot be touched.
- **If your account is in several businesses**, the dashboard shows you the
  business you are working in, with the permissions you have there. You switch
  it from your account menu.

<Note>
**If you have several businesses grouped together**, the group's
super-administrator sees the data of all of them. It is intentional, not a bug —
but if nobody tells you, it looks like a leak when you find out.
</Note>

### Your password is the key

**Today the dashboard has no two-step verification.** Whoever has your email and
your password gets into your account, with everything you can see. Three tips:

- **Use a password you don't use anywhere else.**
- **If you sign in with Google**, turn on two-step verification on your Google
  account: it also protects your way into the dashboard.
- **Have each person on your team sign in with their own account.** If you share
  yours, that person has exactly what you have.

### From inside DarkFunnels: the support team

This is not usually spelled out on a page like this. We would rather spell it
out ourselves.

<Warning>
**The DarkFunnels team can access your business's data to give you support**,
and that access is not limited to your business: it is a platform operations
tool. It includes being able to see what the AI read on a turn — the playbook,
that conversation's history and your customer's message —, your team's
conversations with the copilot, and correcting a piece of data that already
exists when something needs unblocking.
</Warning>

On top of that, a WhatsApp number of the DarkFunnels team receives copies of
some things from your business. We go into detail in section 2.

If your business handles information that should not be visible during a
support session, write to us before connecting and we'll talk it through.

If you need to know in writing who on our team has that access today, ask us.

### Inviting and removing people

You invite by email from **Personas y accesos**. What everyone asks:

- **When you invite, you choose what they will be able to use.** You can start
  from **Equipo de ventas** (*Sales team*) — Ventas, Calendario, Órdenes and
  **Copiloto** (*Copilot*), out of the apps you have active — or choose
  **Personalizado** (*Custom*), app by app. If you don't choose any app, the
  invitation doesn't go out.
- The invitation link **expires after 7 days**, and it can be **revoked** while
  it is still pending.
- The link **is not stored as such** in our system, only a fingerprint of it.
  If the email doesn't go out, whoever invites copies the link at that moment
  and shares it by hand.
- **Removing an access takes effect instantly.** You turn off their switches and
  they stop seeing that data on their next query, without their session being
  closed. From Personas y accesos there is no button to remove someone from the
  business entirely: if you need that, write to us.

### What gets logged (and what doesn't)

In **Personas y accesos**, on the **Pirámide** (*Pyramid*) tab, the right-hand
panel (**Herramientas** — *Tools* — when you don't have anyone open) has the
**Auditoría** (*Audit*) tab. It shows **the last 60 changes** to permissions
and apps in your business: who granted or revoked which permission, which app
was turned on. **Only whoever administers the team sees it with data**; for
everyone else it comes up empty.

<Warning>
**It is not a log of everything that happens.** Changing the playbook, touching
a funnel's **Configuración** (*Settings*) or editing a customer's record **do
not appear there**. And it records actions, not reads: **the dashboard does not
keep track of who opened which chat.** If you were going to make a personnel
decision leaning on that, don't.
</Warning>

<Tip>
**You can audit your own AI.** On a message from your agent, the bubble's menu
has **Ver información** (*View information*). There, the **El cerebro** (*The
brain*) section shows exactly what the AI read on that turn: the full playbook,
the conversation history and the customer's message. **It does not come
switched on out of the box**: new accounts are created without it and you have
to ask the team for it. It works on messages from the **last 14 days**; after
that, what the AI read is deleted (section 5).
</Tip>

<Warning>
**Some things also reach a WhatsApp number of the DarkFunnels team**, from every
business. They don't appear in your lists and today there is no checkbox in
your Configuración to turn them off. The first three carry your customers' data
and **go out from your own line**: in the WhatsApp on your phone you'll see that
chat with our number.

- **The photos and documents a customer sends you**, with their name and number
  at the bottom. They arrive even if you have forwarding turned off in **«Envío
  de imágenes y archivos»** (*Sending images and files*). It does not extend to
  videos or voice notes, and a file that doesn't get stored — beyond 8 files per
  minute in the same conversation — is not copied either.
- **The notification of every sale or service your agent records**, with the
  product, the total, the buyer's name and phone and the captured data.
  Appointments and leads don't trigger it. Emptying your **«Informar de las
  ventas»** (*Report sales*) list turns the notification off for your team, not
  for that number.
- **The summary every morning**, with your customers' names, phones and
  purchases, if you have it switched on in **«Reportes»** (*Reports*).
- **Your funnels' error and balance alerts, and the ones for a line that's down
  or a QR that couldn't be linked**, with the name of your business and of the
  funnel, your name and your email. These go out through our own line.

It is the same support number you see at the end of this page. If this gives
you pause, write to us before connecting.
</Warning>

Five clarifications that don't fit in the table:

- **The relationship with OpenAI and with Google is DarkFunnels', not yours.**
  The AI that talks to your customers and the copilot run on DarkFunnels'
  account. The two AI providers where you can put your own key are voice
  (ElevenLabs) and avatar (HeyGen), in **Personalizar → Medios IA** (*Customize
  → AI media*); invoicing and the shipping carrier run on your own account with
  that service.
- **The PDF file is not sent to the AI provider, but its text does reach the
  AI.** The PDF is read on DarkFunnels' own server and its text (up to about
  4,000 characters) goes into the conversation, which is what the AI that
  answers reads. A scanned PDF, with no text, goes in with only its name. (The
  file also reaches the team WhatsApp, like any document: that is what you just
  read.)
- **With no balance, audio, photos and videos are not processed.** If your
  account runs out of credits or your trial has ended, the message comes in
  without being transcribed or described, but the file is saved and you can
  open it. If more than 8 files per minute arrive in a conversation, the ones
  over that cap are not even saved: in the inbox only the file's mark remains.
- **Your agent does not reply with voice notes.** That feature is turned off at
  platform level; it is not a checkbox of yours. ElevenLabs voices play in the
  dashboard: when you test a voice or when the copilot speaks to you.
- **When the model changes, so does the provider.** You choose your agent's
  model in its funnel's [Configuración](/en/guides/funnel-settings#brain) and the
  copilot's in its **Modelo** (*Model*) menu: the ones that start with «gemini»
  go through Google and the rest through OpenAI. DarkFunnels can also change
  the model each function uses by default, without touching the application. If
  that list matters to you for a contract, ask us for the current version.

<Check>
**Your card number does not pass through DarkFunnels.** Culqi's own form
captures it inside your browser and only a token reaches us. Of the card we
store **the brand, the last four digits and the expiry month and year**; not the
full number.
</Check>

<Note>
**Meta does not get your customer's readable phone number**: it gets an
irreversible fingerprint, and only if we know that contact's country — with no
country, the phone does not travel. The [Meta pixel](/en/guides/meta-pixel)
guide explains how it is switched on and how it is switched off.
</Note>

## 3. Do they train the AI with my data?

It is easy to over-promise here, so let's be exact.

**What the application does:** on one of the ways of calling OpenAI, the code
**expressly asks it not to keep a copy** of the conversation. That is
verifiable.

**What depends on the provider:** depending on your agent's model, the
conversation goes that way or another one — OpenAI's usual one, or Google's. On
those others, what happens with the copy is stated by **the provider's terms,
not by our application**. And the copilot runs through Google today.

<Warning>
What our [Privacy Policy](https://darkfunnels.ai/privacidad) says:
**DarkFunnels does not use your data or your customers' data to train AI
models**, and the providers process it under API terms that do not allow it to
be used to train their general models. What no button of ours can guarantee you
is what each provider does in-house: that is set by their contract and their
type of account. If you need that guarantee in writing for your business, write
to us and we will review it with you against the terms in force.
</Warning>

What you can do yourself, today, without asking anyone's permission: **don't
upload to the Library what you don't want getting out**, and delete a specific
customer when they ask for it (section 6).

## 4. What happens with my WhatsApp number?

**DarkFunnels connects to your WhatsApp as a linked device, just like WhatsApp
Web.** It does not use the official WhatsApp Business API. That explains the QR
code, explains why the phone matters, and explains the scope: **the same one a
WhatsApp Web session open on a computer would have.**

**Because it is not the official API, WhatsApp can block a number** that writes
to many people in a row or cold. The platform puts caps on what goes out
through your line; what depends on you is not using it to cold-message lists of
numbers.

### What is imported when you scan the QR

When you link, previous conversations are pulled in. The defaults are:

| Cap | Default |
| --- | --- |
| Age | **72 hours** |
| Chats | **50** |
| Messages per chat | **40** |
| Messages in total | **400** |

<Warning>
**If you link your personal WhatsApp, part of your personal WhatsApp comes in.**
When you link, whatever those four caps allow comes in, and the platform can
adjust them. And from that moment on, every new message someone writes to you
in an individual chat — your family too — comes into your inbox and your agent
handles it like a customer. Which is why the usual advice stops being vague:
**use a business line, not yours.**
</Warning>

### Pulling in older messages

In a chat, **Ver mensajes anteriores** (*See earlier messages*) only shows you
what is already stored in the dashboard: it does not ask WhatsApp for anything.

Asking WhatsApp for older messages is done by our team, by hand, not by you
from the dashboard. The request we use brings in **up to 200 older messages**
from a chat that is already in your inbox, and it needs a real message from
that chat as a starting point. We also have **experimental** tests that try to
get WhatsApp to send the line's history again; we don't know whether WhatsApp
answers them. If you need it or it worries you, write to us.

### Disconnecting is not deleting

Pressing **Desconectar** (*Disconnect*) deletes the session credentials, so
getting back in takes a new QR.

<Warning>
**What was already imported is not deleted: your conversations stay in the
dashboard.** Disconnecting cuts the flow, it does not clean up the past. Section
6 is what that is for.

And unlinking on WhatsApp's side **is attempted, but not guaranteed**: if the
session was already down, remove the device from your phone as well (WhatsApp →
**Linked devices**; *Dispositivos vinculados* on a phone set to Spanish).
</Warning>

### The emergency button

The switch on each funnel's card, on your **Inicio** (*Home*) (**Activo** /
**Pausado** — *Active* / *Paused*), is not cosmetic. When you pause it, its
agent stops answering in all its conversations, **cancels the sends that were
already queued and the half-done turns**, and its place among your active
funnels is freed up. It is the answer to "what if it goes off the rails?".

<Note>
Between your WhatsApp and the agent's brain there is a shared key, and in
production the service refuses to start without it. In plain terms: **nobody
from outside can inject fake messages into your inbox.**
</Note>

## 5. How long is each thing kept?

| What | How long it lasts |
| --- | --- |
| **Your inbox messages** | **They do not expire** |
| A file you sent to the Library's **Papelera** (*Trash*) | After 30 days the file and its record are deleted |
| The Excel copy of a funnel you deleted | Kept as long as your account exists |
| The attachments you hand to the copilot | 24 hours if nobody uses them; 30 days if someone does |
| The copilot's actions and tasks | 90 days |
| Conversations with the copilot | Archived after 180 days without use and deleted at 365 |
| The internal technical copies of each change | Pruned at 30 days |
| What the AI read and answered on each turn (what you see in **El cerebro**) | 14 days; the technical summary of each turn, 30 |

**What does not expire**, said plainly so nobody gets a surprise:

- **WhatsApp messages are not deleted on their own.** There is no cleanup by
  age. They go when someone deletes them: you, when you delete a customer or a
  funnel or reset the test chat; your customer, when they ask to be forgotten;
  or when the account is closed. The honest answer to "how long do you keep
  them?" is **indefinitely, and you have the button**.
- **The Excel copy of a deleted funnel stays** stored as long as your account
  exists. It carries your customers' personal data; it goes when the account is
  closed.
- The prunes in the table above are **scheduled cleanups**, not an exact timer
  per row: something can last a little longer than the figure.

<Note>
**Where this lives.** The database is in a data center in the United States
(Northern Virginia). The servers that run the application are with another
cloud provider; if you need their location for a contract, ask us. And part of
your data travels to the providers in the section 2 table, each with its own
location.
</Note>

## 6. Deleting a customer, a funnel or your account

### Deleting a customer's data

There are two paths, and both delete the same thing:

<Steps>
  <Step title="You, from the customer's record">
    Open the customer's record, in **Clientes** (*Customers*) or next to their
    chat. At the bottom, in the **Borrar datos** (*Delete data*) card, press
    **Borrar datos de usuario** (*Delete user data*) and confirm with **Sí,
    borrar todo** (*Yes, delete everything*). Your customer is not notified.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Or your own customer, over WhatsApp">
    They write your agent its **deletion phrase** — by default, **borrar**
    ("delete") — as the whole message, with nothing else; upper or lower case
    doesn't matter. Their data is deleted, they get a confirmation and their
    next message starts a conversation from scratch. Each funnel has its own
    phrase, and you change it in its
    [Configuración](/en/guides/funnel-settings#behavior), in the **Frase de
    borrado** (*Deletion phrase*) field.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**What it covers: almost everything of that person's in that funnel.** Their
conversations (even if they wrote to you through several channels of the same
funnel), their record with their details, notes and tags, their photos, audio
and documents, their appointments, the payment screenshots they sent you and the
sales your agent recorded for them. Their orders stay under the name **«Cliente
borrado»** (*Deleted customer*), without their phone or address, so that your
books balance; their shipments are left without a recipient. **The electronic
receipts and invoices you issued them are not deleted.** The copies left in the
internal logs are cleaned up shortly after, in the background.

<Note>
**What already went out doesn't come back.** The button cannot delete what was
already sent over WhatsApp — the forwarding of their photos and documents, the
notification of their sale, the morning summary — neither on your team's phones
nor on DarkFunnels' WhatsApp. Nor does it touch the Excel copy of a funnel you
already deleted.
</Note>

**If that same number writes to another of your funnels, their record there is
not touched.** Each funnel has its own record of each customer: the deletion
applies to the funnel where you do it.

<Warning>
**We delete everything we know how to identify, and this is how we identify it.**
The cleanup of those internal logs searches by the name of the field: **a copy
stored under a different name can survive**. That is assumed and written down,
not a hidden oversight. If you need a certified deletion for a specific case,
write to us.
</Warning>

<Warning>
**If that customer had opted out, the opt-out stays.** **Dar de baja al
contacto** (*Unsubscribe the contact*) applies to your whole business, so
deleting their data in one funnel does not lift it: your business still won't
write to them. Since their record disappears, if you later need to lift that
opt-out, write to us.
</Warning>

### Other kinds of deletion worth knowing about

- **Deleting a funnel deletes its conversations, its contacts, the agent's own
  files and its persona (if it doesn't share it with another funnel)**, and
  closes its WhatsApp session. The files in your Library stay. Before deleting,
  it tells you how much is lost and, if it has contacts, sales or notes, it
  saves an **Excel copy** with all of that and its variables, which is emailed
  to whoever deletes it and to the account owner. If that copy can't be saved,
  nothing is deleted. Anyone with Ventas can do it. The step by step is in
  [Your funnels](/en/guides/your-funnels#delete-a-funnel).
- **Resetting the test chat deletes that thread**, and only that one.
- **Bulk «Borrar datos» (*Delete data*) in Clientes is not being forgotten.** It
  deletes the captured variables and the chapter of the customers you pick, but
  not the conversation, the messages or the sales. To truly delete a person,
  use **Borrar datos de usuario** on their record.
- **Removing a customer variable does not delete the data.** If you withdraw the
  column for ID number (DNI), address or gender, the value stays saved in the
  record and **reappears if you add the variable back**. Removing the column is
  to stop asking for it, not to forget it.

### Closing your account

It is done from **Ajustes** (*Settings*), in the **Cuenta** (*Account*) block of
**Administración** (or **Perfil** — *Profile* — in your account menu). The
screen is titled **Perfil** and, at the bottom, there is the **Eliminar cuenta**
(*Delete account*) card. **Only the owner can do it**, that is, whoever created
the account.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Read the card">
    It tells you what goes: your business, your funnels, the conversations, the
    contacts, the files and the catalog, and that WhatsApp gets unlinked. If you
    have balance left, it warns *«Perderás el saldo restante»* (*You will lose
    the remaining balance*) with the figure. If there are more people on your
    team, it warns that they lose access too.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm with your password">
    Press **Eliminar mi cuenta** (*Delete my account*). Type your **Contraseña
    actual** (*Current password*) and, in the field below, the word
    **ELIMINAR** (*DELETE*).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Press «Eliminar definitivamente» (Delete permanently)">
    The dashboard confirms *«Tu cuenta fue eliminada»* (*Your account was
    deleted*), signs you out, and we send you an email with the confirmation.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
**It is immediate and there is no grace window.** There is no "30 days to change
your mind": on confirming, your data goes. Think about it before pressing,
because afterwards there is nobody to ask to undo it.
</Warning>

The deletion of your data happens in a single step, and it is all or nothing:
either it happens in full or it doesn't happen. Afterwards, and separately, your
access, your stored files and your WhatsApp session are cleaned up. **The screen
confirms the closure whatever happens with those three**, so you won't find out
there: if one of them didn't go through, your data is deleted all the same, and
if you write to us we finish it off.

**What is kept on purpose, and why:** the accounting ledger is archived
separately — charges, credits, subscription and what belongs to DarkFunnels'
referral program (the invitations and what we paid you for them) —, plus your
business's name and an irreversible fingerprint of your email. The card puts it
like this: *«Conservamos únicamente los registros de facturación que exige la
ley.»* (*We keep only the billing records required by law.*) **Your settings,
your numbers and your integrations are not copied into that archive.**

**Cases the button doesn't solve**, and what the screen tells you in each one:

| If… | What the screen tells you |
| --- | --- |
| You sign in **only with Google** | *«Tu cuenta entra con Google y no tiene contraseña que confirmar. Escríbenos y la cerramos por ti.»* (*Your account signs in with Google and has no password to confirm. Write to us and we'll close it for you.*) |
| Your account **is also in another business** | *«Tu cuenta también está en otra empresa. Escríbenos y cerramos este negocio sin quitarte el acceso a la otra.»* (*Your account is also in another business. Write to us and we'll close this business without removing your access to the other one.*) |
| **You are not the owner** | *«Solo el dueño del negocio puede eliminar la cuenta. Pídeselo a quien la creó.»* (*Only the owner of the business can delete the account. Ask whoever created it.*) |
| Your business **is part of a group** with more than one business | *«Tu organización tiene más de un negocio; escríbenos para cerrarla.»* (*Your organization has more than one business; write to us to close it.*) |
| Your business has **too much data** to close it from here | *«Tu cuenta tiene demasiados datos para borrarla desde aquí; escríbenos y la cerramos nosotros.»* (*Your account has too much data to delete it from here; write to us and we'll close it ourselves.*) |
| The deletion **doesn't finish in time** | *«No pudimos terminar de borrar tu cuenta a tiempo y no se borró nada.»* (*We couldn't finish deleting your account in time and nothing was deleted.*) Try again in a moment; if it happens again, write to us. |

## 7. Files: private, with links that expire

The photos, audio and documents your customers send, the files in your Library,
the images you upload to your catalog and what your agent sends **are stored in
private stores, separated by business**.

The dashboard shows them to you with **signed links that expire after 24
hours**, and each business can only sign its own. In practice:

- **A link you copy from the dashboard stops working after 24 hours.** It is not
  a bug: it is the lock.
- **To pass a file to someone outside, send them the file, not the link.**
- **If a document must not circulate, don't upload it.** Send that piece of
  information through another channel.

<Note>
**An honest leftover.** These stores became private in September 2026. A link
from before that change that someone had already opened can keep working for a
while from the network's cache: in the Library, for up to a year. If a specific
file worries you, write to us.
</Note>

## 8. Optional: the connection with Claude or ChatGPT

<Note>
This section **only applies if you connect your assistant** (Claude, ChatGPT or
Codex) to your business from outside the dashboard — what this documentation
calls **the bridge**. **You don't need it to sell.** If you work only in the
dashboard, you're already done.
</Note>

### Your account is the perimeter

The assistant authenticates **with your own DarkFunnels account** and every
access travels with your identity. **The bridge service has no master key**, and
that is not a promise: there is a startup check that prevents one from being
configured for it — by its name or by its shape — and that **makes the
deployment fail** if anyone tried.

- **It sees what you can see.** The bridge works with your permissions in that
  business: the tools of an app you don't have, or that your business doesn't
  have active, don't appear. If your assistant tries to use one, it gets a
  message naming the app and the business.
- **One connection, one business.** If your account is in several businesses,
  the connection stays fixed to the business you were in on its first call. If
  you then switch business in the dashboard, its changes are rejected until you
  go back to that business or reconnect the connector. And what it reads in the
  meantime comes from the business you have active in the dashboard, not from
  the fixed one.

**What your assistant reads goes out to that assistant's company.** The
conversations, records or orders you ask it to review reach Anthropic (Claude)
or OpenAI (ChatGPT, Codex), with **your** account on that service and under its
terms, not DarkFunnels'. Of your customers' data, on this path the phone is
masked (you'll see it below); names, addresses and the text of messages arrive
as they are.

### What it can do, depending on the URL

- **With no parameters, the connection is read-only**, with three writes that
  delete nothing: creating customer tags, assigning them and logging a request
  the assistant couldn't fulfill (those requests are reviewed by the DarkFunnels
  team).
- **The URL the dashboard gives you carries `?features=all`**: it turns on every
  function, including the ones that write, send real messages to your
  customers, pause or create funnels.
- **`&read_only=true` disables every write** and overrides any other option.
- **Destructive actions travel marked as such**: it is the marker assistants
  use to ask you for confirmation before running them.
- **Your customers' messages reach it delimited as data to analyze**, not as
  orders to follow.

### The masked phone is from here, and only from here

On this path the customer's number arrives **masked by default**:

```
51•••••4321
```

<Warning>
**That masking exists only in the bridge.** In your dashboard, in the
notification of each sale and in what your sales agent reads, **the number goes
through in full**. If you ever read that DarkFunnels masks phone numbers, it was
true of one path, not of the product.
</Warning>

The last four digits and a country hint are kept: enough to recognize a number
you already know. If a value is not shaped like a phone number, it is not
half-masked: it comes out as `(oculto)` (*hidden*).

If you need the full numbers, reconnect adding `pii=full` to the URL:

```
https://mcp.darkfunnels.ai/mcp?features=all&pii=full
```

<Warning>
Add `pii=full` **to the URL you were already using**, don't use it on its own.
If you had `?features=all` and connect with just `?pii=full`, you fall back to
the base set and lose orders, metrics and the writes.
</Warning>

It is your decision when connecting: **the assistant cannot change it halfway
through a conversation**, and any misspelled variant leaves it in safe mode.

### It is logged

Every call your assistant makes is logged: which tool, when and with what
result. **Today the dashboard has no screen to browse that log**: in
**Asistentes IA** (*AI assistants*), whoever administers your business's apps
sees the **Conectado · última actividad** (*Connected · last activity*) mark.
If you need the detail of the calls, ask us. Two things, said plainly: that log
could be modified by someone with technical access to our servers, and **today
it has no deletion date**.

### Caps, and it doesn't move money

- **Default caps:** each person can make up to **60 calls per minute and 300 per
  hour**, and each business has a quota of **20,000 calls a day**, which resets
  at midnight (Lima time).
- **It doesn't move money.** The bridge's tools don't charge, don't transfer,
  don't issue payment links and don't touch your gateway or your cards: orders
  and balance, they only read.
- **What does spend:** optimizing the playbook with AI, simulations and asking
  the copilot consume credits from the same dashboard balance. Creating or
  duplicating a funnel takes up a slot in your plan. If you can see the balance,
  every response warns you when it runs low.
