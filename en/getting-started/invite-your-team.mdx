Your business doesn't have to be run by one person alone. From **Personas y
accesos** ("People and access") you invite the people who help you by email —
whoever answers the chats, whoever ships the orders, whoever keeps the books —
and you decide, **app by app**, what each one can use.

This guide covers how to invite, what each person sees depending on what you
give them, how to take an access away, and what happens when the same account
is in two businesses.

The dashboard interface is in Spanish, so buttons and menus are named here in
Spanish with the English meaning in parentheses.

<Note>
**Where it is.** In the left-hand menu, open **Administración**
("Administration") and press **Personas y accesos**. (Direct:
`optimind.darkfunnels.ai/personas-accesos`.)
</Note>

<Info>
**Inviting and handing out access is for whoever manages the team.** At the
start, that's you: whoever creates the account gets full access. Anyone who
doesn't manage the team sees this screen **read-only**: it works as a directory
for them, but they don't get the **Invitar** ("Invite") button and can't switch
anyone's apps on or off.
</Info>

## The screen at a glance

Under the **Personas y accesos** title you'll see how many people your team has
and how many invitations are waiting for an answer. Below are four tabs and, on
the right, the **Invitar** button.

| Tab | What for |
| --- | --- |
| **Equipo** ("Team") | The one that opens: one card per person with the apps they use. This is where you invite and hand out access. |
| **Organigrama** ("Org chart") | Your areas and their positions, with who fills each one. |
| **Historial** ("History") | Who changed what, in sentences and by day. |
| **Avanzado** ("Advanced") | For special cases: detailed permissions, testing a permission, the technical log and the emergency shutdown of an app. |

In **Equipo**, each card shows the person with their initials, their position
and **the apps they use, by name**. If there are more than three, you'll see two
and a **+N**. At the top you have the search box (*"Buscar por nombre o
puesto"*, "Search by name or position") and, next to it, **Todas las apps**
("All apps"), to see only the people who have one (for example, **Con Ventas**,
"With Sales").

- **Pending invitations** come first, with a dashed border.
- Anyone with no app at all carries the notice **"Sin apps: al entrar no verá
  nada"** ("No apps: they won't see anything when they sign in").
- Anyone with full access carries the **Acceso total** ("Full access") mark.
- The last card, **Invitar a alguien** ("Invite someone"), does the same as the
  button at the top.

<Tip>
**Your business already comes with one area and one position.** In the
**Organigrama** tab, the area is called **General** and the position,
**Titular** ("Holder"): you fill it. You can create more positions with
**+ Puesto** ("+ Position"), inside the area (for example, "Salesperson" or
"Dispatch"), and more areas with the **Área** ("Area") button. The position
organizes the org chart: it doesn't give or take away apps.
</Tip>

## Invite a person

<Steps>
  <Step title="Press «Invitar»">
    It's at the top right. The **Invitar a alguien** card, at the end of the
    team, works too. **Invitar a alguien** ("Invite someone") opens on the
    right; on the phone, as a sheet that slides up.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Type their email">
    In **Correo** ("Email"). The invitation will arrive at that email, and
    that's the email they'll sign in with afterwards.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose what they'll be able to use">
    In **¿Qué podrá usar?** ("What will they be able to use?") there are
    starting points, each with the icons of what it brings:

    | Starting point | What it gives them |
    | --- | --- |
    | **Trabajador (solo su espacio de RR. HH.)** ("Worker, only their HR space") | Their HR space — clocking in, requesting vacation, seeing their payslip — and the **Copiloto** ("Copilot"). It shows up if you have the Human Resources app. |
    | **Equipo de ventas** ("Sales team") | **Ventas** ("Sales"), **Calendario** ("Calendar"), **Órdenes** ("Orders") and **Copiloto**, out of those you have active. |
    | **Personalizado** ("Custom") | *"Eliges tú, app por app."* ("You choose, app by app.") |

    Below are the same apps you'll see on their profile card, **one per row**:
    tap one to switch it on or off. As soon as you tap one, the starting point
    changes to **Personalizado**. The **Accesos especiales** ("Special access":
    the balance and the parts of HR) come folded: open them with the arrow.

    <Frame caption="Invitar a alguien (Invite someone): the email, «¿Qué podrá usar?» (What will they be able to use?) with «Equipo de ventas» (Sales team) selected, its apps switched on and, at the bottom, the sentence about what will happen on accepting.">
      <img src="/images/equipo/02-invitar.png" alt="Invitar a alguien (Invite someone) panel with a sample email, the starting points Trabajador, Equipo de ventas (selected) and Personalizado; Ventas, Calendario, Órdenes and Copiloto on and Inventario off; Accesos especiales folded; Más opciones with the position on «Sin puesto por ahora» (No position for now); the sentence «Al aceptar… podrá usar Ventas, Calendario, Órdenes y Copiloto» (On accepting… will be able to use Sales, Calendar, Orders and Copilot) and the Enviar invitación (Send invitation) button" />
    </Frame>

    <Warning>
    **No starting point includes the balance.** If you want that person to see
    the balance, top up or see the invoices, open **Accesos especiales** and
    switch on **Ver saldo y facturación** ("See balance and billing").
    </Warning>
  </Step>

  <Step title="If you want, give them a position">
    Open **Más opciones: puesto y alcance** ("More options: position and
    scope"). In **Puesto (opcional)** ("Position, optional") choose one of your
    positions or leave it on **Sin puesto por ahora** ("No position for now").
    **If they're going to use the Copilot, give them a position**: without a
    position, their copilot can't do some actions (it's explained
    [further down](#each-person-has-their-own-copilot)).

    If you chose a position, or opened the panel from an area's **+** in the
    org chart, the **Sus apps, solo dentro del área…** ("Their apps, only
    within the area…") checkbox also appears. Leave it unticked: *"Sus apps
    valen en toda la empresa (recomendado)."* ("Their apps apply to the whole
    business (recommended).") If you tick it, the panel warns that some apps
    don't recognize area permissions yet.

    <Tip>
    Don't have a position for that person yet? Press **Cancelar** ("Cancel"),
    go to the **Organigrama** tab and press the **+** of the **General** area.
    The panel opens already inside that area and, under **Más opciones**, comes
    with **+ Crear un puesto nuevo aquí** ("+ Create a new position here").
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Check the sentence and press «Enviar invitación»">
    At the bottom, a sentence tells you what will happen: *"Al aceptar, lucia@…
    podrá usar Ventas, Calendario, Órdenes y Copiloto."* ("On accepting,
    lucia@… will be able to use Sales, Calendar, Orders and Copilot.", plus the
    position, if you gave one).

    The invitation **expires in 7 days**, and as long as nobody accepts it
    nothing else changes. On accepting, that person joins your business with
    what you chose. If you gave them a position, they fill it; if not, they
    appear in the org chart under **Sin puesto asignado** ("No position
    assigned").

    If you didn't choose any app, the sentence warns you — *"Elige al menos una
    app: sin ninguna, entraría sin ver nada."* ("Choose at least one app: with
    none, they'd get in without seeing anything.") — and the invitation doesn't
    go out.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Keep the link, in case the email doesn't arrive">
    The panel shows **Invitación enviada** ("Invitation sent"), or
    **Invitación creada** ("Invitation created") if the email didn't go out,
    and below it the **Link de activación** ("Activation link") with the
    **Copiar** ("Copy") button.

    **That link isn't shown again** after you reload the page. If the email
    didn't go out, copy it now and send it yourself, for example by WhatsApp.
    Press **Listo** ("Done") to close, or **Invitar a otra persona** ("Invite
    another person") to keep going.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
**Nobody gives what they don't have.** If someone else manages the team with
you, they can hand out only the access they have themselves. Everything else
shows up locked for them with the notice *"No lo puedes dar: tú no lo tienes."*
("You can't give it: you don't have it.")
</Note>

## What the invited person receives

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="If they don't have an account">
    They get an email with the subject **"Te invitaron a unirte al equipo en
    DarkFunnels"** ("You were invited to join the team on DarkFunnels") and the
    **Activar mi cuenta** ("Activate my account") button. At the bottom it
    reminds them that the link expires in 7 days.

    The button opens the **Activar tu cuenta** ("Activate your account")
    screen, where they type **Tu nombre** ("Your name"), a **Contraseña**
    ("Password") and repeat it in **Confirmar contraseña** ("Confirm
    password"). The password needs at least 8 characters, with letters and
    numbers.

    When they press **Activar mi cuenta**, the account is created and the
    dashboard sends them to sign in: they sign in with their email and that
    password.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="If they already have a DarkFunnels account">
    They don't create another account or another password: **they join with the
    one they already have**. You'll know when you send it, because the
    dashboard tells you that this person already has an account and joins with
    it.

    They get an email with the subject **"Moda Sol te invitó a su equipo en
    DarkFunnels"** ("Moda Sol invited you to their team on DarkFunnels", with
    your business's name) and the **Unirme al equipo** ("Join the team")
    button. They'll also see it when they open their dashboard.

    What they see step by step is in
    [One account in several businesses](#one-account-in-several-businesses).
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Pending invitations

As long as nobody accepts it, the invitation comes **first in Equipo**, as a
dashed card: the email, the proposed position (*"Para Vendedor de Campo"*, "For
Field salesperson") or *Invitación pendiente* ("Pending invitation"), the apps
they'll get and how much time it has left: *Vence en 3 días* ("Expires in 3
days"), *Vence hoy* ("Expires today") or *Vencida* ("Expired").

- **Press the card** and what they'll get on accepting opens on the right, with
  the **Link de activación** (only if you created it during this same visit)
  and the **Revocar invitación** ("Revoke invitation") button, which cancels
  the link. It asks you to confirm first.
- In **Organigrama** they also appear in the tray, under **Invitaciones
  pendientes** ("Pending invitations"), with **Copiar link** ("Copy link", only
  while you're still on the screen where you created it) and **Revocar**
  ("Revoke").

<Warning>
**An expired invitation doesn't delete itself.** It stays in Equipo marked
*Vencida*, and while it's there the dashboard won't let you invite that email
again: under the email it tells you *"Ya le enviaste una invitación que sigue
pendiente."* ("You already sent them an invitation that's still pending."),
with the **Verla** ("See it") link. Revoke the expired one and invite again.
</Warning>

## What each person can see and do

### A person's profile card

Press their card in **Equipo** — or their name in the **Organigrama** — and
their **ficha** ("profile card") opens on the right (on the phone, as a sheet).
At the top, one sentence sums it up. For example:

> Lucía puede usar Ventas, Calendario, Órdenes y el Copiloto. No entra a
> Inventario. No ve el saldo ni la facturación.

("Lucía can use Sales, Calendar, Orders and the Copilot. She doesn't go into
Inventory. She doesn't see the balance or the billing.")

Below are their **Apps**, one per row and with how many are on (for example,
*4 de 5*, "4 of 5"), and their **Accesos especiales**, folded if they have
none. Then comes their **Puesto** ("Position"): whoever manages the team
changes it right there, and it saves itself. At the end, two shortcuts, **Su
historial de cambios** ("Their change history") and **Permisos detallados y
pruebas** ("Detailed permissions and tests"), open the **Historial** and
**Avanzado** tabs already filtered to that person.

### One app per row: tap it to switch it on

There's one row for each app **active in your business**, plus the special
access. If an app isn't active, it doesn't appear here. **The whole row is the
switch**: tap it and it switches on or off.

| App or access | What it opens for them |
| --- | --- |
| **Ventas** ("Sales") | Conversations, contacts, agents and sales funnels. |
| **Calendario** ("Calendar") | The appointment calendar, the services and their reminders. |
| **Órdenes** ("Orders") | The orders with their status, their payments, their vouchers and their delivery. |
| **Inventario** ("Inventory") | Warehouses, stock and movements of each product. |
| **Facturación electrónica** ("Electronic invoicing") | The electronic receipts and invoices for sales (if you have the app). |
| **Envíos** ("Shipments") | The dispatches with their courier, their tracking and their delivery (if you have the app). |
| **Copiloto** ("Copilot") | The dashboard's AI assistant. It sees and does only what that person is allowed to, and it spends the business's balance. |
| **Ver saldo y facturación** ("See balance and billing") | The business's balance, top-ups, cards and invoices. |

If your account has other apps active, their access appears here too.

**Each change applies instantly**: when you switch an app off, that person
stops seeing its data on their next request, without being signed out. The
dashboard confirms it with a notice like *"Órdenes: apagado para Lucía"*
("Orders: off for Lucía").

<Warning>
**Ventas is given whole.** Whoever has Ventas sees **all** your business's
conversations and contacts, and can work with your funnels and their playbook.
There's no finer permission, neither per funnel nor per conversation:
**assigning a chat to someone doesn't stop someone else from opening it**. Give
Ventas to whoever you'd trust with your whole inbox.
</Warning>

If the profile card says **Acceso total** ("Full access"), that person has the
"todos" ("all") permission: they see and manage everything, they have no rows
to switch on, and that access is changed in **Avanzado → Permisos detallados**
("Advanced → Detailed permissions").

Whoever created the account appears as **Acceso total (superadmin)**: they also
see and manage everything, but their apps do show underneath, and only another
superadmin changes them.

### What changes on their screen

Each person sees in their menu **the apps they can use**. An app appears when
it's active in the business **and** that person has it switched on. This
applies to the left-hand menu, the bottom bar on the phone, the **⌘K** search
and **Inicio** ("Home").

Without **Ver saldo y facturación**, also:

- they don't see the **Saldo** ("Balance") or **Embudos contratados**
  ("Contracted funnels") in the top bar;
- they don't get Administración's **Dinero** ("Money") block (**Saldo y
  facturación**, "Balance and billing", and **Consumo**, "Usage") or
  **Referidos** ("Referrals");
- they don't see the **Crear embudo** ("Create funnel") button or the start-up
  list on Home (*"Pon tu empresa a operar"*, "Get your business running"),
  unless they manage the apps.

And without **Ventas**, they don't get the browser notification bell.

**Personas y accesos** is seen by the whole team; by those who don't manage it,
read-only.

If someone types the address of an app they don't have by hand, the page
doesn't break for them: they see **"No tienes acceso a Órdenes"** ("You don't
have access to Orders", or whichever app it is) with the message *"Pídele
acceso a quien administra tu equipo."* ("Ask whoever manages your team for
access.") and a button to go back to Home.

<Tip>
**Their menu catches up without signing out.** Their data is closed off
instantly, but the menu updates when they come back to the dashboard tab — at
most once a minute — or when they reload the page.
</Tip>

### Each person has their own copilot

The copilot is one more app. Without it, that person doesn't see the copilot
button.

With it, the copilot **works with that person's permissions**: if they don't
have Órdenes, their copilot can't read your orders either. And what each person
talks about with their copilot is theirs: **their conversations aren't seen by
anyone else on the team, not even the owner**.

<Warning>
**Give a position to whoever uses the Copilot.** Without a position, there are
actions their copilot can't do: it will tell them they don't have an active
position and to ask for one to be assigned. You give it on their profile card,
under **Puesto**, or in **Organigrama**, with **Asignar a puesto…** ("Assign to
position…") if they appear in **Sin puesto asignado**.
</Warning>

What the AI does spends the business's balance, including your team's copilot.
If you don't want that spending, switch off their Copiloto.

## Taking an access away

<Steps>
  <Step title="Switch off what they shouldn't use any more">
    Open their profile card and tap the apps they shouldn't use any more. It
    applies instantly.

    If switching it off leaves them without **Ventas**, the message sends that
    person left in the queue are cancelled; the notice tells you how many.
  </Step>
  <Step title="What isn't an app, in «Avanzado»">
    Whatever isn't an app — for example, managing the team or the apps — is
    removed in **Avanzado → Permisos detallados**. Choose the person at the
    top, under **Persona** ("Person"), or get there from their profile card
    with **Permisos detallados y pruebas**. In this list each permission shows
    its technical name: managing the team is "manage_grants" and managing the
    apps, "manage_apps".

    Press **Revocar** ("Revoke") on the row that carries it and confirm with
    **Confirmar revocación** ("Confirm revocation"). **Revoking removes the
    whole row**, with everything it carries: before you confirm, the notice
    tells you exactly what they lose.
  </Step>
  <Step title="If they hadn't signed in yet, revoke the invitation">
    In **Equipo**, press their dashed card and then **Revocar invitación**.
    Their link stops working.
  </Step>
</Steps>

With everything switched off, their card says **Sin apps: al entrar no verá
nada**. They can still sign in with their email, but with no apps and no
balance. In Personas y accesos there's no button to remove someone from the
business entirely; if you need that, write to us.

<Note>
**Taking their position away doesn't take apps away.** If you choose **Sin
puesto** ("No position") on their profile card, they leave the position but
keep their account and their apps, and in the org chart they move to **Sin
puesto asignado**. That said, without a position their copilot can no longer do
some actions.
</Note>

<Warning>
**You can't lock yourself out.** If you manage the team thanks to a permission
someone gave you, the dashboard won't let you remove the last one yourself: on
that permission you'll see *"Es tu acceso para administrar: no te lo quitas
tú."* ("It's your access to manage: you don't remove it yourself.") Whoever
created the account doesn't see that notice, because their full access isn't
in that list. That way your business is never left without someone to manage
it.
</Warning>

## Who changed what

In the **Historial** tab you see your team's latest changes in sentences,
grouped by day: *"Mark le dio Ventas y Calendario a Lucía"* ("Mark gave Lucía
Sales and Calendar"), *"Mark le quitó Órdenes a Lucía"* ("Mark took Orders away
from Lucía"), *"Mark activó la app Inventario"* ("Mark activated the Inventory
app"). A green dot marks what was given and a red one, what was taken away. At
the top you can keep only **Accesos** ("Access"), **Apps de la empresa**
("Business apps") or **Equipo** ("Team").

- It shows **the last 60 changes**, and whoever manages the team sees it with
  the data.
- When a whole row is revoked from **Avanzado**, it says what was taken away,
  but not always from whom.
- The log as it is, with its codes, is in **Avanzado → Registro técnico**
  ("Technical log").

## One account in several businesses

The same email can be in **several businesses**: for example, yours and that of
a client who invited you. It's the same account and the same password.

**To join another business, someone who manages its team has to invite you.**
You can't create a new business from your account, and signing up again with
the same email doesn't create one either.

Each business decides its own permissions: you can have full access in yours
and only Ventas in the other. And while you work in one, the dashboard shows you
that business's things: its conversations, its customers, its balance.

### If you're invited from another business

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the invitation email">
    It arrives with the subject **"Moda Sol te invitó a su equipo en
    DarkFunnels"** ("Moda Sol invited you to their team on DarkFunnels", with
    the name of the business inviting you). Press **Unirme al equipo** ("Join
    the team").
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm with the account you already have">
    **Únete a Moda Sol.** ("Join Moda Sol.") opens.

    - If you already had your session open, press **Unirme a Moda Sol** ("Join
      Moda Sol").
    - If not, press **Entrar para unirme** ("Sign in to join"), sign in with the
      email and password you already use, and confirm.

    You don't need another password and you keep access to what you already
    used.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You're in">
    The dashboard tells you *"Te uniste a Moda Sol"* ("You joined Moda Sol")
    and takes you to that business's Home.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
**You also find out when you open your dashboard.** If you have a pending
invitation, when you open the dashboard the notice **"Moda Sol te invitó a su
equipo"** ("Moda Sol invited you to their team") appears. That notice doesn't
make you join: to join you have to open the link in the email, because that's
how it's checked that the email is yours.

- **Más tarde** ("Later") hides it for now.
- **Reenviarme el enlace** ("Resend me the link") sends a new link to your
  email. The previous one stops working.
</Note>

### Switch business

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your account menu">
    It's the circle with your initials, the last one in the top bar on the
    right. At the top of the menu you see your name, your email and the
    business you're in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the business">
    Below, there's one row per business. The current one says **Estás aquí**
    ("You're here"); the others, **Cambiar a esta empresa** ("Switch to this
    business"). Press the one you want.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The dashboard reloads in that business">
    It leaves you on its Home, with its menu, its permissions and its data.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If your account is in only one business, these rows don't appear.

<Warning>
**The active business belongs to your account, not to the tab.** If you switch
business on your phone, you also switch on your computer. The other tabs in the
same browser reload by themselves.

So that you don't save something in the wrong business, before saving the
dashboard checks which business your account is in. If it changed along the
way, it warns you with *"Tu cuenta está trabajando en Moda Sol: te llevamos
ahí."* ("Your account is working in Moda Sol: we're taking you there.") and
takes you to it. Go back to the business you wanted and make the change again.
</Warning>

<Note>
**If you connected Claude or ChatGPT:** each connection stays pinned to the
business you had active the first time your assistant used it, and it works
with your permissions in that business. If you switch business in the
dashboard, that connection doesn't save changes until you go back to that
business or reconnect it, and **what it reads is already from the business you
have active now**. More in [The connection URL](/en/reference/connection-url).
</Note>

## If something goes wrong

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I don't see the «Invitar» (Invite) button">
    Inviting is for whoever manages the team. If you don't see it, the screen
    is read-only for you: ask whoever manages your team to do the inviting, or
    to give you that permission.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="«Ya le enviaste una invitación que sigue pendiente» (You already sent them an invitation that's still pending)">
    You already invited that email. Press **Verla** ("See it") to open it: if
    it says *Vencida* ("Expired"), revoke it and invite again. If it's still
    valid and you no longer have the link at hand, revoke it and invite again.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="An error appears when sending the invitation">
    The notice may not say why. The two most common causes:

    - **That person is already on your team.** There's no need to invite them:
      look for them in **Equipo**, open their profile card and switch their
      apps on.
    - **That email already had access to your business and is now suspended or
      removed.** It can't be invited again from the dashboard: write to us and
      we'll reactivate it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The person didn't get the email">
    Have them check their spam folder. If it still doesn't show up:

    - **If they didn't have an account:** send them the link yourself if you
      still have it at hand. If not, revoke the invitation and invite again.
    - **If they already had an account:** have them open their dashboard and,
      in the invitation notice, press **Reenviarme el enlace**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Opening the link shows «Enlace inválido.» (Invalid link.)">
    The invitation was already used, was revoked, expired, or a new link was
    requested with **Reenviarme el enlace** (the previous one stops working). If
    a more recent email arrived, use that one; if not, ask to be invited again.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="«Esta invitación es para…» (This invitation is for…) another email">
    You opened the link with another account's session. The invitation is for
    the email it arrived at: press **Entrar con la cuenta de…** ("Sign in with
    the account of…") and sign in with that email.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="«quien te invitó ya no puede invitar a esta empresa» (whoever invited you can no longer invite to this business)">
    Between being invited and opening the link, that person stopped managing
    the team or is no longer in the business. Ask someone else who manages it
    to invite you.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="They got in and see nothing, or get «No tienes acceso a…» (You don't have access to…)">
    They're missing that app. Switch it on in their profile card: if they have
    none, their card says **Sin apps: al entrar no verá nada**. The data opens
    up for them instantly; their menu catches up when they come back to the
    dashboard tab or reload the page.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Their copilot says they don't have an active position">
    Give them a position: on their profile card, under **Puesto**, or in
    **Organigrama**, with **Asignar a puesto…** if they appear in **Sin puesto
    asignado**. If you don't have a position that fits, create it with
    **+ Puesto** inside an area.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="An app won't switch on or off">
    The reason is under the app's name:

    - *"No lo puedes dar: tú no lo tienes."* ("You can't give it: you don't
      have it.") — you can hand out only what you have.
    - *"Viene de un permiso de toda la organización: se cambia en Avanzado."*
      ("It comes from an organization-wide permission: it's changed in
      Advanced.") — that access didn't come from the profile card; remove it in
      **Avanzado → Permisos detallados**.
    - On the profile card of whoever created the account, the **Acceso total
      (superadmin)** box warns that their apps *"solo otro superadmin las
      cambia"* ("only another superadmin changes them").
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Where did the pyramid go?">
    The screen changed in October 2026 so that you see your team with its apps
    first. The pyramid is now the **Organigrama** tab. **Probar permiso**
    ("Test permission"), the audit and the detailed list of permissions are in
    **Avanzado**, together with the emergency shutdown of an app (formerly the
    **Módulos** tab). The changes in sentences are in **Historial**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="It won't let me delete my account">
    The **Eliminar cuenta** ("Delete account") card in your **Perfil**
    ("Profile") explains it (your account menu, top right → **Perfil**):

    - If you didn't create the business you have open: *"Solo el dueño del
      negocio puede eliminar la cuenta. Pídeselo a quien la creó."* ("Only the
      business owner can delete the account. Ask whoever created it.")
    - If you created it, but you sign in with Google: *"Tu cuenta entra con
      Google y no tiene contraseña que confirmar. Escríbenos y la cerramos por
      ti."* ("Your account signs in with Google and has no password to confirm.
      Write to us and we'll close it for you.")
    - If you created it and your account is also in another business: *"Tu
      cuenta también está en otra empresa. Escríbenos y cerramos este negocio
      sin quitarte el acceso a la otra."* ("Your account is also in another
      business. Write to us and we'll close this business without removing
      your access to the other one.")
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
