An agent is **a salesperson**: it has a name it introduces itself with, a way of
speaking and a script. To build it you don't have to write anything from scratch
or fill in a form: **you tell your copilot what you sell** and it leaves you the
agent created with a script to start from.

## What a funnel is

**The funnel is the sales conversation, written down.** It isn't a chart or a
report: it's what your agent does, arranged into **chapters**, like the playbook
you would hand a new salesperson.

A typical funnel goes like this: greet and understand what the customer is
looking for → present the product that fits them → handle the price objection →
close and take the shipping details. Each of those stretches is a chapter, with
its rules and its examples.

And the important part: **it's yours and you edit it**. What comes out of the
conversation is a first draft so you don't start from a blank page. From there
on you live in [the seller's playbook](/en/guides/sales-playbook), which is where
the funnel gets corrected a thousand times.

<Note>
**An agent = a script + a character + a WhatsApp number.** The dashboard calls
the script the **funnel**; the character — the salesperson's name, their tone,
their way of speaking — it calls the **persona**, and it doesn't refer to anyone
made of flesh and blood. If you sell two very different things, that's two
agents, not two chapters.
</Note>

## Building it by talking: the normal path

When you finish signing up **the copilot opens by itself** — the right-hand
panel; on the phone, a drawer that comes in from the side — and the onboarding
conversation starts. After that you always have it at **Home → Create agent**.

While it lasts, below the compose box there's a label to remind you:

> We're building your agent.

The conversation spends balance, like everything the AI does: that is what the
**US\$ 10** your account is born with are for (see
[Pricing](/en/getting-started/pricing)).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tell it what you sell">
    The first thing it asks is a single question: **what do you sell**. You can
    answer in your own words or with one of the three one-tap replies the first
    screen offers you: *"I sell over WhatsApp and close in the chat"*, *"I book
    visits or appointments"* or *"I screen applicants or requests"*.

    If you sell over WhatsApp and close in the chat, sign-up is shorter: your
    funnel is born from **the Ecommerce template** and you skip the steps below.
    See [If you sell over WhatsApp: the Ecommerce template](#if-you-sell-over-whatsapp-the-ecommerce-template).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the agent's creation">
    As soon as it knows what your business does, it offers to create the agent
    with your business's name: a card **"Confirm: Creating the funnel"** appears
    with the proposed **name** and **objective**, and the **Approve** and
    **Reject** buttons.

    It asks you because **creating an agent takes up a slot in your plan**, and
    that is your money (the copilot only brings up the slot if the creation
    fails because you have none left). It's born active, with its **persona**
    already active (the name and the way of speaking it serves with; you review
    it at the end) and no WhatsApp number. And it's born with its **chapter 1
    already written**: the greeting, with the salesperson's name and your
    business's name filled in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test chapter 1 and tour the playbook">
    The dashboard takes you to your agent's **Playbook**, and the first thing the
    copilot asks you is to **test it**: write "hola" ("hi") to your agent in the
    test chat the dashboard leaves open for you, as if you were a customer.

    Once you have seen it answer, the copilot explains it on top of that very
    answer and walks you through the playbook screen: the dashboard points at
    and reads out each part — the chapter, its PASOS (steps) and the line that
    moves on to the next one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the chapters that are missing">
    Each stage of your sale is a chapter, and **you write the missing ones
    yourself** in the playbook with **＋ Nuevo capítulo** (*New chapter*), with
    the same shape as chapter 1: the agent's role, the numbered PASOS and the
    line that moves on to the next one.

    If you would rather have the copilot write it, **ask it for the whole
    funnel**. It interviews you first: it isn't a form, it asks you one question
    at a time and in plain language, and it doesn't repeat back something you
    already told it. What it will want to know:

    - **Your business and your audience** — who buys from you, where you sell
      and how people reach your WhatsApp.
    - **The product** — what exactly you sell and what benefits you can state
      with confidence. What you don't confirm to it **doesn't get promised**: it
      is handed off to someone on your team.
    - **Prices and promotions** — each variant or bundle with its price, and the
      discount rules currently in force.
    - **Payment, shipping and delivery** — how they pay you, how long you really
      take and what details you need in order to dispatch.
    - **Objections** — what they ask you most before buying and **the answer you
      give**. That answer, exactly as it is, is the one the agent will use.
    - **What name and what tone** it should introduce itself with.
    - **Which cases always go to a person** on your team.
    - **What files you usually send** (catalog, flyer, price list) and at what
      point in the conversation.

    With that it leaves you **a proposal of the complete funnel**, which you
    approve or reject.

    When you consider the playbook ready, the close comes, in this order: first
    you review the **persona** in the **Persona Studio** — it was born active;
    there you approve or change its name and tone — and then you scan the QR
    code at **Customize → Channels**. The order matters: when you connect,
    WhatsApp takes the persona's name as the line's name.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
**Nothing is written into your agent until you approve it.** If you ask for the
whole funnel, the proposal waits in the thread with **Approve** / **Reject**,
and until you press Approve your playbook stays exactly as it was. Approving it
**replaces the whole playbook**, including the chapter 1 your agent was born
with.
</Warning>

<Tip>
Answer in your own words and with concrete data: real prices, real delivery
areas, the phrases you already use when you sell yourself. A generic answer
produces a generic salesperson, and that gets fixed afterwards by hand in the
playbook.
</Tip>

**Building your agent this way spends cents of your advance**, and that's why
it's the normal path before connecting WhatsApp: your account is born with
**US\$ 10** precisely for this.

<Note>
**It isn't a gift, it's an advance.** Those US\$ 10 are your **whole** welcome
credit, brought forward so you can build and test your agent without stopping
halfway: connecting WhatsApp no longer brings balance (the US\$ 25 bonus, if you
were referred, arrives when you activate your subscription). What starts there is **your trial** —
the days are counted from the day you connect — and your agent begins answering
the number. If the advance ran out before connecting, the copilot tells you —
*"Tu cuenta no tiene saldo disponible"* ("Your account has no balance
available") — and the way out is to **top up**.
</Note>

<Note>
**If you close halfway through you lose nothing.** The conversation lives in
your account, not in a browser draft: going back to **Home → Create agent** from
the same machine reopens your thread just as it was, with everything you already
told it. And if you come in from another device and it starts from scratch, the
first thing it does is look at which agents you have: if one was left without a
script, it picks up on that one instead of creating another.
</Note>

## If you sell over WhatsApp: the Ecommerce template

When what you do is **sell over WhatsApp and close in the chat**, the copilot
does not interview you chapter by chapter. Your funnel is born from a template
proven in shops that already sell that way, and you only fill in what belongs
to your business.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose template or from scratch">
    The copilot asks *«¿Usamos una plantilla de Ecommerce o armamos tu flujo
    desde 0?»* (*Shall we use an Ecommerce template or build your flow from
    scratch?*) and gives you both buttons: **«Usar la plantilla de Ecommerce»**
    (*Use the Ecommerce template*) and **«Armar mi flujo desde 0»** (*Build my
    flow from scratch*).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tell it the salesperson's name and the product">
    Those are the only two questions. From the name it decides between
    **Asesora** and **Asesor** (female or male advisor), and the product is
    written into the greeting and the script. If you already told it what you
    sell at the start, it does not ask again.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve the card">
    The **«Confirmar: Creando el embudo»** (*Confirm: Creating the funnel*) card
    shows the creation mode, the title (for example, *«Carla - Venta de Colágeno
    Plus»*), the product, the objective and the salesperson. When you press
    **Aprobar** (*Approve*), the copilot tells you in one line that the funnel is
    ready and goes quiet: no tour and no more questions.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**What is born when you approve.** A **shared block** with the business data
and three chapters:

| Chapter | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **1 · Saludo** (*Greeting*) | Runs once: greets with the product, its benefits and the promotion, and asks where the customer is writing from. |
| **2 · En venta** (*Selling*) | Answers doubts, asks about shipping, helps pick the promotion and takes the order details (and the deposit if it ships to the provinces). |
| **3 · Pedido confirmado** (*Order confirmed*) | Confirms the order, records the sale and hands the conversation to your team. |

What only you know goes in as **placeholders** of the `«PRODUCTO_MARCA»` or
`«ESCALERA_PRECIOS»` kind: the brand, the presentation, the benefits, the
prices, your payment methods, the frequent objections and the follow-up
messages. The usual Peruvian defaults (cash on delivery in Lima, a S/20 deposit
for the provinces, shipping with Shalom or Olva) come already written, and you
review them before connecting.

<Note>
**From scratch** creates the same three chapters, with their role and the
shared block, but with **empty steps** for you to write.
</Note>

**After that, everything happens in the playbook.** Each placeholder is filled
in once and changed everywhere it appears, from the **«Huecos»**
(*Placeholders*) button in the bar. If you would rather get help, press
**«Ayúdame a rellenarlo»** (*Help me fill it in*): the copilot first asks for
your product files, asks you one question at a time and proposes the changes
for you to approve. See
[The sales playbook](/en/guides/sales-playbook#a-playbook-born-from-the-ecommerce-template).

- **Probar** (*Test*) turns on when chapter 1 and the PRODUCTO and PRECIOS
  sections of the block have no placeholders left. Chapters 2 and 3 can wait.
- **Connecting WhatsApp** requires that no placeholder is left and that you
  confirm the payment, location and shipping details with **«Sí, son
  correctos»** (*Yes, they are correct*). While something is missing, Channels
  tells you what it is, with a link to each item.

Your agent **never says a placeholder to a customer**: whatever you have not
filled in yet does not reach its instructions.

## Right after approving it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Scan the QR code">
    An agent with no number talks to nobody. The QR code is at **Customize →
    Channels**, and it's also what **starts your trial**: the days are counted
    from the day you connect.
    [Connect your WhatsApp](/en/getting-started/connect-whatsapp) covers it in
    full.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload what the agent sends">
    The catalog, the flyer, the price list and the photos are uploaded
    **afterwards**, from the **Library**, and there you decide which ones it can
    send over WhatsApp. When they come up is noted in the script.
    [The file library](/en/guides/library).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Redoing the funnel of an agent that already exists

If you go into an agent's **Playbook** and it still has no chapters, the screen
itself offers you **"Build it by talking"**: it opens the same onboarding, but
with that agent already in focus — it doesn't create a new one or take up
another slot. It's the way out for the agent you created "with no funnel" and
left half-done.

Talking is the only assisted route to the whole funnel, and that's on purpose: it's the one that
checks the script has its numbered steps, its shared context block and its
personality before proposing anything to you. There used to be a button that
wrote the funnel in a single pass, without those checks, and it was retired: the
scripts it produced were worse.

## If the copilot says you have no balance

The onboarding runs against the same balance as everything else. If you see
*"Tu cuenta no tiene saldo disponible. Recarga créditos para seguir usando el
copiloto"* ("Your account has no balance available. Top up credits to keep using
the copilot"), the advance ran out and the way out is to **top up**: your welcome
credit was already in the account in full, so connecting WhatsApp brings no
balance (the US\$ 25 bonus, if you were referred, arrives when you activate your
subscription). If your trial has expired as well, you need a plan.

**The onboarding isn't lost**: the agent and whatever its playbook already has
written are still there, and you can also carry on [in the
playbook](/en/guides/sales-playbook), chapter by chapter, with versions and
without spending anything.

## Common problems

- **I don't know how much detail to give it** — all you have. It's better to
  tell it too much and let it discard, than to leave it gaps: what you don't
  give it doesn't show up in the script.
- **It's asking me things I already told it** — sum it up for it in one message
  and carry on; you can also ask it to "move on to the script" when you feel it
  already has enough.
- **I closed the copilot without finishing** — go back to **Home → Create
  agent** on the same machine and the thread reopens where you left off. From
  another device it starts from scratch, but it recognizes the agent you left
  without a script and picks up on it.
- **I approved the agent but not the script** — the agent already exists with
  its chapter 1 written. You write the missing ones in its **Playbook** with
  **＋ Nuevo capítulo** (*New chapter*), or you ask the copilot for the whole
  funnel in the onboarding conversation, without taking up another slot.
- **The persona doesn't sound like my business** — it's fixed in [the
  playbook](/en/guides/sales-playbook), by clicking the active persona. Nothing
  has to be generated again.
- **It makes up prices or conditions** — what you didn't give it, it imagines.
  Prices and variants live in [the catalog](/en/guides/catalog); the hard rules,
  in [the playbook](/en/guides/sales-playbook).
- **I approved the funnel and it wasn't the one I wanted** — approving
  **replaces the whole playbook**, so from there on it's corrected in [the
  playbook](/en/guides/sales-playbook), chapter by chapter and with versions.
