An agent is **a salesperson**: it has a name it introduces itself with, a way of
speaking and a script. To get started you don't need a blank page: **you tell
your copilot what you sell** and it leaves the agent created for you. If you
sell over WhatsApp with the template, or if you book appointments, it's born
with a starting script, which you finish in the playbook.

The dashboard is in Spanish, so buttons and labels are written here as you will
see them on screen, with an English translation in parentheses the first time.

## What a funnel is

**The funnel is your agent seen as a sales conversation, written down.** It
isn't a chart or a report: it's what your agent does, arranged into
**chapters**, like the playbook you would hand a new salesperson.

A typical funnel goes like this: greet and understand what the customer is
looking for → present the product that fits them → answer their questions →
close and take the shipping details. Each of those stretches is a chapter, with
its rules and its examples.

And the important part: **it's yours and you edit it**. What comes out of the
conversation with the copilot is a first draft. From there on you live in
[the sales playbook](/en/guides/sales-playbook), which is where the funnel gets
corrected a thousand times.

<Note>
**An agent = a script + a character + a WhatsApp number.** The dashboard calls
the whole agent the **funnel** (*embudo*) — that's why the creation card says
«Creando el embudo» (*Creating the funnel*) and asks you for the «Título del
embudo» (*Funnel title*) — and sometimes just its script. The character — the
salesperson's name, their tone, their way of speaking — it calls the
**persona**, and it doesn't refer to any flesh-and-blood person.
If you sell two very different things, that's two agents, not two chapters.
</Note>

## Building it by talking: the normal path

When you finish signing up **the copilot opens by itself**, in the right-hand
panel (unless you arrived through the link of a funnel someone shared with you:
then it takes you to that funnel), and starts the conversation to build your
agent. On screen it's called the **«puesta en marcha»** (*setup*). While it
lasts, above the compose box you see this label:

> «Estamos armando tu agente.» (*We're building your agent.*)

Later on you open it with **«Crear agente»** (*Create agent*): it's on the Home
screen (*Inicio*), in the agent selector and in the search (**⌘K**; **Ctrl+K**
on Windows). Creating agents is a job for whoever manages the company: if you
don't see that button, ask that person to create the agent or to give you
access (see
[Invite your team](/en/getting-started/invite-your-team#what-each-person-can-see-and-do)).

The conversation spends balance, like everything the AI does: that's why your
account is born with a **US\$ 10** advance (see
[Pricing](/en/getting-started/pricing)).

### The first question: what you sell

The first thing the copilot asks you is **what you sell**. Below its question
three one-tap replies appear, and what you answer decides how your agent is
born:

| What you answer | How your agent is born |
| --- | --- |
| **«Vendo por WhatsApp y cierro en el chat»** (*I sell over WhatsApp and close in the chat*) | From the **Ecommerce template**. Continue at [If you sell over WhatsApp](#if-you-sell-over-whatsapp-the-ecommerce-template). |
| **«Agendo visitas o citas»** (*I book visits or appointments*) | With its **chapter 1** already written. Continue at [If you book appointments or screen applicants](#if-you-book-appointments-or-screen-applicants). |
| **«Filtro postulantes o solicitudes»** (*I screen applicants or requests*) | With an empty playbook: you write chapter 1 yourself in the playbook or ask the copilot for it. Continue at [If you book appointments or screen applicants](#if-you-book-appointments-or-screen-applicants). |

You can also answer in your own words. **If what you describe is a product
(«vendo ropa de baño» — *I sell swimwear*), the copilot takes it as selling over
WhatsApp** and takes you to the Ecommerce template, unless you tell it that you
book appointments or screen applicants.

<Tip>
**Would you rather talk than type?** During setup, above the compose box there's
a voice strip. Tap the microphone (when you hover over it, it says «Toca para
hablar» — *Tap to speak*), talk, and tap it again to send; each recording is cut
off at 2 minutes. With **«Escuchar en voz alta»** (*Listen out loud*) the copilot
reads its replies to you, and the speaker button mutes it. Voice comes switched
off, your choice is remembered in that browser and, like all the AI, **it spends
balance**.
</Tip>

## If you sell over WhatsApp: the Ecommerce template

When what you do is **sell over WhatsApp and close in the chat**, the copilot
doesn't interview you chapter by chapter. Your funnel is born copied from a
template designed for shops that sell that way, and afterwards you fill in what
belongs to your business.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the template or from scratch">
    The copilot asks you *«¿Usamos una plantilla de Ecommerce o armamos tu flujo
    desde 0?»* (*Shall we use an Ecommerce template or build your flow from
    scratch?*) and leaves you two buttons below it: **«Usar la plantilla de
    Ecommerce»** (*Use the Ecommerce template*) and **«Armar mi flujo desde 0»**
    (*Build my flow from scratch*). If you're unsure, the template is the short
    path.

    <Frame caption="Below the question, the two one-tap replies.">
      <img src="/images/agente/01-plantilla-o-desde-cero.png" alt="Copilot asking «¿Usamos una plantilla de Ecommerce o armamos tu flujo desde 0?» (Shall we use an Ecommerce template or build your flow from scratch?) with the buttons «Usar la plantilla de Ecommerce» and «Armar mi flujo desde 0»" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tell it your salesperson's name">
    With that name the **persona** that serves your customers is born, and the
    copilot works out whether it's **Asesora** or **Asesor** (female or male
    advisor); if it can't work it out, it asks you. If at the start you didn't
    tell it what product you sell, it asks you that too; if you already told
    it, it doesn't.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve the card">
    The **«Confirmar: Creando el embudo»** (*Confirm: Creating the funnel*) card
    appears with what it's going to create: the **Título del embudo** (*Funnel
    title*; for example, *«Carla - Venta de Vital Colágeno»*), the
    **Objetivo** (*Objective*), the **Forma de creación** (*Creation mode*:
    *Plantilla de Ecommerce* or *Flujo desde 0*), the **Vendedor**
    (*Salesperson*) and the **Producto** (*Product*). At the bottom, the
    **Aprobar** (*Approve*) and **Rechazar** (*Reject*) buttons.

    It asks for your approval because **creating an agent takes up a slot in
    your plan**. When you press **Aprobar**, the copilot tells you in one line
    that your funnel now has its shared block and its 3 chapters, and the
    dashboard takes you to your new agent's **Manual** (*Playbook*). That's
    where the questions end: there's no tour and nothing more to answer. The
    conversation stays saved in **Sesiones** (*Sessions*) as «Tu puesta en
    marcha» (*Your setup*); if you later want to build another agent, archive
    it first (see [If you stop halfway](#if-you-stop-halfway)).
  </Step>
</Steps>

**What is born when you approve.** A **shared block** with the business data
and three chapters:

| Chapter | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **1 · Saludo** (*Greeting*) | Runs once: greets with the product, its benefits and the promotion, and asks where the customer is writing from. |
| **2 · En venta** (*Selling*) | Answers questions, asks about shipping, helps pick the promotion and takes the order details (and the deposit if the order goes to the provinces). |
| **3 · Pedido confirmado** (*Order confirmed*) | Confirms the order, records the sale and hands the conversation to your team. |

Your agent is born active, with its persona already active and **without a
WhatsApp number**: it doesn't serve anyone until you connect it.

What only you know goes in as **placeholders** like `«PRODUCTO_MARCA»` or
`«ESCALERA_PRECIOS»`: the brand, the presentation, the benefits, the prices,
your payment methods, the frequent objections and the follow-up messages. The
salesperson's name, your business's name and the product's name come already
filled in.

The **SOBRE EL PAGO** (*About payment*), **UBICACIÓN DE LA TIENDA** (*Store
location*) and **SOBRE EL ENVÍO** (*About shipping*) sections come written with
typical details of an online shop in Peru (for example, cash on delivery in Lima
and a deposit for the provinces). Read them and change them if your business
works differently: before connecting you'll have to confirm them.

<Note>
**From scratch** creates the same three chapters and the same shared block, but
with **empty steps** for you to write. The shared block's placeholders are there
too, and they're filled in the same way.
</Note>

### Then, in the playbook

**Filling in the placeholders is your next task**, and it's done in the Manual.
The placeholders are highlighted in the text, and above the document a notice
tells you how many are missing (*«Faltan N huecos…»* — *N placeholders
missing…*).

<Frame caption="The notice at the top counts the placeholders that are missing (here, 20: 16 in the block and 4 in the chapters); the «Huecos» (Placeholders) button, which in this bar shows as a tag with the number, lists them so you can fill them in.">
  <img src="/images/agente/02-manual-con-huecos.png" alt="Playbook of a funnel born from the template, with the notice «Faltan 20 huecos: 16 en el bloque y 4 en los capítulos» (20 placeholders missing: 16 in the block and 4 in the chapters) and the «Ayúdame a rellenarlo» (Help me fill it in) button, placeholders such as «HORAS_SEGUIMIENTO_1» highlighted in the text, the index with each chapter's placeholders, the placeholders button with the 20 in the bar, the Recursos (Resources) panel open and empty, and the copilot open on the right" />
</Frame>

- **The «Huecos» (*Placeholders*) button** in the bar lists them one by one. You
  type the detail once and it goes into every place where it appears.
- **File placeholders** (the price image, the product photo) aren't typed: you
  pick one of the files your agent can send, that is, the ones that have **send
  via WhatsApp** (*enviar por WhatsApp*) switched on for that agent in the
  [Library](/en/guides/library) (or the ones you uploaded from the playbook's
  **«Recursos»** (*Resources*) panel). If you don't have it, **«No tengo este
  archivo»** (*I don't have this file*) removes it from the playbook.
- **«Ayúdame a rellenarlo»** (*Help me fill it in*), next to the notice, asks the
  copilot for help: it first asks you for your product's files, asks you one
  question at a time and proposes the changes for you to approve.
- What you fill in by hand is saved when you press **«Guardar cambios»** (*Save
  changes*).

[The sales playbook](/en/guides/sales-playbook) covers it in detail.

<Tip>
**Fill in the placeholders from a computer.** On the phone the playbook lets you
touch up prices, phone numbers and links, but to fill in the placeholders or
rewrite the script it asks you to open it on the computer.
</Tip>

**When you can test and when you can connect:**

- **«Probar»** (*Test*, the Manual's test chat) turns on when chapter 1 and the
  block's **PRODUCTO** (*Product*) and **PRECIOS Y PROMOCIONES** (*Prices and
  promotions*) sections no longer have placeholders. Chapters 2 and 3 can wait.
- **Connecting WhatsApp** asks for three things: that no placeholders are left,
  that the three chapters have their steps written — in **From scratch**, that
  means writing all of them — and that you confirm the payment, location and
  shipping details with **«Sí, son correctos»** (*Yes, they're correct*). While
  something is missing, when you press **«Conectar / Escanear»** (*Connect /
  Scan*) in Canales (*Channels*) you don't see the QR code, but **«Antes de
  conectar:»** (*Before connecting:*) with what's missing and a link to each
  item.

<Frame caption="While the playbook is incomplete, Channels tells you what's missing before showing you the QR code: here, 20 placeholders and the payment and shipping details still unconfirmed.">
  <img src="/images/agente/03-antes-de-conectar.png" alt="The card of the agent «Sofía - Venta de Polos de algodón pima» in Canales (Channels), «Sin número conectado» (No number connected), with the «Antes de conectar:» (Before connecting:) block: «20 huecos (ver)» (20 placeholders, see) and «Datos de pago y envío sin confirmar (confirmar)» (Payment and shipping details unconfirmed, confirm)" />
</Frame>

As long as a placeholder is still unfilled, the line that contains it **doesn't
reach your agent's instructions**: that way it doesn't say a half-written
`«PRODUCTO_MARCA»` to a customer.

## If you book appointments or screen applicants

If you answered **«Agendo visitas o citas»**, your agent is born with its first
chapter written and the copilot teaches you the playbook using it. If you
answered **«Filtro postulantes o solicitudes»**, the agent is born with an empty
playbook: there's nothing to test yet, so the copilot shows you the screen and
asks you to write chapter 1 (or to ask it for the whole funnel).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Confirm the agent's creation">
    As soon as it knows what your business does, it offers to create the agent:
    the **«Confirmar: Creando el embudo»** card appears with the **Título del
    embudo** (your business's name) and the **Objetivo** (*Citas* —
    *Appointments* — or *Captación* — *Lead capture*), and the **Aprobar** and
    **Rechazar** buttons.

    It asks you because **creating an agent takes up a slot in your plan** (the
    copilot brings up the slot when the creation fails because you have none
    left). Your agent is born active, with its **persona** already active —
    with a name and a tone — and without a WhatsApp number. If you book
    appointments, it's born with its **chapter 1 already written**: the
    greeting, with your business's name filled in and a sample salesperson
    name, which you change in the persona (step 4). If you screen applicants,
    it's born with an empty playbook.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test chapter 1 and tour the playbook (appointments)">
    The dashboard takes you to your agent's **Manual**, and the first thing the
    copilot asks you is to **test it**: write «hola» (*hi*) to your agent in the
    test chat the dashboard leaves open for you, as if you were a customer.

    Once you've seen it answer, the copilot explains it to you using that same
    reply and walks you through the playbook screen: the dashboard points at
    each part — the chapter, its PASOS (*steps*) and the line that moves on to
    the next one — and explains it in a box, and you move forward with
    **«Siguiente»** (*Next*). If you turned voice on, it also reads it to you
    out loud and the tour moves on by itself when each step ends.

    If you screen applicants, this step is shorter: the dashboard also takes
    you to the Manual, but since it's empty there's no test. The copilot walks
    you through the screen and asks you to write chapter 1.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the chapters that are missing">
    Each stage of your sale is a chapter, and **you write the missing ones
    yourself** in the playbook, with the same shape as chapter 1: the agent's
    role, the numbered PASOS and the line that moves on to the next one.

    If you'd rather have the copilot write it, **ask it for the whole funnel**.
    It interviews you first, one question at a time and in plain language. It
    starts by asking you to tell it how you sell today, from the customer's
    first message until you consider the sale closed: that's where the stages
    come from. Then it follows up on whatever is missing from each stage, with
    no more than a few questions per stage. What it will want to know:

    - **Your business and your audience** — who buys from you, where you sell
      and how people reach your WhatsApp.
    - **The product** — what exactly you offer and what benefits you can state
      with confidence. What you don't confirm to it **isn't promised**: it's
      handed off to someone on your team.
    - **Prices and promotions** — each variant or bundle with its price, and the
      discount rules currently in force.
    - **Payment and delivery** — how they pay you, how long you really take and
      what details you need from the customer.
    - **Objections** — what they ask you most and **the answer you give**. That
      answer, exactly as it is, is the one the agent will use.
    - **What name and what tone** it should introduce itself with.
    - **Which cases go to a person** on your team.
    - **What files you usually send** (catalog, flyer, price list) and at what
      point in the conversation.

    With that it leaves you **a proposal of the complete funnel**, which you
    approve or reject.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the persona and connect WhatsApp">
    When you consider the playbook ready, the close comes, in this order: first
    you review the **persona** — in the Manual, press the pill with its name;
    there you change its name and its tone — and then you connect your WhatsApp
    at **Personalizar → Canales** (*Customize → Channels*). It's worth reviewing
    it first: when you connect, WhatsApp takes the persona's name as the line's
    name, and it's the first thing your customers see. If you change it later,
    the dashboard tries to update that name on WhatsApp too.

    If you approved the whole funnel, the dashboard takes you straight to
    Channels so you can scan the QR code.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
**Nothing is written into your agent until you approve it.** If you ask it for
the whole funnel, the proposal waits in the thread with **Aprobar** /
**Rechazar**, and until you press Aprobar your playbook stays exactly as it was.
Approving it **replaces the whole playbook**, including the chapter 1 your agent
was born with.
</Warning>

<Tip>
Answer in your own words and with concrete data: real prices, real delivery
areas, the phrases you already use when you sell yourself. A generic answer
produces a generic salesperson, and that gets fixed afterwards by hand in the
playbook.
</Tip>

## The welcome advance

**Building your agent spends part of your advance**: with the template, very
little; if you ask the copilot for the whole funnel or use voice, quite a bit
more. That's why you can do it before connecting WhatsApp: your account is born
with **US\$ 10** precisely for this.

<Note>
**It isn't a gift, it's an advance.** Those US\$ 10 are your **whole** welcome
credit, brought forward so you can build and test your agent without stopping
halfway: when you connect WhatsApp no more balance arrives (the US\$ 25 bonus,
if you were referred, arrives when you activate your subscription). What
happens that day is that **your trial starts over** with its full 7 days (the
clock had been running since you signed up), with up to 5 active agents, and
your agent starts answering the number **if you have balance left**: if you
spent the whole advance building it, you'll have to top up for it to answer.
[Pricing](/en/getting-started/pricing) covers it.
</Note>

## If you stop halfway

**You lose nothing.** The conversation lives in your account, not in the
browser: when you press **«Crear agente»** again, from this computer or from
another one, your setup reopens where you left it, with everything you already
told it.

You'll also find it in the copilot's **«Sesiones»** (*Sessions*) tab: right below
**«Nueva conversación»** (*New conversation*) there's **«Tu puesta en marcha»**
(*Your setup*; with your agent's name, if you already created it) and the
**«Reanudar»** (*Resume*) button.

<Frame caption="The copilot in the right-hand drawer, open on the Chat tab; your setup is in the tab next to it, Sesiones (Sessions).">
  <img src="/images/inicio/04-copiloto-abierto.png" alt="Copilot open to the right of the Home screen (Inicio) on the Chat tab, next to Sesiones (Sessions) and Memoria (Memory), with «¿En qué te ayudo hoy?» (How can I help you today?), four suggested questions and its compose box" />
</Frame>

- **Closing the drawer** doesn't delete anything: your setup waits for you.
- **Leaving it** — with the ✕ on the *«Estamos armando tu agente.»* label or
  with **«Nueva conversación»** — first asks you *«¿Salir de la puesta en
  marcha?»* (*Leave setup?*). Your progress stays saved either way.
- **To start another one from the beginning**, archive the one you have: in
  Sesiones, the **⋯** menu of «Tu puesta en marcha» → **«Archivar»**
  (*Archive*). The next time you press **«Crear agente»** a new one starts (if
  you have another setup that isn't archived, it resumes that one). Archived
  ones stay under **«Otras puestas en marcha»** (*Other setups*), with a
  **«Reactivar»** (*Reactivate*) button.

## Right after approving it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Upload what the agent sends">
    Your PDF catalog, the flyer, the price list and the photos are uploaded in
    the **Library** (*Librería*), and there you decide which ones it can send
    over WhatsApp. If your funnel was born from the template, upload them and
    switch on **send via WhatsApp** before filling in the file placeholders:
    the Placeholders panel only offers you the files your agent can send.
    [The file library](/en/guides/library).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your WhatsApp">
    An agent with no number talks to nobody. You connect it at **Personalizar →
    Canales** (*Customize → Channels*), by scanning the QR code (or, if your
    screen offers you **«Vincular con código»** — *Link with a code* — with a
    code). It's also what **restarts your trial**: when you connect, the 7 days
    start over, in full. Mind you, the clock is already running from the day you
    sign up: if 7 days go by without connecting, your trial shows as ended and
    the copilot pauses until you connect it.
    [Connect your WhatsApp](/en/getting-started/connect-whatsapp) covers it in
    full.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Redoing the funnel of an agent that already exists

If you go into the **Manual** of an agent that has no chapters, the screen
itself offers you where to start:

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **💬 Armarlo conversando** (*Build it by talking*) | Opens setup on **that** agent, instead of creating another one. It's the way out for the agent that was left with an empty playbook. |
| **🛍️ Cargar plantilla de Ecommerce** (*Load the Ecommerce template*) | Asks you for the salesperson's name, whether it's Asesora or Asesor, and the product, and puts the template's funnel into that agent. It appears on sales agents that weren't born from the template, when your account has it. |
| **📄 Cargar un manual de ejemplo** (*Load a sample playbook*) | A sample for you to replace with your own. It appears when the template isn't offered. |
| **＋ Crear el primer capítulo en blanco** (*Create the first chapter blank*) | You start writing yourself. |
| **✦ Redactar un capítulo con IA** (*Draft a chapter with AI*) | You give it your notes and the AI writes up a chapter for you as a draft, which you review before accepting it. |

**«Cargar plantilla de Ecommerce»** also works on a playbook with a single
chapter, from the index: it replaces that playbook, and the previous one stays
in the History (*Historial*). If that agent has ever connected its WhatsApp, you
first have to pause it.
[The sales playbook](/en/guides/sales-playbook) covers it.

Everything except **Armarlo conversando** is done from a computer. On the phone
you'll also see **«Enviármelo a la computadora»** (*Send it to my computer*), to
open that playbook there.

## If the copilot says you have no balance

Setup runs against the same balance as everything else. If it runs out, the
copilot leaves you a notice in the thread:

- **If you haven't connected WhatsApp yet:** *«Tus US\$ 10 de arranque se
  gastaron en armar tu agente: recarga para seguir.»* (*Your US\$ 10 starter
  credit was spent building your agent: top up to continue.*) The way out is
  **«Recargar créditos»** (*Top up credits*): connecting WhatsApp doesn't add
  balance, because your welcome credit was already in the account in full.
- **If you've already connected:** *«Tu cuenta no tiene saldo disponible.
  Recarga créditos para seguir usando el copiloto.»* (*Your account has no
  balance available. Top up credits to keep using the copilot.*), also with
  **«Recargar créditos»**.
  [Credits and top-ups](/en/getting-started/credits-and-top-ups) covers it.
- **If your trial ended:** *«Tu prueba terminó: el copiloto y tus agentes
  quedan en pausa hasta que elijas un plan. Tu saldo de créditos sigue
  disponible.»* (*Your trial ended: the copilot and your agents are paused until
  you choose a plan. Your credit balance is still available.*) If you've already
  connected your WhatsApp, topping up isn't enough: you need a plan, with the
  **«Ver planes»** (*See plans*) button. If you haven't connected it yet,
  connecting it starts your 7-day trial over.

If the dashboard can't check your balance, the notice offers you both ways out:
**«Conectar WhatsApp»** (*Connect WhatsApp*) and **«Recargar créditos»**.

The **«Recargar créditos»** and **«Ver planes»** buttons are seen by whoever has
access to the company's balance.

**What you already built isn't lost**: the agent and whatever its playbook has
written are still there, and you can keep writing
[in the playbook](/en/guides/sales-playbook), chapter by chapter and with
versions.

## Common problems

- **I don't see «Crear agente»** — creating agents belongs to whoever manages the
  company, because it takes up a slot in the plan. Ask that person.
- **I don't know how much detail to give it** — all you have. It's better to
  tell it too much and let it discard, than to leave it gaps: what you don't give
  it doesn't show up in the script.
- **It's asking me things I already told it** — sum it up in one message and
  tell it to carry on with what it has.
- **I closed the copilot without finishing** — press **«Crear agente»**, from
  this computer or from another one, and it reopens where you left off. It's
  also in the copilot's **«Sesiones»** tab → **«Tu puesta en marcha»** →
  **«Reanudar»**.
- **I want to build another agent and «Crear agente» takes me back to the
  previous conversation** — that's on purpose, so you don't lose your progress.
  To start another one, archive the previous one in **«Sesiones»** (**⋯** menu
  → **«Archivar»**) and press **«Crear agente»** again.
- **The card says «No se aplicó: la plantilla de Ecommerce no está disponible
  en este momento…»** (*Not applied: the Ecommerce template isn't available
  right now…*) — the funnel wasn't created and no slot was used. Try again in a
  few minutes.
- **«Probar» is switched off in my playbook** — in a template funnel, Probar
  waits until chapter 1 and the PRODUCTO and PRECIOS Y PROMOCIONES sections have
  no placeholders. The notice at the top of the playbook tells you how many are
  left, and **«Huecos»** lists them.
- **Channels shows me «Antes de conectar:» instead of the QR code** — your
  playbook still has placeholders to fill in, steps missing in some chapter or
  the payment and shipping details unconfirmed. Placeholders and chapters are
  fixed in the playbook (each item on the list takes you there); the details
  are confirmed right there, with **«Sí, son correctos»**.
- **I approved the agent but not the script** (appointments or applicants) —
  the agent already exists: for appointments, with its chapter 1 written; for
  applicants, with an empty playbook. You write what's missing in its
  **Manual**, or you ask the copilot for the whole funnel in your setup.
- **The persona doesn't sound like my business** — it's fixed in
  [the playbook](/en/guides/sales-playbook), by pressing the active persona.
  Nothing has to be generated again.
- **It makes up prices or conditions** — what you didn't give it, it imagines.
  Prices and variants live in [the catalog](/en/guides/catalog); the hard rules,
  in [the playbook](/en/guides/sales-playbook).
- **I approved the whole funnel and it wasn't the one I wanted** — approving
  **replaces the whole playbook**, so from there on it's corrected in
  [the playbook](/en/guides/sales-playbook), chapter by chapter and with
  versions.
