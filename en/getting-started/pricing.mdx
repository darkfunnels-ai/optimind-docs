Three things that are paid for differently and are best not mixed up:

| | What it is | How much |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **The trial** | 7 days, which start over when you connect your WhatsApp | **Free**, no card |
| **The subscription** | What keeps your funnels switched on | **From US\$ 97 a month** |
| **The balance** | The fuel for the AI, spent as you use it | You top it up; **US\$ 50 come back to you** every month you pay the subscription |

The trial, the subscription and the balance are all shown in one place: in the
side menu, **Administración → Dinero → Saldo y facturación** (*Administration →
Money → Balance and billing*).

<Note>
**The subscription and the seats are paid from the dashboard in dollars and by
card.** Top-ups too, unless the **«Recargar créditos»** (*Top up credits*)
window shows you **«Pagar en soles (Yape)»** (*Pay in soles (Yape)*): then you
can top up in soles with Yape. If your card does not go through, the team can
record a top-up or a renewal that you pay by Yape, Plin, bank transfer or in
cash: you will find it in
[How it is charged](/en/getting-started/pricing#how-it-is-charged), in the part
«If the charge fails».
Do not confuse it with the Yape or Plin of your sales: that is the money **your
customers pay you**.
</Note>

<Info>
**Can't see Saldo y facturación in your menu?** First open **Administración**:
that block appears collapsed. If, once it is open, the **Dinero** group is not
there, your user does not have permission to see the business's billing. Ask
whoever manages the team to switch on **«Ver saldo y facturación»** (*View
balance and billing*) for you; how permissions are given is explained in
[Invite your team](/en/getting-started/invite-your-team).
</Info>

<Note>
**Each business pays its own way.** If your account is in several businesses,
each one has its own trial, its own balance and its own subscription. Saldo y
facturación shows you those of the business you are in at that moment.
</Note>

## The trial

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="7 days" icon="calendar">
    The clock runs from sign-up and **starts over with the full 7 days when you connect your WhatsApp**.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Up to 5 active funnels" icon="users">
    The same as fit in one paid seat.
  </Card>
  <Card title="No card" icon="credit-card">
    Signing up does not ask for a single card detail.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

It includes **US\$ 10 of welcome credit**, and you have it **from sign-up**,
advanced so that you can build and test your funnel without getting stuck
halfway. If you signed up with a referral code, you also get **US\$ 25**, but
not during the trial: they arrive **when you activate your subscription**.

<Note>
**Why the clock starts over when you connect:** so that nobody spends their
trial setting up the funnel. If you take a few days to connect, you do not lose
them. That said, connect before 7 days have passed since sign-up: if you
**haven't connected by then**, the trial expires on the date that was set when
you signed up.
</Note>

<Warning>
**On connecting, no balance arrives: you already have all of it.** What happens
that day is that the 7-day clock starts over, and your agent starts answering
your number. The lifetime welcome total is still US\$ 10.
</Warning>

During the trial, the **Suscripción** (*Subscription*) box in Saldo y
facturación tells you how many days you have left: *«Prueba activa · quedan N
días»* (*Trial active · N days left*).

## What happens the day the trial expires

<Warning>
**Your agent stops replying that very instant. There is no grace period.** The
date is checked on every incoming message.
</Warning>

<Frame caption="With the trial expired, a notice at the top tells you so and the Suscripción box offers to activate it.">
  <img src="/images/saldo/04-prueba-vencida.png" alt="Saldo y facturación (Balance and billing) with the notice «Tu prueba gratuita terminó» (Your free trial has ended) and the Suscripción (Subscription) box showing «Prueba vencida — suscríbete para reactivar tus embudos» (Trial expired — subscribe to reactivate your funnels)" />
</Frame>

In **Saldo y facturación** the notice *«Tu prueba gratuita terminó — tus
agentes dejaron de responder turnos nuevos»* (*Your free trial has ended — your
agents stopped replying to new turns*) appears, and the **Suscripción** box
says *«Prueba vencida — suscríbete para reactivar tus embudos»* (*Trial expired
— subscribe to reactivate your funnels*).

Along with your agent, two more things are paused:

- **The copilot.** If you write to it, it replies that your trial has ended and
  that the copilot and your agents are paused until you choose a plan.
- **Switching funnels on.** You cannot activate a funnel until you subscribe.

What does **not** happen, worth knowing because it is what everybody fears:

- **Nothing is deleted.** Your funnel, its playbook, your conversations, your
  contacts and your files stay intact.
- **Not a single message is lost.** What your customers write to you keeps
  being saved and you see it in the inbox, even while the agent is silent.
- **Your balance is still there.** Whatever you have left, you use when you
  subscribe.

<Warning>
**When you subscribe, your agent does not answer the backlog.** The messages
that arrived while it was silent were saved, but they did not sit in a queue:
your agent **picks up with the next message** that customer writes. If nobody
writes again, nobody gets a reply.
</Warning>

To avoid leaving those customers without a reply, go through the inbox. If your
inbox offers you **«Responder con IA»** (*Reply with AI*), you can choose the
chats that were left waiting and ask the AI to answer them: it does so one at a
time, one per minute and only from 8:00 to 21:00 in your agent's time zone, if
the customer wrote to you 7 days ago or less. Each reply spends balance, like
any other. It is explained in
[Reply with AI](/en/guides/conversations).

### Notices you will get beforehand

They reach you by email, and they depend on whether you have already connected
your WhatsApp:

| If… | You get |
| --- | --- |
| **You have already connected your WhatsApp** | One email when there are **3 days** left and another when there is **1**. |
| **You have not connected it yet** | Emails on **days 1, 3 and 5** after your sign-up reminding you to connect it, and the notice when there is **1 day** left. |

And once it has expired, the next time a customer writes you get **a WhatsApp
message** starting with *«⚠️ optiMind:»* at the numbers you have in **Alertas
de error** (*Error alerts*), in your
[funnel settings](/en/guides/funnel-settings).
The notice goes to you, not to the customer. If that list is empty, that notice
does not arrive.

## The subscription

It is made up of two parts. With one seat, the minimum, it comes to US\$ 97 a
month:

| Item | Price | What it gives you |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Base** | **US\$ 50 / month** | A fixed charge per business, however many seats you have. **It comes back to you in full as AI credit balance.** |
| **Seat** | **US\$ 47 / month** | **Up to 5 active funnels.** You pay for at least one. |
| **Real minimum** | **US\$ 97 / month** | Base + one seat, with US\$ 50 of balance inside |

<Tip>
**The seat is not «per funnel».** A business with 5 funnels pays the same as one
with 1: US\$ 97. To switch on a sixth funnel you need a second seat
(US\$ 47 more a month), which you add yourself from the Suscripción box.
</Tip>

<Note>
**The US\$ 50 of the base are not a lost charge.** They come back in full to
your credit balance when you activate the subscription and again on every
renewal that gets charged. In practice, out of the US\$ 97 the net charge for
the platform is the US\$ 47 of the seat; the rest is fuel you have already
bought.
</Note>

### How it is activated

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go into Saldo y facturación">
    In the side menu, **Administración → Dinero → Saldo y facturación**.
    Look for the **Suscripción** box: it shows you the month's amount
    (*«US\$ 97.00/mes»*) and its breakdown, *«Base US\$ 50.00 + 1 cupo ×
    US\$ 47.00 — hasta 5 embudos activos»* (*Base US\$ 50.00 + 1 seat ×
    US\$ 47.00 — up to 5 active funnels*).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Press «Activar suscripción»">
    The button carries the amount: **«Activar suscripción — US\$ 97.00/mes»**
    (*Activate subscription — US\$ 97.00/month*). The secure payment window of
    Culqi, the payment gateway we charge through, opens. Enter your card
    details and pay.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Done: it is active">
    You see the **«Suscripción activada»** (*Subscription activated*) receipt on
    screen and it also reaches you by email. The **Suscripción** box changes to
    say **«Activa»** (*Active*), and the card you paid with is stored: it is the
    one charged every month.
  </Step>
</Steps>

From that moment your agents reply again (starting from each customer's next
message), and the US\$ 50 of the base reach your balance.
If you signed up with a referral code, your US\$ 25 also arrive that day.

### What is not charged today

**Today only the Ventas (*Sales*) app charges a subscription.** Calendario
(*Calendar*), Inventario (*Inventory*), Órdenes (*Orders*), Envíos (*Shipping*)
and Facturación electrónica (*Electronic invoicing*) show as **«Gratis»**
(*Free*): switching them on does not raise your invoice. The dashboard copilot
does not have a monthly charge of its own either, but its conversations **do
consume balance**, like any other AI work.

In **Saldo y facturación** you see it in your invoice table: Ventas appears with
its calculation (*«Cómo se calcula»*, How it is calculated) and its amount, and
the rest is grouped into a row *«N apps sin costo»* (*N apps at no cost*) at
US\$ 0.00. Below the table, **«Activar otra app →»** (*Activate another app →*)
takes you to the **Apps** screen, where each app shows its price. Only whoever
manages the team sees that screen.

<Tip>
**To run the numbers before deciding:** in your invoice table, open the Ventas

need and how much your estimate would go up. It is only a calculation: it does
not change anything.
</Tip>

## How it is charged

<Steps>
  <Step title="It renews on its own every month">
    It is charged to your **main card**: the one you stored when activating, or
    the one you put in later with **«Cambiar tarjeta»** (*Change card*). You
    see the date in the **Suscripción** box: *«Se renueva el … con tu tarjeta
    guardada (culqi)»* (*Renews on … with your stored card (culqi)*).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Three days before, we let you know by email">
    You get *«Tu suscripción se renueva el …»* (*Your subscription renews on
    …*) with the date, the amount and the card that is going to be charged. If
    that card no longer works, you have time to change it before the charge.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Adding a seat is charged prorated">
    In the **Suscripción** box, press **+** next to *«cupos contratados»*
    (*seats contracted*). **«Contratar un cupo más (+5 embudos)»** (*Add one
    more seat (+5 funnels)*) opens and tells you the *«Cobro prorrateado hoy»*
    (*Prorated charge today*), how many funnels you go up to and how your
    estimate for the month changes. You pay only for the part of the period
    that is left, with a minimum of US\$ 1. With a stored card it is charged on
    the spot and the seat is available immediately.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Removing a seat is not immediate">
    Press **−**. The removal is scheduled for your next billing date: until
    then you keep your funnels, and what you have already paid is not refunded.
    The box says so with *«Tu cuota baja a N cupos (M embudos) el …»* (*Your
    plan goes down to N seats (M funnels) on …*). It is rejected if you have
    more funnels switched on than would fit without that seat: switch some off
    first.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
**Seats are managed with the subscription active.** During the trial the **+**
and **−** buttons do not appear. And if the renewal has had three failed
charge attempts, you cannot add seats until you pay it.

If you had a removal scheduled, pressing **+** cancels it without charging you
anything, even if the window shows you the prorated amount.
</Note>

### Your cards: the main one and the backup

They are managed in the **Métodos de pago** (*Payment methods*) box in Saldo y
facturación. Your first card is stored automatically when you activate the
subscription or make your first top-up by card; before that you will see
*«Aún no hay una tarjeta guardada»* (*There is no stored card yet*).

| You want to… | How |
| --- | --- |
| **Change your card** | Press **«Cambiar tarjeta»**. The payment window opens with the title *«Cambiar tarjeta · sin cobro»* (*Change card · no charge*). From that moment, charges use the new one and the previous one is no longer stored (your backup card, if you have one, stays as it is). |
| **Have a backup card** | Press **«Agregar tarjeta de respaldo»** (*Add backup card*); the window says *«Tarjeta de respaldo · sin cobro»* (*Backup card · no charge*). |
| **Make the backup card the main one** | Press **«Usar como principal»** (*Use as main*). The one that was the main card becomes the backup. |
| **Remove the backup card** | Press **«Quitar»** (*Remove*) and confirm with **«Sí, quitar»** (*Yes, remove*). |

<Tip>
**Even if the window's button says «Pagar» (*Pay*), nothing is charged.** When
you change or add a card, the window title warns *«sin cobro»* (*no charge*):
the card is only stored. If the bank rejects it, your previous card stays as it
was.
</Tip>

**What the backup card is for:** if the bank rejects your main card on a
renewal or an auto top-up, the backup card is charged on the spot, and the
email for that charge tells you so. In the payments you make yourself from the
dashboard — topping up, adding a seat, **«Pagar ahora»** (*Pay now*) — the main
card is used.

The main card cannot be removed: it is replaced by another one. If what you
want is to stop paying, you have to cancel (see
[What you cannot do on your own today](/en/getting-started/pricing#what-you-cannot-do-on-your-own-today)).

### If the charge fails

From the **first rejection**, the **Suscripción** box changes its badge to
**«Pago pendiente»** (*Payment pending*) and tells you *«No pudimos cobrar tu
renovación. Volvemos a intentarlo el … con tu tarjeta guardada.»* (*We could
not charge your renewal. We will try again on … with your stored card.*) You
have three ways out:

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **«Pagar ahora — US\$ 97.00»** (*Pay now*) | Charges the renewal on the spot to your stored card. Below it, it tells you which one: *«Se cobra en el acto a tu Visa •••• 1234.»* (*It is charged on the spot to your Visa •••• 1234.*) |
| **«Pagar con otra tarjeta»** (*Pay with another card*) | Opens the payment window. The card you use becomes your main card and the previous one is no longer stored (your backup card, if you have one, stays as it is). |
| **«Pagar de otra forma →»** (*Pay another way →*) | Opens WhatsApp with our team and a message already written. You can pay by Yape, Plin, bank transfer or in cash, and we record it as your renewal. |

The amount on the button is that of your renewal: if you have more seats, it is
more. If you have no stored card, **«Pagar ahora»** opens the payment window
directly. When you pay, you see the *«Renovación pagada»* (*Renewal paid*)
receipt and the subscription goes back to **«Activa»**.

<Note>
**If you pay outside the card, nothing gets out of step.** The renewal the team
records counts just the same: your month renews and the US\$ 50 of balance
arrive. The following month it is charged to your card again, as usual.
</Note>

If you do nothing, this is what happens:

- **It is retried every day** with your stored card. If the bank rejected the
  main card and you have a backup card, the backup is tried in that same
  attempt.
- **We write to you by email** on the first failed attempt and on the third.
- **Your funnel is not switched off because of that:** its agent keeps replying for as
  long as you have balance left.
- **What does stop:** while the renewal is not charged, that month's US\$ 50 of
  balance do not arrive. And from the third failed attempt on, auto top-up stops
  working, so your balance can run out.

### Your receipts

Every charge that goes through the payment gateway reaches you **by email** with
its receipt: the breakdown of what was charged, what you paid with and the
**operation number** (*N.º de operación*). When you activate the subscription,
top up and use **«Pagar ahora»**, you also see it on screen. Automatic charges
(the renewal and the auto top-up) and the charge for a new seat reach you **by
email only**.

What you pay outside the gateway (Yape, Plin, bank transfer or cash) and the
team records **does not generate that email**: keep the proof of your payment.

<Warning>
**The dashboard does not keep a history of your receipts.** If you close the
receipt window, your copy is the one in the email. In Saldo y facturación you
see your balance and the month's estimate, not a list of charges: keep those
emails.
</Warning>

## What you cannot do on your own today

It is solved by writing to the team, but it is honest to say so before you find
out:

| You want to… | Today |
| --- | --- |
| **Cancel the subscription** | There is no button. Write to us and we cancel it. |
| **Go below the last seat** | The **−** button is disabled with one seat: it is the minimum. |
| **Remove your main card without putting in another** | It cannot be done. You can replace it with another one using **«Cambiar tarjeta»**. |
| **Pay by Yape, Plin, bank transfer or in cash** | Not the subscription: write to us and we record your renewal. To top up, if the **«Recargar créditos»** window shows you **«Pagar en soles (Yape)»**, you do it yourself; if not, write to us and we record the top-up. The first subscription does need a card. |
| **A SUNAT receipt or invoice (*boleta* or *factura*) for your subscription** | The dashboard does not issue it. Your proof of payment is the receipt that reaches you by email with each charge through the gateway; if you paid outside it, the proof of your payment. |
| **Delete your account if you sign in with Google, or if your account is also in another business** | Write to us and we close it. If you sign in with email and password and you are the owner, you do it yourself in **Administración → Cuenta → Ajustes** (*Administration → Account → Settings*), at the bottom, in **«Eliminar cuenta»** (*Delete account*). Careful: when you delete it you lose whatever balance you have left. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Am I charged anything during the trial?">
    No, unless you decide to top up balance or subscribe before it ends. No card
    is asked for to start. What is consumed in those 7 days is your welcome
    credit, and only when the AI works.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is the referral code credit collected by whoever referred me?">
    No: the US\$ 25 are collected by **whoever signs up** with the code, and
    they receive them **when they activate their subscription**, not on signing
    up and not on connecting WhatsApp.

    Whoever refers earns **separately**: **US\$ 15 of credit for each charge**
    of their referral — the first payment and every renewal — **up to 6
    charges**. If the referral stops paying, they stop generating rewards; if
    they come back, the count picks up where it left off until the six are
    complete.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where do I see my code to invite other businesses?">
    In **Administración → Plataforma → Referidos** (*Administration → Platform
    → Referrals*). There you have your code, the link to share (**«Copiar
    link»** (*Copy link*) or **«Copiar solo el código»** (*Copy only the
    code*)), how many referrals you have, how many are paying and what you have
    earned so far. You get an email when someone signs up with your code and
    every time your US\$ 15 are credited to you.

    Do not confuse it with the **Red de referidos** (*Referral network*) in the
    Ventas app: that is the program for **your customers** to bring you
    customers. It is explained in
    [Referral network](/en/guides/referral-network).

    If you do not see **Referidos** in your menu, open **Administración**: that
    block appears collapsed. If it is not there once it is open either, your
    user does not have permission to see the business's billing.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is the balance I bought lost when the trial ends?">
    No. The balance **does not expire** and is not reset every month: it is a
    single balance that accumulates and is still there when you subscribe.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I pay in soles or with Yape?">
    Not the subscription or the seats: they are paid in dollars and by card. A
    top-up, yes, you can pay in soles with Yape if the **«Recargar créditos»**
    window shows you **«Pagar en soles (Yape)»**. If it does not appear, write
    to the team: they can record a **top-up** or a **renewal** that you pay by
    Yape, Plin, bank transfer or in cash.

    The **first subscription** does need a card: it is the one that stays
    stored to charge the following months.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="If prices go up, do I keep mine?">
    There is no price frozen per customer: each renewal is calculated with the
    price in force that day. According to the
    [Terms](https://darkfunnels.ai/terminos), a price change applies going
    forward and is announced in advance.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I have two businesses in my account. Do I pay twice?">
    Each business has its own trial, its own balance and its own subscription,
    and each one is charged separately, with the card stored in that business.
    If you are in another business because you were invited, its subscription
    is paid from that business: your account pays nothing for being in it.
    Saldo y facturación shows you those of the business you are in; to see the
    other one, switch business. It is explained in
    [Invite your team](/en/getting-started/invite-your-team).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
