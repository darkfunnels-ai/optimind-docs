Sign-up is at [optimind.darkfunnels.ai/register](https://optimind.darkfunnels.ai/register).
It is **two screens**: the first asks for your details and the second, the name
of your business. **It does not ask for a card.**

The dashboard is in Spanish, so buttons and on-screen messages are quoted here
exactly as you will see them, with the English meaning in parentheses.

<Note>
Your trial is **7 days with up to 5 active agents and US\$ 10 in credit**. Your
account is born with **the full US\$ 10**, advanced at sign-up so that you can
build and test your agent without getting stuck halfway.

**The trial clock starts over on the day you connect your WhatsApp.** It runs
from sign-up, but when you connect it starts again with the full 7 days:
building your agent calmly does not burn your trial. That said, connect it
within those 7 days: if they pass without connecting, your trial shows as
ended, the copilot stops answering and you cannot create new agents. The whole
story is in [Connect your WhatsApp](/en/getting-started/connect-whatsapp).
</Note>

<Tip>
**Were you invited to another company's team?** Do not sign up here. Open your
invitation link (it arrives by email, or the person who invited you passes it
on) and activate your account from there. If you already have an account, that
same account joins the other company, with no new password. More in
[Invite your team](/en/getting-started/invite-your-team).
</Tip>

## With email or with Google

The first screen is titled **«Hora de empezar.»** (*Time to get started.*). At
the top you have **Continuar con Google** (*Continue with Google*); below it,
the form to do it with email and password.

- **With email and password**, when you finish you enter the dashboard with your
  session already open: normally there is no need to log in again (if it asks
  you to, see
  [Common problems](/en/getting-started/create-your-account#common-problems)).
- **With Google** you save yourself the password, not the rest. When you come
  back from Google the screen says **«Confirma tus datos para terminar»**
  (*Confirm your details to finish*): your email comes filled in and cannot be
  changed, your name comes from Google (you can correct it) and your WhatsApp
  is missing. The button is called **Continuar** (*Continue*).

If you had already signed up with that Google account before, there is nothing
to complete: you go straight into the dashboard.

## What it asks for, and what each thing is for

### Screen 1: your details

| Field | What to enter and what it is for |
| --- | --- |
| **Email** | Your email. It is your access to the dashboard and where your welcome email, your account notices and your payment receipts arrive. **Check it carefully: it is not confirmed by email.** |
| **Nombre** (*First name*) and **Apellido** (*Last name*) | How the dashboard names you, for example in your account menu. |
| **Whatsapp** | **Your personal number, not the agent's.** Choose the country (Peru, +51, comes preselected) and type the number without the country code. With Peru, for example: `987654321`. What it is for, you will see further down. |
| **Contraseña** (*Password*) | **At least 8 characters, with letters and numbers.** With Google it is not asked for. |

If you mistype a well-known email provider (for example, «gmial.com»),
**«¿Quisiste decir gmail.com?»** (*Did you mean gmail.com?*) appears below.
Click the domain to correct it, or **No, está bien** (*No, it's fine*) if your
email really is like that. That suggestion does not stop you: you can carry on
without accepting it.

And if the email ends in a typical «.com» typo, such as «.con» or «.cmo», when
you click **Crear organización** (*Create organization*) you get «El correo
termina en «.con»: ¿quisiste decir «.com»? Revísalo antes de seguir» (*The
email ends in ".con": did you mean ".com"? Check it before continuing*) and the
account is not created. Click **Cancelar** (*Cancel*), correct the email on the
first screen and try again.

Click **Registrarse** (*Sign up*) and you move on to the second screen. If any
field has an error, the form marks it in red and does not move on.

### Screen 2: your business

It is titled **«Crea una nueva organización»** (*Create a new organization*).
"Organization" is your business inside DarkFunnels: that is where your agents
will live.

| Field | What for |
| --- | --- |
| **Nombre de la compañía o equipo** (*Company or team name*) | The name of your business inside DarkFunnels. It is required. **Write it carefully: today the dashboard has nowhere to change it later**, even though the footer of the screen says otherwise. It is, for example, the name that the people you invite to your team see. If you came in with Google, the form suggests your own name: change it to the name of your business. |
| **¿Cómo te enteraste de DarkFunnels? (opcional)** (*How did you hear about DarkFunnels? (optional)*) | It is useful to us. You choose between Publicidad / Anuncio (*Advertising / Ad*), Buscador (*Search engine*), Redes sociales (*Social media*), Referido (*Referral*) or Otro (*Other*). |
| **Código de referido (opcional)** (*Referral code (optional)*) | The code of the person who recommended DarkFunnels to you. It adds **US\$ 25** of extra credit, which is credited when you activate your subscription. If you arrived through a link that carried the code, it comes already filled in. |

Click **Crear organización**. **Cancelar** takes you back to the first screen
with your details.

With email and password, this screen may show a Cloudflare **anti-robot
check**. It usually resolves itself in a moment; meanwhile, the footer says
«Verificando que no eres un robot…» (*Checking that you are not a robot…*) and
**Crear organización** waits. With Google there is no check.

<Note>
**If you entered a referral code**, when the account is created a notice tells
you whether it worked. With «Código de referido aplicado» (*Referral code
applied*), your US\$ 25 referral credit is waiting for when you activate your
subscription: even though the notice says «crédito extra sumado a tu cuenta»
(*extra credit added to your account*), today you will not see it in your
balance. With «El código de referido no existía» (*The referral code did not
exist*), the account is created anyway, with your normal trial credit.
</Note>

By continuing you accept the DarkFunnels Terms and Conditions and Privacy Policy
(linked at the foot of the first screen), and you also agree to receive
periodic emails with updates.

## Your sign-up WhatsApp: where it notifies you

<Warning>
**The WhatsApp from sign-up and the number that serves your customers play two
different roles.** You choose your customers' number later, when you connect
the channel, and it can be a different one. The sign-up one is where the system
writes to you, so it is best if it is your personal number.
</Warning>

Every time you create a new agent (with the copilot or from your assistant),
**that number is added by itself to three lists in that agent's
Configuración** (*Settings*):

| List in Configuración | What you receive |
| --- | --- |
| **Informar de las ventas** (*Report sales*) | A notice with 💰 every time the agent records a sale: the product, the total and the customer's details. |
| **Reportes** (*Reports*) | The summary every morning, at 8:00, with what happened the day before. It comes switched on (**Enviar el resumen cada mañana**, *Send the summary every morning*). |
| **Alertas de error** (*Error alerts*) | A notice if the agent fails to reply or to deliver, and the notices that your account has run out of balance or that your trial has ended. |

These messages are sent from your agent's WhatsApp, so they start arriving once
you connect it.

**That number is also useful for testing.** It counts as a test number: if you
write to your agent from it, it answers you as it would a customer. Your
business's WhatsApp lines are no good for testing: if your sign-up number is
one of them, test from another personal phone.

## If you entered your WhatsApp number wrong

There is no need to redo sign-up, but it helps to know two things:

- **The number stays saved in your account**, and your **Perfil** (*Profile*)
  has nowhere to change it.
- That is why **every new agent you create with the copilot or from your
  assistant is born again with the wrong number** in its three lists.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Correct it in each agent">
    In the **Ventas** (*Sales*) menu, choose the agent in the selector at the
    top and, under **AJUSTES** (*SETTINGS*), click **Configuración**
    (*Settings*). In **Informar de las ventas**, **Reportes** and **Alertas de
    error**, remove the wrong number with the trash-can icon. To add the right
    one, in the first list open **Elegir de mis números frecuentes…** (*Choose
    from my frequent numbers…*), choose **＋ Agregar un número nuevo** (*Add a
    new number*), type the name, country and number and click **Guardar**
    (*Save*). In the other two lists it already appears in that same drop-down:
    choose it there. When you are done, click **Guardar cambios** (*Save
    changes*).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask for it to be changed in your account">
    So that the agents you create later are born with the right number, write
    to us from **Ayuda** (*Help*: the question mark in the top bar) →
    **Escríbenos por WhatsApp** (*Message us on WhatsApp*).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## And in the next minute

When you click **Crear organización** you see «¡Cuenta creada! Entrando...»
(*Account created! Going in...*) and you arrive at **Inicio** (*Home*) with your
session open. There **the copilot opens by itself**, in the drawer on the right,
and starts the conversation to create your first agent:

- The dashboard sends the first message for you, **«Quiero crear mi agente de
  ventas»** (*I want to create my sales agent*). You see it in the chat like
  any other.
- The copilot asks you what you sell and offers you three one-tap answers:
  **«Vendo por WhatsApp y cierro en el chat»** (*I sell on WhatsApp and close in
  the chat*), **«Agendo visitas o citas»** (*I book visits or appointments*) and
  **«Filtro postulantes o solicitudes»** (*I screen applicants or requests*).
  You can also answer in your own words.

  <img src="/images/inicio/04-copiloto-abierto.png" alt="Dashboard Home with the copilot open on the right, its text box and the Sesiones (Sessions) tab" />

Your account already comes with **Ventas** (*Sales*), **Calendario**
(*Calendar*), **Órdenes** (*Orders*) and **Inventario** (*Inventory*) active:
there are no apps to choose to get started. The others are covered in
[The apps](/en/getting-started/the-apps).

<Info>
**Did you sign up from the link of a shared funnel?** Then the copilot does not
open: sign-up takes you straight to approving that funnel and, once you accept
it, to scanning the QR code for your WhatsApp. More in
[Your funnels](/en/guides/your-funnels).
</Info>

<Note>
**The copilot** is an AI chat that lives **inside** the dashboard; its header
says **optiMind**. It asks you what you sell and with that it creates your
agent with a script to start from, which you finish in the playbook. It is not
Claude or ChatGPT: it is part of DarkFunnels.
Everything the AI does **spends balance**, this conversation included: that is
why your account is born with **US\$ 10** in advance, meant for building and
testing your first agent. (The guides call something else "your assistant":
Claude or ChatGPT connected from outside, which is optional. They are all in
the [glossary](/en/reference/glossary).)
</Note>

Your **Saldo** (*Balance*), in the top bar, starts at **US\$ 10.00** and is
already usable: it is your welcome credit, **in full, from sign-up**. It goes
down a little with each reply from the copilot, so it may already show a bit
less.

You also receive a **welcome email** with the subject «Bienvenido a DarkFunnels
— conecta WhatsApp y activa tu prueba» (*Welcome to DarkFunnels — connect
WhatsApp and activate your trial*) and the button **Crear mi primer agente**
(*Create my first agent*), which takes you to create your agent with the
copilot.

<Note>
The email says that when you connect «completamos» (*we complete*) your US\$ 10
of trial credit, and when you hover over the **Saldo** chip it reads «Conecta
WhatsApp para activar tus créditos de prueba» (*Connect WhatsApp to activate
your trial credits*). Your US\$ 10 welcome credit is already complete in your
balance: **no more trial balance arrives when you connect. What starts that day
is your 7-day trial**, and your agent starts answering if you still have
balance.
</Note>

## Common problems

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="It says «Ya existe una cuenta con ese correo. Inicia sesión.»">
    (*An account with that email already exists. Log in.*) That email already
    has an account: sign-up stops and takes you to the login. Log in with it.
    If you do not remember the password, click **¿Olvidaste tu contraseña?**
    (*Forgot your password?*) and you receive a link to set a new one. If what
    you wanted was to join another company's team, log in with your account and
    open the invitation link.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="It will not let me past the first screen">
    Look at the red message under the field that is failing: «Por favor ingresa
    un email válido» (*Please enter a valid email*), «La contraseña debe tener
    al menos 8 caracteres» (*The password must be at least 8 characters long*),
    «Debe contener al menos una letra» (*It must contain at least one letter*),
    «Debe contener al menos un número» (*It must contain at least one number*)
    or «Revisa el número de WhatsApp: solo dígitos (8 a 15)» (*Check the
    WhatsApp number: digits only (8 to 15)*). In the WhatsApp field, type only
    the digits of your number: the country code goes in its own box, on the
    left.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="«Crear organización» cannot be clicked">
    The anti-robot check has not finished yet: wait until the footer stops
    saying «Verificando que no eres un robot…». If instead **«No pudimos
    verificar tu navegador.»** (*We could not verify your browser.*) appears,
    click **Reintentar** (*Retry*). It usually happens inside the Instagram or
    Facebook browser: open the page in Chrome or Safari.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="It says «El correo termina en «.con»: ¿quisiste decir «.com»?…»">
    Your email has a typical typo in «.com». The account was not created. Click
    **Cancelar**, correct the email on the first screen and click **Crear
    organización** again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="It says «Estamos recibiendo demasiados registros ahora mismo»">
    (*We are receiving too many sign-ups right now.*) It is a temporary brake on
    our side, not a problem with your details. The account was not created. Wait
    a few minutes and click **Crear organización** again; if it keeps appearing,
    try again later.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I came back from Google and it asks for details again">
    That is the normal step: Google does not provide your WhatsApp number or the
    name of your business. Type your WhatsApp and click **Continuar**; on the
    second screen, type the name of your business and click **Crear
    organización**. If it says «Tu sesión con Google expiró. Vuelve a entrar.»
    (*Your Google session expired. Sign in again.*), sign in with Google again
    from the login page and you will come back to this screen.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="It sent me to log in with «Cuenta creada…»">
    (*Account created…*) Your account was created; only the automatic sign-in
    failed. Log in with your email and your password.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I mistyped my email">
    The email is not confirmed, so with a typo you will not receive the welcome
    email, the notices or the link to recover your password. If you are already
    in, change it from your account menu (top right) → **Perfil** (*Profile*) →
    **Cambiar correo** (*Change email*): a confirmation link arrives at the new
    email.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I entered my WhatsApp number wrong">
    There is no need to redo sign-up: correct it in each agent and ask for it to
    be changed in your account. More in
    [If you entered your WhatsApp number wrong](/en/getting-started/create-your-account#if-you-entered-your-whatsapp-number-wrong).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Sign-up fails and the notice invites me to write to you">
    It is a failure on our side, not with your details. Click **Escríbenos por
    WhatsApp** (*Message us on WhatsApp*) in the same notice and we will look
    into it with you.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
