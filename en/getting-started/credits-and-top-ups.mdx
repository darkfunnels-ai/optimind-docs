Credits are **dollars**: US\$ 1 of credit = US\$ 1. There are no points, no packs
with odd equivalences, and no monthly expiry on the balance you bought.

The dashboard interface is in Spanish, so buttons and menus are named here in
Spanish with the English meaning in parentheses.

## Where the first ones come from

    If it changes there, change it here — these docs are a static deploy with no

Your trial brings **US\$10 in credit and 15 days with up to 5 active agents** —
plus **US\$25** if you signed up with a referral code.

They are credited **when you connect your WhatsApp**, not when you sign up, and
the 15-day clock starts at that very instant. Before that your balance says
US\$ 0 and it is not a fault. It is covered by
[Connect your WhatsApp](/en/getting-started/connect-whatsapp).

<Note>
Welcome credits are granted **once per account**, for life. Disconnecting and
reconnecting the channel neither duplicates them nor takes them away.
</Note>

## What consumes balance

What consumes it is **the AI's work**, and it is measured for real: there is no
flat rate per message and no rounding in your favour or in ours.

- **Each turn your agent answers** — what it reads from the thread and what it
  writes. A long conversation costs more than a "how much is it?" answered in
  one line.
- **The audio it transcribes**, per second of audio.
- **The photos it interprets** when a customer sends an image.
- **The documents it reads** when assembling your product's context.
- **Funnel generation and playbook proposals**, every time you ask for them.

What does **not** consume: having the WhatsApp session connected, or using the
dashboard. The spending is generated by the AI when it works, not by the fact of
being signed up.

## How much each conversation costs you

The unit that really matters is not the token, it is the conversation. That is
why the credits card in **Saldo y facturación** ("Balance and billing")
translates it with your own data: *"This month, each conversation cost you
~US\$ 0.15"*, followed by how many conversations your agents started this month
and how much they consumed between them all.

It is an **average for the current month**, not a rate: it comes from dividing
what your agents consumed by the conversations they started, and it is the same
number you see on Home as **Costo por conversación** ("Cost per conversation").
While there are no conversations in the month, the card tells you so instead of
inventing a figure.

<Tip>
If the average looks high to you, it is nearly always long conversations or lots
of audio: the playbook decides how much your agent talks, and a script that
closes in fewer steps also costs less.
</Tip>

## How long it lasts you

It depends on how many conversations you have and how long they are, so any
number we put here would be a lie for somebody.

The dashboard tells you with your data: in **Saldo y facturación** ("Balance and
billing"), the credits card projects *"At your consumption rate, the balance
lasts ~N days"* using your real consumption for the month. It is the one figure
worth trusting.

## What happens when you reach zero

<Warning>
When you run out of balance **your agents stop answering new turns**. The
conversations are not deleted and the number stays connected: it is simply that
nobody answers until you top up.
</Warning>

<Warning>
**What is lost: the turn that was not answered.** The messages people write to
you while you have no balance **are saved and you see them in the inbox**, but
they do not sit in a queue: on topping up, your agent **does not answer the
backlog**. It picks up with the next message that customer writes. If nobody
writes again, nobody gets a reply — which is why it is better to top up
beforehand, and to review by hand what came in in the meantime.
</Warning>

The cut-off does not wait for exactly US\$ 0: your agent goes quiet when the
balance drops below **US\$ 0.10**, so you can see a remainder on screen and have
it silent all the same. That is why the dashboard distinguishes three notices,
and each one tells the truth about its number:

| Notice | What happened |
| --- | --- |
| **Saldo insuficiente** ("Insufficient balance") | You have something left, but less than a turn costs. |
| **Saldo agotado** ("Balance exhausted") | You reached zero. |
| **Saldo en deuda** ("Balance in debt") | The balance went negative. Your next top-up deducts the debt automatically. |

The **Saldo** ("Balance") chip in the top bar turns amber when little is left and
red when the agent no longer answers: it is the notice you will see first,
without going into any screen.

## How to top up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go into Saldo y facturación">
    In the side menu, block **Administración → Dinero** ("Administration →
    Money"). Or press the **Saldo** ("Balance") chip in the top bar, which leads
    to the same place.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Press 'Recargar créditos'">
    The pack picker opens: **US\$ 5, 10, 25, 50 or 100**. Choose and confirm.
    Everything is paid **in dollars, by card**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay by card">
    The charge goes through the payment gateway; your card is stored for the
    next top-ups, which are charged instantly without typing it again. The
    balance is credited straight away and you will see it in the history as a
    **Recarga** ("Top-up") movement with its reference.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
Top up **before** you hit zero. While the balance is exhausted your agent does
not answer, and a sale that was not answered in time costs quite a bit more than
US\$ 10.
</Tip>

If the payment does not go through or the packs do not load, the dashboard
itself offers **Contactar al equipo** ("Contact the team") over WhatsApp and we
apply it by hand.

## Trial, subscription and balance are three different things

It is worth not mixing them up:

- **The trial** is 15 days with up to 5 active agents. When it ends, your agents
  stop answering new turns even if you have balance to spare.
- **The subscription** is what gives you back the active agents when the trial
  ends.
- **The balance** is the AI's fuel. It is spent with use and topped up whenever
  you like, whether you are on trial or on a subscription.

The balance you bought **does not expire** when the trial ends: it is still
there when you subscribe.
