DarkFunnels isn't a single screen: it's **pieces that switch on and off, one by
one, in your business**. Each piece is an **app**. If an app is on and you have
access to it, it shows up in the left-hand menu. If not, that row simply doesn't
exist for you.

That's where the number-one confusion comes from: **two businesses open the same
dashboard and see different menus.** Neither one is broken. One has more apps
switched on than the other. And the same thing happens inside a single business:
each person on the team sees the apps you gave them access to.

<Note>
**Why this page exists.** Other guides tell you things like *"you need the
Órdenes app active"*. If nobody has explained what an app is, that sentence
makes no sense. This is the explanation, and also the map of the whole menu.
</Note>

The dashboard is in Spanish, so this page names menus, buttons and labels the
way you'll see them on screen, with the English translation in parentheses the
first time.

## The apps that exist today

There are **six business apps**. Four come switched on when you open your
account; you install the other two yourself if you want them.

| App | On from the start? | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Ventas** (*Sales*) | Yes | Your funnels: the script, the conversations, the customers, the catalog, the files and the results. It's the heart of the product. |
| **Calendario** (*Calendar*) | Yes | The schedule: appointments, services, business hours and a public booking page. |
| **Inventario** (*Inventory*) | Yes | Real stock, with every in and every out recorded per warehouse. |
| **Órdenes** (*Orders*) | Yes | The orders your agents close, with what was collected and what was delivered. Also appointments, services and leads. |
| **Envíos** (*Shipping*) | No | Record the shipment for an order, request the waybill from the courier and track it. |
| **Facturación electrónica** (*Electronic invoicing*) | No | Electronic receipts and invoices (*boletas* and *facturas*) for Peru, with your Nubefact account. |

<Warning>
**Envíos and Facturación electrónica don't come installed.** Their card says
**No instalada** (*Not installed*) and you install them yourself from
**Administración → Apps** (*Administration → Apps*). Before you do, read
**[Worth knowing about each app](/en/getting-started/the-apps#worth-knowing-about-each-app)**
further down: both have limits worth knowing about.
</Warning>

<Info>
Some apps only appear in certain accounts while we test them there. If your Apps
panel shows a business app that isn't in this table, it's one of those.
</Info>

### And three Core pieces

In **Administración → Apps**, besides the business apps, you'll see three cards
marked with the **Core** tag. They have no buttons: from **Apps** they can't be
deactivated or uninstalled.

| Piece | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Copiloto optiMind** (*optiMind Copilot*) | The AI that lives inside the dashboard. It helps you build and fix your funnel, and work in your apps by proposing changes that you approve. |
| **Identidad y accesos** (*Identity and access*) | Who gets into your account and with what access. |
| **Puente MCP** (*MCP bridge*) | The optional connection for running your business from Claude, ChatGPT or Codex. |

<Note>
**The Copilot has no menu entry.** It opens with its button in the top bar, or
with **⌘J** (**Ctrl+J** on Windows). On wide screens it docks to the right-hand
side; on screens 1280 px wide or less it opens as a drawer that overlays the
page. It goes with you through every section as you navigate.

**Each person has their own copilot.** Nobody else on the team sees what you
talk about with it, and it only reads and proposes in the apps that person has
access to. The **«Guíame»** (*Guide me*) helper, which points out on screen
where to click, is built on top of the copilot: it takes you to screens of apps
that are active in your business and that you can open.

**The Puente MCP** is Core, but its card doesn't tell you whether you've already
connected an assistant: Core cards show the tag instead of the status. What you
have connected is shown in **Personalizar → Asistentes IA** (*Customize → AI
assistants*). [The connection URL](/en/reference/connection-url) covers it.
</Note>

no gap: they're skipped.

## What each person on your team sees

Your menu depends on two things: **which apps are switched on in the business**
and **which ones you have access to**. An app shows up in your menu when both
are true.

Access is granted person by person and app by app — **Ventas included** — in
**Administración → Personas y accesos**. Whoever opens the account has all of
it.

| If a person is missing… | What happens to them |
| --- | --- |
| Access to an app | That app doesn't show in their menu. If they type its address, they see *«No tienes acceso a…»* (*You don't have access to…*). |
| **Ver saldo y facturación** (*View balance and billing*) | They don't see **Saldo y facturación**, **Consumo** or **Referidos**. |
| The **Copiloto** (*Copilot*) | They don't see the copilot button or the «Guíame» helper. |
| **Administrar el equipo** (*Manage the team*) | They don't see the **Apps** entry. |
| **Administrar las apps** (*Manage the apps*) | Even if they see **Apps**, they can't install, activate, deactivate or uninstall apps. |

How to invite someone and hand out access:
[Invite your team](/en/getting-started/invite-your-team).

<Note>
**One account, several businesses.** Apps and access belong to each business.
If your account is in more than one, open your account menu (the circle at the
top right) and click the other business's name: underneath it says **Cambiar a
esta empresa** (*Switch to this business*). The menu then shows that business's
apps.
</Note>

## App, agent and funnel are not the same thing

Three words that get mixed up on day one:

| Word | What it is | Whose it is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **App** | A capability of your business, like **Ventas** (*Sales*) or **Calendario** (*Calendar*), that's switched on or off. | **The whole business's**. |
| **Funnel** (*embudo*) | What you create inside **Ventas** to sell over WhatsApp: a script, a WhatsApp number and an agent. You select it in the selector, set it up, duplicate it or share it. | It lives **inside the Ventas app**. |
| **Agent** (*agente*) | The AI that chats with your customers inside each funnel, following its script. It introduces itself with a name and a way of speaking: its **persona**. | Each funnel has **its own**. |

In the dashboard, what you do with the funnel says «embudo»: the menu on each
funnel's card, on Inicio, offers **Duplicar embudo** (*Duplicate funnel*),
**Compartir embudo…** (*Share funnel…*) and **Eliminar embudo…** (*Delete
funnel…*), and Calendario with no funnels says *«Aún no tienes embudos»* (*You
don't have any funnels yet*). «Agente» is kept for the AI that chats: the
script it follows is the **Manual del agente** (*Agent playbook*).
[Your funnels](/en/guides/your-funnels) covers it, and each word is in the
[Glossary](/en/reference/glossary).

The practical consequence: **orders, stock, shipments and receipts belong to
the business, not to a funnel.** Switching funnels in the selector doesn't
filter **Órdenes**, **Inventario**, **Envíos** or **Facturación electrónica**.
That's why the selector hangs off **Ventas** and not off the menu's first level.
And switching funnels doesn't take you out of the screen you're on: if you're in
**Conversaciones**, you move to the new funnel's inbox.

<Note>
**Two things that confuse people here, and are worth saying:**

**1. Calendario does distinguish funnels.** Each funnel has its own schedule.
When you go in from the menu, Calendario shows you the schedule of the funnel
chosen in the selector. Inside the screen itself, with the schedule selector at
the top left, you can look at up to five at once.

**2. The Órdenes screen uses the word «embudo» (funnel) in its subtitle**
(*«Todo lo que el embudo logra — pedidos, citas, servicios y leads — del «sí» al
cierre.»* — *Everything the funnel achieves — orders, appointments, services and
leads — from the "yes" to the close.*). It's a turn of phrase: the order list
you see is your whole business's, not the selected funnel's.
</Note>

## Worth knowing about each app

What's worth knowing about each app before you trust it with your operation:

| App | Worth knowing |
| --- | --- |
| **Ventas** | It's where almost all of the product happens: your funnels, their conversations and what their agents sell. It's the app that charges a subscription. |
| **Órdenes** | It brings together the orders your agents close and the ones you note down yourself with **Pedido manual**. |
| **Inventario** | Warehouses, movements and overselling. A product you didn't switch stock control on for shows up as **Sin control**. |
| **Calendario** | Each schedule has its own services, business hours, blocked times, reminders and public booking page. The settings open with the gear next to the schedule selector. |
| **Envíos** | Today the courier connected for requesting waybills from the dashboard is **Shalom**. With another courier, the shipment is recorded just the same, but you get the waybill in that courier's system. |
| **Facturación electrónica** | It issues with your **Nubefact** account, and each receipt comes from a copilot proposal that you approve. |

<Warning>
**If you're going to install Envíos with Shalom, this changes what you expect
from it.** The connection doesn't use an API key: it uses **your courier account
password**. You have to pick from the list the agency you dispatch from (type
your district and choose it). And the **Pedir las guías al courier desde aquí**
(*Request waybills from the courier here*) checkbox starts off on purpose: with
it on, each shipment's **Pedir la guía al courier** (*Request the waybill from
the courier*) button creates a real waybill in your Shalom account, and you're
charged for it. Nothing requests it on its own: a person presses it. Try it
with one real shipment before trusting it with a batch.
</Warning>

<Warning>
**If you're going to install Facturación electrónica: nothing is issued from
its screen.** There you set up the issuer — your tax details, your Nubefact
account and the series for *boletas* and *facturas* — and look at the receipts
already issued. Each issuance arrives as a copilot proposal that you approve, so
don't look for an **Emitir** (*Issue*) button: there isn't one. Before
depending on it, try it with a single receipt.
</Warning>

## What they cost

**Today only the Ventas app charges a subscription.** Calendario, Inventario,
Órdenes, Envíos and Facturación electrónica say **Gratis** (*Free*) on their
card: switching them on doesn't raise your bill. The Core cards also say
**Gratis**.

<Warning>
**«Gratis» means "no subscription", not "no cost".** Everything the AI does
spends your balance: your agents' replies and whatever you ask the copilot,
including when it works in Órdenes, Inventario or Facturación electrónica. In
**Consumo** you see it split between **Ventas** and **Copiloto** (*Copilot*).
</Warning>

The Ventas arithmetic — the US\$ 50 monthly base that comes back to you as
balance, and the funnel quota — is set out in full in **[Plans and
pricing](/en/getting-started/pricing)**.

## Where they're switched on and off

In **Administración → Apps**. That entry is visible to whoever can manage the
team; to begin with, the account owner. To install, activate, deactivate or
uninstall apps you also need to be able to **manage the apps**. The account
owner can do both from sign-up.

Each app is a card with its status, its price and, if it's active, the month's
estimated charge. At the top you have the **«Buscar apps…»** (*Search apps…*)
search box and the buttons to filter by category (**Todas**, **Comercial**,
**Operación**, **Plataforma** — *All, Commercial, Operations, Platform*).

### How to install an app

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Apps">
    In the left-hand menu, open **Administración** and click **Apps**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the card and click Instalar">
    Type its name in **«Buscar apps…»** or filter by category. On the app's
    card, click **Instalar** (*Install*).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the impact and confirm">
    A window opens with **your monthly estimate, before and after**. On a free
    app you'll see *«Sin costo: esta app no factura.»* (*No cost: this app
    doesn't bill.*) If it works for you, confirm with the **Instalar** button
    followed by the app's name (for example, **Instalar Envíos**). If not,
    **Cancelar** (*Cancel*).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give your team access">
    The app becomes **Activa** (*Active*) and shows up in your menu. Whoever
    installs it can use it right away. For someone else to see it, give them
    access in **Personas y accesos**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### An app's statuses

| Status | What it means | Button you'll see |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Activa** (*Active*) | Switched on and in the menu. | **Desactivar** (*Deactivate*) · **Desinstalar** (*Uninstall*) |
| **Desactivada** (*Deactivated*) | Installed but switched off. It doesn't show in the menu. | **Activar** (*Activate*) · **Desinstalar** |
| **Archivada** (*Archived*) | Uninstalled. Its data is still stored. | **Instalar** |
| **No instalada** (*Not installed*) | You've never had it. | **Instalar** |

<Check>
**Every button asks for confirmation before acting.** Installing, activating or
deactivating opens the window that shows you **what happens to your monthly
estimate**. Uninstalling asks for its own confirmation. You can look around
without fear of breaking anything.
</Check>

<Note>
**Deactivating and uninstalling are not the same**, even though they look
alike:

- **Desactivar** leaves the app installed and switched off. It's undone with
  one click on **Activar**.
- **Desinstalar** archives it. It requires the app to be deactivated first, but
  the button chains the two steps for you.

The three **Core** pieces allow neither: they have no buttons.
</Note>

<Check>
**Uninstalling an app doesn't delete your data: it archives it.** If you
install it again, everything shows up as you left it. The dashboard itself tells
you so in its subtitle: *«Desinstalar archiva: nada se borra.»* (*Uninstalling
archives: nothing is deleted.*) Switching Inventario off doesn't make you lose
your stock.
</Check>

<Warning>
**A rare case worth recognizing:** an app can show as **Activa** and carry, next
to it, a red pill that says **kill-switch**. It's an emergency brake that
overrides the status: with that pill in place, the app leaves the menu and its
screen says «App no activa» (*App not active*), even though the card says
«Activa».

It's set and removed by whoever manages the apps in your business, in
**Administración → Personas y accesos**, **Módulos** (*Modules*) tab. There the
braked app shows up marked **APAGADO** (*OFF*): click it and confirm with
**Encender** (*Switch on*). If nobody on your team set it, write to us.
</Warning>

## Common problems

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The documentation mentions an entry I don't have in my menu">
    That's normal, and it's what this page is about. There are three possible
    causes:

    - **The app is switched off in your business.** If you can see
      **Administración → Apps**, look at its card: if it says
      **Desactivada**, **Archivada** or **No instalada** — or carries the red
      **kill-switch** pill next to it — there's your answer.
    - **You don't have access to that app.** Ask whoever manages your team for
      it.
    - **It isn't available in your account yet.** That's what happens with
      **Anuncios**: it only appears in the accounts that already have it.
      There's nothing to switch on in **Apps** for that; if you want it, write
      to us.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I typed the address and got «App no activa»">
    It isn't a technical error: it's the screen that comes up when you type the
    address of an app that's switched off in your business. If you manage the
    apps, it comes with an **Ir a Apps** (*Go to Apps*) button. If not, it tells
    you to ask your administrator to activate it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I get «No tienes acceso a…»">
    The app is switched on in your business, but you don't have access to it.
    The screen tells you *«Pídele acceso a quien administra tu equipo.»* (*Ask
    whoever manages your team for access.*) and offers **Volver al Inicio**
    (*Back to Home*).

    If you're the one who manages the apps, the message is different: *«Ya
    está activa en tu empresa. Para usarla, date acceso en Personas y
    accesos.»* (*It's already active in your business. To use it, give
    yourself access in People and access.*), with the **Ir a Personas y
    accesos** (*Go to People and access*) button.

    If you were just given access and still don't see the app, reload the page.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My menu ran out of apps">
    If you have no apps left, the menu doesn't leave you a gap. To whoever can
    manage the team it shows an **Añadir apps** (*Add apps*) button that leads
    straight to the Apps panel. With the sidebar shrunk to icons only, that
    button doesn't appear: expand it first.

    If you don't manage the apps, Inicio explains it with *«Aún no tienes apps
    asignadas»* (*You don't have any apps assigned yet*): ask whoever manages
    your team for access.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="On the phone the menu names are different">
    On screens 768 px wide or less a bar of buttons appears at the bottom:
    **Inicio**, up to three shortcuts and **Más** (*More*). The shortcuts in the
    middle use short names and come from your apps in order of importance. With
    the four apps a new account comes with, they are **Chats**, **Agenda**
    (*Schedule*) and **Pedidos** (*Orders*). Everything else lives inside
    **Más**.

    | On the desktop | On the phone bar |
    | --- | --- |
    | Conversaciones | **Chats** |
    | Calendario | **Agenda** |
    | Órdenes | **Pedidos** |
    | Inventario | **Stock** |
    | Facturación electrónica | **Facturas** (*Invoices*) |
    | Envíos | **Envíos** |
    | Resultados | **Resultados** |

    The bar takes the first three on this list that you have. If you're missing
    one of the first apps, the next one moves up.

    <Frame caption="On the phone, the bottom bar: Inicio, Chats, Agenda, Pedidos and Más.">
      <img src="/images/inicio/05-movil.png" alt="Inicio on a phone with the bottom bar Inicio, Chats, Agenda, Pedidos and Más" />
    </Frame>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The menu looks different on another computer">
    What the menu remembers about you — which submenus you left collapsed and
    whether you shrank the bar — **is saved in your browser, not in your
    account**. On another computer, or after clearing the site's data, it goes
    back to its factory state. What does travel with you is the business's:
    which apps are switched on and which ones you have access to.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I shrank the sidebar and things disappeared">
    The bar has three shapes: **expanded** with the names, **shrunk** to icons
    only (with tooltips when you hover) and, in windows 1024 px wide or less, a
    **drawer** that overlays the page and opens with the **Abrir menú** (*Open
    menu*) button in the top bar.

    In the shrunk shape each app is a single icon, **Administración** too (a
    gear that takes you to its first screen), the **Añadir apps** button isn't
    there and the funnel selector moves up, next to **Inicio**.

    The button to change shape is right at the bottom: expanded, it reads
    **Colapsar** (*Collapse*); shrunk, only its icon is left, and on hover the
    tooltip says **Expandir** (*Expand*).

    <Tip>
    The bar also shrinks on its own when the screen runs short of room, for
    example when you open the copilot in a medium-sized window. It comes back
    on its own as soon as there's space.
    </Tip>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="There are screens I can't reach from the menu">
    Some open from inside others. The two people look for most:

    - **Personas**, where you define who your agent is, what face it has and
      how it sounds: it opens from the **Manual** (in the persona panel,
      **Gestionar Personas** — *Manage Personas*) or from
      **Personalizar → Medios IA** (*Customize → AI media*), with
      **Configurar por persona** (*Set up per persona*).
    - **The schedule settings**: they open with the gear next to the schedule
      selector on the **Calendario** screen.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
