Right after sign-up, the dashboard leaves you on **Inicio** ("Home"), the screen
you are going to look at every day. To the side, the copilot opens by itself so
you can build your first agent through conversation. This guide names each piece
of Home and tells you what to do with it.

You can come back to Home whenever you like from the logo, top left, or from
**Inicio**, the first item in the menu.

The dashboard interface is in Spanish, so buttons and menus are named here in
Spanish with the English meaning in parentheses.

<Note>
**Your Home is built from your apps and from what you are allowed to see.** It
brings the sections of your account's active apps (Ventas "Sales", Calendario
"Calendar"…) and only what each person has permission for, so someone on your team may see a different
Home from yours. Some apps only appear in certain accounts.
</Note>

## The first thing you see: the copilot building your agent

On the right, the **copilot** opens by itself, with **optiMind** in its header.
On a wide screen it takes up a column next to Home; on a narrower screen or on
the phone it comes in as a drawer from the side.

You do not have to write the first message: the dashboard sends it for you,
**"Quiero crear mi agente de ventas"** ("I want to create my sales agent"), and
you see it in the thread as yours. The copilot answers by asking what you sell
and, below its reply, leaves you three one-tap answers:

- "Vendo por WhatsApp y cierro en el chat" ("I sell on WhatsApp and close in the
  chat")
- "Agendo visitas o citas" ("I book visits or appointments")
- "Filtro postulantes o solicitudes" ("I screen applicants or requests")

You answer in your own words or with a tap, it asks you a few more questions if
needed and proposes creating the agent. **Nothing is created until you press
Aprobar** ("Approve"). While it lasts, above the text box you will see the label
**"Estamos armando tu agente."** ("We're building your agent.")

The conversation spends balance, like everything the AI does: that is why your
account is born with **US\$ 10** advanced. The full step by step is in
[Your first agent](/en/getting-started/your-first-agent).

<Frame caption="The copilot open on the right, with its text box and the Sesiones tab, where you pick the setup back up.">
  <img src="/images/inicio/04-copiloto-abierto.png" alt="The optiMind copilot panel open to the right of Home, with the text box and the Chat, Sesiones and Memoria tabs" />
</Frame>

<Tip>
**Closing the copilot loses nothing.** Close it with the ✕ or with ⌘J (Ctrl+J
on Windows) and, when you open it again, it carries on in the same thread.

Leaving the setup is something else. If you press the × on the "Estamos armando
tu agente." label or the **+** in the copilot header (**Nueva conversación**,
"New conversation"), the copilot asks you **"¿Salir de la puesta en marcha?"**
("Leave the setup?"). Your progress is saved in your account, not in the
browser: you pick it back up in the **Sesiones** ("Sessions") tab → **Tu puesta
en marcha** ("Your setup") → **Reanudar** ("Resume"), or with **Crear agente**
("Create agent").
</Tip>

With the copilot open, Home is still there beside it. That is what comes next.

## Home, from top to bottom

The order is the same on every visit:

1. **The greeting.** **"Hola"** ("Hello") and your name, with the line *"Lo que
   pasa hoy en tu negocio: conversaciones, ventas y embudo, en vivo."* ("What is
   happening in your business today: conversations, sales and funnel, live.")
   Just below, on the right: **Cómo funciona** ("How it works"), the text
   "Actualizado hace …" ("Updated … ago") and a refresh button (**Actualizar
   datos**, "Refresh data").
2. **Notices and start-up.** The banner for your trial or your balance, a funnel
   someone shared with you, and the start-up list.
3. **Pendientes** ("Pending"). What is waiting for an action from you.
4. **Ventas** ("Sales"). Your agents, their figures and the charts.
5. **Calendario** ("Calendar"). The pulse of today's appointments.
6. **At the bottom.** **Ver con datos de ejemplo** ("View with sample data"),
   when most of the charts are empty, and the **Vota las próximas funciones**
   ("Vote on upcoming features") bar.

The charts, your agents and their figures refresh themselves every minute while
the tab is in view, and new people, sales and appointments also arrive live. The
refresh button fetches them instantly. **Esperan respuesta** ("Waiting for a
reply") is read when you open Home: to see it up to date, go back into Home or
reload the page.

## The banner at the top: your trial

Under the greeting **a banner** may appear with the status of your trial or your
balance. What it says depends on the moment:

| When | What it says | Button |
| --- | --- | --- |
| You do not have an agent yet, or the setup is open | It does not appear, so it does not distract you from the step at hand. It only shows up in red, if there is a cutoff. | — |
| You already have an agent and have not connected WhatsApp | **"Tienes US\$ … para armar y probar tu agente — al conectar WhatsApp arranca tu prueba y tu agente empieza a atender."** ("You have US\$ … to build and test your agent — connecting WhatsApp starts your trial and your agent begins answering.") | **Conectar WhatsApp** ("Connect WhatsApp") |
| You connected and are in the trial | **"Prueba gratuita — quedan N días con 5 agentes activos incluidos."** ("Free trial — N days left with 5 active agents included.") | **Suscribirse** ("Subscribe") |
| The trial ended | In red: **"Tu prueba gratuita terminó"** ("Your free trial has ended"). Your agents stop answering new messages; your balance is still available. | **Suscribirse** |
| Your balance is not enough to answer | In red: **"Saldo agotado"** ("Balance exhausted"), **"Saldo insuficiente"** ("Insufficient balance") or **"Saldo en deuda"** ("Balance in debt"). | **Recargar créditos** ("Top up credits") |
| You have a subscription and balance | Nothing. | — |

**Conectar WhatsApp** takes you to **Personalizar → Canales** ("Customize →
Channels"). It is explained in
[Connect your WhatsApp](/en/getting-started/connect-whatsapp).

The figure in "Tienes…" is **your balance at that moment**: it goes down as you
build and test your agent. If you have already spent it, the headline changes to
"Tus US\$ 10 de arranque se gastaron en armar tu agente" ("Your US\$ 10 starter credit was spent building your agent").
While the banner is inviting you to connect, spending the balance does not turn
it red, unless it goes negative. (If the dashboard could not read your balance,
the headline just says "Tu prueba está lista", "Your trial is ready".)

If you run out of balance before you have your agent, or with the setup open,
you will see the red balance notice.

The three red balance notices mean the same thing for your agent — it stops
answering new messages — and they differ by the figure:

- **Saldo agotado** ("Balance exhausted"): you reached zero.
- **Saldo insuficiente** ("Insufficient balance"): you have a few cents left,
  less than it costs to answer a message.
- **Saldo en deuda** ("Balance in debt"): you went negative; your next top-up
  deducts that debt.

In all three, your apps and background jobs stay active. It is covered in
[Credits and top-ups](/en/getting-started/credits-and-top-ups).

### If someone shares a funnel with you

When another account shares its funnel with you, in this same area you see
**"Nombre de la cuenta te compartió el embudo «Nombre del agente»"** ("Account
name shared the funnel "Agent name" with you"), with **Revisar** ("Review") and
**Rechazar** ("Decline"). If you opened the link and have not approved it yet,
you will see **"Tienes un embudo compartido por aprobar"** ("You have a shared
funnel waiting for approval") with **Revisar**. What gets copied and how it is
accepted is in [Your funnels](/en/guides/your-funnels).

## The start-up list: "Pon tu empresa a operar"

It is the card with the progress ring and the label **"N de 4 pasos
completados"** ("N of 4 steps completed"). Its name means "Get your business
running", and these are the four steps that take your account from empty to
selling, in order. It is seen by whoever manages the business.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Crea tu agente (Create your agent)">
    **Crear agente** ("Create agent") button. It opens the copilot, which asks
    you what you sell. If you sell over WhatsApp, it also asks whether you use
    the Ecommerce template or build your flow from 0, what your salesperson is
    called and, if you have not said it, what product you sell. Then it proposes
    creating the agent. You complete its script later in
    [the playbook](/en/guides/sales-playbook).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Conecta WhatsApp (Connect WhatsApp)">
    **Conectar WhatsApp** button, which takes you to **Personalizar → Canales**
    to scan the QR. It is the step that starts your trial and puts your agent to
    work answering.

    It stays ticked as long as at least one of your switched-on agents has its
    WhatsApp connected. If the number disconnects, the step goes back to pending.

    <Warning>
    **If your agent sells over WhatsApp, complete its playbook first.** A funnel
    born from the Ecommerce template (or "desde 0", "from 0") does not link its
    line while something is missing. Instead of the QR, the Canales card shows
    you **"Antes de conectar:"** ("Before connecting:") with what is pending:
    blanks to fill in, chapters with no steps or the typical details to
    confirm. The start-up list does not warn you ahead of time: you see it when
    you get to Canales. It is explained in [the playbook](/en/guides/sales-playbook).
    </Warning>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ten tu primera conversación (Have your first conversation)">
    It has no button: it ticks itself when someone writes to your number. When
    it is its turn, the row says *"Tu agente está en línea — esto se marca solo
    con tu primera conversación"* ("Your agent is online — this ticks itself
    with your first conversation"). Test conversations from the dashboard do not
    count.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Registra tu primera venta (Record your first sale)">
    It has no button either: *"Cuando tu agente cierre la primera venta, aparece
    aquí sola"* ("When your agent closes the first sale, it shows up here on its
    own"). A sale of a product or of a service counts; an appointment or a
    contact detail does not tick it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Next to the button for the step that is up is **Guíame** ("Guide me"): the
helper points out on screen where to click. It is covered in
["Guíame"](#guiame-guide-me-it-shows-you-where-to-click), further down.

Below the four steps there is a discreet queue, **"Cuando quieras"** ("Whenever
you like"), with two optional improvements that do **not** count towards the
ring:

- **"Carga tu catálogo para que dé precios exactos"** ("Load your catalog so it
  quotes exact prices"), with **Añadir producto →** ("Add product"). It is
  covered in [the catalog](/en/guides/catalog).
- **"Conecta tu asistente de IA y pregúntale por tus ventas"** ("Connect your AI
  assistant and ask it about your sales"), with **Ver cómo →** ("See how"). That
  is [the connection with your assistant](/en/reference/connection-url).

<Note>
**There are no boxes to tick by hand here.** Each step is checked against your
real data, and the card checks itself again when you come back to the dashboard
tab. If you have just connected the number or received your first message and
still see it pending, go back to Home and wait a few seconds.
</Note>

Once you have at least one step done, the card starts **collapsed** into a
single row: the ring, the **"N de 4"** ("N of 4"), the **"Siguiente: …"**
("Next: …") with the step that is up — or **"Esperando: …"** ("Waiting: …") if
it is one of the two that tick themselves — and two buttons, **Ver pasos** ("View
steps") and **Ocultar** ("Hide").

<Warning>
**Ocultar** hides the card in that browser only, and there is no button there to
show it again. It does not cancel anything or tick steps: the four still count
the same, and if you sign in from another browser the card is still there.
</Warning>

With your first sale your business is up and running and the card goes away,
on your other devices too. It does not come back even if you later disconnect
the number.

## "Pendientes" (Pending): what is waiting for your answer

Below the notices, the **Pendientes** card appears when something is waiting for
**an action from you**. If there is nothing, the card does not appear.

For example, a **copilot proposal** waiting for your approval: a change to the
playbook that you asked for, or an action that asks for your confirmation, such
as creating your agent. Each row shows the title, the detail, which app it comes
from and how long ago it arrived.

Tapping a row takes you straight to resolving it. If it is a proposal, the
copilot opens in the conversation where it is waiting, with its **Aprobar**
("Approve") and **Rechazar** ("Reject") buttons.

- Five are shown at a time; the rest, with **Ver N más** ("View N more"). The
  number in the header is the total.
- A proposal leaves the list when you approve or reject it. If you do not answer
  it, it leaves the list in about **7 days**, but it is not lost: it stays in its
  copilot conversation, where you can still approve or reject it. And if the
  agent's playbook has changed since it was made, it withdraws itself, because it
  can no longer be applied.
- Each person sees only their own. The copilot reads this same list: if you ask
  it what you have pending, it tells you the same as the card.

The card refreshes itself every minute and when you come back to the tab.

## Your agents: "Personas que venden por ti." (People who sell for you.)

This is the **Ventas** section. On the left is a headline and on the right the
**period selector**.

**If you do not have any agent yet**, you will see **"Crea tu primera persona"**
("Create your first persona") — *"Una persona virtual que conversa y vende por ti
en WhatsApp. La configuras en un par de minutos."* ("A virtual persona that talks
and sells for you on WhatsApp. You set it up in a couple of minutes.") — with the
**Crear agente** button, which opens the same copilot from the beginning. That is
what whoever manages the business sees; the rest of your team sees *"Aún no hay
agentes"* ("No agents yet").

**Once you have agents**, the headline counts their work in the chosen period:
*"Tu agente atendió 12 conversaciones."* ("Your agent handled 12 conversations.")
or *"Tus agentes atendieron 54 conversaciones."* ("Your agents handled 54
conversations."). If there were no conversations in the period, it says
*"Personas que venden por ti."*.

The period starts on **Hoy** ("Today"). Tapping it lets you choose another
(**Últimos 7 días** "Last 7 days", **Este mes** "This month", **Mes anterior**
"Previous month", **Rango personalizado** "Custom range"…), and the arrows on
either side move you to the previous or next period. Your browser remembers the
last one you chose.

### The strip of figures

The first figure is **the only one that asks for action**: **Esperan respuesta**
("Waiting for a reply"), the conversations from the last 30 days whose last
message is from the customer. It does not depend on the period.

- **In amber**, with the number, *"la más antigua hace …"* ("the oldest one …
  ago") and **Responder** ("Reply"), which takes you to the inbox of the agent on
  the first card. The figure adds up the chats of all your agents: if you have
  several and do not see the one that is waiting, switch agent above the menu.
- **In green**, *"Todo al día"* ("All caught up"), when nobody is waiting.
- If it says *"No se pudo leer · reintentar"* ("Could not be read · retry"), tap
  it to try again.

Next to it go five figures to look at, all for the chosen period:

| Figure | What it counts |
| --- | --- |
| **Conversaciones** ("Conversations") | The conversations started. |
| **Conversiones** ("Conversions") | The goals achieved by your agents. |
| **Tasa de conversión** ("Conversion rate") | Conversions over conversations. |
| **Ingreso atribuido** ("Attributed revenue") | What your agents sold, in the currency of each sale. If you sold in more than one currency, it says *"varias monedas — sin convertir"* ("several currencies — not converted"). |
| **Costo por conversación** ("Cost per conversation") | What the AI spent per conversation. Below it, the total consumed. |

If the dashboard could not read the figures of some agent, *"Faltan las métricas
de N de M agentes: las cifras son un mínimo"* ("Metrics are missing for N of M
agents: the figures are a minimum") appears, with **Reintentar** ("Retry").

### Your agents' cards

Each agent has its card, with its name, its WhatsApp number if it is connected
and switched on, and its own figures for the period. Tapping the name takes you
to its **Conversaciones** ("Conversations").

- **The switch** turns the agent on or pauses it; the badge says **Activo**
  ("Active") or **Pausado** ("Paused"). When paused it stops answering and frees
  up its slot.
- **The ⋯ menu** has **Duplicar embudo** ("Duplicate funnel"), **Compartir
  embudo…** ("Share funnel…") and **Eliminar agente…** ("Delete agent…"). All
  three are explained in [Your funnels](/en/guides/your-funnels).
- **The dotted Crear agente card** opens the copilot to add another one.

<Tip>
**Crear agente** resumes your setup if it was left open. To start a new one,
archive it in the copilot: **Sesiones** tab, ⋯ menu of **Tu puesta en marcha** →
**Archivar** ("Archive").
</Tip>

If you want to switch an agent on and you already have as many switched on as
your plan allows, the dashboard asks you which one to switch off: **"Para encender
«Nombre» hay que apagar otro"** ("To switch on "Name" you need to switch off
another one"), with **Apagar y encender** ("Switch off and switch on"). If there
is nobody to switch off, it explains your limit — during the trial, *"Tu prueba
incluye 5 agentes activos"* ("Your trial includes 5 active agents") — with
**Suscribirse** ("Subscribe") or **Contratar un cupo más** ("Buy one more slot").
The slots are in [Pricing](/en/getting-started/pricing).

## The Home charts

Below the cards, **"Gráficas de"** ("Charts for") has one button per agent: you
choose which agent the charts are for. By default it is the one that brought the
most new people.

<Frame caption="The Ventas charts: new people, revenue and the funnel by chapter for the chosen agent.">
  <img src="/images/inicio/03-graficas.png" alt="Home charts with «Gráficas de» set to Agente de Ventas: Personas nuevas and Facturación with one bar per hour, Embudo with the chapters Saludo, Cotización and Cierre plus Compraron, and the start of Pulso de citas" />
</Frame>

- **Personas nuevas** ("New people"). How many new people wrote to that agent in
  the period, compared with the previous period. A person who writes through two
  channels counts once.
- **Facturación** ("Revenue"). How much that agent sold in the period, in the
  currency of each sale. It does not convert currencies: if you sold in two, you
  choose which one to see inside the chart.
- **Embudo** ("Funnel"). Of the new people in the period, how many reached each
  chapter of that agent's playbook and how many bought. It marks the step where
  the most people drop off.
- **Pulso de citas** ("Appointment pulse") (if you have Calendario), in its own
  section, below the Ventas charts. Today's appointments hour by hour, compared
  with yesterday. It does not follow the period or the agent button: it adds up
  all your calendars and shows today.

Each chart has **Ver todo** ("View all"), which takes you to its screen:
Clientes ("Customers"), Resultados ("Results") or Calendario. To read the
numbers more calmly, see [Orders and metrics](/en/guides/orders-and-metrics).

## "Guíame" (Guide me): it shows you where to click

**Guíame** is a helper that shows you, on the screen itself, where to click to get
something done. It is the most direct help when you cannot find a button.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open it">
    Tap the small atom floating on the screen (*"¿Cómo hago…? Pregúntame"*,
    "How do I…? Ask me"), or go to **Ayuda** → **Guíame paso a paso**. In the
    start-up list, the **Guíame** button already carries the question for the
    step that is up.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tell it what you want to do">
    It asks you **"¿Qué quieres hacer?"** ("What do you want to do?") and
    suggests ideas such as *"¿Cómo conecto WhatsApp?"* ("How do I connect
    WhatsApp?") or *"¿Cómo cargo mi catálogo?"* ("How do I load my catalog?").
    You can write your own.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow the steps">
    Each step highlights where to click, with a bubble that counts the steps
    (for example, **"Paso 1 de 3"**, "Step 1 of 3"; the total depends on what
    you ask for) and the **Siguiente** ("Next") and **Salir** ("Exit") buttons.
    The last one says **Terminar** ("Finish").
  </Step>
</Steps>

It only takes you to screens of the apps your account has active and that you
are allowed to open. Like everything the AI does, it spends a few cents of your
balance.

If the atom gets in your way, hide it with the atom in the top bar or in
**Ayuda** → **Ocultar el ayudante** ("Hide the helper").

## The "Cómo funciona" (How it works) tour

It is a one-minute guided tour, with four stops anchored to the screen itself
and a closing.

It opens by itself **once per browser**, while your WhatsApp is not connected
and the copilot's setup is not open. That is why, on day one, it usually appears
when you close the copilot or when you come back to Home after building your
agent. In demo mode it does not open by itself.

Before you connect WhatsApp, this is what each stop says:

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Your start-up list">
    Four steps, in order: create your agent, connect your WhatsApp, and the last
    two tick themselves when your agent talks and sells. It always tells you
    which one is next.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Your agent is a virtual salesperson">
    It answers on WhatsApp with a script you build here by answering a few
    questions about your business. Building and testing it is paid for with the
    US\$ 10 starter credit your account comes with.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The step that switches everything on">
    Your agent serves customers on your WhatsApp number: you scan a QR, like in
    WhatsApp Web. When you connect, your agent starts answering and your free
    trial begins.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Your balance is dollars that the AI spends">
    Your account was born with US\$ 10 to build and test your agent; when you
    connect WhatsApp, your agent starts answering. Each reply from your agent
    takes off cents; the chip turns amber when little is left and tapping it is
    how you top up.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="That's it">
    When your agent is online, write to it yourself from another phone: that is
    the honest test. And if you get stuck, we are on WhatsApp.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

If you open it after connecting, the WhatsApp stop reminds you that, if it
disconnects, you link it again in **Personalizar → Canales**, and the balance
stop adds the red: the chip turns red when the agent stops answering.

You move forward with **Siguiente** ("Next"), go back with **Atrás** ("Back")
and leave whenever you want with **Saltar el recorrido** ("Skip the tour") or the
**Escape** key. The closing has **Escribir a soporte** ("Write to support") and
**Listo** ("Done"). A click outside the bubble also closes it, and that click
reaches whatever you tapped.

<Tip>
The tour is not lost: repeat it whenever you like with the **Cómo funciona**
button in the Home header, next to "Actualizado hace …". On the phone it is just
a ▶ icon.
</Tip>

## "Ver con datos de ejemplo" (View with sample data)

On day one the dashboard is almost empty, and an empty screen teaches you
nothing. That is why, when most of the charts have no data, a discreet button
appears at the bottom, **Ver con datos de ejemplo**: it paints Home with a
fictional business so you can see what it looks like working.

While it is on, at the top you see the strip **"Datos de demostración — los
cambios no se guardan"** ("Demo data — changes are not saved"). Nothing you see
there is yours. You exit with **Salir del modo demo** ("Exit demo mode"), in that
same strip.

You can also turn it on and off from the ⌘K search: **Activar modo demo** ("Turn
on demo mode") and **Salir del modo demo**. Demo mode stays on in that browser
until you turn it off.

## At the bottom: "Vota las próximas funciones" (Vote on upcoming features)

At the end of Home there is a bar, **Vota las próximas funciones**. When you
open it you see a list of proposed features, each with its status
(**Planeado** "Planned", **En progreso** "In progress" or **Listo** "Done") and a
thumbs-up button to vote. If something is missing for you, **Sugerir una función
→** ("Suggest a feature") opens a conversation with us on WhatsApp. The bar
appears when there are features to vote on.

## On the phone

Home is the same, but a few things change:

- **At the bottom there is a bar** with **Inicio**, three main shortcuts and
  **Más** ("More"), which opens the rest. With the apps your account is born
  with, they are usually **Chats**, **Agenda** ("Schedule") and **Pedidos**
  ("Orders").
- **The copilot comes in as a drawer** from the side.
- **Not everything fits in the top bar.** "Agentes contratados", the ⌘K

  figure.
- **"Cómo funciona"** becomes a ▶ icon, and the live activity in the middle is
  not shown.

## Common problems

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I already have balance and haven't connected WhatsApp yet">
    That is expected: it is your whole welcome credit, US\$ 10 advanced at
    sign-up so you can build and test your agent without stopping halfway.
    **Connecting your WhatsApp** does not bring more trial balance (you already
    have all of it): what it starts is the clock of your 7-day trial. If it is
    already less, that is what building and testing the agent spent.

    If, when you hover over the **Saldo** chip, you read *"Conecta WhatsApp para
    activar tus créditos de prueba"* ("Connect WhatsApp to activate your trial
    credits"), do not wait for a deposit: your credits are already in your
    balance.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="“Agentes contratados” shows 0">
    As long as you do not have a subscription, that is normal: that figure counts
    the agents you paid for. What your trial includes is stated by the banner
    once you connect WhatsApp (*"…con 5 agentes activos incluidos"*, "…with 5
    active agents included"). When you subscribe, the figure goes up.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The steps card disappeared">
    The most common reason is one of these two:

    - **You hid it with Ocultar.** It is only hidden in that browser: you have
      not lost progress and you still see it from another browser.
    - **You recorded your first sale.** With it your business is up and running
      and the card goes away.

    If it is neither, check that you still have permission to manage the
    business: the list is only seen by whoever manages it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The tour didn't open for me">
    It opens by itself once per browser, while your WhatsApp is not connected
    and without the setup open, and not in demo mode. If you already saw it,
    skipped it or have already connected your number, open it with the **Cómo
    funciona** button, in the Home header.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I tested my agent and “Ten tu primera conversación” is still pending">
    The test conversations you launch from the dashboard do not count: that step
    is waiting for a **real** conversation, from someone writing to your WhatsApp
    number. Write to it yourself from another phone and it ticks itself.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I connected WhatsApp and Home still asks me to connect it">
    Go back to the dashboard tab and wait a few seconds: Home checks itself again
    when you come back. If it stays the same, reload the page. If in Canales the
    agent was left on **Conectando…** ("Connecting…") or on **Acción requerida**
    ("Action required"), the connection did not finish: it is resolved in
    [Connect your WhatsApp](/en/getting-started/connect-whatsapp#common-problems).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="In Canales I get “Antes de conectar:” instead of the QR">
    Your agent was born with the Ecommerce template (or "desde 0", "from 0") and
    its playbook still has something to complete. The list tells you what: blanks,
    chapters with no steps or the typical details to confirm. Complete it and
    connect again. It is explained in [the playbook](/en/guides/sales-playbook).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I see a business that isn't mine">
    You are in demo mode. Leave it with **Salir del modo demo**, in the strip at
    the top, or from the ⌘K search.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Someone on my team sees an empty Home">
    If it says *"Aún no tienes apps asignadas"* ("You don't have any apps
    assigned yet"), that person does not yet have access to any business app
    that is switched on in your business. Give it to
    them in **Personas y accesos** ("People and access"): it is covered in
    [Invite your team](/en/getting-started/invite-your-team).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
