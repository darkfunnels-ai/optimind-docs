Your agent closes the sale in the chat. From there on the work is yours: get
paid, dispatch and know whether the month is going well. This guide is the tour
of the screens where that happens, click by click.

<Note>
**Where everything is.** Go into the dashboard at
[optimind.darkfunnels.ai](https://optimind.darkfunnels.ai). In the menu on the
left:

| Screen | What it is for | How to get there |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Órdenes** ("Orders") | Your queue for the day: who owes you, what is left to dispatch | A menu row of its own, outside Ventas: **Órdenes** |
| **Clientes** ("Customers") | The people who have talked to your agent, with what it learned about them | **Ventas** ("Sales") → **AGENTE** ("AGENT") block → **Clientes** |
| **Resultados** ("Results") | How the business is doing over a period | **Ventas** → **Resultados** (the first one) |
| **Envíos** ("Shipments") | The parcels, the courier and the waybill | **Envíos**, if it is active in your account |

The dashboard is in Spanish, so throughout this page screens, buttons and labels
are named as you will actually see them, with the English meaning in
parentheses.
</Note>

<Tip>
**The golden rule of this guide: here money is recorded, not collected.** The
order stores what was sold and what is still to be collected. The money comes in
through your Yape, your Plin, your bank transfer or your cash, and **a person
records it here**. Your agent does not confirm payments. Normally someone on
your team confirms them, in the order record or by approving the payment
receipt.
</Tip>

## Orders: the screen

In the desktop menu it is called **Órdenes** ("Orders"). On mobile, the same
entry is called **Pedidos** ("Orders"). The direct address is
`optimind.darkfunnels.ai/ordenes` (if you had the old `/orders` link saved, it
takes you there by itself).

The title is **Órdenes** ("Orders") and underneath, in small type, it says what
it is: *Everything the sales funnel achieves — orders, appointments, services
and leads — from the "yes" to the close.* That sentence explains the four tabs.

**Órdenes shows the orders of your whole business**, not those of one agent. It
does not matter which agent you have chosen in the menu: the list and the totals
are the same.

<Note>
**If you were looking for «Conversiones» ("Conversions") in the menu, it is no
longer there as a separate screen:** it was merged in here. Sales are the
**Pedidos** ("Orders") tab. Appointments, services and interested parties are
the other three.
</Note>

### Do I have to sign up for this?

You do not need to install it. New accounts are born with Órdenes switched on,
and businesses that already existed with their sales agent active had it
switched on for them. It can be switched off and back on from
**Administración** → **Apps** ("Administration → Apps"), a screen that only the
person who administers the business can see ([The
apps](/en/getting-started/the-apps) explains it).

**If you do not see the Órdenes entry in your menu, there are two possible
causes:** the app is switched off in your business, or you do not have access to
it. It is not a fault. Each person's access is explained in
[Invite your team](/en/getting-started/invite-your-team).

Contrast that with **Envíos** ("Shipments"), which does not come switched on.

### The four tabs

At the top, in a row: **Pedidos** ("Orders") · **Citas** ("Appointments") ·
**Servicios** ("Services") · **Leads**. They are the four kinds of outcome your
agent can achieve. This guide covers the first one, which is the one that moves
money.

<Note>
**If someone on your team has access to Órdenes but not to Ventas ("Sales")**,
they see a shorter screen: only the orders, without the Citas, Servicios and
Leads tabs and without the **Avisos automáticos** ("Automatic notifications")
button. On their orders they see the customer's initial instead of their photo,
and they do not see the linked chat sale. It is on purpose: that data belongs to
Ventas.
</Note>

### The four totals

The band of large figures: **POR COBRAR** ("AWAITING PAYMENT") · **POR
DESPACHAR** ("TO DISPATCH") · **EN CAMINO** ("ON THEIR WAY") · **ENTREGADOS**
("DELIVERED").

<Warning>
**They are your business's historical total, not the month's or the week's.**
That band has no date selector: the **ENTREGADOS** number is everything you have
delivered since you started. For "how much did I sell in August", the screen is
**Resultados** ("Results").
</Warning>

Each total groups more than one status, and that is why the figures do not add
up at first glance:

| Total | Which statuses it sums |
| --- | --- |
| **POR COBRAR** ("AWAITING PAYMENT") | Por cobrar ("Awaiting payment") + Pago parcial ("Partial payment") |
| **POR DESPACHAR** ("TO DISPATCH") | Pagado ("Paid") + Facturado ("Invoiced") |
| **EN CAMINO** ("ON THEIR WAY") | En camino ("On its way") |
| **ENTREGADOS** ("DELIVERED") | Entregado ("Delivered") + Cerrado ("Closed") |

**Cancelled orders do not go into any of the four.** If you add up the four
totals and it does not match what you see in the table, that is one of the
reasons: cancelled orders appear in the list, but in none of the totals.

<Note>
**The numbers are not buttons.** Pressing a figure in that band does not filter
the table. The buttons below are what you filter with.
</Note>

### The three views

Three ways of looking at the same thing, with three different jobs:

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Tabla (Table)" icon="list">
    The list. **For finding** a specific order and opening it.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Pipeline" icon="table-columns">
    A board of cards by stage. **For moving** orders by dragging.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Vouchers" icon="receipt">
    The payment receipts your customers sent. **For getting paid.**

In **Tabla** ("Table") there are also four quick filters: **Todos** ("All"),
**Por cobrar** ("Awaiting payment" — unpaid or half paid), **Pagados** ("Paid")
and **Cerrados** ("Closed").

<Warning>
**«Cerrados» ("Closed") puts cancelled orders in the same bag as delivered
ones.** If you are looking for a sale that fell through, it is in there. And it
also shows up under **Todos** ("All").
</Warning>

### The table columns

Out of the box, and in this order: **pedido** ("order") · **cliente**
("customer") · **detalle** ("detail") · **total** · **saldo** ("balance") ·
**estado** ("status"), and at the end an **Abrir** ("Open") button.

- **saldo** ("balance") is the column that tells you how much they still owe
  you.
- **detalle** ("detail") shows the first line of the order (with «+N más» —
  "+N more" — if there are others) and the delivery district, if there is one.
- You can widen and reorder the columns by dragging. The dashboard remembers the
  result for each agent separately.
- **Abrir** ("Open") stays fixed on the right: it does not move and it does not
  narrow.

### The order number

Every order has a short number like **#ORD-0012**. The system assigns it, you do
not choose it, and the count is your business's own: **your first order is
#ORD-0001** even if other companies are running into the thousands. It is there
so you can talk about the order with your team and find it in the box at the top
(*«Buscar cliente o #ORD…»* — "Search customer or #ORD…"). The box also finds
orders by the customer's phone and by product.

<Warning>
**The search only looks at the orders already loaded.** The table loads 50 at a
time as you scroll down. If you search for an old order and it does not turn up,
it has not been deleted: it just is not loaded yet. The screen itself says so
when it finds nothing: *«la búsqueda mira los pedidos ya cargados»* ("the search
looks at the orders already loaded").

**Careful: with something typed in the box, scrolling down no longer loads
more.** The trick is to clear the search, scroll down until it loads (or filter
by status first) and search again.
</Warning>

<Tip>
**To point someone on your team to a specific order, give them its number** (for
example #ORD-0012). They find it by typing it in the search box. If it is an old
one, remember that the search only looks at what is already loaded.
</Tip>

## How an order is born

There are two paths: your agent opens it when it sells, or you open it by hand.

### 1. On its own, from a WhatsApp sale

<Steps>
  <Step title="The customer closes in the chat">
    Your agent talks, resolves things and the customer says yes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The agent records the sale">
    In that same turn it records the sale. **Recording the sale IS opening the
    order**: there is no second step and no button to press.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The order appears in Órdenes">
    With its number, its total, its balance and its status. In the record it
    reads **creado por el agente** ("created by the agent").
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
**Without the Órdenes app active, the sale is recorded all the same but no order
is opened.** The sale counts in Resultados. What you do not have is the
collection queue or the dispatch queue.
</Note>

**And if the same customer buys again in the same chat?** As long as the
previous order is still open — **Por cobrar** ("Awaiting payment") or **Pago
parcial** ("Partial payment") —, the agent **rewrites that order** with the new
lines instead of duplicating it. Careful: it rewrites it, it does not add to it.
If the previous order was already **Pagado** ("Paid") or beyond, the second sale
opens a new order.

<Warning>
**"I sold something and no order appeared."** Almost always it is the catalog.
If the agent records a sale and cannot put a price on some line — because the
product is not in the catalog and nobody said the price —, the sale **is**
recorded but **the order is not opened**. The same happens if the sale arrives
with no product line at all. Having the product loaded in your
[catalog](/en/guides/catalog) removes that cause.

A different case, and one that comes before this one: **a sale with no amount is
not even recorded**. The amount is the order's total value, even if the customer
pays on delivery and has not put down a single sol.
</Warning>

#### Where the prices come from

When the line matches a product in your catalog — by its identifier or by its
name —, **the catalog price wins** and any number the agent may have written is
discarded. That is why, with the product loaded, the price is not set by the AI.
That applies to the order's lines. The amount the sale counts with in Resultados
is the total the agent states, if it gives one.

Two exceptions worth knowing about:

- **A product that is not in the catalog** goes into the order at the price the
  agent said in the chat.
- **If the product has quantity-based pricing**, the unit price is recalculated
  according to the volume and the line comes out labelled *(precio por volumen)*
  ("volume price").

#### Where the currency comes from

Nobody declares it: it is inferred, in this order. First, what the agent
understood from the chat by reading the symbol that was written (`S/` or `$`).
If not that, the currency of the catalog price. And if not that either, the
agent's **Moneda de las ventas** ("Sales currency") setting, which is in
**Ventas** → **Configuración** ("Sales → Settings") (soles, unless you change
it).

<Warning>
**This is where periods with "several currencies" creep in.** If both symbols
get written in your chats, you will end up with sales in soles and in dollars in
the same month, and Resultados will no longer be able to add them up. Mind the
order: the **Moneda de las ventas** ("Sales currency") setting is the last
resort, not the first word. If you sell in a single currency, what really
matters is that the catalog is in that currency and that the two symbols do not
get mixed in the chat.
</Warning>

### 2. By hand, with «Pedido manual»

For when you sold over the phone, at the counter or through another channel. The
button is at the top right, in the **Pedidos** ("Orders") tab.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Press «Pedido manual» (Manual order)">
    The **Nuevo pedido manual** ("New manual order") panel opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type in the customer">
    **Nombre** ("Name", required) and **Teléfono** ("Phone", optional). Without
    a name it will not let you save: *«El cliente necesita un nombre»* ("The
    customer needs a name").
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the lines">
    In **Producto** ("Product") start typing and choose from the dropdown: the
    SKU and the price fill themselves in. You can also type a product by hand,
    with its price and its quantity. For more products, **+ Otra línea** ("+
    Another line").
  </Step>
  <Step title="Delivery and payment">
    **Dirección (opcional)** ("Address (optional)"), **Distrito** ("District"),
    the currency, the **Envío** ("Shipping") cost and the payment plan. If you
    choose **Adelanto + saldo** ("Deposit + balance"), type in the deposit and
    the panel shows you the balance right away.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Press «Crear pedido» (Create order)">
    The system sets the total and the order number. The new order's record
    opens, where it will read **creado a mano** ("created by hand").
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
**The product dropdown only brings in what the agent you have selected sells**
in the menu on the left. If you cannot find a product, check the agent first.
</Note>

<Warning>
**An order carries a single currency for everything.** If you mix a product in
soles with another in dollars, the screen blocks it and tells you which line is
the culprit. The platform does not convert currencies.

**With quantity-based pricing, check the total.** Here the panel multiplies the
price by the quantity and warns you: *«Alguno de estos productos tiene precio
por cantidad: verifica el total antes de crear.»* ("Some of these products have
quantity-based pricing: check the total before creating.")
</Warning>

<Warning>
**A manual order has no chat behind it**, and that has three consequences:

- **It does not add up in Resultados.** Resultados counts recorded sales, and
  this order has none linked to it.
- **Your agent does not write anything to the customer**: not when you collect
  it, not when you dispatch it, not when you deliver it.
- **You collect it with «Registrar pago» ("Record payment")** in its record: the
  receipts that come in over WhatsApp do not get hooked to it.

If that person has already talked to your agent and you want the sale to count
in Resultados as well, record it also from their customer record (you will see
how just below).
</Warning>

### The third path, which does NOT open an order

In **Clientes** ("Customers"), each person's record has a **Conversiones**
("Conversions") card. There you can leave a record of a sale, an appointment, a
service or a lead:

- Press **Registrar conversión** ("Record conversion") and the AI reads the
  conversation and leaves the form pre-filled for you (type, amount, products),
  with its explanation in view.
- Or fill it in yourself: type, **Monto** ("Amount"), currency, **SKU
  (opcional)** ("SKU (optional)").
- You confirm with **Registrar** ("Record").

That **notes the sale in their history and counts in Resultados, but does not
open any order**.

| If you want to… | Go to |
| --- | --- |
| Get paid for something and dispatch it | **Órdenes** → **Pedido manual** ("Manual order") |
| Have a sale count in Resultados | **Clientes** → record → **Conversiones** → **Registrar** ("Record") |

### The three payment plans

Both in the manual entry and when your agent records it, there are three
options: **Pago completo** ("Payment in full"), **Contra entrega** ("Cash on
delivery") and **Adelanto + saldo** ("Deposit + balance"). Plans with freely
defined instalments cannot be built from the screen, from the agent, or from
your connected assistant.

## The statuses of an order

Eight labels. These are the ones you will see written on your screen:

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Por cobrar** ("Awaiting payment") | It is open and nothing has come in |
| **Pago parcial** ("Partial payment") | Something came in and a balance is left |
| **Pagado** ("Paid") | There is nothing left to collect |
| **Facturado** ("Invoiced") | Inherited label (see below) |
| **En camino** ("On its way") | It has left for the customer |
| **Entregado** ("Delivered") | It arrived |
| **Cerrado** ("Closed") | Inherited label (see below) |
| **Cancelado** ("Cancelled") | It fell through |

<Note>
**«Facturado» ("Invoiced") and «Cerrado» ("Closed") are inherited labels.** They
exist in the system and the totals above still sum them, but **today no
dashboard action takes an order into those two statuses**. If you see an order
in "Cerrado", it comes from an old migration of your data, not from something
you did.
</Note>

## The order record

It opens with the **Abrir** ("Open") button in the table, with the Pipeline
card, or straight from the chat (further down). At the top it carries the
number, the customer and a line with who created it, the payment plan and when.
Below, four blocks:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Detalle (Detail)" icon="list-check">
    What was sold, the shipping and the total. Also the delivery and the phone,
    if there are any.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Plan de pago (Payment plan)" icon="flag">
    The collection instalments, if there are any (for example, Adelanto and
    Saldo — deposit and balance).
  </Card>
  <Card title="Pagos (Payments)" icon="hand-holding-dollar">
    What has already come in, what is missing and this order's unreconciled
    payment receipts.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Historia (History)" icon="clock-rotate-left">
    Who created the order, the notes and, if it came from a chat, each delivery
    step (Preparando, En camino, Entregada — preparing, on its way, delivered)
    with its notification to the customer.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

The line at the top reads **creado por el agente** ("created by the agent") or
**creado a mano** ("created by hand"). It is the way to audit, without leaving
the screen, what the AI sold and what your team sold.

### Recording a payment

In the **Pagos** ("Payments") block: type the amount, choose the method
(**Efectivo** — "Cash" —, **Yape**, **Plin**, **Transferencia** — "Bank
transfer" — or **Otro** — "Other") and press **Registrar pago** ("Record
payment"). That row only appears while the order is **Por cobrar** ("Awaiting
payment") or on **Pago parcial** ("Partial payment"). As soon as it goes to
**Pagado** ("Paid"), it is no longer there.

### The other actions

At the bottom, depending on where the order is, you will see **Marcar
despachado** ("Mark dispatched"), **Marcar entregado** ("Mark delivered") and
**Cancelar pedido** ("Cancel order"). In the Historia ("History") you can leave
a note (*«Escribe una nota…»* — "Write a note…" — and **Guardar**, "Save"): it
stays in the record and **the customer does not see it**.

When you press **Cancelar pedido** ("Cancel order") it asks you for the reason
(optional). Cancelling does two more things:

- **It voids the linked sale**, so it stops counting in Resultados.
- **It returns to inventory** the stock that may have been deducted when it was
  collected or when its shipment was dispatched.

<Warning>
**The «Cerrar» ("Close") button at the bottom closes the record, not the
order.** There is no action that closes an order. To declare a sale dead, use
**Cancelar pedido** ("Cancel order").

**An order that has already left a physical trace cannot be cancelled**: if it
has already gone past **Pagado** ("Paid") and has a shipment created in
**Envíos** ("Shipments") (even if it does not have a waybill yet, and even if
that shipment was cancelled) or a stock movement, the system rejects it and
tells you why.
</Warning>

### The trap that confuses people most

<Warning>
**Marking an order with an outstanding balance as dispatched or delivered does
NOT change its status.** The dispatch is noted, but the order stays where it
was — **Por cobrar** ("Awaiting payment") or **Pago parcial** ("Partial
payment") —. It is deliberate: that way the money you are owed does not
disappear from your collections view.

If you drag it in the Pipeline, the on-screen notice says so: *«(anotado; sigue
por cobrar)»* ("(recorded; still awaiting payment)"). It will move to
**Pagado** ("Paid") when you record the payment.
</Warning>

### The record also lives inside the chat

It is the same block, in two places: the **Órdenes** ("Orders") drawer and the
**Órdenes** tab in the conversation's right-hand panel. From the chat, with the
customer right there, you can record their payment, approve their payment
receipt and mark the dispatch without switching screens. For day-to-day work it
is usually the most convenient route.

## Getting paid over WhatsApp: the payment receipts

Your customer sends the screenshot of their Yape, their Plin or their bank
transfer. Two things happen:

1. **Your agent replies that it received the receipt** (if it is the one
   handling that chat), with the amount if it can read it clearly. It does not
   tell them the payment is verified or that the money has already arrived.
2. **The image goes into the Vouchers view** of Órdenes, so that someone on your
   team can reconcile it.

### Approving a receipt

Each card in **Vouchers** shows the thumbnail, what the AI read (*«Detectado:
monto · quién pagó · fecha»* — "Detected: amount · who paid · date") and the
order it goes to, with its balance.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Look at the receipt">
    Press the thumbnail: the receipt opens in another browser tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the amount and the method">
    The AI **pre-fills the amount**, but the one that counts is the one you
    leave. Choose the method (**Yape**, **Plin**, **Transferencia** — "Bank
    transfer" — or **Efectivo** — "Cash"). If the order is **Adelanto + saldo**
    ("Deposit + balance"), also choose which instalment it pays (the screen
    suggests one).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Press «Aprobar pago» (Approve payment)">
    The payment is recorded on the order. If it is not a real receipt, press
    **Descartar** ("Discard").
  </Step>
</Steps>

### The inbox tags

Each one is a decision you have to make yourself:

| Tag | What it is telling you |
| --- | --- |
| **difiere del saldo** ("differs from the balance") | The amount is not what is left to collect |
| **no cubre el hito** ("does not cover the milestone") | It is less than what was due at that instalment of the plan |
| **revísalo a ojo** ("check it by eye") | The AI read the image with low confidence |
| **moneda distinta** ("different currency") | The receipt is not in the order's currency |
| **se asocia al aprobar** ("linked on approval") | It is not hooked to an order yet; it gets hooked when you approve it |
| **Sin pedido abierto en este chat — créalo antes de aprobar** ("No open order in this chat — create one before approving") | There is nothing to apply it to: the approve button is disabled |

If the image is illegible, the screen says so plainly: *«El OCR no pudo leerlo —
abre el comprobante y concílialo a ojo»* ("OCR could not read it — open the
receipt and reconcile it by eye").

<Note>
**The inbox goes in batches, it is not the complete list.** At the bottom it
reads *«Se muestran los N comprobantes más antiguos. Concilia estos para ver los
siguientes.»* ("Showing the N oldest receipts. Reconcile these to see the next
ones."). It is a queue: the old ones first.
</Note>

<Note>
**An image that reached the chat more than 48 hours ago no longer goes into
Vouchers.** It happens, for example, right after switching Órdenes on: old
screenshots do not sneak into the inbox. The 48 hours are counted from when the
image came into the chat, not from the payment date: if your customer forwards
you today the screenshot of a payment from three days ago, it is a new message
and it goes in normally. Record the payments that were left out by hand with
**Registrar pago** ("Record payment").
</Note>

<Tip>
**You do not have to go to the inbox.** The **Pagos** ("Payments") block in the
order record lists that order's unreconciled receipts, with their image and
their approval row. It is more direct when you already know which order you are
talking about.
</Tip>

## When the order is paid in full, three things happen on their own

The moment the balance reaches zero, three things happen on their own, and each
one has its condition:

1. **Stock is deducted**, if you have the **Inventario** ("Inventory") app
   active. Only from the lines chosen from the catalog whose product has
   **Controlar stock** ("Track stock") switched on (if the shipment went out
   earlier, the deduction was made when it was dispatched). If you later cancel
   the order, that deduction is returned.
2. **The shipment is born**, if you have the **Envíos** ("Shipments") app active
   with a single courier enabled, the order was opened by your agent in a chat,
   it carries some physical product (or one that is not in the catalog) and it
   has not already had a shipment. With **Contra entrega** ("Cash on delivery")
   it is born earlier, when the order is created, and with **Adelanto + saldo**
   ("Deposit + balance"), when the deposit comes in (see further down). An order
   from **Pedido manual** ("Manual order") does not get its shipment created on
   its own.
3. **The customer receives a message**, only if the order was born from a
   conversation. If they bought an info product, they receive what you put in
   its **Entrega automática** ("Automatic delivery") in the
   [catalog](/en/guides/catalog). If not, your agent tells them their order is
   now being prepared, as long as that notification is still switched on in
   **Avisos automáticos** ("Automatic notifications") (out of the box it is).
   Like every notification, it does not go out if the chat is in manual mode or
   if the contact is unsubscribed. If your WhatsApp is disconnected, it waits:
   it goes out if the line comes back within a few hours and, if not, it is
   lost.

<Tip>
**If you use the Red de referidos ("Referral network")**, there is one more
notification: with **Cuando confirmas su pago** ("When you confirm their
payment") switched on, about 10 minutes after you approve the receipt or record
the payment (during the day), the agent asks the customer to recommend someone
to you. It is set up in [Referral network](/en/guides/referral-network).
</Tip>

## Moving orders: the Pipeline

A board with four columns: **Por cobrar** ("Awaiting payment"), **Por
despachar** ("To dispatch"), **En camino** ("On its way") and **Entregado**
("Delivered"). Each column says how many orders it has and how much they add up
to. Cards with an outstanding balance say so next to the total: *«debe 120,00
PEN»* ("owes 120.00 PEN").

<Warning>
**You can only drag into the last two.** The collection columns will not accept
anything dropped on them, because **money is recorded, not dragged**. To get
paid, open the order and use **Registrar pago** ("Record payment"), or approve
its payment receipt.
</Warning>

Three more things about the Pipeline that come as a surprise:

- **Cancelled orders do not appear** in any column.
- **The board only distributes the orders already loaded in Tabla.** Here
  nothing more loads as you scroll down. The four totals at the top do count all
  your orders. That is why you can see "POR COBRAR 180" at the top and a handful
  of cards in that column: nothing is missing, it just has not been loaded. To
  see them, go to **Tabla** ("Table") with **Todos** ("All"), scroll down until
  what you need is loaded and go back to **Pipeline**.
- **On entering the Pipeline the status filter goes back to «Todos» ("All")**,
  on purpose. And whatever you type in the search box also filters the cards.

## Automatic notifications

The button at the top right, next to **Pedido manual** ("Manual order"). It
opens the panel where you decide **what your agent tells the customer at each
step** of the order. Whoever has access to Ventas ("Sales") can see it.

Mind what you edit in there: **you do not write the literal message, you write
the task** the agent carries out when notifying. It words it in its own way of
speaking and with the context of that conversation. Each notification has its
own switch: switching it off keeps the text for when you want to switch it back
on.

Under **Pedidos — al cambiar de estado** ("Orders — on status change") there are
three notifications, which are born switched on with these out-of-the-box tasks:

| Notification | When it goes out | Out-of-the-box task |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Preparando** ("Preparing") | When it is paid in full | *«Cuéntale al cliente que su pedido ya se está preparando y agradécele la compra.»* ("Tell the customer their order is now being prepared and thank them for the purchase.") |
| **En camino** ("On its way") | When you dispatch it | *«Avísale al cliente que su pedido ya salió. Si hay courier o dato de seguimiento, dáselo…»* ("Let the customer know their order has left. If there is a courier or tracking detail, give it to them…") |
| **Entregada** ("Delivered") | When you deliver it | *«Confirma con el cliente que recibió bien su pedido, pregúntale cómo le fue…»* ("Confirm with the customer that they received their order in good order, ask them how it went…") |

Below, **Citas — al marcar la asistencia** ("Appointments — when attendance is
marked"), with two more: **Asistió** ("Attended") and **No asistió** ("Did not
attend").

Each notification also has an optional line: **Al enviarse, mover al capítulo**
… **y arrancar en el PASO** … ("When sent, move to chapter … and start at STEP
…") of the agent's playbook. It is there so the customer's reply lands in the
chapter you want (for example, an after-sales one). Left empty, nothing moves.

When you are done, **Guardar** ("Save").

<Note>
**"Why did my agent write to the customer on its own?"** Because of this. That
is the answer, and it is switched off from here.
</Note>

### Cancelling a notification: «Deshacer» and the History

When you move an order to **En camino** ("On its way") or **Entregado**
("Delivered"), the notification to the customer goes out roughly **a minute
later**. For about **55 seconds** a **Deshacer** ("Undo") button appears.

<Warning>
**«Deshacer» ("Undo") cancels the message to the customer. It does NOT revert
the order's status.** The screen confirms it: *«Aviso cancelado; el estado del
pedido queda»* ("Notification cancelled; the order's status stays"). If you got
the wrong order, you have to correct it by hand in its record.
</Warning>

Did the «Deshacer» minute slip past you? In the order's **Historia** ("History"),
each delivery step shows its notification: **aviso programado** ("notification
scheduled") with the **Cancelar aviso** ("Cancel notification") button while it
has not gone out, and afterwards **el agente avisó** ("the agent notified") or
**aviso cancelado** ("notification cancelled").

For a notification to be scheduled (and therefore for **Deshacer** to appear),
four things are needed:

1. **The order has to come from a conversation.** An order created with
   **Pedido manual** ("Manual order") has no chat behind it: there is no
   notification and no button there.
2. **It has to be a new step for that sale.** Marking again something that has
   already been notified does not notify again. Careful: even if the order stays
   in **Por cobrar** ("Awaiting payment") because a balance is left, that step's
   notification is scheduled.
3. **The notification has to be switched on** in **Avisos automáticos**
   ("Automatic notifications").
4. **Whoever moves it has to have access to Ventas ("Sales").** If not, the
   order is marked all the same but the notification is not scheduled. From the
   order record the screen tells you so: *«El pedido avanzó. El aviso al cliente
   lo agenda Ventas y no tienes ese permiso.»* ("The order moved forward. The
   notification to the customer is scheduled by Ventas and you do not have that
   permission.")

<Warning>
**«Deshacer» appearing does not guarantee that the message goes out.** At the
moment of sending, the notification goes through the same gates as everything
else: if that chat is in manual mode (autopilot off) or if the contact is
unsubscribed, it does not go out, and in the Historia you will see **aviso
cancelado** ("notification cancelled"). If the WhatsApp is disconnected, the
notification waits: it goes out if the line comes back within a few hours and,
if not, it is lost.
</Warning>

to your agent, with what it learned about them.

<Note>
**It needs an agent selected.** Without one, the screen says *«Sin agente
seleccionado»* ("No agent selected"). And the list changes when you change agent
in the menu: you have not lost contacts.
</Note>

### The three views

Below the title there are three tabs: **Cola** ("Queue"), **Embudo**

**The Cola is titled «Por cerrar» ("To close")** and it has a single criterion:

- **Atender** ("Attend to") opens that person's record without leaving the

- **Ignorar** ("Ignore") takes them out of the queue until they write to you

<Tip>
**Dragging a card in the Embudo ("Funnel") view changes that person's
chapter:** your agent will carry on from that chapter on its next turn. It is
the quickest way to rescue someone who got stuck.
</Tip>

### One record per agent

**Each agent (each funnel) has its own record of each customer.** If the same
number writes to two of your agents, it appears in both lists, and each record
keeps its own: captured data, tags, notes, sales and orders. A customer no
longer "jumps" from one agent's list to another's.

**Of the record, what applies to the whole business is the unsubscribe:** if you
unsubscribe a number, it stops receiving messages on all your agents. The rest
of the record belongs to each agent, including the data deletion.

<Note>
That is why an agent you duplicate, or one shared with you from another account,
starts with no customers. [Your funnels](/en/guides/your-funnels) explains it.
</Note>

### The Table columns

Fixed and in this order: **cliente** ("customer") · **estado** ("status") ·
**capítulo** ("chapter") · **piloto** ("autopilot") · **etiquetas** ("tags") ·
**conversiones** ("conversions") · **costo IA** ("AI cost") · **última
actividad** ("last activity"). At the end, the **Ficha** ("Record") and
**Chat** buttons.

After the fixed ones, **one column appears for each piece of data your agent
captures** about that person, and most of them can be edited (the ones the
system writes come out locked). Left out are those that already have a column of
their own (the chapter, the autopilot, the phone and the photo) and those for
internal use. To add a new piece of data, the **+ Variable** button.

<Frame caption="The Tabla view of Clientes: the search, «Filtrar», the segments and the country tabs at the top; each row is a person with their status, their chapter, their autopilot, their tags and what you have spent on AI with them.">
  <img src="/images/apps/08-clientes-tabla.png" alt="Clientes screen in Tabla view with the search box, the Filtrar button, the country filter and one row per person" />

The **estado** ("status") column has three values and calculates itself, it is
not editable:

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Baja** ("Unsubscribed") | The contact unsubscribed |
| **Espera respuesta** ("Awaiting reply") | They wrote to you and are still without a reply |
| **Al día** ("Up to date") | Nobody is waiting for a reply |

<Note>
**«Al día» ("Up to date") does not mean "they bought from me".** It means that
conversation has nothing pending on your side.
</Note>

On the right of the bar there are two switches (**Orden en tiempo real** —
"Real-time order" — and **Compacta** — "Compact", which tightens the rows) and
the **Etiquetas** ("Tags") and **+ Variable** buttons.

### Filtering the list

In the **Embudo** ("Funnel") and **Tabla** ("Table") views (the Cola cannot be
filtered):

- **The search box** searches by name or phone.
- **Filtrar** ("Filter") opens three filters: **Capítulo** ("Chapter" — not in
  Embudo, where the chapter is already the column), **Piloto automático**
  ("Autopilot") and **Etiqueta** ("Tag").
- **Todos · Con compra** ("All · With a purchase"): two segments (below we
  explain what "purchase" means).
- **The country.** If that agent's customers are from two or more countries,
  tabs with their flag appear: **Todos** ("All") and the countries with the most
  customers. If there are many, the rest go in the **Más países** ("More
  countries") dropdown.

<Note>
**Customers with no known country only show up under «Todos» ("All").** It
happens, for example, when WhatsApp does not let the customer's number be seen.
And when you change agent, the country goes back to **Todos**.
</Note>

If there are no results, the screen offers you **Limpiar filtros** ("Clear
filters").

### Three columns that do not measure what their name suggests

<Warning>
**«conversiones» ("conversions") are not sales.** It counts all the recorded,
non-voided outcomes for that person **with this agent**: sales, but also
appointments, services and leads. If your funnel books appointments, that number
is inflated relative to what you sold.

**«Con compra» ("With a purchase") is not "they paid me" either.** That segment
marks whoever has at least one recorded, non-voided outcome with this agent. It
does not look at whether they paid or whether the order has been collected. For
the money, the place is the **Por cobrar** ("Awaiting payment") filter in
Órdenes.

**«costo IA» ("AI cost") falls short.** It is what the AI cost to serve that
person with this agent, in dollars, but it only counts what happens inside a
conversation turn. Transcribing the audio the customer sent or reading an image
fall outside it. If you add up the column across all your customers and compare
it with your usage, it is not going to match.
</Warning>

### Sorting the table: careful

<Warning>
**Sorting by a column only sorts the rows already loaded** (200 per page), not
your whole base. If you sort by "conversiones" looking for your best customer,
they may not even be on screen.

When more rows are left, the screen itself says so at the bottom — *«el orden se
aplica solo sobre los cargados»* ("sorting applies only to the ones loaded") —
and offers you **Cargar 200 más** ("Load 200 more") and **Quitar orden**
("Clear sorting").

**Filter first, sort afterwards.**
</Warning>

**«Orden en tiempo real» ("Real-time order")** does the opposite: with the
switch on, the list refreshes every 10 seconds and moves whoever has just
written to you up to first place (at the bottom it reads *«en vivo»* — "live").
It comes switched off, and the dashboard remembers how you left it.

The two modes do not live together: if you sort by a column, real time switches
off, and switching it on clears the sorting. While either of the two is active,
the table stops loading by itself as you scroll down, so the rows you are
looking at do not jump around.

### The customer record

From the **Ficha** ("Record") button (or **Atender**, "Attend to", in the Cola)
you can change their chapter, switch their autopilot on or off, put tags on
them, record or void a sale, see their referrals and reminders, unsubscribe them
and delete their data.

<Frame caption="A customer's record: the chapter and the autopilot, the tags, the internal notes and, below, what the agent knows about them (Datos capturados).">
  <img src="/images/apps/11-ficha-cliente.png" alt="Customer record open in Clientes with the chapter, the Piloto automático switch, Etiquetas, Notas internas and the start of Datos capturados" />
</Frame>

<Tip>
**«Notas internas» ("Internal notes") is the dashboard's most useful and least
known feature.** It is where you tell your agent *"treat this one as a
wholesaler"* without touching the playbook: it reads them and applies them in
its replies, and **the customer does not see them**.

Write them short. Your agent receives **the 5 most recent ones, trimmed to 300
characters each**: piling up dozens of long notes does not help, it gets in the
way.
</Tip>

<Warning>
**Voiding a sale from the record also cancels the linked order.** There are
these cases:

- **The order is Por cobrar ("Awaiting payment"), Pago parcial ("Partial
  payment") or Pagado ("Paid"):** it is cancelled along with the sale.
- **The order was already on its way to delivery and left a trace** (a shipment
  created or stock moved): the sale is voided, the order is not, and you will see
  *«el pedido enlazado ya no admite cancelación — revísalo en /ordenes»* ("the
  linked order no longer accepts cancellation — check it in /ordenes"). You
  review that order yourself.
- **You do not have the Órdenes permission:** nothing is voided. The notice says
  *«Esta venta tiene un pedido y anularla lo cancela: para eso necesitas permiso
  de Órdenes…»* ("This sale has an order and voiding it cancels it: for that you
  need the Órdenes permission…"). Ask someone who has it to cancel the order in
  Órdenes: the sale is voided on its own.
- **The Órdenes app is switched off in your business:** it is not voided either,
  and the notice says *«…pero la app Órdenes no está activa en esta empresa»*
  ("…but the Órdenes app is not active in this business"). Whoever administers
  the business has to switch it on in **Administración** → **Apps**
  ("Administration → Apps"); afterwards, whoever has the Órdenes permission
  cancels the order and the sale is voided on its own.

A sale with no order is voided without further ado.
</Warning>

<Warning>
**Unsubscribing and deleting data do not reach the same things.**

- **Unsubscribing the contact** applies to your whole business: they stop
  receiving messages from the agent and from the operator on all your agents. It
  is reverted from the same record with **Reactivar contacto** ("Reactivate
  contact"), but only whoever administers your business can do that.
- **Borrar datos de usuario** ("Delete user data") deletes that person **only on
  this agent** and cannot be undone. Their records on your other agents are not
  touched, and if they were unsubscribed, the unsubscribe stays.

The details are in [Conversations](/en/guides/conversations).
</Warning>

### Deleting data for several at once

In the **Tabla** ("Table") you can tick several rows and press **Borrar datos**
("Delete data"). Careful, it is not the same as the deletion in the record: **it
deletes the captured data and the chapter** of those people, but not their
conversation, their messages or their sales. If they write again, they appear
again in chapter 1 and with no data. With 10 or more selected, it asks you to
type **BORRAR** ("DELETE") to confirm.

## Results: what each number really measures

`optimind.darkfunnels.ai/resultados`. It is the "how is my business doing"
screen.

At the top there are three controls: the **period**, the **scope** and, if it
applies, the **country**. The scope is two tabs: the one carrying the name of
your selected agent and **Toda la organización** ("The whole organization").
The verdict writes out what you are looking at, for example *«Veredicto · Moda
Sol · 120 conversaciones»* ("Verdict · Moda Sol · 120 conversations").

<Warning>
**Do not try to reconcile the two views.** The sum of your agents individually
does NOT give the "Toda la organización" ("The whole organization") number, and
that is on purpose: a conversation that was not assigned to any agent does not
belong to any funnel, but it does count in the organization's total. And
percentages and latency do not add up.
</Warning>

<Frame caption="Resultados with Semana 42 (week 42) and the «Toda la organización» (Whole organization) scope: the 0-to-100 verdict (here 77, «Bueno», Good) and the «Resultado de venta» (Sales outcome) cards. The «Operativo» (Operational) and «Calidad» (Quality) groups show no cards here.">
  <img src="/images/apps/06-resultados-con-datos.png" alt="Dashboard Resultados screen with the week selector, the Toda la organización scope, the verdict 77 «Bueno» and the Tasa de conversión, Conversiones and Ingreso atribuido cards" />
</Frame>

### The date selector

It offers: Hoy ("Today") · Últimos 7 días ("Last 7 days") · Últimos 30 días
("Last 30 days") · Últimos 90 días ("Last 90 days") · Esta semana ("This week")
· Este mes ("This month") · Mes anterior ("Last month") · Este año ("This year")
· Año anterior ("Last year") · Rango personalizado ("Custom range"). The first
time it opens on **Esta semana** ("This week"). After that it remembers, in that
browser, the last period you chose. The most that can be asked for is a little
under 400 days.

<Note>
**The "change against the previous period" is calculated by shape, not by
days.** The previous period for March is February — 28 days —, not "March minus
31 days". That is why the change percentage does not add up if you try to
reproduce it with a subtraction by hand.
</Note>

### Filtering by country

If the customers in the scope are from two or more countries, a **Todos los
países** ("All countries") button appears next to the period. Choose one and
**all the figures and their charts are trimmed to that country's customers**:
conversations, sales, quality and what the agent did in those conversations
(handovers, tools and latency). The verdict says so, for example *«Veredicto ·
Moda Sol · Bolivia · 40 conversaciones»* ("Verdict · Moda Sol · Bolivia · 40
conversations") (with its flag).

- The country is the customer's (that of their number), not your business's.
- The list carries no counts, on purpose: here the figures belong to the period.
- If you switch to a scope with no customers from that country, the filter is
  removed on its own.

### The big number

A verdict from 0 to 100 with three bands: **Bueno** ("Good", 75 or more),
**Regular** ("Fair", 50 to 74) and **Malo** ("Poor", under 50). It is not a mark
the AI gives: it is a weighted average of the seven indicators below, with these
weights:

| Indicator | Weight |
| --- | --- |
| Tasa de conversión ("Conversion rate") | 30 % |
| Latencia de respuesta ("Response latency") | 13 % |
| Conversiones ("Conversions") | 12 % |
| Derivación a humano ("Handover to a human") | 12 % |
| Calidad de venta ("Sales quality") | 12 % |
| Éxito de herramientas ("Tool success") | 11 % |
| Ingreso atribuido ("Attributed revenue") | 10 % |

Indicators that could not be calculated are discarded and their weight is
shared out among the rest. **Ingreso atribuido** ("Attributed revenue") is also
discarded when there were sales in more than one currency during the period,
because that sum would not mean anything.

<Note>
**When it does not grade you.** If in the period there was not a single new
conversation or a single conversion, instead of the verdict you see a notice
that tells you why. For example, *«Sin actividad en este período»* ("No activity
in this period") (with a button to see the last 90 days, if you chose a shorter
period) or *«Conecta WhatsApp para empezar a medir»* ("Connect WhatsApp to start
measuring"). It is deliberate: we do not want to grade an agent badly when it
simply had nobody to talk to.
</Note>

### The seven indicators, without frills

They are grouped in three blocks: **Resultado de venta** ("Sales outcome" —
Conversion rate, Conversions, Attributed revenue), **Operativo** ("Operational"
— Handover to a human, Tool success, Response latency) and **Calidad**
("Quality" — Sales quality).

This table is the most important thing on this page. There are indicators whose
name suggests one thing and which measure another:

| Indicator | What it really measures | When NOT to trust it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Tasa de conversión** ("Conversion rate") | The outcomes recorded in the period divided by the **new** conversations of the period | **It can go over 100 %** and that is not an error: a sale made today can close on a chat that started last week. It is shown raw on purpose |
| **Conversiones** ("Conversions") | **All** the outcomes of the period: sales, appointments, services and leads. Voided ones and your test chat are left out | If your funnel books appointments or captures leads, **these are not sales**. The number is bigger than what you sold |
| **Ingreso atribuido** ("Attributed revenue") | The sum of the amounts of those outcomes, **in the currency they were recorded in**, not in dollars | An appointment or a service with an amount also swells the number. And if the period crossed two currencies, the number comes out with no symbol, with no comparison and with the note *«varias monedas — sin convertir»* ("several currencies — not converted") |
| **Derivación a humano** ("Handover to a human") | Of the conversations your agent genuinely worked, in how many it tried to pass you the chat. Less is better, and it does not go above 100 % | It is **a ceiling, not an exact figure**: attempts the system rejected or that changed nothing count just the same. Real handovers may be fewer |
| **Éxito de herramientas** ("Tool success") | Of the actions your agent tried to carry out (search the catalog, book, record a sale), how many went well | The ones the system stopped on policy grounds count as failures, on purpose. If it drops, your playbook is asking it for things it cannot do |
| **Latencia de respuesta** ("Response latency") | The **median** time from the customer's last message to the first reply, including the agent's deliberate wait | It only measures replies to a customer. The reminders and re-engagement the agent sends on its own are left out, so a campaign does not wreck the number |
| **Calidad de venta** ("Sales quality") | A 0-to-100 mark that an AI judge gives on the conversation: whether it qualifies the customer before offering, whether it persuades, whether it stays in character and whether it closes with a next step | The platform gives it on its own; it is not switched on from the dashboard. It only marks conversations of 6 messages or more, that have been still for 2 hours and are from the last 72 hours, and it does not repeat the same one within 24 hours. If your account runs out of balance, it does not mark. **In a slow period it can come out empty** |

### How to read a card with no data

<Note>
**A dash means "there is no data". A 0 means "there is data and it is zero".**
No fake 0 is painted in to fill the gap. When the data is missing, the card's
value is a dash **—** and where the comparison with the previous period would go
it says *«sin datos»* ("no data").

**Ingreso atribuido** ("Attributed revenue") with several currencies is the case
apart: the card stays on — there is data — and what it says is *«sin comparar»*
("no comparison").
</Note>

<Check>
**Your test chat does not count as a conversation or as a sale.** Neither the
conversations nor the conversions of the simulation go into Resultados. Careful:
what your agent does in those tests can weigh on **Derivación a humano**
("Handover to a human"), **Éxito de herramientas** ("Tool success") and
**Latencia de respuesta** ("Response latency"). Your balance is another matter:
your agent answers the test turns, and that
[consumes credits](/en/getting-started/credits-and-top-ups) like any other turn.
</Check>

<Note>
**A manual order does not show up here.** Resultados counts recorded sales, not
orders. If you want it to count, record it as well from the customer record.
</Note>

## What reaches you without opening the dashboard

In **Ventas** → **Configuración** ("Sales → Settings"), each agent has two
settings that send you figures over WhatsApp:

- **Informar de las ventas** ("Report sales"). Every sale the agent records
  arrives instantly: the product, the total and the details it captured from
  the customer. You choose which numbers it goes to, from your frequent
  numbers.
- **Reportes** ("Reports") → **Enviar el resumen cada mañana** ("Send the
  summary every morning"). At 8:00 in your time zone, what happened the
  previous day arrives: the sales closed with what each customer ordered, the
  appointments booked, how many people were talked to and an idea for selling
  more.

## Shipments (only if your business has it switched on)

It lives at `optimind.darkfunnels.ai/envios`, menu **Envíos** ("Shipments").
Unlike Órdenes, **Envíos does not come switched on**. If you do not see the
entry in your menu, it is not active in your account (or you do not have access
to it); it is not an error.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The three views and the statuses">
    The screen has three views: **Envíos** ("Shipments" — the parcel queue),
    **Guías del courier** ("Courier waybills" — waybills that were issued by
    hand in the carrier's own dashboard and have to be matched up) and
    **Couriers** (registering carriers and rates).

    The columns are **envío** ("shipment") · **courier** · **destino**
    ("destination") · **receptor** ("recipient") · **estado** ("status"), plus
    the **Abrir** ("Open") button. The seven possible statuses are **Por
    despachar** ("To dispatch"), **Pidiendo guía** ("Requesting waybill"),
    **Despachado** ("Dispatched"), **En camino** ("On its way"), **Entregado**
    ("Delivered"), **Fallido** ("Failed") and **Cancelado** ("Cancelled").

    **«Pidiendo guía» ("Requesting waybill") is confusing:** it means the system
    is waiting for the carrier, not for you.

    <Note>
    **The SHIPMENT statuses are not the same as the ORDER ones**, even though
    they share some names. They are two different cycles. That said: when you
    dispatch or deliver a shipment, its order moves forward on its own (with the
    same outstanding-balance rule) and, if it came from a chat, the notification
    to the customer from **Avisos automáticos** ("Automatic notifications") is
    scheduled.
    </Note>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The filters and the counters (and where they do not add up)">
    Filters: **Todos** ("All"), **Por despachar** ("To dispatch"), **Pidiendo
    guía** ("Requesting waybill"), **En camino** ("On its way"), **Entregados**
    ("Delivered") and **Incidencias** ("Issues"). At the top there are five
    counters with those same names, and — as in Órdenes — they are historical
    totals with no date selector.

    Two warnings:

    - **«En camino» ("On its way") groups two statuses**: the dispatched ones
      and the ones already travelling.
    - **«Incidencias» ("Issues") does not count the same thing it lists**: the
      counter counts only the failed ones; the filter also lists the cancelled
      ones. A cancelled shipment shows up in the list and not in the number.

    And the search box, just as in Órdenes, **only looks at the shipments
    already loaded**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="When a shipment is born">
    The shipment is born on its own, without anyone asking for it, at three
    moments: when the order is **contra entrega** ("cash on delivery" — at
    creation), when the agreed **deposit** came in, or when the order was **paid
    in full**. The rule is that the parcel goes out when there is nothing left
    to collect before dispatching it.

    For that, three more things are needed: that the order was opened by your
    agent in a chat (a **Pedido manual** — "Manual order" — does not get its
    shipment created), that it carries some physical product or one that is not
    in the catalog (an order made only of services or info products does not
    send a parcel) and that it has not already had a shipment, even if you
    cancelled it.

    You can also create one by hand with **Envío manual** ("Manual shipment"),
    on the Envíos screen. It asks you for the order's internal identifier, which
    is not its #ORD number and which the dashboard does not show in view today.
    Normally the shipment is born on its own, so this path is the exception.

    <Warning>
    **Adding a second courier SWITCHES OFF the automatic creation of
    shipments.** With two or more carriers enabled the system refuses to choose
    for you, and shipments stop being born on their own. With a single courier
    enabled (apart from your own delivery), the shipment is born with that one.
    With none declared, nothing is born either. If from one day to the next "the
    shipments stopped appearing", look at this first.
    </Warning>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Couriers and waybills: what is automatic and what is not">
    **The carrier with an integration is Shalom**: it is the one with which the
    waybill can be issued from the platform. Any other courier can be registered
    with its name and its rate, but **the waybill is noted down by hand**. The
    **Entrega propia** ("Own delivery") option is for when you take it yourself
    (motorbike, counter).

    Requesting the waybill from the platform **is born switched off** and you
    switch it on yourself, courier by courier, because each waybill is real and
    gets charged to your account with the carrier.

    <Warning>
    **Even switched on, the waybill is not requested on its own.** The switch
    gives permission; it does not trigger anything. A person requests it with
    the **Pedir la guía al courier** ("Request the waybill from the courier")
    button inside the shipment, and the screen warns you first: *«Se crea una
    guía real y se cobra en tu cuenta del courier»* ("A real waybill is created
    and charged to your account with the courier").
    </Warning>

    Before letting you request it, the record shows you a **Para pedir la guía**
    ("To request the waybill") block with a **Listo** ("Ready") or **Faltan
    datos** ("Missing details") tag and the list of what is missing. The origin
    branch is chosen once only, in the **Couriers** tab, and applies to all your
    shipments.

    If the request is left half done, a block appears warning that the waybill
    needs you to look at it. That does not resolve itself and it has to be
    looked at, because if it is requested again you could end up with two
    waybills charged.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The shipment record">
    It shows **Destino** ("Destination"), **Destinatario** ("Recipient"),
    **Envío** ("Shipment" — order, courier, cost, waybill number) and a **Línea
    de tiempo** ("Timeline") with every movement of the parcel. Depending on
    where it is, the blocks for requesting the waybill, dispatching or noting
    down the waybill also appear.

    **Why it asks you for so many details:** when the recipient is a person, the
    courier requires an ID document and the two surnames separately —
    **Apellido paterno** ("Paternal surname") and **Apellido materno**
    ("Maternal surname") —, and without that it does not deliver. If the
    recipient is a company, **Razón social** ("Company name") is asked for
    instead.

    **The recipient's details can be edited while the shipment is Por
    despachar ("To dispatch").** As soon as you request the waybill or dispatch,
    they become read-only, because they are the ones that travel printed on the
    waybill.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Two things about money and tracking">
    <Warning>
    **Shalom's freight is paid by the recipient.** If you do the sums on the
    shipment's **Costo** ("Cost") field as if you were paying it, you get it the
    wrong way round.
    </Warning>

    **You can dispatch without having the waybill number.** The screen tells you
    so: *«La guía es opcional: si el courier todavía no te la dio, despacha
    ahora y anótala cuando llegue»* ("The waybill is optional: if the courier
    has not given it to you yet, dispatch now and note it down when it
    arrives"). Noting it down afterwards does not change the shipment's status.

    **The «Rastrear» ("Track") link only appears if someone pasted in a tracking
    link by hand** (it has to start with `https`). A waybill issued from the
    platform stores the waybill **number**, not the link: that is why you can
    have a number and not have a tracking button.

    And the **Guías del courier** ("Courier waybills") view is the safety net
    for when someone issued the waybill outside: a mirror of your Shalom account
    arrives, each row with a proposal of which shipment it belongs to, **which
    you have to confirm by hand**. Linking it wrongly dispatches someone else's
    order with that waybill.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## If something does not work

| What you see | Almost always it is | How it gets fixed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **I cannot find «Órdenes» ("Orders") in the menu** | The app is switched off in your business, or you do not have access to it | Whoever administers the business switches it on in **Administración** → **Apps** ("Administration → Apps"), or gives you access |
| **I do not see Citas, Servicios, Leads or «Avisos automáticos»** | You have access to Órdenes but not to Ventas ("Sales") | Ask whoever administers the business for access to Ventas |
| **I sold something and the order does not appear** | Some line was left without a price: the product is not in the catalog | Load the product in the [catalog](/en/guides/catalog) for the next sales. For this one, create the order with **Pedido manual** ("Manual order") and collect it with **Registrar pago** ("Record payment") (the sale already counts in Resultados) |
| **I look for an old order and it does not come up** | The search only looks at what is already loaded, and with text typed in it does not load more | Clear the search, scroll down until it loads (or filter by status) and search again |
| **I marked it dispatched and the status did not change** | There is still a balance to collect | It is on purpose. Record the payment and it will go to **Pagado** ("Paid") |
| **I pressed «Deshacer» ("Undo") and the order is still moved** | «Deshacer» cancels the message to the customer, not the status change | Correct it by hand in the order record |
| **«Deshacer» appeared but the customer received nothing** | The chat was in manual mode, the contact was unsubscribed or your WhatsApp was disconnected | Look at the order's **Historia** ("History"): with manual mode or an unsubscribe it will say **aviso cancelado** ("notification cancelled"). With WhatsApp disconnected, the notification waits for the line to come back and, if it takes hours, it is lost |
| **My agent wrote to the customer on its own** | A notification from **Avisos automáticos** ("Automatic notifications") | Switch it off from that panel; it keeps the text |
| **I add up the four totals and it does not match the table** | Cancelled orders do not go into any total | Look at them in the **Cerrados** ("Closed") filter |
| **The Pipeline has fewer cards than the total at the top** | The board only distributes the orders already loaded | Go to **Tabla** ("Table") with **Todos** ("All"), scroll down until what you need is loaded and go back to **Pipeline** |
| **I cannot drag into «Por cobrar» ("Awaiting payment")** | Money is recorded, not dragged | Open the order and use **Registrar pago** ("Record payment") |
| **A manual order does not add up in Resultados** | Resultados counts recorded sales, and the manual order has none | Record the sale as well from the customer record |
| **A customer does not appear in Clientes** | You have another agent selected, or a filter on (country, «Con compra», chapter, tag) | Change agent in the menu, or press **Limpiar filtros** ("Clear filters") |
| **The same customer shows up under two agents** | They talked to both: each agent has its own record | It is expected |
| **It will not let me void a sale** | It has an order and you do not have the Órdenes permission, or the Órdenes app is switched off | Without the permission: have someone with Órdenes cancel the order (the sale is voided on its own). With Órdenes switched off: whoever administers switches it on in **Administración** → **Apps** and then the order is cancelled |
| **«Calidad de venta» ("Sales quality") comes out empty** | In the period there were no already-marked conversations of 6 messages or more | Widen the period. The judge only marks a conversation once it has been still for 2 hours |
| **An order "will not let itself be cancelled"** | It was already on its way to delivery and left a trace: a shipment created or stock moved | Review it in **Órdenes** ("Orders") |
| **Shipments stopped being born on their own** | You have two or more couriers enabled, or the order is manual | With several couriers the system does not choose for you: leave just one enabled or create the shipment by hand. A **Pedido manual** ("Manual order") does not get its shipment created |
| **The receipt my customer sent is not in Vouchers** | The inbox goes in batches, it was already reconciled or discarded, or the AI did not take it for a receipt | Reconcile the first ones to see the next, look at the order's **Pagos** ("Payments") block, or record it with **Registrar pago** ("Record payment") |

## What cannot be done today

Said once and plainly, so you do not go looking for it:

- **There is no export to Excel or to CSV** in Órdenes, Clientes, Envíos or
  Resultados. The screens that do download a CSV are the
  [Catalog](/en/guides/catalog) and the
  [Referral network](/en/guides/referral-network). If what you need is to take
  out an agent's contacts, with their data, their sales and their notes, there
  is an Excel copy: it is downloaded from **Eliminar agente…** ("Delete
  agent…") → **Descargar copia ahora** ("Download copy now"), without deleting
  anything ([Your funnels](/en/guides/your-funnels) explains it).
- **Órdenes, Clientes and Envíos do not filter by dates.** Órdenes filters by
  status and by text; Clientes by chapter, autopilot, tag, text, «Con compra»
  ("With a purchase") and country; Envíos by status and by text. For "how much
  did I sell in August", Resultados (or the Inicio charts).
- **Closing an order does not exist.** To declare a sale dead, **Cancelar
  pedido** ("Cancel order").
- **There are no payment plans with freely defined instalments**: only the three
  plans above.

## The same thing, from your assistant

<Note>
This section is **optional and only applies if you connected Claude, ChatGPT or
Codex** to your business. If you work in the dashboard, you have already
finished.
</Note>

<Warning>
**Your assistant looks at orders; it does not operate them.** It cannot create
an order, record a payment, approve a payment receipt, dispatch, request a
waybill or register a courier. Of shipments it has nothing. Everything
operational is done from the screen, or done by your agent when it sells.
</Warning>

What it can do: **read**. And for that it is very convenient, because it
cross-references things the screen does not cross-reference.

Orders and metrics are groups that have to be enabled when connecting. The
convenient thing is to connect with the URL that already brings everything — it
is ready to copy in [The connection URL](/en/reference/connection-url) — and not
touch it again. If you have just enabled a group, open a **new** chat: the tool
catalog is fixed per conversation.

And the same apps apply as in the dashboard: to read orders you need the
**Órdenes** ("Orders") app active, and for stock, the **Inventario**
("Inventory") app, as well as you having access to them. If not, your assistant
gets a notice saying so.

Phrases that work:

- *"Show me the orders awaiting payment and tell me how much money they add up
  to in outstanding balance."*
- *"Bring the last 50 orders and group them by status, with the total for each
  group."*
- *"Of the orders delivered this week, which came out of a chat and which were
  opened by a person by hand?"*
- *"Open the order for me and tell me what is missing for it to count as
  collected."*
- *"Compare the catalog stock against the inventory stock and tell me which
  variants do not match."*
- *"How much did the AI cost me over the last 30 days and which agent took the
  biggest share?"*
- *"How is my balance doing and for how many days will it last at this week's
  rate?"*

Details that avoid misunderstandings when you ask it for numbers:

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Orders arrive in pages and with no date filter">
    The list comes from the most recent to the oldest, 25 at a time (50 at
    most). By date there is only an **upper bound**: for a window with a
    beginning and an end — "this week", "July" — your assistant asks for pages
    going backwards and trims off what is left over itself. It can filter by
    status and by chat (the orders of one conversation), but not by amount.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Rates arrive from 0 to 1, not as a percentage">
    A conversion rate of `0.057` is **5.7 %**, not 0.057 %. If your assistant
    reads it to you just as it is, that is what it is.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="«No data» is not zero">
    The indicators that could not be measured arrive set aside in a list of
    their own, precisely so that your assistant **does not read them as zeros**.
    Careful: **Éxito de herramientas** ("Tool success"), **Latencia de
    respuesta** ("Response latency") and **Calidad de venta** ("Sales quality")
    always reach your assistant in that list, because they are not measured
    through this route. To see them, look at **Resultados** in the dashboard.
    Apart from those three, in a just-switched-on agent or a dead week all the
    others can arrive there too: that means a period with no activity, not a
    breakdown.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Revenue does not always come in dollars">
    The field name is historical. The amount goes in the currency of your sales
    and travels accompanied by a code that states it. If the period crossed
    currencies, it arrives marked as mixed: that sum cannot be read as a single
    number. And if no outcome in the period carries a currency (for example,
    because there were no sales and no other outcome with an amount), no
    currency arrives: it comes blank on purpose, because there is nothing to
    infer it from.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The customer's phone number arrives masked">
    You will see something like `51•••••4321`. It is on purpose. To see it in
    full, either you look at it in the dashboard, or the account owner
    reconnects the assistant asking for it explicitly. The why of it is in
    [Security](/en/security).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
