The [playbook](/en/guides/sales-playbook) says **what** your agent does. The
**Configuración** (*Settings*) screen says **with what details and whom it
notifies**: which phones get each sale and the morning summary, your Yape and
your bank account, which numbers you can test it from, which AI model it uses
and at what hours it does not bother your customers. This guide walks through
it from top to bottom, in the same order you see it.

<Note>
**Where it is.** In the left-hand menu, inside **Ventas** (*Sales*), choose
your funnel in the selector (its name, with its agent's photo or an initial).
Then, under the **AJUSTES** (*Settings*) divider, click **Configuración**. The
screen is titled **«Configuración»** and underneath it says *«Configura cómo
trabaja»* (*Set up how it works*) followed by your funnel's name. (Direct:
`optimind.darkfunnels.ai/ajustes`.) In some notices in the playbook this screen
appears as **Ajustes**: it is the same one.

The dashboard is in Spanish, so buttons and labels are written here as you see
them on screen, with their English meaning in parentheses.
</Note>

<Warning>
**Each funnel has its own Configuración.** What you change applies to the
funnel chosen in the selector, not to the others. If you have two funnels,
check both. There are two exceptions, and you will see them in their section:
**Números de prueba** (*Test numbers*) applies to all your business's lines,
and the **Zona horaria del negocio** (*Business time zone*) belongs to the
whole company.
</Warning>

<Tip>
**Nothing is saved until you click «Guardar cambios»** (*Save changes*), in the
bar that stays fixed at the bottom of the screen. You can touch several
sections and save everything at once.
</Tip>

<Frame caption="The top of Configuración: the funnel's name and the first sections; at the bottom, the fixed bar with «Guardar cambios» (Save changes).">
  <img src="/images/configuracion/01-ajustes.png" alt="Funnel Configuración (Settings) screen: the Identidad (Identity) section with the Nombre del embudo (Funnel name) field; below, Objetivo del embudo (Funnel objective) with Venta (Sale) ticked, Números frecuentes (Frequent numbers) with Sin contactos guardados (No contacts saved) and Datos del negocio (Business details) with Sin datos guardados (No details saved); at the bottom, the Sin cambios pendientes (No pending changes) bar with the Guardar cambios (Save changes) button" />
</Frame>

The funnel's WhatsApp connection is not here: it is in **Personalizar**
(*Customize*), the entry just above it in the menu.

## The sections at a glance

| Section | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| **Identidad** (*Identity*) | The funnel's name inside your dashboard. |
| **Números frecuentes** (*Frequent numbers*) | Your contact book: the name and WhatsApp of the people you notify. |
| **Datos del negocio** (*Business details*) | Your Yape, your account, your social media… written once for the whole playbook. |
| **Envío de imágenes y archivos** (*Sending images and files*) | Who gets forwarded what the customer sends you: photos, documents, videos. |
| **Moneda de las ventas** (*Sales currency*) | The currency of a sale when the chat does not make it clear. |
| **Informar de las ventas** (*Report sales*) | Who gets the 💰 notice for each sale. |
| **Números de prueba** (*Test numbers*) | The phones you test your agent from. |
| **Reportes** (*Reports*) | The summary every morning on WhatsApp. |
| **Alertas de error** (*Error alerts*) | Who is notified if the agent fails or runs out of balance. |
| **Herramientas** (*Tools*) | The «Consultar stock» (*Check stock*) switch. It only appears with the Inventario (*Inventory*) app active. |
| **Cerebro** (*Brain*) | The AI model and how much of the conversation it remembers. |
| **Escritura** (*Writing*) | Typing slips so it sounds more human. |
| **Comportamiento** (*Behavior*) | Time zones, do-not-disturb hours, voice-note language, deletion phrase and location. |

Some accounts also see a section that is still in testing. If you do not see it
in yours, you are not missing anything.

## Identity

A single field: **Nombre del embudo** (*Funnel name*). It is the name you see
this funnel by in your dashboard —the menu, **Inicio** (*Home*)— and the one
that appears in the summary and in the notices you get on WhatsApp. If you have
several funnels, give them names that tell them apart («Moda Sol · Store», «Moda
Sol · Wholesale»).

It cannot be left empty: the screen warns *«El nombre no puede estar vacío»*
(*The name cannot be empty*) and does not let you save.

<Warning>
**Changing the name requires permission to manage the company's apps.** If you
do not have it, when you save you get *«Cambiar el nombre del embudo requiere
administrar las apps de la empresa»* (*Changing the funnel's name requires
managing the company's apps*) and that save is not applied, nor is anything
else you had changed in the funnel. Put the name back the way it was and save
again, or ask whoever manages your account. Permissions are explained in
[Invite your team](/en/getting-started/invite-your-team).
</Warning>

## Frequent numbers: your contact book

It is the list of people your agent can notify: you, your salesperson, the
warehouse. **Each contact is a name and a WhatsApp number.** You save the
number once and afterwards you pick it by its name: in the lists on this same
screen and in the playbook.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click «Agregar contacto» (Add contact)">
    A new row appears with three fields: name, country and number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type the name">
    The way you recognize them: «Andrea», «Warehouse». Up to 40 characters.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the country and type the number without the code">
    The country comes set to **Perú (+51)**. The number goes without the
    country code, which the selector already adds: `987654321`. The list has 16
    countries: a good part of Latin America, Spain and the United States /
    Canada.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Guardar cambios** in the bar at the bottom.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If the row is missing something, the field tells you: *«Ponle un nombre»*
(*Give it a name*), *«Falta el número»* (*Number missing*), *«Elige el país»*
(*Choose the country*) or *«Número demasiado corto»* (*Number too short*).
While there is a half-finished contact, the screen does not save.

### How it is used in the playbook

In the playbook editor, type `@` (or use the **＋** button) and look for the
**Números** (*Numbers*) group: when you choose a contact, its full number, with
the country code, is pasted in **as text**. If that number changes, you will
have to fix it in the playbook too.

What does stay up to date is the notice by name. In the **Avisar a un tercero**
(*Notify a third party*) tool you choose the person **by their name**: the
playbook says «Andrea» and the number stays here. If tomorrow Andrea changes
phones, you fix it on this screen and that notice is not touched.
[The agent's tools](/en/guides/tools) explains it.

### Rules worth knowing

- **If you fix a contact's number, the lists it is in update themselves.** You
  do not have to look for it in Reportes or in Alertas.
- **A contact that is in a list cannot be deleted.** The trash can is greyed
  out and the row says, for example, *«Se usa en Informar de las ventas y
  Reportes. Quítalo de ahí primero.»* (*Used in Informar de las ventas and
  Reportes. Remove it from there first.*) Take it out of those lists and then
  delete it.
- **If a notice in the playbook names it, the contact book and the playbook
  move together.** Renaming it here also renames it in the playbook's notices,
  and a new version is left in the **Historial** (*History*). That holds if you
  only change its name: if in the same save you change its name and its
  number, it is treated as removing it. Do it in two saves. Removing it, on the
  other hand, is refused on save with *««Andrea» avisa en el manual de este
  embudo: cámbialo en el manual antes de quitarlo de la agenda»* (*«Andrea»
  sends a notice in this funnel's playbook: change it in the playbook before
  removing it from the contact book*).
- **There can be «pending» contacts.** If you received a funnel that another
  account shared with you, it may bring contacts with a name and no number:
  their field says *«Pendiente: escribe su WhatsApp»* (*Pending: type their
  WhatsApp*). They can be saved like that, but the notices to that contact do
  not go out until you give it its number. See
  [Your funnels](/en/guides/your-funnels).

## Business details

Your Yape number, your bank account, the account holder, your Instagram, your
payment link. **You write them once here and in the playbook you insert them
as a mention.** The agent reads the value from this screen: if tomorrow you
change banks, you change it in a single place and every chapter is up to date.

<Frame caption="Datos del negocio, still empty («Sin datos guardados» — No details saved): the one-click suggestions and «Agregar dato» (Add detail).">
  <img src="/images/configuracion/03-datos-del-negocio.png" alt="Datos del negocio (Business details) section of Configuración with the text Sin datos guardados (No details saved), suggestion buttons such as Datos de pago (Payment details), Yape, Plin and Cuenta bancaria (Bank account), and the Agregar dato (Add detail) button; below, Envío de imágenes y archivos (Sending images and files) and Moneda de las ventas (Sales currency)" />
</Frame>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the item">
    Click one of the suggestions —**＋ Datos de pago**, **＋ Yape**,
    **＋ Plin**, **＋ Cuenta bancaria**, **＋ CCI**, **＋ Titular**,
    **＋ Instagram**, **＋ TikTok**, **＋ Facebook**, **＋ Página web**,
    **＋ Link de pago**, **＋ Calendly**, **＋ Ubicación**, **＋ Grupo de
    WhatsApp**— or **Agregar dato** (*Add item*) for one with another name.
    (In English: payment details, Yape, Plin, bank account, CCI, account
    holder, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, website, payment link, Calendly,
    location, WhatsApp group.) The suggestions you have already used disappear
    from the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type the value">
    In the value field: Moda Sol's Yape number, the link to your Instagram. The
    name takes up to 40 characters and the value up to 500.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    When you save, its code appears under the row, for example
    `{{dato:yape}}`. It is the way the playbook names it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Insert it in the playbook">
    In the playbook editor, type `@` and look for the **Datos del negocio**
    group. The item goes in as a pill with a tag icon.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
**The value does not accept double quotes (`"`), `###` or double braces.** They
would break the playbook at the point where it is substituted. If you type any
of them, the field warns *«Escríbelo sin comillas, ### ni llaves dobles»*
(*Write it without quotes, ### or double braces*) and the screen does not save.
</Warning>

What happens next:

- **An item with no value stays pending.** You can save «Titular» (*Account
  holder*) empty and fill it in later; the field says *«Pendiente: escribe el
  dato»* (*Pending: type the detail*). Meanwhile, the agent reads «Titular
  (pendiente)» instead of the value and does not make it up. The
  [playbook Doctor](/en/guides/sales-playbook#the-doctor) flags it for you:
  *«Titular» no tiene valor en Ajustes* (*«Titular» has no value in
  Settings*).
- **You can rename an item without breaking anything.** The code is born from
  the first name and does not change after that: if «Yape» becomes «Yape de la
  tienda» (*Store Yape*), the playbook keeps working.
- **You cannot remove an item the playbook uses.** On save you get *«El manual
  usa* `{{dato:yape}}`*: cámbialo en el manual antes de quitar ese dato»*
  (*The playbook uses* `{{dato:yape}}`*: change it in the playbook before
  removing that item*). Remove it from the playbook first.
- **«Copiar de otro embudo…»** (*Copy from another funnel…*), which appears if
  you have more than one funnel, adds the items from another funnel that you do
  not have here yet, with their values.
- **They can also be created from the playbook.** In the `@` menu, the option
  **«＋ Crear dato del negocio «X»»** (*Create business detail «X»*) creates it
  right here, with no value: then you come back to this screen to write it.
- **When you share the funnel with another account, your values do not
  travel.** The item's name travels and whoever receives it puts in their own.
  When you duplicate it within your account, on the other hand, the copy keeps
  your values. See [Your funnels](/en/guides/your-funnels).

## How the numbers in the lists are chosen

Five sections on this screen are lists of phones: **Envío de imágenes y
archivos**, **Informar de las ventas**, **Números de prueba**, **Reportes** and
**Alertas de error**. In them you do not type a loose number: **they are chosen
from your contact book**, or added to it at that moment.

<Frame caption="The notice lists are filled by choosing contacts from Números frecuentes; here, still empty.">
  <img src="/images/configuracion/02-numeros.png" alt="Informar de las ventas (Report sales) and Números de prueba (Test numbers) sections with no numbers set, each with the Elegir de mis números frecuentes… (Choose from my frequent numbers…) selector, and the start of Reportes (Reports)" />
</Frame>

- **To add someone**, open **«Elegir de mis números frecuentes…»** (*Choose
  from my frequent numbers…*) and click their name. Each contact comes with its
  number, for example «Andrea · +51 987654321».
- **If they are not in your contact book yet**, choose **«＋ Agregar un número
  nuevo»** (*Add a new number*): type name, country and number and click
  **Guardar** (*Save*). As the screen itself warns, *«Se guardará también en
  «Números frecuentes»»* (*It will also be saved in «Números frecuentes»*).
- **To take someone out**, click the trash can on their row. They leave that
  list, but stay in your contact book.
- **The same contact can be in several lists at once.** The usual thing is for
  your own number to be in Informar de las ventas, Reportes and Alertas de
  error.

<Note>
**Do you see a number with «Sin nombre» (*No name*) and the notice «No está en
tus números frecuentes» (*Not in your frequent numbers*)?** It is a number that
was already in the list without going through the contact book. It is usually
the WhatsApp you signed up with, which was copied automatically to Informar de
las ventas, Reportes and Alertas de error when the funnel was created. **It
still receives the notices**; it only lacks a name. Click **Guardar en la
agenda** (*Save to contact book*), type whose it is and click **Guardar**.
</Note>

## Sending images and files

What the customer sends your agent —the Yape screenshot, the photo of the
product they want, a PDF— **is forwarded just as it arrived** to the numbers on
this list, with the customer's name and number at the bottom. It goes out
**before** the AI reads it, so it arrives even if the AI fails to read it. It
is the most practical way to check payment receipts without opening the
dashboard.

In **Qué se reenvía** (*What gets forwarded*) you choose the types with three
switches: **Fotos** (*Photos*), **Documentos** (*Documents*) and **Videos**.
Videos come switched off: as the screen explains, they are heavy and take a
while to reach each phone.

- **Voice notes are not forwarded.** They are conversation, not receipts; you
  see them in the inbox.
- **With the list empty, nothing is forwarded to your team.** The screen says
  so: *«Sin números configurados: no se reenvía nada.»* (*No numbers set up:
  nothing is forwarded.*) Watch out: photos and documents still reach a
  WhatsApp of the DarkFunnels team, with the list empty or full.
  [Security and privacy](/en/security#2-which-outside-companies-does-my-data-travel-to)
  explains it.
- **If you choose numbers but switch off all three types, it does not let you
  save**: *«Elige al menos un tipo de archivo para reenviar»* (*Choose at least
  one file type to forward*).

## Sales currency

When the agent records a sale, it needs to know in what currency. **What the
chat says wins**: if the customer wrote the symbol (for example «S/ 120») or
the product already has a price in the [catalog](/en/guides/catalog), that
currency is used. The **Moneda por defecto** (*Default currency*) in this
section is the one used when there is neither of those two clues.

The list has eight: **PEN** (soles), **USD** (dollars), **COP**, **MXN**,
**CLP**, **ARS**, **BOB** and **EUR**. It comes set to soles.

<Warning>
**The platform does not convert currencies.** Each sale is saved in the
currency it came in. Changing this currency does not touch sales already
recorded: only the ones that come afterwards.
</Warning>

## Reporting sales

**Every sale the agent records arrives instantly on WhatsApp** to the numbers
on this list, with a 💰 in front: the product, the total and the details the
agent captured from the customer. Empty fields are not sent.

- **It reports sales and services.** Appointments and leads do not come in
  here: appointments appear in the morning summary.
- **It reports the sales that get recorded.** Almost always your agent records
  them with the **Registrar una venta / conversión** (*Record a sale /
  conversion*) tool: if your playbook does not ask for it, do not count on the
  agent recording sales. With the **Órdenes** (*Orders*) app, you also get the notice
  when an order from a chat is paid in full and nobody had recorded that sale.
  See [The agent's tools](/en/guides/tools).
- **With the list empty, the notice does not reach anyone on your team**:
  *«Sin números configurados: no se avisa ninguna venta.»* (*No numbers set
  up: no sale is reported.*) A copy of each notice still reaches a WhatsApp of
  the DarkFunnels team.
  [Security and privacy](/en/security#2-which-outside-companies-does-my-data-travel-to)
  explains it.

<Tip>
This same list is the playbook's **«Equipo de ventas»** (*Sales team*). In the
**Avisar a un tercero** tool you can choose «Equipo de ventas» instead of a
person, and the notice reaches everyone on this list.
</Tip>

## Test numbers

The phones you test your agent from as if you were a customer. The screen
explains it like this: *«El agente les contesta como a un cliente en todas las
líneas de tu negocio, aunque también estén en Reportes, Informar de las ventas
o Alertas de error.»* (*The agent replies to them as to a customer on all your
business's lines, even if they are also in Reportes, Informar de las ventas or
Alertas de error.*)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the phone you are going to test with">
    It is mainly useful for someone on your team who is already in Reportes,
    Informar de las ventas or Alertas de error, like your salesperson: that way
    the agent replies to them as to a customer. A personal phone that is not in
    those lists on any of your funnels does not need to be added: the agent
    already replies to it like any customer. Choose it from your contact book or add it with
    **«＋ Agregar un número nuevo»**, and save.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write to your funnel's number from that phone">
    A «hola» (*hello*) is enough. You will see the conversation come into
    [Conversations](/en/guides/conversations).
  </Step>
</Steps>

- **Your own WhatsApp already counts as a test number**: the one you entered
  when signing up does not need to be added. The exception: if that same
  number is the line you connected to a funnel, it does not work for testing
  (see the next point).
- **It applies to all your business's lines**, even if you save it in a single
  funnel.
- **Your business's WhatsApp lines do not work as test numbers**, even if you
  put them on this list: it is the protection so that the agents of two of your
  funnels do not reply to each other in a loop. If you have two funnels, do not
  test one by writing to it from the other's line: test from a personal phone.

<Note>
If you only want to see how the script responds, without sending anything over
WhatsApp, use the inbox's **chat de prueba** (*test chat*).
[Conversations](/en/guides/conversations) explains it.
</Note>

## Reports

Every morning you get on WhatsApp **what happened the day before**: the sales
closed with what each customer ordered, the appointments booked, how many
people were talked to and an idea for selling more.

<Frame caption="Reportes, with the «Enviar el resumen cada mañana» (Send the summary every morning) switch (here Apagado — Off — and with no numbers); below, Alertas de error, Herramientas and Cerebro.">
  <img src="/images/configuracion/04-reportes.png" alt="Reportes (Reports) section of Configuración with the Enviar el resumen cada mañana (Send the summary every morning) switch on Apagado (Off) and its list of numbers empty; below, Alertas de error (Error alerts), Herramientas (Tools) and the start of Cerebro (Brain)" />
</Frame>

If you gave your WhatsApp when signing up, a new funnel usually comes with the
summary **switched on** and your number on the list. To check or change it:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check that «Enviar el resumen cada mañana» is switched on">
    Next to the switch it reads **Encendido** (*On*) or **Apagado** (*Off*).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose who receives it">
    In the list below, like the other lists on this screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Guardar cambios** in the bar at the bottom.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**It goes out at 8:00, in the funnel's time zone** (the one in
[Behavior](#behavior)), and summarizes from 00:00 to 24:00 of the previous
day. Each funnel sends its own summary.

<Warning>
**The summary includes your customers' names and phone numbers.** Only put it
on trusted phones.
</Warning>

If you switch it on with nobody on the list, the screen warns *«Nadie lo
recibirá: agrega abajo al menos un número, o apaga el resumen.»* (*Nobody will
receive it: add at least one number below, or switch the summary off.*) and
does not let you save.

**When it does not arrive even though it is on:**

- The list is empty: *«Sin números configurados: no se envía el resumen.»*
  (*No numbers set up: the summary is not sent.*)
- The funnel is **paused** and had no activity yesterday. With the funnel
  active, the summary goes out even if the day was a zero: that zero also
  tells you something.
- The summary goes out through your funnel's WhatsApp line. If that line has
  been disconnected for more than a day, or you never connected it, it does
  not go out that day. See
  [Connect your WhatsApp](/en/getting-started/connect-whatsapp#common-problems).

## Error alerts

**Who is notified on WhatsApp if the agent fails** when replying or when
delivering a message. The notice names the funnel and what happened, and asks
you to check that conversation.

It does not notify every bump. If the agent could not reply, it is retried
first, and the notice goes out when the retry also fails. If what failed is
the sending, it goes out when the failure is not a passing one.

This list also gets **your account's notices**: that your agent stopped
replying because you ran out of enough balance, or because your trial ended.
Those notices do not reach your customer. See
[Credits and top-ups](/en/getting-started/credits-and-top-ups).

<Warning>
**Keep your number on this list.** If you leave it empty (*«Sin números
configurados.»* — *No numbers set up.*), those notices do not reach anyone in
your business, and you find out that the agent went quiet when a customer
complains.
</Warning>

## Tools

**This section only appears if the Inventario (*Inventory*) app is active** in
your business. It has one switch: **Consultar stock (Inventario)** (*Check
stock (Inventory)*). Switched on, the agent checks the real availability
before promising units or delivery times, and does not offer what is not
there.

For it to work you also need products in the catalog.
[The agent's tools](/en/guides/tools) explains it.

## Brain

**Modelo LLM** (*LLM model*) is the AI model that reasons and writes the
replies. As the screen says, *«Determina la capacidad de razonamiento y el
costo por mensaje»* (*It determines the reasoning ability and the cost per
message*): how much each conversation spends is explained in
[Credits and top-ups](/en/getting-started/credits-and-top-ups).

<Warning>
**If your model appears with «(fuera de catálogo)» (*out of catalog*), that
model is no longer offered.** While it is still selected, this screen does
not save any change to the funnel: when you click **Guardar cambios** a notice appears that
starts with *«modelo inválido»* (*invalid model*). Choose another model from
the list and save again.
</Warning>

**Ventana de contexto** (*Context window*) is how much conversation the agent
reads before replying. It is measured in **intercambios** (*exchanges*): each
customer message with its reply. It goes from **1 to 50** and comes set to
**15**. With more exchanges, the agent remembers further back in the
conversation.

## Writing

**Deslices de tipeo** (*Typing slips*) makes some bubbles come out with a
small, natural mistake, the way a person in a hurry would write:

- a stretched letter («holaa»),
- an irregular laugh («jajaj»),
- a missing accent («esta» for «está»),
- or the first letter in lowercase.

The slider goes from **0% to 100%** and comes **Apagado** (*Off*, 0%). Each
bubble carries **at most one slip**. It does not touch any word that has a
number, an @ or a web address, so prices, phone numbers, emails and links come
out as they are.

<Tip>
Start low, at 5 or 10%. With a high percentage the agent seems careless more
than human.
</Tip>

## Behavior

The last section, **Comportamiento**, brings together several loose settings.

### Time zone and business time zone

- **Zona horaria** (*Time zone*) is **the funnel's**: its agent uses it to
  greet («buenas noches», *good evening*) and to talk about dates and times. It
  also rules the 8:00 summary and the do-not-disturb hours.
- **Zona horaria del negocio** (*Business time zone*) is **the company's**: the
  copilot and the dates across your whole business use it. It comes set to
  *«Default de la plataforma (Lima)»* (*Platform default (Lima)*). Changing it
  requires admin permissions.

They can be different on purpose: a funnel that sells in Madrid works on
Madrid time even if your company is in Lima.

### Do not disturb: from / to

**No molestar: desde / hasta** (*Do not disturb: from / to*) is a range of
hours in which **scheduled follow-ups do not go out**: the ones due within the
range wait until it ends. It is there so that a follow-up does not reach your
customer at 3 in the morning. It goes by the funnel's time and comes set to
**12:00 a.m. to 6:00 a.m.**

Reminders of a booked appointment and notices to the customer about their
order do not wait: they go out on time.

What piled up does not all go out at once when the range ends: it is spread at
random over the following hour. With the factory range, between 6:00 a.m. and
7:00 a.m.

To remove it, choose **«Sin franja (a cualquier hora)»** (*No range (any
time)*) in either of the two fields: both are cleared and follow-ups go out on
time, also in the small hours.

### Voice-note language

**Idioma de las notas de voz** (*Voice-note language*) is what language your
customers speak to you in their audio messages. It is passed on to the system
that turns them into text. It comes set to **Español** (*Spanish*); there are
also Inglés, Portugués, Francés, Italiano, Alemán (*English, Portuguese,
French, Italian, German*) and **Detectar automáticamente** (*Detect
automatically*).

Leave it on your customers' real language. If you set it to «Detectar
automáticamente», an audio in Spanish may come out transcribed into another
language and the agent reply that it did not understand.

### Deletion phrase

**Frase de borrado** (*Deletion phrase*) is the word a customer writes to your
agent to **delete their data**. It comes set to **«borrar»** (*delete*). It
works when the message is only that phrase: upper or lower case does not
matter, but it does not work inside a longer sentence. On receiving it, their
data in this funnel is deleted and their next message starts a conversation
from scratch. What exactly the deletion reaches is covered in
[Security and privacy](/en/security).

You can change it to another one (up to 64 characters). If you leave the field
empty, it goes back to «borrar». The change can take up to a minute to take
effect.

<Tip>
If in your business it is normal for a customer to write «borrar» for another
reason («borrar mi pedido», *delete my order*), nothing happens: it has to be
the whole message. But if it worries you, choose a phrase nobody would write on
its own by accident.
</Tip>

### Base location: name and address

**Ubicación base** (*Base location*) is where your business is, as a reference
for the agent: a name («Downtown Store») and an address. **This does not send
the map pin.** For the agent to send your location on WhatsApp, save the
branch in the Library, **Ubicaciones** (*Locations*) tab:
[The file Library](/en/guides/library) explains it.

## Saving: the bar at the bottom

The bar stays fixed at the bottom while you move around the screen. On the left
it says what state you are in:

| What it says | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Sin cambios pendientes** (*No pending changes*) | Everything you see is already saved. |
| **Cambios sin guardar** (*Unsaved changes*) | You touched something. The **Descartar** (*Discard*) button appears. |
| **Guardando…** (*Saving…*) | It is sending your changes. |
| **Guardado** (*Saved*) | Done. The notice *«Ajustes guardados»* (*Settings saved*) also appears. |

**Descartar** takes the screen back to the last thing you saved. **Guardar
cambios** saves all the sections at once.

If **Guardar cambios** is grey even though you changed something, the bar
tells you why:

| Message in the bar | What is missing |
| --- | --- |
| *«Escribe un nombre para guardar»* (*Type a name to save*) | The **Nombre del embudo** is empty. |
| *«Completa el nombre y el número de cada contacto»* (*Complete the name and number of each contact*) | There is a half-finished contact in **Números frecuentes**. |
| *«Elige al menos un tipo de archivo para reenviar»* (*Choose at least one file type to forward*) | In **Envío de imágenes y archivos** there are numbers but no type switched on. |
| *«Elige a quién le llega el resumen, o apágalo»* (*Choose who gets the summary, or switch it off*) | **Reportes** is on with nobody on the list. |

If the problem is in **Datos del negocio** (an item without a name or with
characters it does not accept), the bar does not name it: the warning is in
red on the row itself.

## If something does not work

| What you see | It is almost always | How to fix it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The screen says **«Selecciona un embudo»** (*Select a funnel*) | No funnel chosen in the selector | Choose one at the top of the menu, inside **Ventas** |
| I changed something and **on the other funnel it is still the same** | Configuración is per funnel | Repeat the change with the other funnel selected |
| **Guardar cambios** cannot be clicked | Something is missing: the bar says what, or there is a business detail in red | See the [Saving](#saving-the-bar-at-the-bottom) table and the **Datos del negocio** section |
| *«Cambiar el nombre del embudo requiere administrar las apps de la empresa»* | You do not have that permission | Leave the name as it was and save, or ask whoever manages the account |
| *«El manual usa* `{{dato:…}}`*: cámbialo en el manual…»* | You tried to remove an item the playbook uses | Remove it from the playbook first |
| *««Andrea» avisa en el manual de este embudo…»* | You tried to remove a contact that a notice names | Change that notice in the playbook and try again |
| A contact's trash can **is greyed out** | The contact is in a list | Take it out of the list the row names |
| A number shows as **«Sin nombre»** | It entered the list without going through the contact book | **Guardar en la agenda** and give it a name; meanwhile, it keeps receiving |
| **I do not get the 💰 notice** | Empty list, or the agent did not record the sale | Check **Informar de las ventas** and that the playbook uses **Registrar una venta / conversión** |
| **I do not get the summary** | Switch off, empty list or WhatsApp line disconnected | Check **Reportes** and the connection in **Personalizar** |
| **The summary arrives at an odd time** | The funnel's time zone is not yours | Change it in **Comportamiento → Zona horaria** |
| The agent **did not reply to my test number** | You wrote from another of your business's lines, or the funnel is paused | Test from a personal phone and check that the funnel is active in **Inicio** |
| The agent says **«Yape (pendiente)»** | That item has no value | Write it in **Datos del negocio** and save |
| **«No se pudieron cargar los ajustes»** (*Settings could not be loaded*) | A failure when opening the screen | Reload the page; if it persists, write to us from the dashboard's **Ayuda** (*Help*) button |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I put the same number in several lists?">
    Yes. A contact can be in Informar de las ventas, Reportes, Alertas de
    error, Envío de imágenes y archivos and Números de prueba at the same time.
    Each list sends it its own thing: the sale notices through one, the summary
    through another.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I entered my WhatsApp number wrong when signing up">
    Fix it here, **funnel by funnel**: that number was copied to Informar de las
    ventas, Reportes and Alertas de error of every funnel you created. Add the
    right number to the contact book and choose it in those three lists. Also
    add it in **Números de prueba**, so that the agent replies to you as to a
    customer when you write to it from that number. Then remove the old one.

    The wrong number is still saved in your account, so the funnels you create
    later will be born with it again. To change it in your account, write to
    us. [Create your account](/en/getting-started/create-your-account) explains
    it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens to these settings if I duplicate or share the funnel?">
    When you **duplicate** it within your account, the copy is born with the
    same settings, numbers and details included. When you **share** it with
    another account, your numbers and the values of your business details do
    not travel: whoever receives it puts in their own.
    [Your funnels](/en/guides/your-funnels) explains it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does changing the model affect conversations already in progress?">
    The new model is used in the replies that come after you save. What the
    agent already sent does not change.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## The same thing, from your assistant

<Note>
This section is **optional and only applies if you connected Claude, ChatGPT or
Codex** to your business. If you work in the dashboard, you are already done.
</Note>

Your assistant can **read** these settings without switching anything on. To
**change** them it needs the `agents_write` group in your connection URL (or
`features=all`); how the URL is put together is explained in
[The connection URL](/en/reference/connection-url). With that group it can
change the name, the model, the context window, the typing slips, the deletion
phrase, the funnel's time zone, the do-not-disturb hours, the voice-note
language, the currency, the daily summary, the notice lists, the file
forwarding, «Consultar stock» and the location.

It **cannot touch** Números frecuentes, Números de prueba, Datos del negocio
or the business time zone: that belongs to the dashboard. Two more things:

- A list you ask it to change **replaces the existing one entirely**. Always
  give it the complete list.
- If your assistant puts a number on a list that is not in your contact book,
  in the dashboard it shows as **«Sin nombre»** until you save it in Números
  frecuentes. It receives the notices all the same.

Phrases that work:

- *"Tell me how my sales funnel is set up: model, daily summary and who gets the
  notices."*
- *"Raise my agent's typing slips to 5%."*
- *"Set my agent's do-not-disturb hours from 10 at night to 7 in the
  morning."*
