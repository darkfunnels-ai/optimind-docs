Your agent does not know what anything costs until you tell it. **The catalog is
where you tell it**: a product, a price, its sizes, its photo. That is where it
takes the numbers from when the customer asks "how much is it?".

This guide is the full walkthrough from the screen, click by click. The examples
use a made-up clothing store, **Moda Sol**, and its star product: the **Polo
oversize de algodón pima** (an oversized pima cotton T-shirt).

The dashboard is in Spanish, so buttons and on-screen messages are quoted here
exactly as you will see them, with the English meaning in parentheses.

<Note>
**Where it is.** Go into the dashboard at
[optimind.darkfunnels.ai](https://optimind.darkfunnels.ai) and, in the left-hand
menu, inside **Ventas** (*Sales*), under the **Comercio** (*Commerce*) divider,
click **Catálogo** (*Catalog*). (If you prefer to go straight there, type
`optimind.darkfunnels.ai/catalog` into the browser's address bar. The old
address, `/catalogo`, takes you to the new one on its own.)

The catalog is part of the **Ventas** app. If you do not see **Ventas** in your
menu, your user does not have access to that app: ask whoever manages your
account for it (see [Invite your team](/en/getting-started/invite-your-team)).
</Note>

## What the catalog is for

The screen itself sums it up under the title: *«El precio sale de aquí»* (*The
price comes from here*). (If you have more than one agent, that sentence starts
by saying which agent you are looking at: *«Lo que vende «…», más los productos
sin embudo asignado»*, *What «…» sells, plus the products with no funnel
assigned*. The list is trimmed to that agent.)

That is literal: the agent carries a written instruction to check the catalog
every time the customer asks about a product or its price, and not to make
prices up. The practical consequence is simple: **if a product is not in the
catalog, your agent has nowhere to get its exact price from.**

<Tip>
**Loading it is all you have to do.** There is no need to link the product to
the sales playbook, to name it in any chapter, or to switch anything else on. As
soon as you save it, the agent can already find it and offer it. Two honest
caveats: what you change can take **up to a minute** to reach the agent, and if
you tick specific funnels on the product, the others stop seeing it (see
[Visible and Active](#visible-and-active-are-not-the-same)).
</Tip>

## The words on this screen

| Word | What it means, in plain language |
| --- | --- |
| **Producto** (*Product*) | The complete record: name, description, photos and price. |
| **Variante** (*Variant*) | Each sellable version of that product: the black size M, the 500 ml bottle. Each one has **its own price and its own code**. |
| **SKU** | The code you use to identify a variant (`POLO-NEGR-M`). It is optional; it is there for searching. |
| **Tipo** (*Type*) | Whether what you sell is a **Producto físico** (*Physical product*), a **Servicio** (*Service*) or an **Infoproducto** (*Info product*). It is chosen first and it changes the rest of the form. |
| **Activo** (*Active*) | Whether the agent can find it when it searches. |
| **Visible en el catálogo** (*Visible in the catalog*) | Whether it appears in the catalog WhatsApp shows inside your business profile. **It is not the same as Activo.** |

## Your first product, click by click

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click «Añadir producto» (Add product)">
    It is at the top right, next to another button called **Plantilla CSV**
    (*CSV template*). With the catalog empty you also have the same button in
    the middle of the screen, under *«Aún no hay productos»*.

    **No new page opens.** A panel slides in from the right-hand side, top to
    bottom, over the dimmed background. It is titled **Nuevo producto** (*New
    product*) — and **Editar producto** (*Edit product*) when you open one that
    already exists. Do not look for a "back" button: there is none. It closes
    with **Cerrar** (*Close*, at the top), with **Cancelar** (*Cancel*, at the
    bottom) or with the **Esc** key.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose what you are going to sell">
    The first and only thing you will see is the heading **«¿Qué vas a
    vender?»** (*What are you going to sell?*) with three cards: **Producto
    físico** (*Physical product*), **Servicio** (*Service*) and **Infoproducto**
    (*Info product*). Until you choose one, the rest of the form does not exist
    and the **Guardar** (*Save*) button is greyed out: it will not let you click
    it.

    For the Moda Sol T-shirt: **Producto físico**.

    <Frame caption="The panel starts by asking what you sell. Each card tells you what the agent will do if you choose it.">
      <img src="/images/catalogo/02-nuevo-producto-tipo.png" alt="«Nuevo producto» panel at the «¿Qué vas a vender?» step, with the Producto físico, Servicio and Infoproducto cards" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Give it a name and describe how it is sold">
    Once the type is chosen, the whole form unfolds. In **Título y
    descripción** (*Title and description*) there is only one field with an
    asterisk: **Nombre \*** (*Name*). Type `Polo oversize de algodón pima`.

    The **Descripción** (*Description*) is optional, but it is not decorative:
    the field says so — *«Detalles que el agente puede usar al vender.»*
    (*Details the agent can use when selling.*) — and it is text the agent
    reads when it finds the product. It reaches the agent **trimmed to around
    240 characters**, so put what sells in the first lines: *"Peruvian pima
    cotton, unisex oversize cut. Machine washable. Shipping anywhere in Peru."*

    <Warning>
    **The name is what the customer has to be able to say.** The agent searches
    by the product name and by the name or the code of its variants — **it does
    not search inside the description**. Putting "polo", "camiseta" and
    "remera" (the words for a T-shirt in different countries) only in the
    description does not help it find the product: put them in the name.
    </Warning>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Upload the photos">
    In **Medios** (*Media*), drag the images in or click where it says
    **«Arrastra o haz clic para subir»** (*Drag or click to upload*).
    Underneath it reads *«PNG, JPG o WEBP. La primera imagen es la principal.»*
    (*PNG, JPG or WEBP. The first image is the main one.*) — that is a
    **recommendation**, not a filter: the dashboard accepts any image file.

    The first photo carries the **Principal** (*Main*) label. Any of them, the
    main one included, is removed with the **×**. **They cannot be reordered by
    dragging**: to change the main one you have to remove the ones in front of
    it.

    <Warning>
    **Photos do not wait for the Guardar button.** They are uploaded on the
    spot, and removing one deletes it from storage at that same moment. If you
    upload a photo and then cancel the product, the photo has already been
    uploaded. If you remove a photo from a product that already existed and
    then cancel, the photo really has already been deleted, but the product's
    record still names it: it will be left as a broken photo. Open the product,
    remove that photo and save.
    </Warning>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set the price">
    In **Precios** (*Prices*) there are two fields: **Precio \*** (*Price*) and
    **Moneda** (*Currency*). The currency has **only two options — «PEN (S/)»
    and «USD (\$)» — and comes set to PEN**. From this screen there are no other
    currencies.

    For the T-shirt: `79` and `PEN (S/)`. If you have cents, type them with a
    point, not a comma: `79.90`. If you leave the price empty or type something
    that is not a number, on saving you get *«Pon un precio válido.»* (*Enter a
    valid price.*)

    Below it you will see the **Precios por cantidad** (*Quantity pricing*)
    switch. Leave it off for now: we cover it in
    [its own section](#quantity-pricing-wholesale).

    <Frame caption="The form for a physical product, from the top down to Precios: Tipo, Título y descripción with the polo shirt, Medios, and Precios with 79 in PEN (S/) and «Precios por cantidad» off.">
      <img src="/images/catalogo/03-formulario-fisico.png" alt="Nuevo producto panel with the Producto físico type, the name Polo oversize de algodón pima and its description, the Medios area, the price 79 in PEN (S/) and the Precios por cantidad switch off" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Decide whether you keep count of stock">
    In **Inventario** (*Inventory*) there is a **Controlar stock** (*Track
    stock*) switch and a **SKU** field (with the example `XL-25`). Turn it on
    only if you really want the agent to stop offering what has run out — it
    explains it right there: *«El agente avisa cuando algo se agota y no ofrece
    lo que no tienes.»* (*The agent warns when something runs out and does not
    offer what you do not have.*)

    <Warning>
    **Quantities are not typed here.** When you turn on **Controlar stock**,
    **Cantidad disponible** (*Available quantity*) appears, but it is
    **read-only**: it will not let you type. Underneath, the panel itself says
    so — *«Lo lleva Inventario. Para moverlo, registra un movimiento en
    Inventario»* (*Inventario handles it. To change it, record a movement in
    Inventario*) — with a link to that screen. It is the most common confusion
    in this form. See [Stock: when to turn it on](#stock-when-to-turn-it-on).
    </Warning>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add sizes and colors, if you have them">
    On a physical product the block is called **Variantes** (*Variants*) and
    comes with the **Generar variantes por opciones** (*Generate variants from
    options*) button: you give it the axes (Color: Negro, Blanco, Arena /
    Talla: S, M, L, XL) and it creates the combinations in one click, each with
    its own code. It takes up to **3 axes** and **100 combinations**.

    The [Variants](#variants-sizes-and-colors) section tells the whole story.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save — and check that it shows up in the table">
    The last block is **Disponibilidad** (*Availability*), with **Visible en el
    catálogo** and **Activo** switched on. Leave them like that and click
    **Guardar**.

    The product appears at the very top of the table, with its columns:
    *producto, sku, precio, inventario, embudos, visible, origen* (*product,
    SKU, price, inventory, funnels, visible, source*) and a last one with the
    actions.

    <Frame caption="The table with three products: each row with its SKU, its price, its inventory («Sin control»), the funnels that sell it («Todos»), the «visible» switch on and the Editar button.">
      <img src="/images/catalogo/05-tabla-con-producto.png" alt="Catalog table with three products, Polo cuello V de algodón pima, Pack 3 polos básicos pima and Polo oversize de algodón pima, with inventario Sin control, embudos Todos, visible on and origen Propio" />
    </Frame>

    <Warning>
    **Closing the panel throws away everything you typed, with no warning.** It
    makes no difference whether you click **Cerrar** at the top, **Cancelar**
    at the bottom, press the **Esc** key or click outside the panel, on the
    dark area: there is no confirmation and no saved draft. The only thing that
    keeps your work is **Guardar**.
    </Warning>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How to read the table

Each cell in the table tells you something about the product. These are the
ones that raise the most questions:

| What you see | Where | What it means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **«Desde …»** (*From …*) before the price | precio | The product has variants and that is the price of the cheapest one. |
| **«· 3 tramos»** (*· 3 tiers*) after the price | precio | It has [quantity pricing](#quantity-pricing-wholesale). The price you see is the 1-unit price. |
| **«PROMO»** before the price | precio | There is a promotion in force, and that is its 1-unit price. |
| **«12 variantes»** (*12 variants*) under the name | producto | How many versions it has. On a service it says **«Servicio · 3 modalidades»** (*Service · 3 options*). |
| **«Infoproducto · ⚠️ sin entrega»** (*Info product · ⚠️ no delivery*) | producto | It is an info product with no delivery file or link. See [Automatic delivery](#if-you-sell-a-course-or-an-ebook-automatic-delivery). |
| A number, **«Agotado»** (*Out of stock*) or **«Sin control»** (*Not tracked*) | inventario | Explained in [Stock: when to turn it on](#stock-when-to-turn-it-on). |
| **«Todos»** (*All*) | embudos | You did not tick any funnels: all your agents sell it. |
| Agent names | embudos | Only those agents sell it. |
| **«Embudo eliminado»** (*Funnel deleted*) | embudos | It was ticked for an agent that is no longer in your list. Open it and tick the agents that should sell it. |

The dimmed rows, with lighter text, are products with **Activo** switched off.

## The three types, and how they really differ

The type is not a label: **it changes which fields you see and what the agent
does afterwards**. Each card tells you so when you choose it.

| Type | What the card says | What changes in the form |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Producto físico** (*Physical product*) | *«El agente cotiza el envío y genera la guía.»* (*The agent quotes shipping and generates the waybill.*) | A **Variantes** (*Variants*) block, with the size and color generator. |
| **Servicio** (*Service*) | *«El agente lo vende y coordina por chat. No genera envío.»* (*The agent sells it and arranges it over chat. It does not generate a shipment.*) | The block is called **Modalidades** (*Options*): it asks for the name and price of each option. |
| **Infoproducto** (*Info product*) | *«Su envío es digital: se entrega solo, apenas se confirma el pago.»* (*Its delivery is digital: it is delivered on its own as soon as payment is confirmed.*) | It has **no** variants block. Instead, **Entrega automática** (*Automatic delivery*) appears. |

The stock-tracking notice changes too: on a physical product it talks about
stock on hand, on a service about **cupos disponibles** (*available slots*)
(*«El agente no ofrece lo que se agotó.»*, *The agent does not offer what has
run out.*) and on an info product about **limited licenses or accesses**. So
"stock" here is not just boxes in a warehouse.

<Note>
**Does your service need a date and time?** When you choose **Servicio**, the
panel warns you: *«¿Necesita fecha y hora (citas)? Eso se programa en
Calendario; aquí solo se vende y cotiza.»* (*Does it need a date and time
(appointments)? That is scheduled in Calendario; here it is only sold and
quoted.*) The catalog sets the price; the schedule lives in the **Calendario**
(*Calendar*) app.
</Note>

<Note>
**Changing the type of a product that already exists does not delete anything
straight away.** The panel itself says: *«Cambiar el tipo oculta lo que ya no
aplica. Nada se borra hasta que guardes.»* (*Changing the type hides what no
longer applies. Nothing is deleted until you save.*) If the change is going to
remove data — turning a product that has several variants into an info product,
or turning an info product that had automatic delivery into another type — saving
asks you for a two-step confirmation with the **Convertir y guardar** (*Convert
and save*) button.
</Note>

### If you sell a course or an ebook: automatic delivery

On an info product a block appears that is not in the other two, and it
explains on its own what it is for: *«Cuando el pago se confirma (voucher
aprobado), el agente le manda esto al cliente sin que tú hagas nada.»* (*When
payment is confirmed (voucher approved), the agent sends this to the customer
without you doing anything.*) You have two options:

- **A file from the Librería** (*Library*) — only the files already marked as
  sendable for the agent you have open are offered. If there are none, the
  panel tells you: *«Sube un archivo a la Librería y márcalo como enviable para
  poder entregarlo aquí.»* (*Upload a file to the Library and mark it as
  sendable to be able to deliver it here.*) They are prepared in
  [The file Library](/en/guides/library).
- **A link** — it has to start with `http://` or `https://`. If not, on saving
  you get *«El enlace debe empezar con http:// o https://»* (*The link must
  start with http:// or https://*).

If you save with neither of the two, the product **is saved anyway**, but the
panel warns you: *«Ojo: sin archivo ni enlace, el agente no podrá entregarlo
solo.»* (*Careful: with no file and no link, the agent will not be able to
deliver it on its own.*) And in the list, that product is marked under its name
as **«Infoproducto · ⚠️ sin entrega»**. It is worth fixing: otherwise the agent
can charge for something it then does not know how to deliver.

## The form, block by block

Once the type is chosen, the panel shows **seven blocks**. Six are the same for
all three types; the seventh depends on the type and sits between
**Inventario** and **Disponibilidad**.

| Order | Block | What it is for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Tipo** (*Type*) | What you sell. It is chosen first and it conditions the rest. |
| 2 | **Título y descripción** (*Title and description*) | The name (required) and the text the agent uses when selling. |
| 3 | **Medios** (*Media*) | The photos. |
| 4 | **Precios** (*Prices*) | Price, currency and the quantity scale. |
| 5 | **Inventario** (*Inventory*) | The stock-tracking switch and the SKU. |
| 6 | **Variantes** / **Modalidades** / **Entrega automática** | Depending on the type. The info product has no variants. |
| 7 | **Disponibilidad** (*Availability*) | Visible, Activo and — if you have more than one agent — which funnels sell it. |

### What is really required

Not much. **Three things**, in this order:

1. **The type.** Without it no other field is shown.
2. **Nombre \***. If it is missing: *«El nombre es obligatorio.»* (*The name
   is required.*)
3. **Precio \***. If it is empty or not a number: *«Pon un precio válido.»*

Everything else — description, photos, SKU, variants, stock, funnels — is
optional and can be filled in later.

<Note>
**With «Precios por cantidad» on, the required price moves.** The field at the
top is locked and the one you have to fill in becomes the **1 unit** row of the
scale. The warning changes too: it will ask you for the 1-unit price, not
*«Pon un precio válido»*.

**With variants, the price is asked for on each one**, not at the top. See
[Variants](#variants-sizes-and-colors).
</Note>

<Warning>
**Do not leave products with a price of 0.** They are saved without complaint,
but the agent would see a price of 0 and, on top of that, **they do not appear
in the product dropdown** when you record a sale or an order by hand: that
dropdown only brings in active products whose variants cost more than 0. Watch
out for variants generated without a base price: they are born at 0.
</Warning>

## Quantity pricing (wholesale)

It is the most powerful feature on this screen and the least obvious. Turn on
**Precios por cantidad** and you declare the **exact total price for each
quantity**, not a percentage discount:

| Quantity | Total price |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | S/ 79 |
| 2 | S/ 149 |
| 3 | S/ 209 |

Rows are added with **+ Añadir tramo** (*+ Add tier*). And with **+ De N a más
(precio por unidad)** (*+ From N up (price per unit)*) you define the open tier:
*from 6 up, S/ 65 each*. Each tier has to cost more than the previous one: if
the 3-unit tier costs less than the 2-unit one, the panel will not let you save
and tells you which one to fix.

<Frame caption="Quantity pricing switched on: the total price for each quantity (1 → 79 and 3 → 199 PEN) and, underneath, the promotion (1 → 69 PEN) with its end date.">
  <img src="/images/catalogo/07-precios-por-cantidad.png" alt="Precios block with the Precios por cantidad switch on, the tiers 1 → 79 and 3 → 199 PEN, and the promotion 1 → 69 PEN with Vigente hasta set to 31/10/2026" />
</Frame>

<Warning>
**The moment you turn it on, the «Precio» field at the top is locked** and it is
renamed **Precio (1 unidad, de la escala)** (*Price (1 unit, from the scale)*).
It is not a fault: the price is now typed into the 1-unit row of the table.
</Warning>

**For quantities that are not on your scale, the agent combines tiers and adds
them up.** If the customer asks for 10 T-shirts and your scale goes up to 3, it
charges 3 + 3 + 3 + 1. If you prefer a clean per-unit price from a certain
quantity onwards, define the open **De N a más** tier. The agent does not do the
arithmetic: when it looks up the product with the quantity the customer asked
for, it receives the total already calculated.

There is also **Promoción con fecha de fin** (*Promotion with an end date*): a
separate scale, with its own tiers and the **Vigente hasta** (*Valid until*)
field. While it is in force, the promotion overrides the list scale in the
conversation.

<Note>
**The scale belongs to the whole product.** If you turn it on for a product with
sizes, the agent quotes with the scale and stops looking at each size's price.
If your sizes cost different amounts, do not use the scale on that product, or
split it into separate products.
</Note>

<Note>
**The promotion does not travel to the WhatsApp catalog.** In the catalog
WhatsApp shows on your business profile, the 1-unit **list** price is
published, not the promotional one. It is on purpose: if the promo were
published, it would stay stuck there once it expired. In the chat, the
promotion does apply.
</Note>

<Note>
**In the CSV file, leaving the scale cell blank does not delete it**: it leaves
it as it was. To remove it, turn off **Precios por cantidad** on the product and
save.
</Note>

## Variants: sizes and colors

Moda Sol sells the same T-shirt in 3 colors and 4 sizes. That is **12
variants**, and there is no need to type them one by one.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Type the base price first">
    In **Precio**, at the top. New combinations are born with that price; if
    the field is empty, they are born at 0.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click «Generar variantes por opciones»">
    It is in the **Variantes** block. Two axes open, already named **Color**
    and **Talla** (*Size*).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type the values for each axis">
    Separated by commas. *Color*: Negro, Blanco, Arena (black, white, sand).
    *Talla*: S, M, L, XL. With **+ Añadir eje** (*+ Add axis*) you can go up to
    **3 axes** (color, size and, for example, sleeve). Underneath, the panel
    keeps telling you how many will come out: *«Se generarán 12 variantes.»*
    (*12 variants will be generated.*)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click «Generar» (Generate)">
    The 12 combinations come out in one click, with titles like «Negro / M».
    The **SKU automático** (*Automatic SKU*) checkbox comes ticked: each one is
    born with its code (`POLO-NEGR-M`, for example). The generator goes up to
    **100 combinations** at once.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Adjust the prices that fall outside the norm">
    Each row carries its own price. If the XL costs S/ 10 more, change it on its
    row. And if you want to change the price of all of them at once, use the
    **Mismo precio para las 12 variantes** (*Same price for the 12 variants*)
    row and click **Aplicar a todas** (*Apply to all*). Then click **Guardar**:
    until then nothing is saved.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
**With variants, the price at the top is no longer typed.** The field is
renamed **Desde (el más barato)** (*From (the cheapest)*), it is locked and it
shows the price of the cheapest variant. Underneath it explains: *«Lo lleva
cada variante. Para cambiarlo, edítalo abajo en Variantes.»* (*Each variant
carries it. To change it, edit it below in Variantes.*) The **SKU** at the top
gets the same treatment.

**The currency is changed at the top**, and it applies to all the variants. But
it does not convert anything; the panel warns you: *«La moneda se aplica a las
12 variantes. Los precios no se convierten: 79 sigue siendo 79, solo cambia la
moneda.»* (*The currency applies to the 12 variants. Prices are not converted:
79 is still 79, only the currency changes.*) If you switch from PEN to USD,
correct the prices.
</Note>

<Tip>
**If you generate again on a product that already had variants**, the ones that
fit the new combination are kept with their price, and those that do not fit are
left at the end of the list with a warning, so that you remove them yourself if
they are not needed. This also happens to the single variant of a product you
created without sizes.
</Tip>

<Note>
**On a service they are called «Modalidades» and they are the same thing** under
another name: *Express consultation*, *Full consultation*, *Package of 4
sessions*. They are added with **Agregar modalidad** (*Add option*) and ask for
a name and a price. The **Cupos** (*Slots*) are shown but not typed — same as
stock, Inventario handles them. An info product does not have this block: a
single presentation.
</Note>

## Stock: when to turn it on

**Turn on «Controlar stock» only if you are going to record the movements.** If
you turn it on and never load any stock, the agent will believe you have none
left.

| What you see in the **inventario** column | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| A number | You are tracking and you have that many units left. |
| **Agotado** (*Out of stock*) | You are tracking and you are at 0 (or below): it reaches the agent marked as out of stock, with the instruction not to promise it. |
| **Sin control** (*Not tracked*) | You are not keeping count: the agent can offer it without looking at stock. Careful: it also shows like this if you turned on **Controlar stock** and have not yet recorded any movement in Inventario. In that case the agent treats it as **out of stock**: do the initial load. |

With **Controlar stock** off (the table reads **Sin control**) there is nothing
that can run out: it is the right option for a service you can provide with no
cap on slots, or for a product you make to order.

**Quantities are loaded in another app: Inventario.** From the catalog you only
declare **whether** the product is tracked. The first load is done in
Inventario with **Registrar movimiento** (*Record movement*) → **Carga inicial
(+)** (*Initial load (+)*); after that, every purchase, return or adjustment is
also recorded there.

<Frame caption="The Inventario screen, still empty («El catálogo está vacío»): this is where quantities are loaded and moved, with «Registrar movimiento».">
  <img src="/images/apps/02-inventario.png" alt="Inventario screen with the four counters at 0, the Stock and Movimientos tabs, the Registrar movimiento button and the «El catálogo está vacío» notice" />
</Frame>

<Warning>
**Inventario comes switched on in new accounts, but it can be switched off.** If
the *«Inventario»* link in the product panel takes you to a screen that says
**«App no activa»** (*App not active*), it is switched off in your company:
switch it on in **Administración → Apps** (*Administration → Apps*) (or ask
whoever manages the account). If it says **«No tienes acceso a Inventario»**
(*You do not have access to Inventario*), the app is on but your user does not
have it: ask to be given it in **Administración → Personas y accesos**
(*Administration → People and access*).

In both cases, sort it out **before** turning on **Controlar stock**. Otherwise
the agent will treat the product as **out of stock** (even though the
inventario column reads «Sin control») and you will not be able to load units
into it.
</Warning>

## Visible and Active are not the same

This is the costliest confusion on the screen. Both switches are in
**Disponibilidad** and **only one of them silences the agent**.

| Switch | What it does when you turn it off |
| --- | --- |
| **Activo** (*Active*) | The agent **stops finding it** when it searches the catalog. It is the right way to withdraw something. |
| **Visible en el catálogo** (*Visible in the catalog*) | It takes it out of the catalog WhatsApp shows on your business profile, **but the agent still finds it** and can carry on offering it in the conversation. |

<Warning>
**To withdraw a product, turn off «Activo».** Turning off only «Visible en el
catálogo» does not stop the agent from carrying on selling it over chat.
</Warning>

### Funnels that sell it

That list, **Embudos que lo venden** (*Funnels that sell it*), **only appears if
your business has more than one agent**. If you have one, you will not even see
it. And the rule is not the one it looks like: *«Sin marcar ninguno, lo cotizan
todos los embudos.»* (*With none ticked, every funnel quotes it.*) — **ticking
nothing means "all", not "none"**. As soon as you tick one, the text changes:
*«Solo los embudos marcados podrán cotizarlo.»* (*Only the ticked funnels will
be able to quote it.*)

<Note>
**It is an organizing rule, not a security barrier.** It is there so that your
wholesale agent does not quote the retail one's products. But do not use it to
hide sensitive information: if the system cannot read the assignments, it opens
up instead of closing down, and the agent sees everything.
</Note>

**Funnels are saved in a second step, after the product.** If that step fails,
you will see an error message and the panel will stay open, even though the
product has already been saved. If it was a new product, **do not click Guardar
again**: you would create an identical one. Close the panel, reload the page,
open it with **Editar** (*Edit*) and tick the funnels again.

And if on saving you take the product away from the agent you have open, it
warns you — *«Ya no lo vende el embudo abierto: sale de esta lista.»* (*The open
funnel no longer sells it: it leaves this list.*) — and it disappears from the
table. **It has not been deleted**: it stopped belonging to that funnel.

Two things other screens do with your products, so they do not take you by
surprise (see [Your funnels](/en/guides/your-funnels)):

- **If you duplicate a funnel**, the copy is born selling the same products the
  original had ticked.
- **If you accept a funnel that another account shared with you**, its products
  arrive in your catalog as new products, ticked only for that funnel and
  **without stock tracking**.

<Warning>
**With more than one agent, the list you see is trimmed.** `/catalog` shows what
the agent you have chosen in the agent selector sells — the dropdown with its
name, just below **Ventas** in the left-hand menu (with the menu collapsed it
sits at the top; on the phone, inside **Más**, *More*) — plus whatever is not
assigned to any agent. The counter at the foot (*«Mostrando N de M
productos»*, *Showing N of M products*) counts what is already trimmed, and the
CSV template you download will come out trimmed the same way. Before concluding
that you lost products, **switch agents in that selector**.

One exception: a product ticked **only** for an agent you have already deleted
does not appear in any agent's list, and your agents stop offering it. That is
why, **before deleting an agent**, open each product ticked only for it and tick
it for another agent too, or untick all the funnels so that all of them sell it.
</Warning>

## Editing many products at once: the CSV template

To load a hundred products, or to raise the price of a whole season, the
one-by-one form is no use. The **Plantilla CSV** button (next to «Añadir
producto») opens the **«Editar catálogo en bloque (CSV)»** (*Bulk-edit catalog
(CSV)*) box, with two numbered steps.

<Frame caption="The «Editar catálogo en bloque (CSV)» box: first you download, you edit in your spreadsheet, and then you upload.">
  <img src="/images/catalogo/06-plantilla-csv.png" alt="«Editar catálogo en bloque (CSV)» window with the Descarga la plantilla and Sube tus cambios steps" />
</Frame>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Download the template">
    The **Descargar plantilla (.csv)** (*Download template (.csv)*) button. It
    downloads a file with your current products, which you can open in Excel or
    Google Sheets.

    Watch what it contains. The box itself says so: *«los productos editables
    de la lista que tienes debajo»* (*the editable products in the list you
    have below*). In other words, **only those of the agent you have open**,
    and at most **1000 products, the most recent ones**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit it in your spreadsheet">
    Change what you need and save as CSV. Do not delete any column. The `id`,
    `handle` and `variant_id` columns are the ones that let what already exists
    be updated, and if the `handle`, `name` or `currency` column is missing,
    the whole file is rejected.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload it and check the preview">
    Click **Elegir archivo CSV** (*Choose CSV file*). Before applying anything,
    the box shows you a summary — how many products, how many **nuevos**
    (*new*), how many **a actualizar** (*to update*) and how many **variantes**
    (*variants*) — and, if there are rows with errors, the list of those that
    *«se omitirán»* (*will be skipped*) and why.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply the changes">
    **Aplicar cambios** (*Apply changes*). When it finishes you will see how
    many were created, how many were updated and how many gave an error, and
    the reason for the errors. The cap is **500 products per upload**: with
    more, the whole upload is rejected; split it into several files.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### The rules of the file

- A row **with an id** updates the product that already exists.
- A row **without an id** creates a new product. **Careful:** if you delete the
  id from a template row, the original is not updated: an identical one is
  born, because the handle the template comes with is not the one the product
  has stored. To change a product that already exists, always keep its id.
- And the other way round: if you type by hand a **handle** equal to the one the
  system stores for an existing product, that row without an id **updates it**.
  For a product created in the dashboard, that handle is its name in lowercase
  with hyphens (`polo-oversize-de-algodon-pima`), not the one the template
  comes with.
- **A product that is not in the file is not touched.** Deleting a product row
  does not delete the product.
- **Removing a variant's row deletes that variant** from the product.
- **But removing all the variant rows of a product that has several does not
  delete anything**: that row is rejected with an error warning that the
  product *«tiene N variantes y esta fila no las trae»* (*has N variants and
  this row does not include them*), and the product is left intact. The same
  happens if none of its variant rows carries its `variant_id`.
- **With variant rows, the price goes in `variant_price`**, not in `price`. If
  you write a price in the product row, that product is skipped with the
  warning *«el precio de la fila maestra no se aplica cuando hay filas de
  variante»* (*the master row's price is not applied when there are variant
  rows*).
- For clothing, one row per combination, with `variant_title` («Negro / M»)
  **and** `variant_options` («Color:Negro; Talla:M»).

<Warning>
**What is in charge is the «handle» column, not the name.** It is the grouping
key the template comes with already filled in (something like
`polo-oversize-de-algodon-pima-` followed by a long code). **All the rows with
the same handle are a single product.** If you copy a row to create another
product and leave the same handle, the product you expect is not born: the two
rows are treated as one thing, and one is ignored or overwrites the other.

To really create one: leave the **id** empty and put in a clearly new
**handle**, for example with `-copia` at the end. Do not use the product's bare
name, in lowercase with hyphens: it is usually the handle the original already
has stored, and you would overwrite it. A row without a handle is not accepted
— it is skipped with *«Falta el handle (clave de agrupación).»* (*The handle
(grouping key) is missing.*)

From the **Añadir producto** button this does not happen: two products with the
same name created there are two different products.
</Warning>

<Note>
**What the file does not change.** **Photos are not edited by CSV** (only from
the product editor), nor is an info product's **automatic delivery**. And **the
stock quantity is exported but ignored on import**: you can write «40» in the
sheet, upload it, and nothing will happen and you will see no error. Stock is
moved in **Inventario**. What the file does declare is *whether* the product is
tracked, in the `track_inventory` column.
</Note>

| In the file | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `id` | The product identifier. With an id, it updates. Always keep it on products that already exist. |
| `handle` | The grouping key. **It is the one that decides which rows are the same product.** |
| `product_type` | `fisico`, `servicio` or `infoproducto` (physical product, service or info product). Left empty, it does not touch the type (and a new product is born physical). |
| `variant_id` | The identifier of each variant. It is what lets the variant be updated in place. |
| `price_tiers` | The quantity pricing scale, like `1:79; 2:149; 3:209`. The open tier goes at the end, like `6+:65`. Leaving it blank does not delete it. |

<Tip>
**There is no «Duplicar» (*Duplicate*) button.** If you do not want to fill in
the form again, the shortcut is the template: download the file, copy the
product's rows, **delete their id and give all of them the same new handle**
(for example, the one they come with plus `-copia` at the end), and upload it
again. The copy arrives **without photos**, without **automatic delivery** and
with no funnels ticked (all your agents sell it). You complete that afterwards
with **Editar**.
</Tip>

## How your catalog reaches the customer

There are two different routes, and it is worth not mixing them up.

### 1. In the conversation (the important one)

When the customer asks about a product or its price, the agent searches the
catalog and answers with what it finds. It searches by **the product name** and
by **the name or the code (SKU) of its variants**. Useful things to know:

- **It does not search inside the description**, although it does show it to
  the agent (trimmed) once it has found the product.
- It has to match **at least half the words**. And one- or two-letter words —
  «S», «M», «XL» — have to stand on their own to count.
- **It returns at most 5 products.** If you have forty similar items, the agent
  will name a few, not the whole list.
- The variant's color and size help to **narrow down among what it already
  found**, not to find something that did not match by name or by code.
- When the agent looks up a product with the quantity the customer asked for,
  it receives **the total already calculated**, with the scale and the
  promotion if there are any.

Translated into a recommendation: **the product name has to be the name your
customer would ask for it by.**

### 2. In the native WhatsApp catalog

It is the products tab WhatsApp shows inside your business profile. It fills
itself, but with conditions:

- Your number has to be a **WhatsApp Business account** and be connected. If it
  is not, nothing is published there (and the agent carries on selling over
  chat all the same).
- **One entry is published per variant**, not per product. That is why the
  12-combination T-shirt appears 12 times, each with the product's main photo.
- Only the products that are **both «Activo» and «Visible en el catálogo»** get
  in, and only if they have a price and a currency.
- With quantity pricing, the 1-unit **list** price is published, not the
  promotion.
- If you ticked funnels on the product, each number publishes its own. **A
  product with no funnels ticked — which is the normal case — is published by
  all your numbers.**

<Note>
**Deactivating or hiding also deletes it from WhatsApp.** It is not left
hanging there. And do not worry: only the products the dashboard published are
touched; the ones you had created by hand in WhatsApp stay as they are.
</Note>

### If the playbook says a different price

Your agent takes prices from the catalog, but also from the text of its
[sales playbook](/en/guides/sales-playbook) and from the files in the
[Library](/en/guides/library) it reads to know how to answer (a price list in
PDF, for example). If the playbook says "the Polo oversize de algodón pima costs
S/ 89" and the catalog has S/ 79, the agent can contradict itself in front of
the customer.

That is what the playbook's **Doctor** is for: when a line of the playbook puts
a price near the full name of a catalog product (or its SKU) and it does not
match, it warns you. If there is only one possible price, it offers to fix it
in one click with the **Poner S/ 79 (precio del catálogo)** (*Set S/ 79
(catalog price)*) button; if the product has several variants, it shows you
their prices and you decide. Products with quantity pricing are not compared.
See [The Doctor](/en/guides/sales-playbook#the-doctor).

<Tip>
The healthiest approach is **not to write prices in the playbook**: leave them
in the catalog and let the agent look them up there.
</Tip>

## Things you are going to need later

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Editing a product that already exists">
    In the actions column of its row, click **Editar** (*Edit*). **Clicking the
    row does not open the product** — in this table it only opens with that
    action — so if you click on it and it does not open, it is not broken: use
    the action.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Withdrawing a product without deleting it (recommended)">
    Tick the checkbox on its row and click **Desactivar** (*Deactivate*) in the
    bar that appears at the top. The product stays in your catalog with all its
    history, but the agent stops finding it.

    In that same bar (*«N seleccionado(s)»*, *N selected*) you have the four
    bulk actions — **Activar** (*Activate*), **Desactivar** (*Deactivate*),
    **Mostrar** (*Show*) and **Ocultar** (*Hide*) — which apply straight away to
    everything you have ticked. It is the quick way to withdraw a whole season.
    **Limpiar** (*Clear*) removes the selection.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Really deleting a product">
    There is no trash can and no delete button inside the product panel. From
    the dashboard you delete it like this: **tick the checkbox on the row and
    click «Eliminar»** (*Delete*, in red) in the selection bar.

    <Warning>
    **That button does not ask for confirmation and cannot be undone.** One
    click and the product disappears with its variants; it is not archived.
    Having orders or sales already recorded does **not** stop it: the product is
    deleted anyway and those records lose their link to it. That is why, almost
    always, what you want is **Desactivar**, not **Eliminar**.
    </Warning>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Finding something in a large catalog">
    Two tools work: the **«Buscar productos...»** (*Search products...*) search
    box (it searches by product name and by the SKU of its variants; it does
    not search the description) and the **Filtrar** (*Filter*) button, which
    opens **«Filtrar catálogo»** (*Filter catalog*) with three filters: **Tipo**
    (*Type*), **Visibilidad** (*Visibility*: Visible / Oculto, *Hidden*) and
    **Estado** (*Status*: Activo / Inactivo, *Active / Inactive*).

    The list loads **50 at a time** as you scroll down, with no page numbers,
    and at the foot it says **«Mostrando N de M productos»**.

    <Warning>
    **Two controls on this screen do nothing today, and we would rather tell
    you.** The **Todos · Manual · Shopify** (*All · Manual · Shopify*) tabs do
    not filter: switching tabs does not change what you see. And the
    **Ordenar** (*Sort*) button opens its box with «Nombre», «Precio»,
    «Inventario» and «Actualizado» (*Name, Price, Inventory, Updated*), but the
    list does not change order: it comes out from the most recent product to
    the oldest (the column headers do not sort either). Use the search box and
    **Filtrar**, which do work.
    </Warning>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Who on my team can touch the catalog?">
    **Anyone with access to the Ventas app.** Inside Ventas there are no finer
    permissions for the catalog: that person can create, edit, hide and also
    **delete** products, with the **Eliminar** button that does not ask for
    confirmation. Anyone without Ventas does not see the catalog.

    Each person's access is granted in **Administración → Personas y accesos**.
    See [Invite your team](/en/getting-started/invite-your-team).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Who sees my product photos?">
    Photos are stored in a private space belonging to your company, not at a
    public internet address. The dashboard shows them to you with temporary
    links that expire on their own.

    What is public is what you publish: **the main photo of each product that
    goes into the WhatsApp catalog can be seen by anyone who opens your
    business profile**. If a photo must not be seen by anyone outside, do not
    make it the main one on an active, visible product.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Things this screen does not have">
    So that you do not go looking for them: **there are no categories, no tags,
    no weight, and no "was price / struck-through price"**. There is no
    **duplicate** button (it is done by CSV), **no undo** and **no trash can**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## The same thing, from your assistant

<Note>
This section is **optional and only applies if you connected an AI assistant**
(Claude, ChatGPT, Codex or another compatible one) to your business. If you
work in the dashboard, you are already done.
</Note>

Your assistant can read the whole catalog, load it in bulk from a file you hand
it, and correct it. With the link the dashboard gives you (**Personalizar →
Asistentes IA → Copiar link**, *Customize → AI assistants → Copy link*, the same
one as in the connection guides), your assistant can already **read and write**
the catalog, and also **delete products**. If you connected with the base
address, with nothing after it, it can only read the catalog: to write, you
need to add `catalog_write`. See [The connection URL](/en/reference/connection-url).
And, as in the dashboard, your user has to have access to the **Ventas** app.

| Tool | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `list_products` | A paginated list. It filters by text, visibility, active and type. |
| `get_product` | The complete record of a product: description, variants, images and price scale. |
| `upsert_products` | Creates or updates up to 100 products per call. **Destructive.** |
| `delete_product` | Deletes a product. **Final, with no trash can.** |

**The rule that avoids disasters:** when writing, each product is a **complete
row that replaces the one that was there** — whatever is not sent may be left
empty. That is why the right order is to read the record, change only your
part, send it back whole and read it again to check. The system helps quite a
bit: it rejects incomplete rows before touching anything.

<Warning>
**Two things that really can cost you dearly from the assistant.**

1. When updating a product with variants you have to send **all** of them,
   **each one with its identifier**. A variant that is not sent **is deleted**,
   and one sent without its identifier comes in as new and the old one is
   deleted — the orders and stock that pointed at it are left with no
   reference. There is a safety net: on a product with several variants, if
   none arrives with its identifier, or if the assistant says the product has
   no variants, that row is rejected without touching anything.
2. A product **with no identifier** is looked up by name: a name equal to that
   of an existing one **overwrites it**. Ask your assistant to update using the
   identifier.
</Warning>

And three limits worth keeping in mind:

- **Photos are not touched** from the assistant: uploading a batch does not
  delete or change your products' images. They are managed in the dashboard.
- **Nor is the stock quantity.** Even if your assistant sends a number, it is
  not written: it lives in Inventario. What it can declare from outside is
  *whether* the product is stock-tracked.
- **The price scale** stays as it is if the assistant does not mention it, and
  is deleted if you ask it to remove it.

Phrases that work:

- *"List my catalog before writing anything, so as not to duplicate."*
- *"Raise the price of the Polo oversize de algodón pima to 89 soles. Read its
  record first and send the whole row back with that single change."*
- *"Set the Combo Verano to inactive: read its record, change only that and
  send the complete row back."*
- *"Open three of the ones you just uploaded and show me the price and currency
  of each variant."*

<Tip>
That last phrase matters. In the listing, the price you see is **the cheapest**
among the variants and the code is that same variant's, so a batch that
uploaded the wrong currency on a single variant **can look correct from a
distance**.
</Tip>
