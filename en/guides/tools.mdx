Your agent does not only write. In the middle of a conversation it **does
things**: it looks up the price in your catalog, sends a photo, saves the
customer's district, schedules a follow-up for tomorrow, records the sale and,
when the time comes, hands the chat to a person. Each of those actions is a
**tool**.

This guide lists them, says what each one needs in order to work and shows how
they are asked for from the playbook.

<Note>
**Two different things are called «tool» in this documentation.** The ones on
this page are **the agent's**: the actions it carries out while talking to a
customer on WhatsApp. The ones in the
[Tool reference](/en/reference/tools) are **the bridge's**: what Claude,
ChatGPT or Codex can do on your business if you connect them. They are separate
lists and do not mix.
</Note>

## Three things worth knowing first

**1. Almost all of them come ready.** They are available to every agent from
day one. The rest depend on something you prepare: a catalog with products, a
saved location, an active app, Slack connected or a switch in the agent's
**Configuración** (*Settings*). The table below says so, tool by tool.

**2. There are two families, and they are asked for differently.** Some **are
written into the playbook**, at the exact point of the script where they must
happen («record the sale here», «if they do not reply, remind them tomorrow»).
Others **the agent uses on its own** when the conversation calls for it —
looking up a price, sending the location, checking the calendar — and **are not
named in the playbook**: if you write their technical name in a chapter, the
Doctor asks you to delete it.

**3. You can see every time one ran.** In the chat thread, each action leaves a
chip with a lightning bolt (⚡), the tool's name and how it ended. In
**Resultados** (*Results*) there is a number that sums it up: **Éxito de
herramientas** (*Tool success*). More below, in
[How to know whether it worked](#how-to-know-whether-it-worked).

## Every tool at a glance

| Tool | What it does | How it is asked for | What it needs |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Avanzar de capítulo** (*Advance chapter*) | Moves the conversation to another chapter of the funnel. | Written into the playbook (`@` menu → Capítulos). | The destination chapter must exist. |
| **Registrar una venta / conversión** (*Record a sale / conversion*) | Records the sale, the appointment, the lead or the service; on a product sale, opens the order. | Written into the playbook. | Nothing. For it to open the order, the **Órdenes** (*Orders*) app. |
| **Activar Modo Manual** (*Activate Manual Mode*) | Switches off autopilot for that chat: the agent goes quiet and a person takes over. | Written into the playbook. The agent also uses it on its own if the customer asks for a person or gets genuinely angry. | Nothing. |
| **Guardar un dato** (*Save a piece of data*) | Saves what the customer said (name, district, size…) to their record. | Written into the playbook. | The variable must exist. |
| **Leer un dato del cliente** (*Read a customer's data*) | Re-reads a piece of data already saved so as not to ask for it again. | Written into the playbook. | Nothing. |
| **Recordatorio / seguimiento** (*Reminder / follow-up*) | Schedules a message to pick the conversation up later. | Written into the playbook. The agent also uses it on its own when the customer agrees on a day. | Nothing. |
| **Programar una acción** (*Schedule an action*) | Schedules a task for later: send something, save a piece of data, change chapter. | Written into the playbook. | Nothing. |
| **Registrar un referido** (*Register a referral*) | Records the person your customer recommends to you and sets your referral program in motion. | Written into the playbook. | The referred person's WhatsApp with its country code, and your WhatsApp connected. |
| **Avisar a un tercero** (*Notify a third party*) | Sends a WhatsApp message to your team or to another person, outside the chat with the customer. | Written into the playbook. | Whom to notify: **«Equipo de ventas»** (*Sales team*), a contact from **Números frecuentes** (*Frequent numbers*) or a number with its country code. |
| **Consultar conocimiento** (*Consult knowledge*) | Searches what the agent studied (policies, warranties, frequently asked questions). | Written into the playbook; the agent also uses it on its own. | Files with the **Memoria del agente** (*Agent memory*) permission, or your **Memoria a Largo Plazo** (*Long-Term Memory*). |
| **Enviar un archivo** (*Send a file*) | Sends the customer a photo, a PDF or an audio from the Library. | Written into the playbook with a marker. | The file with the **Enviar por WhatsApp** (*Send via WhatsApp*) permission. |
| **Buscar en el catálogo** (*Search the catalog*) | Gives the exact price and the total per quantity. | Used on its own. | Active products in the catalog. |
| **Enviar la ubicación** (*Send the location*) | Sends your premises as a WhatsApp map pin. | Used on its own. | A location saved under **Librería → Ubicaciones** (*Library → Locations*). |
| **Consultar stock** (*Check stock*) | Checks how many units there really are before promising them. | Used on its own. | The **Inventario** (*Inventory*) app, its switch in **Configuración**, and a catalog. |
| **Citas** (*Appointments*: see the calendar, book, move, cancel) | Four tools for booking in your calendar. | Used on their own. | The **Calendario** (*Calendar*) app, with your services and opening hours loaded. |
| **Contar cómo van sus referidos** (*Tell them how their referrals are doing*) | Answers «how are my referrals doing?» with the customer's real figures. | Used on its own. | Nothing. |
| **Anotar que no quiere recomendar** (*Note that they do not want to refer*) | Records a clear «no» to passing on contacts, so the agent does not insist again. | Used on its own. | Nothing. |
| **Avisar al equipo por Slack** (*Notify the team on Slack*) | Posts a sale, a hot lead or a handoff to Slack. | Used on its own. | Slack connected under **Personalizar → Canales** (*Customize → Channels*). |
| **Video del avatar** (*Avatar video*) | Generates a short video of the agent's persona saying a text. | Asked for in prose. | A face with a voice set up. |
| **Your own tools (MCP)** | Whatever you connect yourself: your ERP, your booking system, your CRM. | Written into the playbook (**Otra herramienta** — *Other tool*). | An MCP server under **Personalizar → Herramientas** (*Customize → Tools*). |

<Note>
**The voice note is switched off.** There is a tool that turns text into audio
with the agent's voice, but today it is disabled. If an old playbook asks to
«reply with a voice note», the agent replies with text. The audio files **you
upload** to the Library are sent, like any other file.
</Note>

## How they are written into the playbook

A playbook tool is asked for with **one exact sentence**, on the line of the
script where it must happen. You do not need to type it: in the
[Playbook](/en/guides/sales-playbook#the-menu-mentioning-things) editor, type
**`@`** and choose an entry from the **Herramientas** (*Tools*) group. There are
ten: **Recordatorio / seguimiento**, **Programar una acción**, **Registrar un
referido**, **Avisar a un tercero**, **Consultar conocimiento (RAG)**, **Leer un
dato del cliente**, **Guardar un dato**, **Activar Modo Manual**, **Registrar
una venta / conversión** and **Otra herramienta**.

<Frame caption="In the @ menu, the Herramientas (Tools) group gathers the ones that are written into the playbook.">
  <img src="/images/herramientas/01-menu-herramientas.png" alt="The playbook editor's @ menu with the search box and the Herramientas (Tools) group: in view, from Recordatorio / seguimiento (Reminder / follow-up) to Activar Modo Manual (Activate Manual Mode); the rest further down the list" />
</Frame>

If the tool takes data, a short form opens to ask for it. Press **Insertar**
(*Insert*) and the sentence is written correctly and **tinted** like a mention.
A click on a tool already written lets you change it: if it takes data, its form
opens with the data inside (change them and press **Guardar** — *Save*), and in
every case you have **✕ Quitar mención** (*✕ Remove mention*) to delete it.

The sentence left in the text has this shape (in Spanish, as the editor writes
it):

```
Ejecuta la herramienta "Recordatorio Tool" con el query "en 24 horas recuérdame: retomar con el descuento"
```

And two of them go without data, as they are:

```
Ejecuta la herramienta "Activar Modo Manual Tool"
```

```
Ejecuta la herramienta "Memoria A Largo Plazo Tool"
```

<Warning>
**Written in prose, it is not a tool.** «Record the sale», «schedule a reminder
for tomorrow» or «hand it to a human», written without the sentence, are text
the agent reads as a suggestion: the editor does not recognise them and the
Doctor cannot check them. The same goes if someone wrote a tool's technical name
on its own: the Doctor flags it with **«… está escrita sin formato de
herramienta»** (*… is written without tool format*) and, if there is an
equivalent, offers to fix it with one click.
</Warning>

Three rules of the form that prevent broken sentences:

- **Reminder, Schedule an action, Register a referral and Notify a third party
  require the data.** Without it, the **Insertar** button stays disabled: a
  sentence with an empty slot would be of no use. In Notify a third party, also
  fill in **A quién** (*To whom*): with no destination, the agent does not know
  whom to notify.
- **Activate Manual Mode and Consult knowledge take no data.** The handoff
  notifies on Slack, if you connected it (more below, in
  [Activate Manual Mode](#activate-manual-mode)), and the agent decides on its
  own what to look up.
- **The data goes inside the sentence, not next to it.** If the Doctor tells you
  that a tool **«tiene sus parámetros sueltos»** (*has its parameters loose*),
  someone typed the delay or the destination by hand outside the quotes; accept
  the fix it proposes.

Everything that happens around the script — the PASOS (*steps*), the §
sections, the Doctor — is in [The sales playbook](/en/guides/sales-playbook).
Here goes only what concerns each tool.

## The ones written into the playbook

### Advance chapter

It is the most important tool in the product, which is why it has its own
section in the playbook guide:
[Making the sales funnel advance](/en/guides/sales-playbook#making-the-sales-funnel-advance).
In short: it is inserted with **`@` → Capítulos** (*Chapters*) and the literal
sentence is `Llama a la herramienta advance_chapter con el capítulo 3`. Writing
«move on to the next one» in prose is not the way to do it: the advance lives in
that line.

### Record a sale / conversion

In the playbook it appears as **«Registrar Conversion Tool»**, and its data is
**what gets recorded**: `venta` (sale), `cita` (appointment), `lead calificado`
(qualified lead) or `servicio` (service).

```
PASO 5: Cuando el cliente confirme el pedido y la dirección, Ejecuta la herramienta "Registrar Conversion Tool" con el query "venta"
```

What it does when it runs:

- **A sale is not recorded without an amount.** The agent takes it from the
  catalog (price per quantity, plus shipping if you charge it). It is what the
  order is **worth**, not what has already been collected: a cash-on-delivery
  sale with no advance is recorded all the same, at the product's price.
- **On a product sale, it opens the order.** With the **Órdenes** app active,
  that same call creates the conversation's order with its lines, the agreed
  payment plan (all upfront, cash on delivery, or advance plus balance), the
  shipping cost and the address. If the chat already had an open order, **it
  replaces it** with the final lines.
- **It does not charge anything.** Payment is confirmed when your team verifies
  the receipt. See [Orders and metrics](/en/guides/orders-and-metrics).
- **It notifies you.** If in the
  [agent's settings](/en/guides/agent-settings), under **Informar de las
  ventas** (*Report sales*), you chose whom to notify, every sale arrives
  instantly on WhatsApp with a 💰: the product, the total and the customer data
  the agent captured. It notifies sales and services; appointments and leads,
  no. If you connected Slack, it also goes to the channel you chose for
  **Conversión** (*Conversion*).
- **It does not count the same sale twice.** If within the following 24 hours
  the agent records a sale again in the same chat, the system takes it as the
  same one: it does not add another sale or notify you again. If that chat's
  order is still **Por cobrar** (*Awaiting payment*) or in **Pago parcial**
  (*Partial payment*), it rewrites it with the new lines; if it was already
  **Pagado** (*Paid*) or further along, a separate order is opened. After 24
  hours, a sale in that chat does count as another purchase.

<Tip>
**Put it once, in the closing chapter.** It is the source of your sales numbers
and of **Resultados**. If you repeat it in chapters that are reached days later
(a follow-up, an after-sale), a conversation that comes back can add a second
sale.
</Tip>

### Activate Manual Mode

In the playbook it appears as **«Activar Modo Manual Tool»** and is written
without data. When it runs, it **switches off autopilot** for that
conversation: the agent stops replying and the conversation waits for a person,
just as if you switched it off yourself from the inbox, as described in
[Taking over the conversation yourself](/en/guides/conversations#taking-over-the-conversation-yourself).

The agent uses it in these situations:

| When | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **A step of the current chapter orders it** (the typical case: order taken, delivery left to coordinate). | Switches autopilot off. This is the normal use of writing it into the playbook. |
| **A shared § section orders it for the case the customer has just raised** (for example: «if it is a wholesale order, notify the team and activate manual mode»). | Switches autopilot off in that case. |
| **The customer's last message asks to talk to a person**, or expresses real anger. | Switches autopilot off even if the playbook does not say so. |
| **The customer asks something the playbook does not cover.** | **Does not switch autopilot off.** The agent keeps serving and, if you have Slack connected, notifies your team that this piece of information was missing. In the thread you will see it as «bloqueado por política» (*blocked by policy*). |

<Warning>
**Switching it off leaves the customer without replies from the agent.** From
that moment on, that conversation waits for a person: autopilot is switched back
on from the dashboard or, from the phone of that line, by repeating the first
word of the message («ok ok…»), as described in
[Taking over the conversation yourself](/en/guides/conversations#taking-over-the-conversation-yourself).
That is why the agent is ordered not to switch it off «by inertia»: because the
history mentions an earlier handoff, because it offered one itself before or
because the customer asks about their data. If the customer says they would
rather carry on with the agent, it carries on with the agent.
</Warning>

Each handoff notifies on Slack, in the channel you chose for **Te toca
atender** (*Your turn to serve*), if you have it connected, and stays in the
thread as an **Activar Modo Manual** chip. **Without Slack, the handoff does not
notify your team at that moment:** the conversation stays in your inbox with the
**Manual** mark. If you want your team to find out on WhatsApp, write a
**Notify a third party** in the same STEP, before the handoff (for example, to
«Equipo de ventas»).

While the conversation is in manual mode, reminders and scheduled actions that
come due are cancelled instead of going out.

### Save a piece of data · Read a customer's data

In the playbook they appear as **«Actualizar Datos Tool»** and **«Leer Datos
Tool»**; the data is **what to save** or **what to read**:

```
PASO 2: Pregunta a qué distrito se envía. Ejecuta la herramienta "Actualizar Datos Tool" con el query "distrito"
```

What it saves goes to the customer's record, the one you see under
[«Datos capturados»](/en/guides/conversations#datos-capturados-captured-data-what-the-agent-knows-about-that-customer)
(*Captured data*), and is added to what was already there.

<Warning>
**It only saves variables that exist.** The tool does not create new fields: if
the playbook asks it to save «talla» (size) and the record has no «talla»
variable, it saves nothing. Create the variable first, in **Clientes**
(*Customers*, **Tabla** view — *Table*). From the `@` menu you can start it with
**＋ Crear variable** (*＋ Create variable*), but it stays as a draft until you
create it in Clientes. And if it is an options variable (a closed list), the
agent will only be able to save one of those values.
</Warning>

**Read a piece of data** exists so as not to repeat questions: if the customer
already gave their name in chapter 1, chapter 4 can re-read it instead of asking
for it. Since the agent already receives the customer's record with every
message, you will need it rarely; it is useful when you want to make it explicit
in the script that it **must** use that piece of data.

### Reminder / follow-up

In the playbook it appears as **«Recordatorio Tool»** and its data is **when
and what**. It is written in two ways, depending on who supplies the words.

**A note for the agent**, which is the normal case. When the delay is up, the
agent **writes** the message from that note, with the whole context of the
chat, without telling the customer there was a reminder. If by then there is
nothing useful left to say, it may send nothing:

```
PASO 3: Si no responde, Ejecuta la herramienta "Recordatorio Tool" con el query "en 24 horas recuérdame: retomar preguntando si tuvo dudas con el precio"
```

**An exact text for the customer.** If after the delay you write **«dile
exactamente esto:»** (*tell them exactly this:*) (or «dile esto:», or «dile al
cliente:») followed by the text, when it comes due it is sent **as is**, word
for word, without the AI rewriting it:

```
PASO 3: Si no responde, Ejecuta la herramienta "Recordatorio Tool" con el query "en 24 horas dile exactamente esto: Hola, ¿pudiste ver el catálogo? Si quieres, te separo tu talla."
```

Three things worth knowing about the exact text:

- **It carries a colon.** «dile al cliente que confirme la fecha» (*tell the
  customer to confirm the date*) is an instruction for the agent; «dile al
  cliente: ¿confirmamos la fecha?» (*tell the customer: shall we confirm the
  date?*) is an exact text.
- **It goes out with nothing added.** No greeting is added to it: if you want a
  «hola», write it yourself.
- **If, when it comes due, there is a customer message still unanswered**, the
  agent answers it normally instead of sending the text.

And for both cases:

- **The agent keeps a single reminder of its own per conversation.** When it
  schedules another, the previous one it had scheduled is cancelled. That is why
  a follow-up cadence is **chained** — each follow-up schedules the next —
  rather than stacked. The reminders you schedule yourself from the chat's
  record are not touched.
- **If you give no delay, it is scheduled for 24 hours by default.**
- **It can be cancelled if the customer writes first.** For a «re-engagement» of
  someone who does not answer, that makes sense; for a «call me on Monday», it
  does not. The agent decides from the meaning of the text.
- **It can carry files.** In the form, type `@` inside the text and choose a
  file from the Library: a marker is left at that point of the message and the
  file is attached when it comes due, not now. Up to 4 per reminder.
- **It respects your «No molestar» (*Do not disturb*) window.** If it comes due
  inside the window you set in **Configuración** (from the factory, from
  midnight to 6 in the morning, in the agent's time), it waits for the window to
  end and goes out within the following hour.
- **If more than 8 hours have passed since the agent's last message, it greets
  first** (only in the note case). A reminder that comes due the next morning
  opens with a «good morning» and only then picks up where it left off.
- **If the conversation is in manual mode when it comes due, it is cancelled**
  instead of going out: a person is serving and the agent does not cut across.
- **If your WhatsApp is disconnected when it comes due, it waits.** It is
  retried and goes out if the line comes back in less than 6 hours; if not, it
  expires.

There is one reminder the agent schedules **without the playbook asking for
it**: when the customer agrees on a day («I'll pay you by Yape on Monday», «I'll
pay on the 15th», «I'll confirm at 6»). It is set for that day, at the time the
customer said or, if they did not say a time, at 10 in the morning, in the
agent's time zone. A day of the week means the next one: if today is Monday and
they say «on Monday», it is next week's. That reminder replaces the follow-ups
and actions the agent had scheduled in that chat, and the agent is ordered to
leave it standing even if the customer writes first. If the agent later
schedules another reminder, that one replaces it, as happens with any reminder
of its own.

Each chat's reminders are seen in the conversation, on the **Ficha** (*Record*)
tab, sections **Recordatorios** (*Reminders*) and **Acciones programadas**
(*Scheduled actions*), each one with its time and a **Cancelar** (*Cancel*)
button.

### Schedule an action

In the playbook it appears as **«Programar Accion Tool»** and its data is **the
delay and the task**:

```
PASO 4: Ejecuta la herramienta "Programar Accion Tool" con el query "en 48 horas: envía el archivo ###SEND_FILES: descuento_final### y pasa al capítulo 6"
```

It resembles the reminder, but it is not the same thing:

| | Reminder | Scheduled action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **What for** | Talking again to someone who went quiet. | Carrying out a concrete task at a given time: send something, change chapter, save a piece of data. |
| **How many at once** | One of the agent's own: the new one cancels the previous one. | Up to **10** active per conversation, without cancelling each other. |
| **What it carries** | A note on what to pick up, or an exact text. | The task, which the agent will carry out as written. |

If the task changes chapter, it can also say **from which STEP** of that chapter
to continue. With no delay, it is also 24 hours. And like the reminder, it
respects the «No molestar» window, it is cancelled if the conversation is in
manual mode when it comes due, and the agent does not announce to the customer
that it is scheduling something.

### Register a referral

In the playbook it appears as **«Registrar Referido Tool»** and its data is
**whom the customer recommends to you**: the name, their WhatsApp with its
country code and whatever context the customer gives (where they live, what they
do). The agent calls it **once per person** the customer shares, whether as a
WhatsApp contact card or by typing the number:

```
PASO 3: Si no tiene lo que buscamos, pregúntale si conoce a alguien que sí. Por cada persona que comparta, Ejecuta la herramienta "Registrar Referido Tool" con el query "el nombre y WhatsApp de la persona referida, y lo que el cliente cuente de ella"
```

What happens next is up to you, in **Red de referidos → Programa** (*Referral
network → Program*), under **«¿Quién escribe primero?»** (*Who writes first?*):

- **El cliente lo presenta** (*The customer introduces them*, the factory
  setting): the agent gives your customer, in that same chat and in a separate
  message, an introduction ready to forward, with your agent's WhatsApp number
  as text (if that number is not saved for your line, the introduction goes out
  without it and the agent asks your customer to pass it on to their
  acquaintance). The referred person writes to you themselves: your number does
  not write to the referred person first.
- **El agente le escribe primero** (*The agent writes first*): your WhatsApp
  sends the referred person a single message, during the day (from 8:00 to 20:00
  in the agent's time and outside your «No molestar» window), with a cap on
  referrals per day to look after your number.

The introduction text, the attachments, the chapter the referred person enters
through, the rewards and the automatic notices are configured in
[The referral network](/en/guides/referral-network).

The referred person's record gets **Referido por** (*Referred by*), with the
name of whoever recommended them, and, if the customer said something about that
person, **Contexto del referido** (*Referral context*).

Rules the agent already knows:

- **Someone who already wrote to this agent does not count.** If that number
  already talked with your funnel, the agent tells the customer kindly and
  records nothing.
- **If the same customer passes you again someone they already recommended**,
  that person still counts for them.
- **The first one wins.** If another customer had already recommended that
  number, the new registration does not count. The exception: if in Programa you
  set **«Un referido vale (días)»** (*A referral is valid for (days)*), that
  period passed without a deposit and that person has not written to you yet,
  the recommendation passes to the new customer and Referidos marks it **«Pasó
  a otro cliente»** (*Passed to another customer*).
- **Nobody recommends themselves**, and the agent does not invent or complete
  numbers: if one arrives without a country code, it asks the customer for it.

<Note>
**When registering a referral, the agent is ordered not to talk about
rewards.** What the customer is told about money is down to the notices in
**Red de referidos → Avisos** (*Notices*), such as **«Cuando su referido da el
adelanto»** (*When their referral pays the deposit*) or **«Cuando le pagas»**
(*When you pay them*), which you switch on, switch off and write. In «El cliente
lo presenta» mode, two more also come switched on from the factory: **«Si su
conocido no escribe»** (*If their acquaintance does not write*: after 3 days it
asks your customer whether they managed to forward the introduction) and
**«Cuando su conocido escribe»** (*When their acquaintance writes*: it thanks
them for the recommendation). And if the customer asks how their referrals are
doing, the agent answers with their real figures: see
[Tell them how their referrals are doing](#tell-them-how-their-referrals-are-doing).
</Note>

### Notify a third party

In the playbook it appears as **«Enviar Mensaje A Tercero Tool»**. It sends a
WhatsApp message from the agent's number to another person — your sales team,
the warehouse manager — outside the conversation with the customer.

The `@` menu form asks you for two things:

- **A quién** (*To whom*). Press **＋** or type `@` in that field and choose from
  the list: **Equipo de ventas** (your **Informar de las ventas** list) or one
  of your **Números frecuentes**, which show up as «name · number». You can
  choose several. Writing a number with its country code also works.
- **Qué avisar** (*What to say*). The notice itself: «the customer wants an
  appointment», «there is a new order to coordinate the delivery».

<Frame caption="The «Avisar a un tercero» (Notify a third party) form, with «A quién» (To whom) still empty: there you press ＋ or type @ to choose from the list, or write a number.">
  <img src="/images/herramientas/02-avisar-a-un-tercero.png" alt="Avisar a un tercero (Notify a third party) form with the empty A quién (To whom) field and its ＋ button, the note Para avisar a otras personas por su nombre, guárdalas en Ajustes → Números frecuentes (To notify other people by name, save them in Settings → Frequent numbers), the notice el cliente quiere comprar al por mayor (the customer wants to buy wholesale) and the Volver (Back) and Insertar (Insert) buttons" />
</Frame>

When you choose a contact, the sentence keeps **their name in «» quotes**, not
their number:

```
PASO 6: Ejecuta la herramienta "Enviar Mensaje A Tercero Tool" con el query "Dile a «Equipo de ventas» que hay un pedido nuevo para coordinar la entrega"
```

You can mix several destinations («Dile a «Equipo de ventas» y «Almacén»
que…»): nobody receives the same notice twice. And a bare number still works:

```
PASO 6: Ejecuta la herramienta "Enviar Mensaje A Tercero Tool" con el query "Dile al número 51987654321 que hay un pedido nuevo para coordinar la entrega"
```

**Better by name than by number.** The number lives in the
[agent's settings](/en/guides/agent-settings), not in the playbook text: if you
change salesperson, you change it there and the playbook is not touched. If in
**Números frecuentes** you rename a contact that a notice names, without
changing its number, the notice in the playbook renames itself (a new version is
left in the **Historial** — *History*). And if you remove from the list a
contact the playbook uses, when you press **Guardar cambios** (*Save changes*)
the Configuración is not saved and it warns you: change it in the playbook
first.

<Frame caption="In Configuración (Settings), «Informar de las ventas» (Report sales) is the list the notices call «Equipo de ventas» (Sales team); it is filled from «Elegir de mis números frecuentes…» (Choose from my frequent numbers…), here still empty.">
  <img src="/images/configuracion/02-numeros.png" alt="Configuración (Settings) screen with the Informar de las ventas (Report sales) and Números de prueba (Test numbers) sections, with no numbers set, each with the Elegir de mis números frecuentes… (Choose from my frequent numbers…) selector, and the start of Reportes (Reports)" />
</Frame>

What reaches that person: the notice, with the customer's WhatsApp at the end so
they can write to them. If the playbook asks for it, the agent can also attach
the conversation with the customer.

<Warning>
**A destination that cannot be found receives nothing.** If the playbook names
someone who is not in your Números frecuentes (or who has no number), the
notice does not go out and the Doctor flags it as an error: **«El aviso a «…»
no sale: no está en tus Números frecuentes o le falta el número»** (*The notice
to «…» does not go out: they are not in your Frequent numbers or their number is
missing*) (the Doctor and some dashboard notices call the Configuración
«Ajustes»). If it names **«Equipo de ventas»** and your **Informar de las
ventas** list is empty, the notice does not go out either, and there the Doctor
does not warn you: check that list in the Configuración.

**A bare number goes with its country code, joined up and without `+`.**
`51987654321`, not `987654321` or `+51 987 654 321`. If the playbook carries the
number in local format, the tool **rejects it** and the notice does not go out;
and the agent is ordered not to complete or correct it on its own. What has to
be fixed is the playbook: the Doctor flags it too.
</Warning>

### Consult knowledge

In the playbook it appears as **«Memoria A Largo Plazo Tool»** and is written
without data. It searches what the agent **studied**:

- the files that in the Library have the **Memoria del agente** permission
  (policies, warranties, shipping times, spec sheets; see
  [The file Library](/en/guides/library)), and
- the **Memoria a Largo Plazo** (*Long-Term Memory*) document you write from the
  Playbook itself, if your agent has that button
  ([how it is written](/en/guides/sales-playbook#long-term-memory-rag)).

If that text uses a piece of business data (for example `{{dato:yape}}`), the
agent receives the value you have saved under **Configuración → Datos del
negocio** (*Settings → Business details*).

You do not need to write it for it to work: **the agent is ordered to use it on
its own** when the customer asks something that is neither in the chapter nor in
its data. And if what it finds does not answer the question, it is ordered not
to use it: it tells the customer it will confirm and follows the script. Writing
it into a chapter serves to force it at a specific point («before answering
about warranties, look it up»).

### Send a file

It is not a sentence but a **marker** with the file's nickname, and it has its
own section:
[Sending a file from the script](/en/guides/sales-playbook#sending-a-file-from-the-script).

```
PASO 2: Si pide ver el catálogo, envíalo aquí: ###SEND_FILES: catalogo###
```

The essentials: the file has to be in the Library **with the «Enviar por
WhatsApp» permission** before you name it, and the default nickname is the file
name without its extension. If the marker is missing a `#`, that text may reach
the customer's chat as is; if the nickname is not that of a file the agent can
send, the marker disappears and the message goes out without the attachment.

## The ones the agent uses on its own

These **are not written into the playbook with the tool sentence**. The agent
has them at hand and decides when to call them depending on what the customer
asks. The exception is the avatar video: the agent is ordered to send it only if
the playbook asks for it in prose. If you write their technical name in a
chapter, the Doctor asks you to delete it; what you can do is prepare what they
need.

### Search the catalog

Every time the customer asks about a product or a price, the agent searches your
[catalog](/en/guides/catalog) and answers with the exact price; if the customer
said how many units they want, it receives the total already calculated, with
quantity pricing and promotions. **It is ordered not to invent prices or
calculate totals on its own.**

If your business has no active products in the catalog, the agent does not
receive this tool. The same search gives it each variant's identifier, which is
what **Record a sale** uses to tie the sale to the right product.

### Send the location

When the customer asks where you are or how to get there, the agent sends your
premises as a **WhatsApp map pin**. The address and the point on the map come
from what you saved under **Librería → Ubicaciones** for that agent: the agent
only chooses which premises to send if you have several («the Surco one», «the
warehouse»), and if it does not recognise which one, it sends the first. One pin
goes per reply.

<Frame caption="The premises the agent sends are saved on the Library's Ubicaciones (Locations) tab; here, still empty.">
  <img src="/images/libreria/12-ubicaciones.png" alt="The Library's Ubicaciones (Locations) tab, empty (Aún no hay ubicaciones — No locations yet), with the Nombre, Dirección, Cuándo enviarla, Coordenadas and Acciones (Name, Address, When to send it, Coordinates and Actions) columns and the Agregar ubicación (Add location) button" />
</Frame>

With no locations saved for that agent, there is no pin to send.

### Check stock

Before promising units, large quantities or delivery times, the agent checks how
much there **really** is in the inventory, per variant, and does not offer what
there is not. It does not use it to give prices: that is the catalog's job.

It needs **three things**: the **Inventario** app active in your business, the
**«Consultar stock (Inventario)»** (*Check stock (Inventory)*) switch on in that
agent's **Configuración** (**Herramientas** section — *Tools* — which only
appears with Inventario active) and a catalog with products. If one fails, the
agent does not receive it.

### Appointments: see the calendar, book, move and cancel

With the **Calendario** app active, the agent receives four tools at once:
**check availability** (the bookable services and the real free slots),
**book**, **reschedule** and **cancel** the customer's appointment. It is
ordered not to propose a slot without checking first, and the price, the
duration and the deposit come from your services, not from its head. If the
service carries a deposit, it collects it by payment receipt and your team
confirms it. See
[The DarkFunnels apps](/en/getting-started/the-apps#the-apps-that-exist-today).

For it to really book, load into the Calendario, in that agent's calendar, your
**services** and your **opening hours**: without them the agent does not offer
appointments and tells the customer that someone in charge will coordinate the
time.

### Tell them how their referrals are doing

When a customer asks about their recommendations or their rewards («how are my
referrals doing?», «have I been paid yet?»), the agent checks their real status
and answers in its own words: whom they recommended and whether that person has
already written to you or left their deposit, how many people there are in their
network (in figures only, without names), how much they earned, how much they
were paid and how much is still owed. It does not promise payment dates. If they
have not recommended anyone yet, it tells them so and invites them to pass you a
contact.

### Note that they do not want to refer

If the agent asked the customer for referrals and they say no clearly («I don't
know anyone», «no, thanks»), the agent notes it down. From that moment on, the
referral reminder and the automatic requests from **Red de referidos → Avisos**
no longer reach them. An «I'll think about it» or «I'll pass it on later» does
not count as a no.

### Notify the team on Slack

If you connected Slack under **Personalizar → Canales**, the agent posts to the
channel you chose for each notice: **Conversión** (*Conversion*: when it records
a conversion), **Te toca atender** (*Your turn to serve*: when it hands over a
chat or a piece of information from the playbook is missing) and **Lead
caliente** (*Hot lead*). Without Slack connected, the agent does not notify
there and everything else stays the same: the WhatsApp notices in
**Configuración** (Informar de las ventas, Reportes, Alertas de error) go their
own way.

### Avatar video

If the agent's persona has a **face with a voice** set up, the agent can
generate a short video of the avatar saying a text and send it when it is ready;
it takes a few minutes and the conversation carries on meanwhile. **Each video
spends balance**, unless you use your own key for the video provider (see
[Credits and top-ups](/en/getting-started/credits-and-top-ups)). The agent is
ordered to do it only if the playbook asks for it expressly, and it is asked for
**in prose** («in the greeting, send them a welcome video»). It does not repeat
an identical video in the same conversation. See
[The agent's persona](/en/guides/sales-playbook#the-agent-s-persona).

## Your own tools (MCP)

If your business has a system of its own — an ERP, a booking system, a CRM —
that exposes an **MCP server**, you can connect it and your agent will use it as
one more tool. It is under **Personalizar → Herramientas**: **«Añadir servidor
MCP»** (*Add MCP server*), with a name, the server's URL and, if it asks for
one, its key.

<Frame caption="Under Personalizar → Herramientas (Customize → Tools), «Añadir servidor MCP» (Add MCP server) opens the form to connect your server.">
  <img src="/images/herramientas/03-servidor-mcp.png" alt="Herramientas (Tools) tab of Personalizar (Customize), still with no servers (Aún no hay herramientas MCP — No MCP tools yet), with the Añadir servidor MCP (Add MCP server) button" />
</Frame>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect the server">
    **Personalizar → Herramientas → Añadir servidor MCP.** Write the name, the
    URL and, if your server asks for it, the **API key (opcional)** (*API key
    (optional)*), and press **Guardar**. The card shows its status
    (**Activo** / **Inactivo** — *Active* / *Inactive*) and a **Con
    credencial** (*With credential*) label if you saved the key.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write it into the playbook">
    In the `@` menu → Herramientas, the **«Otra herramienta»** (*Other tool*)
    entry is the generic one for everything that comes from an MCP server. Its
    data is the instruction: what it has to do, naming your system if you have
    several.
    ```
    Ejecuta la herramienta "MCP Tool" con el query "busca en el sistema de reservas la reserva a nombre del cliente"
    ```
    The agent picks, among the tools your server publishes, the one that
    fulfils that instruction.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test it">
    The agent discovers the server's tools on its own; a freshly added one can
    take a few minutes to show up for it. Test it with **Probar** (*Test*) in
    the [Playbook](/en/guides/sales-playbook#testing-the-playbook): under each
    reply you see which tools it used. In a real chat, look at the ⚡ chip to
    see whether it ran and what it returned.
  </Step>
</Steps>

What to keep in mind:

- **Limits:** up to **5 servers** per business and **20 tools** in total.
  Anything beyond that is not loaded.
- **If the server is down, it is skipped.** The agent keeps serving without
  that tool: someone else's server does not bring the conversation down.
- **What your server returns reaches the agent unfiltered.** The agent reads it
  like any other tool result: there is no filter that separates information from
  orders. The platform does not give the agent payment routes of its own
  (payments come in by receipt), but a tool on your server can do whatever your
  system allows: connect only servers you control and that return trustworthy
  data.
- **Use a public address that starts with `https://`.** The dashboard also
  accepts `http://`, but the agent will not use it. Local or private-network
  addresses (`localhost`, `192.168…`) are rejected.

## How to know whether it worked

Every time the agent runs a tool, a chip appears in the chat thread with a
lightning bolt, the tool's name and a word that says how it ended (some tools
show up with their name in English):

| The chip says | What happened |
| --- | --- |
| **ejecutado** (*run*) | The tool ran without a technical error. Careful: some refusals also come out this way, for example a sale that was not recorded because the amount was missing, or a piece of data that was not saved because the variable does not exist. Unfold the chip to read what it returned. |
| **falló** (*failed*) | The tool errored or rejected the data (for example, a number without a country code). The agent kept serving without it. |
| **bloqueado por política** (*blocked by policy*) | The system stopped it on purpose: a handoff without a reason, a question the playbook does not cover (the agent does not switch autopilot off and carries on), one scheduled action too many, a conversation that was already with a person. It does not count as run. |
| **omitido** (*skipped*) | There was nothing to do: the file was not found or had already been sent, the referred person had already written to you, the turn was no longer live. |
| **descartado (turno interrumpido)** (*discarded (turn interrupted)*) | It ran, but the customer wrote over it, the turn was cut off and the customer did not receive that reply. |

If a turn uses several tools in a row, they fold into **a single row** — for
example, «Conversión registrada · Acción programada ×2» (*Conversion recorded ·
Scheduled action ×2*). In that row success goes without a word and only what
went off course is marked (**falló**, **bloqueado**, **omitido**,
**descartado**). Press it to see the chips one by one.

If a chip surprises you, press it: it unfolds with the data the agent passed to
the tool and what the tool returned. It is the fastest way to understand **why**
it did what it did. In the playbook's **Probar** something similar happens:
under each reply you see the tools it used, with «error», «bloqueada»,
«omitida» or «descartada» if something went off course.

The summary of all this lives in **Resultados**, in the **Operativo**
(*Operational*) group: **Éxito de herramientas** says what share of the tool
calls went well. It is explained in
[Orders and metrics](/en/guides/orders-and-metrics#results-what-each-number-really-measures).

## If something is not working

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The notice to a third party reached nobody">
    Look in the chat thread for the notice's chip. If it says **falló**, unfold
    it: the most common cause is a number without a country code or a contact
    that is not in **Números frecuentes**. Also open the playbook's **Doctor**,
    which flags those two cases as errors.

    If you named **«Equipo de ventas»**, check that the **Informar de las
    ventas** list in the Configuración has at least one number.

    If the chip says **ejecutado** and the notice still did not arrive, unfold
    it: if some destination was not valid, it says so there (*«NO avisé a: …»*
    — *I did NOT notify: …*), and if the same notice had already gone out a few
    minutes before, it is not repeated. If it is not that, that person may have
    gone more than 24 hours without writing to your agent's line: in that case
    the sending can be held back to look after your number. Ask them to send a
    message to that line.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A reminder shows as cancelled and nobody cancelled it">
    The possible causes:

    1. **The agent scheduled another one.** The new one replaces its previous
       one; an agreed day also replaces its scheduled actions.
    2. **It was set to be cancelled if the customer replied, and they replied.**
    3. **When it came due, the conversation was in manual mode.**
    4. **The turn that scheduled it did not reach the customer** (the customer
       wrote over it): whatever was scheduled in that turn is cancelled with it.
    5. **The customer unsubscribed** and no longer receives messages from the
       agent.

    You can see it all in the chat's **Ficha**, sections **Recordatorios** and
    **Acciones programadas**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The customer got the reminder in different words">
    That is expected when the reminder is a note: the agent writes the message.
    If you want it to go out word for word, write **«dile exactamente esto:»**
    after the delay, with the colon, and the text after it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I did not get the 💰 notice for a sale">
    Check in the agent's Configuración that **Informar de las ventas** has
    numbers: without them, the notice does not reach your team. Remember that it
    notifies sales and services, not appointments or leads, and that a second
    sale in the same chat within 24 hours does not notify again. And check in the
    thread that the sale was recorded: unfold the **Conversión registrada**
    chip and read what it returned. That chip appears even if the sale did not
    go in (for example, if the amount was missing) or if it was the same sale
    repeated within 24 hours.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The agent does not give prices, does not send the location or does not check stock">
    They are tools that depend on something of yours. Prices: active products in
    the catalog. Location: premises under **Librería → Ubicaciones** for that
    agent. Stock: the **Inventario** app, the **Consultar stock** switch in that
    agent's Configuración, and the catalog.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My MCP tool does not show up">
    Wait a few minutes after adding the server. Check that the card says
    **Activo**, that the URL starts with `https://` and is public, and that you
    do not go over 5 servers or 20 tools in total.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
