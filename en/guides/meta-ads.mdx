The **Anuncios** (*Ads*) page brings together two things that until now lived in
different places: what you spent on Meta on each click-to-WhatsApp ad, and what
happened afterwards in your chats: who wrote, who qualified and who bought.
DarkFunnels already knows the second half: the first message from someone who
writes to you from an ad usually carries, hidden, the ad they came from, and we
store it with the chat. The first half is in your Meta ad account, and to read
it you have to connect it. This guide is that step, screen by screen.

Without connecting, the page already shows you, per ad, the chats, the qualified
leads, the sales and the revenue they brought in. Connecting adds the **spend**,
the **clicks**, what each chat, each qualified lead and each sale cost you, the
**ROAS**, the **engagement** (reactions, comments, shares), each ad's
**thumbnail** and **video** (when Meta shares it), and **«rinde»** (*yield*):
which is your best ad by what each sol (or dollar) you put into it brings back.

The dashboard is in Spanish, so its buttons and messages are quoted here as you
will see them, with the English meaning in parentheses. The screenshots of
Meta's windows are in Spanish too, and their buttons are named the same way.

## Before you start: what you need to know

<Warning>
**1. Connect with the Facebook account that runs your ads.** It must have access
to the ad account that pays for your click-to-WhatsApp ads (in Ads Manager, it's
the account you pick at the top left). If chats from ads have already reached
you and you connect with a Facebook account that can't see that ad account,
DarkFunnels doesn't find your ads and tells you so. If none has reached you yet,
it has no way of knowing: check beforehand which Facebook account you're logged
in with.

**2. DarkFunnels recognizes your account by the ads that bring you chats.**
If nobody has written to you from a click-to-WhatsApp ad yet, you can still
connect: if your Facebook account sees a single ad account, that one is
connected; if it sees several, we add yours on our own when your first chat
arrives from an ad paid by one of the accounts that Facebook account can see.

**3. To see Facebook's «organic» engagement, that same Facebook account must
manage the Page that publishes your ads.** Without that, everything else works
the same and that part doesn't show up.

**4. The Anuncios page is in a pilot and lives in the Ventas (*Sales*) app.** You
need access to Ventas. If you still don't see it in the menu, or when you open it
it says *«Todavía no está disponible para tu cuenta»* (*Not available for your
account yet*), it isn't open for your business yet: if you'd like to try it,
write to us.
And until Meta approves the DarkFunnels app for everyone, there's one more step:
accepting an invitation from Meta (we explain it right below).
</Warning>

### For now: accept Meta's invitation

The app DarkFunnels uses to connect to Meta doesn't have Meta's approval for
everyone yet, and in the meantime Meta only lets the people the app invited
connect. So, the first time:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Send us your Facebook profile">
    Write to us on the support WhatsApp or at **soporte@darkfunnels.ai** with the
    profile of the person who will connect (the one who runs your ads): their
    name as it appears on Facebook or the link to their profile. We invite you as
    a **tester** of the DarkFunnels app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Accept the invitation">
    Go to `developers.facebook.com/requests` with that Facebook account and
    accept the DarkFunnels invitation. If Meta asks you to register as a
    developer, do it: it's free and doesn't publish anything in your name.
  </Step>
</Steps>

From then on you can connect. This step goes away when Meta approves the app for
everyone; this guide will be updated then.

## Connect your ad account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Anuncios">
    In the left menu: **Ventas → Anuncios** (*Sales → Ads*).

    <Frame caption="The page lives in Ventas (Sales), below Resultados (Results).">
      <img src="/images/anuncios-de-meta/01-menu-ventas-anuncios.png" alt="Side menu with the Ventas section and the Anuncios entry selected" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click «Conectar Meta»">
    Top right, next to **Capítulo calificado** (*Qualified chapter*).

    <Frame caption="Without a connection, the button says «Conectar Meta» (Connect Meta). Once connected, it will say «Meta conectado» (Meta connected).">
      <img src="/images/anuncios-de-meta/02-boton-conectar-meta.png" alt="Anuncios page toolbar with the search, the funnel, the period and the Conectar Meta button" />
    </Frame>

    The **Conexión con Meta** (*Connection with Meta*) sheet opens. Click
    **Conectar con Meta** (*Connect with Meta*): it takes you to Meta's window.

    <Frame caption="The sheet before connecting. We only ask for permission to read: we don't create, change or publish anything.">
      <img src="/images/anuncios-de-meta/03-hoja-conectar-con-meta.png" alt="Conexión con Meta sheet with the Conectar con Meta button" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm it's your Facebook account">
    Meta asks **¿Continuar como …?** (*Continue as …?*) with the name of the
    Facebook account you have open in that browser. If it's the one that runs
    your ads, click **Continuar** (*Continue*). If not, click **Iniciar sesión en
    otra cuenta** (*Log in to another account*) and sign in with the right one.

    <Frame caption="Meta's window. Your name and photo appear here; in the screenshot they are covered.">
      <img src="/images/anuncios-de-meta/04-meta-continuar-como.png" alt="Meta window asking ¿Continuar como …? (Continue as …?) with the Cancelar and Continuar buttons" />

  <Step title="Choose your Pages">
    Meta asks you to choose which Pages DarkFunnels can access. Leave the first
    option selected (all your Pages, the current ones and the ones you create

    <Frame caption="The Pages: from them DarkFunnels reads the organic engagement of the posts your ads use.">
      <img src="/images/anuncios-de-meta/05-meta-elige-paginas.png" alt="Meta window to choose the Pages you want DarkFunnels to access, with the options of all Pages or only the current ones" />

    Meta sums up what DarkFunnels asks for under **Revisa la solicitud de acceso
    de DarkFunnels** (*Review DarkFunnels' access request*): *Acceder a tus
    anuncios de Facebook y estadísticas relacionadas* (access your Facebook ads
    and related statistics), *Leer el contenido publicado en la página* (read
    content posted on the Page), *Leer contenido del usuario en tu página* (read
    user content on your Page) and *Mostrar una lista de las páginas que
    administras* (show a list of the Pages you manage). Click **Guardar**
    (*Save*).

    <Frame caption="The four permissions. The Page ones are what let us count the post's reactions, comments and shares: we count how many there are, we don't read what they say.">

    The Page permissions are what let you see Facebook's organic engagement. If
    you don't grant them, the connection still works, but that part doesn't show
    up and the ad's detail sheet will ask you to reconnect.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click «De acuerdo»">
    Meta confirms that your Facebook account **se conectó a DarkFunnels**
    (*connected to DarkFunnels*). **De acuerdo** (*OK*) takes you back to
    DarkFunnels.

    <Frame caption="Meta's confirmation. The «ir a las integraciones comerciales» (go to business integrations) link is where, from Facebook, you can remove DarkFunnels' access whenever you want.">
      <img src="/images/anuncios-de-meta/06-meta-se-conecto.png" alt="Meta window confirming the connection with DarkFunnels and the De acuerdo button" />

  </Step>
  <Step title="Check that it worked">
    Back in Anuncios, the sheet says **Listo: conectaste 1 cuenta publicitaria**
    (*Done: you connected 1 ad account*, or however many there are) and shows you

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
**Which accounts get connected.** If your Facebook account has access to several
ad accounts (for example, those of other businesses you manage), DarkFunnels
keeps the ones that pay for ads that brought chats to your business. The rest
aren't connected: we don't bring in their spend or their figures. If you connect
before your first chat from an ad and your Facebook account sees several
accounts, the sheet says *«Todavía no sabemos cuál de las cuentas publicitarias
de tu Facebook es la de esta empresa»* (*We don't know yet which of your
Facebook's ad accounts belongs to this business*) and yours is added on its own
when that chat arrives. And if you later start paying for ads from another
account, it's also added on its own. In both cases it comes in with the daily
update that follows that account's first chat, if the Facebook account you
connected can see it.
</Note>

## What you see once it's connected

<Frame caption="The page with Meta connected (demo data). At the top, the spend and the cost per chat; below, «Dónde invertir» (Where to invest) and the table sorted by «rinde» (yield).">
  <img src="/images/anuncios-de-meta/08-pagina-con-gasto.png" alt="Anuncios page with the figures strip, the Dónde invertir sentence and the table with the anuncio, rinde, embudo, gasto, reacciones, comentarios and compartidos columns" />
</Frame>

- **The top strip** adds two figures, **Gasto en Meta** (*Meta spend*) and
  **Costo por chat** (*Cost per chat*: all the spend divided by all the chats
  from those ads), to the four you see without connecting: **Chats de
  anuncios** (*Ad chats*), **Calificados** (*Qualified*), **Ventas** (*Sales*)
  and **Sin segundo mensaje** (*No second message*).
- **Each ad** shows its name in Meta and its thumbnail, what it **spent** in the
  period (with its clicks underneath), the **reactions, comments and shares** it
  brought in the period (underneath, with a purple dot, its post's organic ones:
  those that came on their own, unpaid, since it was published), and what **each
  chat, each qualified lead and each sale** cost you, plus the **ROAS** (what it
  sold divided by what it spent).
- **Ads from those accounts that spent without bringing in any chat also show
  up, with 0 chats** (for example, campaigns that aren't click-to-WhatsApp), when
  you look at all funnels. Their spend counts toward your average.
- **«rinde»** and the **Dónde invertir** (*Where to invest*) sentence, explained
  below.
- **The toolbar** filters the page: the search (**Buscar anuncio o ID…**,
  *Search ad or ID…*, by the ad's name, its campaign or its ID), the funnel
  (**Todos los embudos**, *All funnels*, or one) and the period (by default, the
  last 30 days). The longest period is about three months (**Últimos 90 días**,
  *Last 90 days*, or a **Rango personalizado**, *Custom range*, of that length):
  that's why «Este año» (*This year*) and «Año anterior» (*Previous year*)
  aren't there.
- **The country**: if your contacts are from more than one country, a selector
  appears in the toolbar. When you pick one, the page counts the chats from
  contacts in that country and the spend Meta delivered there. The country stays
  in the page's address: if you copy the link and pass it to someone on your team
  with access to Ventas, they open it with the same country.

### Your chats' figures (with or without Meta)

These columns come from your chats, not from Meta: you see them even if you
haven't connected anything.

**A chat enters the period by the day that person first arrived from an ad, and
it counts for the ad of that first click.** If they had already written to you
from an ad in that same funnel, they aren't counted again. Their sale counts even
if it comes later, up to 30 days afterwards.

| Column | What it tells you |
| --- | --- |
| **chats** | How many people wrote from that ad. Underneath, «N recientes» (*N recent*): the ones who arrived in the last 72 hours and can still move forward. |
| **sin respuesta** (*no reply*) | What share didn't send a second message in their first 24 hours. In the strip it's called **Sin segundo mensaje**. |
| **calificados** (*qualified*) | What share reached their funnel's qualified chapter, or beyond (underneath, how many chats that is, or its mark if it has one). It shows «—» if that funnel doesn't have a qualified chapter yet. |
| **ventas** (*sales*) | How many of those chats bought. Underneath, «+N sin confirmar» (*+N unconfirmed*): sales where it isn't clear the person came from this ad (for example, because they also arrived through another one). They're shown apart and don't count in the sales or in the revenue. |
| **conversión** (*conversion*) | Sales divided by chats. |
| **ingreso** (*revenue*) | What the first sale of each chat added up to (without the «sin confirmar» ones), in the currency of most of those sales. If a customer buys again, that second sale doesn't add up here, and neither do sales in another currency. |
| **ingreso por chat** (*revenue per chat*) | Revenue divided by chats. |
| **ticket** (*average order*) | What each sale is worth, on average. |
| **tiempo a la venta** (*time to sale*) | How long the sale took from when the person wrote. It's the median: half bought sooner and the other half, later. |

A mark can show up in **conversión** and in **calificados**. It compares the ad
with your average: that of all the ad chats you're looking at (the same period,
funnel and country).

- **Replicar** (*Replicate*): it's clearly doing better than your average, by a
  margin that chance no longer explains. In conversion, it also brings more
  revenue per chat.
- **Revísalo** (*Review it*): it's clearly doing worse. In conversion, it also
  brings less revenue per chat.
- **Inestable** (*Unstable*, in conversion): more than 15% of its sales are «sin
  confirmar», so its figure isn't reliable yet.

**The fainter rows are ads with fewer than 150 chats in the period.** With so
few, the difference from your average may still be chance, so they don't carry
the conversion or qualified marks (Replicar, Revísalo, Inestable). The «rinde»
ones (Invertir más, Iterar) can show up: «rinde» already pulls ads with little
data close to your average.

### Choose your qualified chapter

An ad chat counts as **qualified** when it reaches a chapter you choose in each
funnel: the chapter from which it's already a serious customer. While a funnel
doesn't have one, its chats don't count in **calificados**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open «Capítulo calificado»">
    It's the **Capítulo calificado** (*Qualified chapter*) button, top right of
    the page. A sheet opens with all your funnels.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a chapter in each funnel">
    In each funnel's list you choose **Sin capítulo calificado** (*No qualified
    chapter*) or one of its chapters, from 2 onwards: everyone starts in 1, so it
    doesn't separate anyone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Close the sheet">
    It saves on its own: next to the funnel you'll see **Guardado** (*Saved*).
    When you close it, the table is recalculated with the new chapter, also for
    the chats that arrived before the change.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
**The chapter is saved by its number.** If you later move the chapters around in
the [Manual](/en/guides/sales-playbook) (*Playbook*), that number may become a
different chapter. The sheet warns you below the funnel: *Elegiste «…» y hoy el
capítulo N es «…»: revisa si sigue siendo el que quieres.* (*You chose «…» and
today chapter N is «…»: check whether it's still the one you want.*) If it no
longer is, choose the right one again.
</Warning>

### «rinde»: which is your best ad

«rinde» answers a single question: **for each sol (or dollar) you put into this
ad, how much does it bring you compared with an average one across all your
ads?** «2.0×» is double; «0.4×», less than half. The table comes sorted by this
column: the best one on top.

What counts as «what it brings you» depends on what your business already
measures:

| If in the period, among your ads with spend… | «rinde» compares… |
| --- | --- |
| **10 or more sales** were registered | what each ad **sold**, in money |
| there are **10 or more qualified leads** (and fewer than 10 sales) | the **qualified leads** each ad brought |
| neither of the two | the **chats** each ad brought |

The line below **Dónde invertir** tells you which of the three it's using. For
it to count qualified leads, your funnels need their **qualified chapter** (see
«Choose your qualified chapter», above).

An ad with little data **doesn't shoot up by luck**: its «rinde» is estimated
close to your average and moves away from it as it gathers chats. One that spent
21 cents and brought 2 chats comes out near 1×, not above all the others.

The column's two marks:

- **Invertir más** (*Invest more*): it yields more than your average with good
  confidence (80% or more) and **is still running** (it spent in the three days
  before the last update). It's the candidate for a bigger budget.
- **Iterar** (*Iterate*): it yields more than your average with the same
  confidence, but **is no longer running**. It's the base for your next
  variations: the same hook, another format or copy.

**Dónde invertir**, the sentence above the table, brings it all together: which
ad to invest more in and which one to start from for the next version. If no ad
clearly yields more than your average yet, it tells you which one shows the most
promise and that it needs more data; and if none is even above your average,
the sentence doesn't show up. The ad's name is a link that opens its detail
sheet.

<Tip>
«rinde» compares each ad with your own average, so it works even if you spend in
dollars and sell in soles: it doesn't need an exchange rate. If you pay from
accounts in two currencies, it only compares the ads in the currency you spend
the most in. And it recommends, it doesn't act: you change the budget yourself
in Ads Manager.
</Tip>

(*What they saw*): the video (or the image), how many times it was shown and the
people who reacted. On the right, **Lo que pasó** (*What happened*): wrote →
qualified → bought, what each step cost, and on one line what it spent, what it
brought in, the ROAS and its «rinde».

## How often it updates

- **When you connect**, DarkFunnels brings in the last 93 days. It takes a few
  minutes.
- **After that, once a day, on its own.** Each day it also re-reads the last 7
  days, so if Meta corrects a recent day's spend, the correction arrives on its
  own. The **Conexión con Meta** sheet says when the last time was.
- **Days follow your ad account's time zone.** Today's spend is completed by the
  next update: don't expect it to match Ads Manager minute by minute.
- **The post's engagement** (the organic part) is the total since it was
  published, read in the daily update.

## When to reconnect

Reconnecting is the same steps as above, starting from the button at the top
(**Meta conectado** or **Reconectar Meta**, *Reconnect Meta*) → **Volver a
conectar** (*Reconnect*). Since you've connected before, Meta asks whether you
want to **continuar con tu configuración anterior** (*continue with your previous
settings*): **Continuar** reuses what you already accepted and finishes there;
**Editar configuración** (*Edit settings*) takes you to choose the Pages and
review the permissions again. If the detail sheet asked you for your Pages
permission, use **Editar configuración**.

<Frame caption="When reconnecting: «Continuar» (Continue) repeats what you already accepted; «Editar configuración» (Edit settings) lets you review Pages and permissions.">
  <img src="/images/anuncios-de-meta/11-meta-configuracion-anterior.png" alt="Meta window asking whether you want to continue with your previous settings, with the Editar configuración and Continuar buttons" />
</Frame>

The spend we already brought in stays. You need to reconnect in three cases:

1. **Before it expires.** The permission Meta gives you lasts a limited time: at
   most about 90 days from when you accept the permissions, and it can be less.
   The exact date is on the sheet: *«La conexión vence el …»* (*The connection
   expires on …*). In the last 14 days, that same line warns you so you renew it
   before it's cut off. That warning shows up inside **Meta conectado**: open it
   every now and then.
2. **If Meta stops accepting the connection**, for example because you removed
   the permission from Facebook. The button at the top changes to **Reconectar
   Meta** and the sheet explains that Meta no longer accepts the connection.
3. **If the detail sheet asks you to.** If you didn't grant the Pages
   permissions (or you connected before we asked for them), the Facebook part of
   **Desde que se publicó** says *«Para leerla falta el permiso de tus
   páginas.»* (*Reading it needs your Pages permission.*) with a **Volver a
   conectar** button. When you go back, go in through **Editar configuración**
   and accept the Pages permissions.

If you start paying for ads from another ad account, you don't need to: it's
added on its own (see «Which accounts get connected», above).

<Note>
When you reconnect, the spend for the days the connection was cut off comes in
on its own, up to 93 days back.
</Note>

## Disconnect

**Meta conectado → Desconectar** (*Disconnect*), and confirm. DarkFunnels stops
reading your accounts, and the page stops showing the spend, the costs, «rinde»
and the thumbnails. What we already brought in stays stored: if you connect
again, it shows up again.

That cuts the connection on DarkFunnels' side. To also remove its access on
Meta's side, go on Facebook to **Integraciones comerciales** (*Business
integrations*, the link Meta shows you when you connect) and remove DarkFunnels.

## What we read from Meta and what we don't

**We read, once a day:** each ad's spend, impressions, clicks and engagement, by
day and by country; the names of the ad, the ad set and the campaign; and the
totals of the post each ad uses (reactions, comments and shares on Facebook;
likes and comments on Instagram). We keep a copy of each ad's thumbnail and, the
first time you open its detail sheet, of its video (if Meta shares it), to show
them to you without depending on Meta's links, which expire.

**We don't:** we don't create, change, pause or publish anything. Of the
comments we only count how many there are; we don't read what they say.

The permission Meta gives you is stored encrypted. We also store the name and
Facebook ID of whoever connected, and the connected ad accounts (name, ID and
currency). Within your business, the figures are only visible to the people
with access to Ventas. Like the rest of your data, the DarkFunnels support team
can also see them: [Security](/en/security) explains it. To have us delete what
we've already stored, write to us.

    that runs your ads and accept all the permissions.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="It says the connection link isn't valid, was already used or expired">
    Each connection attempt is valid for 10 minutes, only once, and for the same
    business and the same DarkFunnels user that started it. If you took longer
    in Meta's window, came back with an old link, or switched business or user
    halfway through, start again from **Conectar Meta**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="An ad shows no spend («—»)">
    It may be that it spent nothing in the period you have selected or, if you
    picked a country, that it had no delivery in that country. It may also be
    that it's paid by another ad account that isn't connected yet (it's added on
    its own with the daily update if the Facebook account you connected can see
    it). Right after connecting, wait a few minutes: the spend is still being
    brought in. Try a longer period; if it still shows «—» the next day, connect
    with a Facebook account that can see that other account.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The spend doesn't match Ads Manager">
    Check that the period is the same, that days follow your ad account's time
    zone and that today's spend is only completed by the next day's update (the
    corrections Meta makes to the last few days arrive on their own). Also: with
    a funnel selected, the strip only adds up the ads that brought chats to that
    funnel; with accounts in two currencies, it shows the one with the most
    spend («Gasto (moneda principal)», *Spend (main currency)*); and clicks are
    Meta's «Clicks (all)», not only link clicks.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I don't see Facebook's organic engagement">
    In the table, the purple figure doesn't show up; in the detail sheet, the
    Facebook part of **Desde que se publicó** tells you why. If it says *«Para
    leerla falta el permiso de tus páginas.»*, click **Volver a conectar** and
    accept the Pages permissions. If it says *«Todavía no la leemos…»* (*We
    haven't read it yet…*), it shows up with the next update; if it's still the
    same the day after, your Facebook account doesn't manage the Page that
    publishes the ad: reconnect with one that does.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The button says «Reconectar Meta» (Reconnect Meta)">
    Meta stopped accepting the connection: it expired or the permission was
    removed. Click the button and repeat the steps. The spend we already brought
    in isn't lost, and the spend for the days it was cut off comes in on its own
    when you reconnect (up to 93 days back).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The sheet says «La última actualización del gasto falló: …» (the last spend update failed)">
    What follows the colon is the error we got when updating: it may be Meta's
    answer or a failure on our side. If it asks you to reconnect or mentions the
    session, the permission or the token, reconnect. If not, wait for the next
    day's update; if it happens again, write to us with that text.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Instead of the table it says «El cálculo tardó más de la cuenta» (the calculation took longer than expected)">
    The first time you ask for a long period, the calculation can take longer
    than we expect. Click **Reintentar** (*Retry*): the second time it usually
    comes out right away. If it happens again, try a shorter period.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Instead of the table it says «No se pudieron calcular tus anuncios» (your ads couldn't be calculated)">
    Something failed while calculating the table. Click **Reintentar**; if it
    happens again, reload the page and, if it's still the same, write to us.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Below the table it says «no se pudo traer el gasto de Meta» (couldn't bring in the Meta spend)">
    The chats table looks the same, but this time the spend didn't arrive.
    Reload the page; if it happens again, write to us.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The detail sheet says «No pudimos traer el video de este anuncio desde Meta.» (we couldn't bring this ad's video from Meta)">
    We can only copy videos that were uploaded to the ad account, in mp4 and up
    to 50 MB. The ones the ad takes from a post on your Page, Meta doesn't let
    us copy. If the detail sheet shows the **Ver el anuncio en Facebook** (*See
    the ad on Facebook*) link, you can still see it there.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="ROAS shows «—» with «otra moneda» (other currency) underneath">
    Your ad account spends in one currency and your sales are in another.
    DarkFunnels doesn't convert currencies, so it doesn't divide one by the
    other. «rinde» does work in that case, because it compares each ad with your
    own average.
  </Accordion>

## What this page doesn't do

- It doesn't touch your ads, your campaigns or your budget: it only reads.
- It doesn't count as a sale anything that wasn't registered as a sale in
  DarkFunnels. It counts registered sales (by your agent or by your team),
  collected or not, the first one up to 30 days after the click. Cancelled ones
  don't count, and the «sin confirmar» ones go apart. The table footer says
  «ventas que registró tu agente, sin cobrar» (*sales your agent registered, not
  collected*) and the detail sheet «(todavía no cobradas)» (*(not collected
  yet)*): it means they don't need to be collected to count. Collected ones and
  the ones your team registers count too.
- It doesn't convert currencies.
- It doesn't bring in the spend or the figures of ad accounts that aren't
  connected. The ones that pay for ads that bring you chats are added on their
  own (see «Which accounts get connected», above).
