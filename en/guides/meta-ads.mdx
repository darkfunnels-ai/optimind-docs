The **Ads** page brings together two things that until now lived in different
places: what you spent on Meta for each click-to-WhatsApp ad, and what happened
next in your chats: who wrote, who qualified and who bought. DarkFunnels already
knows the second half: the first message from someone who writes from an ad
usually carries the ad they came from, hidden, and we store it with the chat. The
first half lives in your Meta ad account, and to read it you have to connect it.
This guide is that step, screen by screen.

Without connecting, the page already shows, per ad, the chats, the qualified
leads and the sales. Connecting adds the **spend**, the **clicks**, what each chat,
each qualified lead and each sale cost you, the **ROAS**, the **engagement**
(reactions, comments, shares), each ad's **thumbnail** and **video** (when Meta
shares it), and **yield**: which ad is your best one by what each sol (or dollar)
you put into it brings back.

## Before you start: what you need to know

<Warning>
**1. Connect with the Facebook account that runs your ads.** It must have access
to the ad account that pays for your click-to-WhatsApp ads (in Ads Manager, it's
the account you pick at the top left). If you connect with a Facebook account
that can't see that ad account, DarkFunnels can't find your ads and tells you
so.

**2. DarkFunnels recognizes your ad account by the ads that already brought you
chats.** If nobody has written to you from a click-to-WhatsApp ad yet, wait for
the first chat before connecting.

**3. To see Facebook's "organic" engagement, that same Facebook account must
manage the Page that publishes your ads.** Without that, everything else works
the same and that part doesn't show up.

**4. The Ads page is in a pilot and lives in the Sales app.** You need access to
Sales. If you still don't see it in the menu, or it says *"Not available for your
account yet"* when you open it, write to us to join. While the pilot lasts there
is one extra step: accepting an invitation from Meta (explained right below).
</Warning>

### While the pilot lasts: accept Meta's invitation

The app DarkFunnels uses to connect to Meta doesn't have Meta's approval for
everyone yet, and in the meantime Meta only lets people the app invited connect. So, the first time:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Send us your Facebook profile">
    The profile of the person who will connect (the one who runs your ads): their
    name as it appears on Facebook, or the link to their profile. We invite you
    as a **tester** of the DarkFunnels app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Accept the invitation">
    Go to `developers.facebook.com/requests` with that Facebook account and accept
    the DarkFunnels invitation. If Meta asks you to register as a developer, do
    it: it's free and doesn't publish anything in your name.
  </Step>
</Steps>

From then on you can connect. This step goes away once Meta approves the app for
everyone; this guide will be updated then.

## Connect your ad account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Ads">
    In the left menu: **Sales → Ads**.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Click «Connect Meta»">
    Top right, next to **Qualified chapter**.

    <Frame caption="Without a connection, the button says «Connect Meta». Once connected, it says «Meta connected».">
      <img src="/images/anuncios-de-meta/02-boton-conectar-meta.png" alt="Ads page toolbar with the search box, the funnel, the period and the Connect Meta button" />
    </Frame>

    The **Connection with Meta** sheet opens. Click **Connect with Meta**: it
    takes you to Meta's window.

    <Frame caption="The sheet before connecting. We only ask for permission to read: we don't create, change or publish anything.">
      <img src="/images/anuncios-de-meta/03-hoja-conectar-con-meta.png" alt="Connection with Meta sheet with the Connect with Meta button" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm it's your Facebook account">
    Meta asks **Continue as …?** with the name of the Facebook account open in
    that browser. If it's the one that runs your ads, click **Continue**. If not,
    **Log in to another account** and sign in with the right one.

    <Frame caption="Meta's window. Your name and photo appear here; in the screenshot they are covered.">
      <img src="/images/anuncios-de-meta/04-meta-continuar-como.png" alt="Meta window asking Continue as …? with the Cancel and Continue buttons" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your Pages">
    Meta asks you to choose which Pages DarkFunnels can access. Leave the first
    option selected (all your Pages, current ones and those you create later), or
    pick the second one (current ones only) and check, at least, the Page that
    publishes your ads. Click **Continue**.

    <Frame caption="The Pages: from them DarkFunnels reads the organic engagement of the posts your ads use.">
      <img src="/images/anuncios-de-meta/05-meta-elige-paginas.png" alt="Meta window to choose which Pages DarkFunnels can access, with the all-Pages and current-Pages-only options" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the permissions and save">
    Meta sums up what DarkFunnels asks for under **Review DarkFunnels' access
    request**: *access your Facebook ads and related statistics*, *read content
    posted on the Page*, *read user content on your Page* and *show a list of the
    Pages you manage*. Click **Save**.

    <Frame caption="The four permissions. «Read user content» is what lets us count the post's reactions and comments: we count how many there are, we don't read what they say.">
      <img src="/images/anuncios-de-meta/05b-meta-revisa-permisos.png" alt="Meta window Review DarkFunnels' access request with four permissions and the Back and Save buttons" />
    </Frame>

    The Page permissions are what make Facebook's organic engagement visible. If
    you don't grant them, the connection still works, but that part doesn't show
    up and the ad's detail sheet will ask you to reconnect.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click «OK»">
    Meta confirms your Facebook account **connected to DarkFunnels**. **OK** takes
    you back to DarkFunnels.

    <Frame caption="Meta's confirmation. The «go to business integrations» link is where, from Facebook, you can remove DarkFunnels' access whenever you want.">
      <img src="/images/anuncios-de-meta/06-meta-se-conecto.png" alt="Meta window confirming the connection with DarkFunnels and the OK button" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check that it worked">
    Back in Ads, the sheet says **Done: you connected 1 ad account** (or however
    many) and shows each account with its name, ID and currency. At first you'll
    see *"We're bringing in the spend for the last 93 days. Come back in a few
    minutes."*; when it's done, it tells you when the spend was updated and until
    when the connection is valid.

    <Frame caption="Connected. From this same sheet you reconnect or disconnect.">
      <img src="/images/anuncios-de-meta/07-hoja-listo.png" alt="Connection with Meta sheet with the Done: you connected 1 ad account notice, the account, the update date and the expiry date" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
**Which accounts get connected.** If your Facebook account has access to several
ad accounts (for example, those of other businesses you manage), DarkFunnels
keeps the ones that pay for your most recent ads that brought chats to your
business. The rest are not read. Accounts are fixed when you connect: if you later
start paying for ads from another one, reconnect (see "When to reconnect" below).
</Note>

## What you see once it's connected

<Frame caption="The page with Meta connected (demo data). At the top, the spend and the cost per chat; below, «Where to invest» and the table sorted by yield.">
  <img src="/images/anuncios-de-meta/08-pagina-con-gasto.png" alt="Ads page with the figures strip, the Where to invest sentence and the table with the yield, spend, reactions, comments, shares and chats columns" />
</Frame>

- **The top strip** adds two figures: **Meta spend** and **Cost per chat** (all
  the spend divided by all the chats from those ads).
- **Each ad** shows its name in Meta and its thumbnail, what it **spent** in the
  period (with its clicks underneath), the **reactions, comments and shares** it
  brought in the period (underneath, with a purple dot, its post's organic ones:
  those that came on their own, unpaid, since it was published), and what **each
  chat, each qualified lead and each sale** cost you, plus the **ROAS** (what it
  sold divided by what it spent).
- **Ads from those accounts that spent without bringing a single chat also show
  up, with 0 chats** (for example, campaigns that aren't click-to-WhatsApp), when
  you look at all funnels. Their spend counts toward your average.
- **Yield** and the **Where to invest** sentence, explained below.
- **Country**: if your contacts come from more than one country, a selector shows
  up in the toolbar. When you pick one, the page counts the chats from contacts in
  that country and the spend Meta delivered there.

### Yield: which ad is your best one

Yield answers a single question: **for each sol (or dollar) you put into this ad,
how much does it bring you compared with an average one across all your ads?**
"2.0×" is double; "0.4×", less than half. The table comes sorted by this column:
the best one on top.

What counts as "what it brings you" depends on what your business already
measures:

| If in the period, among your ads with spend… | yield compares… |
| --- | --- |
| your agent registered **10 or more sales** | what each ad **sold**, in money |
| there are **10 or more qualified leads** (and fewer than 10 sales) | the **qualified leads** each ad brought |
| neither | the **chats** each ad brought |

The line below **Where to invest** tells you which of the three it's using. For
qualified leads to count, each funnel needs its **qualified chapter** (the
**Qualified chapter** button, top right): the chapter from which a chat is
already a serious customer.

An ad with little data **doesn't shoot up by luck**: its yield is estimated close to
your average and moves away from it as it gathers chats. One that spent 21 cents
and brought 2 chats comes out near 1×, not above all the others.

The column's two marks:

- **Invest more**: it yields more than your average with good confidence (80% or
  more) and **is still running** (it spent in the three days before the last
  update). It's the candidate for a bigger budget.
- **Iterate**: it yields more than your average with the same confidence, but
  **is no longer running**. It's the base for your next variations: the same
  hook, another format or copy.

**Where to invest**, the sentence above the table, puts it all together: which ad
to invest more in and which one to start from for the next version. If no ad
clearly yields more than your average yet, it tells you which one looks most
promising and that it needs more data. The ad's name is a link that opens its
detail sheet.

<Tip>
Yield compares each ad with your own average, so it works even if you spend in
dollars and sell in soles: it doesn't need an exchange rate. If you pay from
accounts in two currencies, it only compares the ads in the currency you spend
the most in. And it recommends, it doesn't execute: you change the budget
yourself in Ads Manager.
</Tip>

### An ad's detail sheet

Click an ad's name to open its detail sheet. On the left, **What they saw**: the
video (or the image), how many times it was shown and the people who reacted. On
the right, **What happened**: wrote → qualified → bought, what each step cost, and
on one line what it spent, what it brought in, the ROAS and its yield.

<Frame caption="«What happened»: each step with its cost, the money on one line and, below, how much it brings per S/ 100 against your average.">
  <img src="/images/anuncios-de-meta/09-ficha-lo-que-paso.png" alt="Ad detail sheet: wrote, qualified and bought with their cost, the spent, brought in, ROAS and yield line, and how far they got" />
</Frame>

Under **The people**, each interaction is read per 1,000 times the ad was shown,
with a mark for your average: that's how you compare a big ad with a small one.
Below, **Since it was published** splits the post's engagement into what the ads
brought (gray) and what came on its own (purple), on Facebook and on Instagram.

<Frame caption="«The people» per 1,000 times shown and, below, the post's paid and organic engagement.">
  <img src="/images/anuncios-de-meta/10-ficha-la-gente-y-organica.png" alt="Ad detail sheet: reactions, comments, shares and saves per 1,000, and Facebook and Instagram engagement split into paid and organic" />
</Frame>

## How often it updates

- **When you connect**, DarkFunnels brings in the last 93 days. It takes a few
  minutes.
- **After that, once a day, on its own.** Each day it also re-reads the last 7
  days, so if Meta corrects a recent day's spend, the correction arrives on its
  own. The **Connection with Meta** sheet says when the last update was.
- **Days follow your ad account's time zone.** Today's spend is completed by the
  next update: don't expect it to match Ads Manager minute by minute.
- **The post's engagement** (the organic part) is the total since it was
  published, read in the daily update.

## When to reconnect

Reconnecting is the same steps as above, starting with the button at the top
(**Meta connected** or **Reconnect Meta**) → **Reconnect**. Since you connected
before, Meta asks whether you want to **continue with your previous settings**:
**Continue** reuses what you already accepted and finishes there; **Edit
settings** takes you to choose the Pages and review the permissions again. If the
detail sheet asked you for your Pages permission, use **Edit settings**.

<Frame caption="When reconnecting: «Continue» repeats what you already accepted; «Edit settings» lets you review Pages and permissions.">
  <img src="/images/anuncios-de-meta/11-meta-configuracion-anterior.png" alt="Meta window asking whether you want to continue with your previous settings, with the Edit settings and Continue buttons" />
</Frame>

The spend we already brought in stays. You need to reconnect in four cases:

1. **Every 90 days.** Meta grants access to the data for 90 days from when you
   accept. The sheet says *"The connection expires on …"* and, in the last 14
   days, it warns you so you renew it before it's cut off. That warning shows up
   inside **Meta connected**: open it every now and then.
2. **If Meta stops accepting the connection**, for example because you removed
   the permission from Facebook. The button at the top changes to **Reconnect
   Meta** and the sheet explains that Meta no longer accepts the connection.
3. **If the detail sheet asks you to.** If you didn't grant the Page permissions
   (or connected before we asked for them), the Facebook part of **Since it was
   published** says *"Reading it needs your Pages permission."* with a
   **Reconnect** button. When you come back, go through **Edit settings** and
   accept the Page permissions.
4. **If you start paying for ads from another ad account.** Accounts are fixed
   when you connect. Once a chat from those ads has reached you, reconnect to add
   it.

<Note>
After reconnecting, each day re-reads the last 7 days. If the connection was cut
for longer, the days in between don't come back on their own: write to us and
we'll bring them in.
</Note>

## Disconnect

**Meta connected → Disconnect**, and confirm. DarkFunnels stops reading your
accounts, and the page stops showing the spend, the costs, yield and the
thumbnails. What we already brought in stays stored: if you reconnect, it shows
up again.

That cuts the connection on DarkFunnels' side. To also remove its access on
Meta's side, go to **Business integrations** on Facebook (the link Meta shows you
when connecting) and remove DarkFunnels.

## What we read from Meta and what we don't

**We read, once a day:** each ad's spend, impressions, clicks and engagement, by
day and by country; the names of the ad, the ad set and the campaign; and the
totals of the post each ad uses (reactions, comments and shares on Facebook;
likes and comments on Instagram). We store a copy of each ad's thumbnail and, the
first time you open its detail sheet, of its video (if Meta shares it), to show
them to you without depending on Meta's links, which expire.

**We don't:** we don't create, change, pause or publish anything. For comments
we only count how many there are; we don't read what they say.

The permission Meta gives you is stored encrypted. We also store the name and
Facebook ID of whoever connected, and the connected ad accounts (name, ID and
currency). The figures are only visible to the people in your business with
access to Sales. To have us delete what we've already stored, write to us.

  <Accordion title="It says «Meta didn't accept the connection» or «Meta didn't respond as expected»">

  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="It says «In your ad accounts we couldn't find the ads that bring chats to this business»">
    The Facebook account you connected with doesn't have access to the ad

    an ad yet. Connect with the Facebook account that manages those ads (or ask
    to be given access to the ad account in Meta), or wait for the first chat

  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="It says «You didn't choose any ad account»">
    Your Facebook account doesn't have access to any ad account, or you didn't
    accept the permission to read your ads. Connect with the Facebook account
    that runs your ads and accept all the permissions.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="It says the connection link isn't valid, was already used or expired">
    Each connection attempt is valid for 10 minutes, only once, and for the same
    business and DarkFunnels user that started it. If you took longer in Meta's
    window, came back with an old link or switched business or user halfway,
    start again from **Connect Meta**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="An ad shows no spend («—»)">
    Either it spent nothing in the period you have selected, or it's paid by
    another ad account that isn't connected. Try a longer period; if it still
    shows "—", connect with a Facebook account that can see that other account.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The spend doesn't match Ads Manager">
    Check that the period is the same, that days follow your ad account's time
    zone and that today's spend is only completed by the next day's update (the
    corrections Meta makes to the last few days arrive on their own). Also: with
    a funnel selected, the strip only adds the ads that brought chats to that
    funnel; with accounts in two currencies, it shows the one with the most spend
    ("Spend (main currency)"); and clicks are Meta's "clicks (all)", not only
    link clicks.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I don't see Facebook's organic engagement">
    In the table, the purple figure doesn't show up; in the detail sheet, the
    Facebook part of **Since it was published** tells you why. If it says
    *"Reading it needs your Pages permission."*, click **Reconnect** and accept
    the Page permissions. If it says *"We haven't read it yet…"*, it shows up
    with the next update; if it's still the same the day after, your Facebook
    account doesn't manage the Page that publishes the ad: reconnect with one
    that does.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The button says «Reconnect Meta»">
    Meta stopped accepting the connection: it expired or the permission was
    removed. Click the button and repeat the steps. The spend we already brought
    in isn't lost (if it was cut for more than 7 days, see the note under "When
    to reconnect").
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The sheet says «The last spend update failed: …»">
    What follows the colon is Meta's answer. If it talks about the session, the
    permission or the token, reconnect. If not, wait for the next day's update;
    if it happens again, write to us with that text.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Below the table it says «couldn't bring in the Meta spend»">
    The chats table looks the same, but this time the spend didn't arrive. Reload
    the page; if it happens again, write to us.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The detail sheet says «We couldn't bring this ad's video from Meta.»">

  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="ROAS shows «—» with «other currency» underneath">
    Your ad account spends in one currency and your sales are in another.

    other. Yield does work in that case, because it compares each ad with your
    own average.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## What this page doesn't do

- It doesn't touch your ads, your campaigns or your budget: it only reads.
- It doesn't count as a sale anything that wasn't registered as a sale in
  DarkFunnels. It counts registered sales (by your agent or your team), collected
  or not, the first one up to 30 days after the click.
- It doesn't convert currencies.
- It doesn't read ad accounts that aren't connected.
