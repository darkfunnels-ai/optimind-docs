Your agent doesn't only write: it sends the product photo, the PDF catalog, the
price list, the welcome audio. **All those files live in the Library.** Some of
them it can also *read* to answer questions. This guide is the full
walkthrough, click by click.

The dashboard is in Spanish, so buttons and menus are named here the way you'll
see them on screen, with the English meaning in parentheses the first time they
appear.

<Note>
**Where it is.** Go to the dashboard at
[optimind.darkfunnels.ai](https://optimind.darkfunnels.ai). In the left-hand
menu, inside **Ventas** (*Sales*), press **Librería** (*Library*) — it's in the
**Embudo** (*Funnel*) group. The screen that opens is titled **«Archivos»**
(*Files*): it's the same one. You can also type `optimind.darkfunnels.ai/files`
in the browser's address bar. On your phone, you'll find it under **Más**
(*More*).

The people on your team who have access to the **Ventas** app can see it. How
that access is given is explained in
[Invite your team](/en/getting-started/invite-your-team).
</Note>

<Tip>
**The golden rule, before anything else: first you grant the permission, then
you name the file in the script.** If you do it the other way round, the
customer gets a sentence promising a photo that doesn't arrive — or, worse, a
different photo arrives — and they don't see any error. Everything else in this
guide exists so that order is kept.
</Tip>

## The five words on this screen

These five words come up throughout this guide. Once they're clear to you, the
rest is mechanical.

| Word | What it means, in plain language |
| --- | --- |
| **File** (*Archivo*) | The PDF, the photo or the audio itself. It's uploaded **once for your whole business**, and each funnel uses it with its own permissions. |
| **Permission** (*Permiso*) | What each funnel's agent can do with that file. There are two, independent of each other: **send it** or **study it**. |
| **Send via WhatsApp** (*Enviar por WhatsApp*) | The permission to send it to the customer. It's the switch in the **enviar por WhatsApp** column. |
| **Agent memory** (*Memoria del agente*) | The permission to *read* it in order to answer questions. With this permission alone, the agent doesn't send it: it learns what it says. |
| **Send trigger** (*Condición de envío*) | The file's nickname: the word you call it by from the agent's script. By default it's the file's name without the extension. |

The table's columns can be dragged to another position, and the table remembers
the order. Look for them by their title, not by where they sit.

<Note>
**Three words that look alike and aren't the same thing.** **The customer** is
the person who writes to you on WhatsApp — the one who buys from you. **The
copilot** is the help that lives inside the dashboard. And **your assistant** is
Claude, ChatGPT, Codex or another assistant connected from outside: optional,
and it only shows up in the last section.
</Note>

    With the menu open, that selector is inside **Ventas**, right below its

    Press it and choose the funnel whose agent you want to send the file. With
    the menu collapsed, the selector moves to the very top; on your phone it's
    under **Más**.

    permissions they are. Check which funnel is selected before concluding that

  <Step title="Upload the file with the «+ Insertar» (Insert) button">
    The button is called **Insertar** (*Insert*), not «Subir» (*Upload*). It's
    at the top, next to the three tabs (**Archivos · Ubicaciones · Papelera** —
    *Files · Locations · Trash*), and it **only appears on the Archivos tab**: if
    you're on Papelera, you won't see it.

    A window opens titled **«Agregar archivos enviables»** (*Add sendable
    files*). Drag the files onto it or press **Seleccionar archivos** (*Select
    files*). You can drop several at once.

    <Frame caption="The upload window accepts drag and drop, or your computer's file browser.">
      <img src="/images/libreria/03-insertar-modal.png" alt="«Agregar archivos enviables» (Add sendable files) window with the drop zone, the Seleccionar archivos (Select files) link and the Cancelar and Agregar archivos buttons" />
    </Frame>

    Below it appears **«Archivos seleccionados (N)»** (*Selected files (N)*)
    with each one's size, a trash-can icon to take it off the list and a
    **Sticker** checkbox. That checkbox **can only be ticked on images**: on a
    PDF or an audio it's greyed out, and if you hover over it, it says *«Sólo
    las imágenes pueden ser stickers»* (*Only images can be stickers*). Finish
    with **Agregar archivos** (*Add files*).

    <Warning>
    **The error notice arrives AFTER the window closes.** When you press
    «Agregar archivos», the window closes right away and the list empties. If
    some file wasn't valid, the error shows up afterwards as a floating message
    (*«El archivo X excede el límite de 20MB»* — *File X exceeds the 20MB
    limit* — or *«El tipo de archivo X no está permitido»* — *File type X is
    not allowed*), along with *«Error al agregar archivos»* (*Error adding
    files*). In that case the table may not show the ones that did go in yet:
    press **Actualizar** (*Refresh*), at the top right, and count the rows.
    </Warning>

    <Note>
    **An audio can take as long to upload as it lasts.** Before uploading it,
    the dashboard converts it to the WhatsApp voice-note format, and that
    conversion runs in real time: a one-minute audio takes about a minute.
    Don't close the tab in the meantime.
    </Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Switch on «enviar por WhatsApp» (send via WhatsApp)">
    Here's the trap that trips everyone up: **the window is called «archivos
    enviables» (sendable files), but what you upload with Insertar is not born
    sendable.**

    - A PDF, an image, a DOCX or a CSV are born with **memoria del agente**
      (*agent memory*) switched on and sending switched **off**.
    - An audio, a video or an image you marked as **Sticker** are born with no
      memory and with sending switched off. In audio and video the memory can't
      even be switched on: the switch is locked.

    <Frame caption="Two files in the table: the PDF with «enviar por WhatsApp» (send via WhatsApp) already on and its condición de envío filled in by itself (catalogo-moda-sol); the image, with sending off and the dash. Both have «memoria del agente» (agent memory) on.">
      <img src="/images/libreria/05-tabla-recien-subido.png" alt="Library table with two files: catalogo-moda-sol.pdf with enviar por WhatsApp on, condición de envío catalogo-moda-sol and the status «Hubo un problema»; polo-pima-blanco.png with sending off, condición de envío and mensaje showing a dash, and the status «Aprendido ✓»; memoria del agente on for both" />
    </Frame>

    Press the switch in the **enviar por WhatsApp** column: it saves instantly.
    The first time you switch it on, the **condición de envío** (*send
    trigger*) column fills itself in with the file's name without the
    extension: `catalogo_verano.pdf` ⇒ `catalogo_verano`. It's the nickname the
    script will ask for the file by, so it's best not to touch it.

    <Note>
    **The switch goes first.** With sending off, the «condición de envío» and
    «mensaje» (*message*) cells show a dash **—**. They let you open them and
    type, but when you confirm, the change is discarded with the notice
    *«Activa "Enviar por WhatsApp" para editar la condición de envío»* (*Switch
    on "Send via WhatsApp" to edit the send trigger*) (or *«…para editar el
    mensaje»* — *…to edit the message*). What you typed may stay on screen until
    the table refreshes (for example, when you press **Actualizar**), but it
    wasn't saved.

    Careful: the dash means «nothing is written here», not «this file isn't
    sent». With sending on, «mensaje» also shows **—** if you didn't give it a
    message. To know whether a file is sent, look at the switch, not the dash.
    </Note>

    If you upload the file from the playbook, on the other hand, it's born
    sendable: you'll see that in step 6.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Write the message that goes with the file">
    A click on the **mensaje** cell opens a box over the cell itself. **Enter**
    leaves the text ready, **Esc** cancels and **Shift+Enter** starts a new
    line. If the text is long, the **Abrir en grande** (*Open large*) button
    opens it in a window: there, Enter starts a new line and the text is
    confirmed with **Guardar cambios** (*Save changes*).

    That text is the **caption** the file travels with. If you leave it empty,
    the file goes out without a text caption.

    <Tip>
    **You can use your business details.** If next to the photo of your QR
    code you write `Paga al {{dato:yape}}` (*Pay to …*), the customer receives
    the value you have saved in **Configuración → Datos del negocio**
    (*Settings → Business details*), not the label. Copy the label exactly as
    it appears next to each detail in that section. If the detail is empty, the
    customer reads its name followed by «(pendiente)» (*pending*), so fill it
    in first. It's all explained in
    [Funnel settings](/en/guides/funnel-settings).
    </Tip>

    <Warning>
    **On audio and on stickers, that message isn't sent.** WhatsApp doesn't
    show a text caption on voice notes or on stickers, so whatever you write
    there is wasted work. Whatever you want to say around an audio, write it in
    the script, before or after the marker. Photos, videos, PDFs and other
    documents, on the other hand, **do** carry it visibly.
    </Warning>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save — because half the table doesn't save itself">
    This is the number-one cause of «my changes were lost»:

    - The **two switches** (enviar por WhatsApp, memoria del agente) save
      **instantly**, without pressing anything.
    - The **name**, the **send trigger** and the **message** stay *pending*
      until you press the button at the top right. Neither Enter in the cell
      nor «Guardar cambios» in the large window saves them yet: they only leave
      them ready.

    While something is pending, that button stops saying «Actualizar» and
    says **«Guardar N cambio(s)»** (*Save N change(s)*), and next to it the
    notice **«Cambios sin guardar»** (*Unsaved changes*) appears.

    <Warning>
    **Switching tab, funnel or page discards the pending changes, without
    asking.** If you have «Cambios sin guardar» and you press **Ubicaciones**
    or **Papelera**, choose another funnel or go to another screen, what you
    wrote is lost silently. Save before you move.
    </Warning>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Tell the agent WHEN to send it (in the playbook)">
    Granting the permission doesn't make the file go out: it only authorizes
    it. What decides the moment is **the agent's script**, in
    [the playbook](/en/guides/sales-playbook) — **Ventas → Manual** (*Sales →
    Playbook*).

    Inside a chapter's text you write a mark at the exact point where you want
    the file to go out. We call that mark a **marker**, and it looks like this:

    ```
    Te dejo el catálogo completo. ###SEND_FILES: catalogo_verano### Dime si lo abres bien.
    ```

    *(Here's the full catalog. ###SEND_FILES: catalogo_verano### Let me know if
    it opens fine.)*

    **You don't need to type it by hand** — and it's better not to. In the
    playbook editor you have three ways to insert it correctly:

    1. The **Recursos** (*Resources*) button in the bar opens the **«Recursos
       del agente»** (*Agent resources*) drawer on the **Archivos** tab: hover
       over the file and press **Insertar**.
    2. Type **`@`** and choose the file in the **Archivos** group.
    3. The **✦ ¿Dónde?** (*✦ Where?*) button, which appears on files that no
       chapter uses yet: the AI suggests a chapter and a sentence with the
       marker already inside, and you decide whether to insert it.

    <Frame caption="The «Recursos del agente» (Agent resources) drawer on the Archivos (Files) tab: the search box, the ↑ Subir (Upload) button and, since this funnel has no files yet, «Sin recursos de este tipo para el agente» (No resources of this type for the agent). At the bottom, the «Gestionar en Librería →» (Manage in Library) link.">

    </Frame>

    <Frame caption="The playbook's @ menu. This funnel has no files yet, so you see the Herramientas, Variables, Capítulos and Envío groups; once you upload files, the Archivos (Files) group appears too.">
      <img src="/images/manual/02-menu-arroba.png" alt="The playbook's @ menu open with the «Buscar recurso…» search box and the Herramientas, Variables, Capítulos and Envío groups (Tools, Variables, Chapters, Sending)" />
    </Frame>

    In the drawer, each file tells you whether the playbook already uses it
    (*en N cap.* — *in N chapters*) or not (*sin usar* — *unused*), and it has
    *pausado* (*paused*) in front of it if its sending is switched off in the
    Library.

    <Tip>
    **Shortcut: upload the file without leaving the playbook.** In the Recursos
    drawer, Archivos tab, the **↑ Subir** (*↑ Upload*) button uploads one file
    at a time. That file is born **already sendable** for the selected funnel,
    with its send trigger set and **no memory**: *«Archivo subido — ya puedes
    insertarlo con @ o desde esta lista.»* (*File uploaded — you can now insert
    it with @ or from this list.*) If you also want the agent to study it,
    switch on **memoria del agente** in the Library.
    </Tip>

    <Note>
    **This is a computer job.** On a screen less than 900 px wide, the playbook
    opens **read-only**: from your phone you can't rewrite the script or insert
    files. What you can fix from your phone is the data — prices, phone
    numbers, links — with the **Retocar datos** (*Touch up data*) button.
    </Note>

    <Warning>
    **If you write the marker by hand, copy its exact shape.** Three `#` signs,
    the word `SEND_FILES`, a colon, the nickname and `###` again. The space
    after the colon doesn't matter.

    With **fewer than three `#`** (`##SEND_FILES: catalogo##`) the system
    doesn't recognize it as a marker and **that text may reach the customer's
    chat as-is**. If the mistake is a different one — you're missing the colon,
    or you write another word — the mark is deleted from the text and the file
    may not go out.
    </Warning>
  </Step>

    **1. In the dashboard, with Probar.** The **Probar** (*Test*) button in the

    WhatsApp, but nothing goes out on WhatsApp. In the test chat the file shows
    up as *«Archivo adjunto»* (*Attached file*), or as «Archivo ·» followed by
    its message if you gave it one.

    Below each reply, the trace tells you which tools the agent used. If the
    sending tool couldn't send anything, it's marked as skipped. Careful: the
    test thread consumes balance like a real conversation. More in
    [the playbook guide](/en/guides/sales-playbook).

    **2. On a real phone.** That way you see the file exactly as it reaches the
    customer: the PDF thumbnail, the voice note, the photo caption. Write to
    your funnel's number from a personal phone and ask for it the way a customer
    would. Yours works if it's the one you entered when you signed up. Another
    WhatsApp line connected to your business won't work. If you use another
    phone that receives reports, sales notices or alerts — yours or someone on
    your team's — add it first under **Números de prueba** (*Test numbers*), in
    [Settings](/en/guides/funnel-settings).

## What files you can upload

| Family | Formats |
| --- | --- |
| Image | JPG, PNG, GIF, WebP, SVG |
| Document | PDF, DOCX, CSV |
| Audio | MP3, WAV, OGG, M4A, AAC, OPUS |
| Video | MP4, MKV |

For photos, use JPG or PNG; for videos, MP4. An SVG or an MKV can be uploaded,
but we can't guarantee that WhatsApp will show them as a photo or a video.

**Word (DOCX)**, PDF and lists saved as **CSV** (a spreadsheet saved as text)
are accepted. **Excel (XLSX), PowerPoint (PPTX), old Word (DOC), ZIP and TXT
are not.** If your price list is in Excel, open it and use «Save as → CSV», or
export it to PDF.

**The limit is 20 MB per file.** There's no limit on the number of files.

<Note>
**Audio files usually change extension when you upload them, and that's
normal.** Unless they already come as a WhatsApp voice note, the dashboard
converts them: you upload `bienvenida.mp3` and the table shows
`bienvenida.webm` (or `.opus`, depending on the browser). It's the same audio.
If your browser can't do that conversion, the audio is uploaded as-is, with its
original name.
</Note>

<Note>
**Uploading the same document or photo twice doesn't duplicate it.** The system
compares the content, not the name: if those bytes were already in your Library
(outside the trash), it tells you *«Ya tenías este archivo»* (*You already had
this file*) and reuses the row that already existed, with the name it already
had. If the selected funnel is missing the permission that upload is born with
(memory, for PDFs, photos, DOCX and CSV), it gets it. Careful: if you had
switched that file's memory off, uploading it again switches it back on and it's
processed again. Renaming it on your computer and uploading it again doesn't
create a copy either.
</Note>

## How it reaches the customer

How the message looks on WhatsApp is decided by the format, not by you:

| What you upload | How it arrives | Does it carry the «mensaje» (message)? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Image or video | Photo or video, with the text underneath | Yes |
| PDF | Document, with a thumbnail of the first page | Yes |
| DOCX, CSV | Document, as a card with an icon | Yes |
| Audio | **Voice note** | **No** |
| Sticker | Sticker (if the image can't be converted, it arrives as a photo) | **No** |

The name you gave the file is the one the customer sees on documents. Give it a
readable one.

<Note>
**A PDF looks better than a DOCX.** A PDF usually arrives with a preview of its
first page; a DOCX arrives as a card with an icon. For a catalog, that
difference sells — but the preview isn't guaranteed: PDFs over 15 MB and those
the system can't render also arrive as a plain card.
</Note>

### The voice note keeps them waiting

Audio goes out as a voice note, and **the sending is delayed by the length of
the audio**, as if a person were recording it: the customer may see «recording
audio…» during that time. The ceiling on that wait is **2 minutes**, even if
the audio is longer.

Count that time: a two-minute welcome audio leaves the customer two minutes
without receiving anything else. **For greetings, stay under 20 or 30
seconds.**

### The same file isn't repeated within 6 hours

If the agent already sent that file to that conversation less than **6 hours**
ago, it doesn't repeat it: the customer who insists three times in five minutes
gets the quote only once. The text message does go out; what's missing is the
attachment.

<Warning>
**Two consequences that come as a surprise.** (1) The block counts per
**Library row**, not per document: two PDFs of the same catalog exported
separately aren't the same file even if they look identical, and if both are
sendable, each one has its own block — the customer may receive it twice. (2) A
scheduled reminder is subject to the same block: if you send the catalog now
and the reminder falls due within those 6 hours, the follow-up **arrives
without the attachment**.
</Warning>

### Each reply has a ceiling of 12 messages

Each time the agent replies it can send at most **12 messages in a row**, and
each file takes up one. Normally the text isn't lost: if it doesn't fit, the
ending is merged into the last message, so the closing arrives cramped instead
of nicely spread out. But if a single reply sends so many files that they alone
nearly fill the 12, the reply can go over the ceiling, and what's left over
doesn't arrive: precisely the closing. One file per chapter is the healthy
amount.

## How the agent chooses which file to send

It's the part that causes the most problems, so it's worth understanding. When
the time comes to send, the agent looks for the file among **its funnel's
sendable files**, **in this order**:

1. **The file's name, as-is** (with its extension).
2. **The file's name without the extension.** Here capitals, accents and dashes
   don't bother it.
3. **The send trigger**, with that same tolerance — but it has to be the
   **whole** nickname, not part of it.
4. If none of the above matches: **word similarity**.

Three practical rules come out of that order:

<Warning>
**1. The send trigger is a nickname, not a sentence.** The field is called
«condición» (*condition*), and that invites you to write *«cuando el cliente
pida la lista de precios»* (*when the customer asks for the price list*).
**Written like that, it only matches if the agent copies it whole, word for
word**: to find the file by nickname, the nickname has to match in full. If the
agent changes a single word when copying it, a condition written as a sentence
can only win on the last layer, word similarity — the same one that can deliver
the wrong file. Leave the default value (the name without the
extension) or put a single short word. Capitals and accents don't matter.
</Warning>

<Warning>
**2. If the marker names a file that doesn't exist, ANOTHER file may go out.**
This is the expensive failure on this screen. When the nickname doesn't match
anything, the system doesn't always stay quiet: it falls back to word
similarity and **may deliver a different file that shares a word** — the price
list for another city, last year's catalog. If no file resembles it at all,
what you'd expect happens: the marker is deleted from the text and the message
goes out without an attachment, without the customer seeing any error.

That's why the golden rule holds: **permission first, marker second**, copying
the nickname exactly as it appears in the table.
</Warning>

<Warning>
**3. Give each file a different nickname.** Watch out for extensions:
`catalogo.pdf` and `catalogo.jpg` are two different files, but without the
extension both are called `catalogo` and inherit the same nickname. If the
marker says `catalogo`, the system doesn't choose on its own: it hands both
names back to the agent so it asks for an exact one, and the agent may end up
sending the one you didn't want, or neither. (If both names are identical — a
re-upload — the most recent one wins.) Rename one before making both sendable
for the same funnel.
</Warning>

**The playbook helps you follow the rule.** If you hover over a file mention in
the script, you see its status: *Activo* (*Active*), *Pausado en la Librería*
(*Paused in the Library* — it exists, but the agent won't send it) or *No
existe en la Librería* (*Doesn't exist in the Library*). And the playbook's
**Doctor** lists the mentions whose status is *No existe en la Librería*, with a
button to reconnect them when it finds a candidate. More about the Doctor in
[the playbook guide](/en/guides/sales-playbook).

<Frame caption="The playbook's Doctor: at the top, the count («0 errores · 15 avisos», 0 errors · 15 warnings); below, each warning with its «Ir al sitio» (Go to the spot) button. Here they are all «El capítulo N no es alcanzable» (Chapter N is unreachable).">
  <img src="/images/manual/03-doctor.png" alt="«Doctor del manual» (Playbook Doctor) panel with 0 errors and 15 warnings of the «El capítulo N no es alcanzable» kind, each with the Ir al sitio button" />
</Frame>

<Note>
**If you don't write any marker, the file may go out anyway** — but at the end
of the reply, not where you wanted it. The marker doesn't authorize the
sending: it decides **where** it lands. And if the marker ended up inside a
passage you marked with «Enviar en bloque» (*Send as a block*, so it all goes
out together in a single message), the file goes out right **after** that
passage, not in the middle. If your playbook has no passages marked with
«Enviar en bloque», forget about this.
</Note>

## The other switch: «memoria del agente» (agent memory)

With this permission the agent **reads** the file so it can answer questions
about its content. It's what you want for the warranty manual, the shipping
policies or a spec sheet. With images, the agent «looks at» the photo and learns
the text, the figures and the details visible in it.

When you switch it on, the system starts reading the file by itself and cutting
it into pieces so it can search inside it. The **estado** (*status*) column
keeps you posted, and **the table refreshes itself every 5 seconds** while
something is in progress:

| What «estado» says | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **El agente está aprendiendo…** (*The agent is learning…*) | In progress. It can't answer with that content yet. |
| **Aprendido ✓** (*Learned ✓*) | Ready. It can use it now. |
| **Hubo un problema** (*There was a problem*) + **Reintentar** (*Retry*) | It failed. Leave the cursor over it for a second and a small label comes up with the reason. |

The most common reasons you'll see in that label, and what to do about each one
(if you see a different one, write to us from **Ayuda → Escríbenos por
WhatsApp** — *Help → Write to us on WhatsApp*):

| The reason says | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| *sin créditos: recarga el saldo para procesar este archivo* (*no credits: top up your balance to process this file*) | It's not a fault in your file: top up and press **Reintentar**. It's explained in [Credits and top-ups](/en/getting-started/credits-and-top-ups). |
| *no se pudo extraer texto del archivo (¿escaneado sin OCR?)* (*couldn't extract text from the file (scanned without OCR?)*) | It's a PDF made of photos of the pages, with no text inside. Export it again from the original program, or upload the pages as images (JPG or PNG): images do get read. |
| *el CSV no tiene filas con datos* (*the CSV has no rows with data*) | Check that the CSV has rows below the column headings. |
| *no se pudo descargar el archivo* (*couldn't download the file*) | Press **Reintentar**. If it happens again, write to us from **Ayuda → Escríbenos por WhatsApp**. |

Other things worth knowing:

- **Audio and video don't add memory.** The switch won't even let you touch it
  and warns *«Este formato aún no aporta memoria»* (*This format doesn't add
  memory yet*). If the content matters to you, write it as text in the
  playbook.
- **Switching memory off isn't cosmetic: the agent stops seeing that content
  immediately.** Switching it back on recovers it at once, but it also
  processes the file again from scratch, so it consumes balance again.
  **Careful:** if that file was born with memory switched on (you uploaded it
  with **Insertar**) and you never switched «enviar por WhatsApp» on for it in
  this funnel, switching memory off makes it disappear from the funnel's table,
  even though the file is still stored in your business. To get it back, upload
  it again with **Insertar**: the system recognizes it's the same file and gives
  it its memory back, which is processed again.
- **A very long document is learned up to 200,000 characters.** Whatever goes
  beyond that, the agent doesn't see. If your catalog is huge, split it into
  several files.
- **To check what it learned**, open the playbook and press **Memoria a Largo
  Plazo (RAG)** (*Long-term memory*), above the document. Its **Probar**
  (*Test*) search box runs the same search as the agent: type a question the way
  a customer would and you'll see which passages it finds and which file each
  one comes from. If you don't see that button in your playbook, this check
  isn't available for your funnel.

<Note>
**Audio files do get transcribed, even though they don't add memory.** In the
«estado» column you'll see *Transcribiendo…* (*Transcribing…*), *Transcrito ✓*
(*Transcribed ✓*) or *No se pudo transcribir* (*Couldn't be transcribed*). It
lets the agent know what the voice note it sends says, so it writes what goes
around it without contradicting it; it doesn't use it to answer questions.
Press the file's name in the **archivo** (*file*) column and the viewer shows
you, under the player, **«Lo que dice (transcripción automática):»** (*What it
says (automatic transcription):*). If it failed, there's no retry button: use
**Reemplazar** (*Replace*), choose the same audio, and it's transcribed again.
</Note>

## Things you'll need later

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Swap the file for a new version (without breaking the script)">
    Use **Reemplazar** (*Replace*): the paperclip button in the **acciones**
    (*actions*) column, with the hint *«Sube una versión nueva de este
    archivo»* (*Upload a new version of this file*). It keeps the name, the
    nickname, the message and the switches; only the content changes. If the
    file was memory, the agent learns it again (and that consumes balance); if
    it's an audio, it's transcribed again.

    **It's the right way to update a price list in this funnel.** Sending it to
    the trash and uploading another one leaves the markers that named it
    without a file, or pointing to a similar one, until the new one has the
    same name and sending switched on; if the name changes, they stay broken.
    Replacing it doesn't touch them.

    <Warning>
    **If several funnels use that file, the replacement doesn't reach them.**
    When you replace it, a copy is split off for the funnel you have selected
    and the others keep the old version. Repeat the replacement in each funnel.
    </Warning>

    If the new version is in another format and the name had an extension, the
    system corrects it and keeps the stem: `catalogo.pdf` becomes
    `catalogo.png` — and then it will reach the customer as a photo, not as a
    document. If the name had no extension, none is made up for it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Rename a file (and what happens to the playbook)">
    Click the **nombre** (*name*) cell, then **Guardar N cambio(s)**. Careful:
    if the nickname was the default name, renaming **also changes the nickname
    and rewrites the playbook**, and it tells you so when you save: *«También
    se actualizó 1 mención de ese archivo en el manual.»* (*1 mention of that
    file in the playbook was also updated.*)

    If that notice doesn't appear and you expected the playbook to change, open
    the playbook and search for the old name. It happens, for example, when
    another file of the same funnel uses the same nickname: this file's
    nickname does change, but the playbook isn't touched, and the markers with
    the old nickname will keep delivering the **other** file. Fix them by hand.

    If several funnels use the file, the new name only applies to the selected
    funnel; the others keep theirs.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Take a file away from a funnel (without deleting it)">
    Switch off **enviar por WhatsApp**. The file stays in the Library and stays
    available to your other funnels; what disappears is that funnel's permission
    to send it. With it, the markers that named it in its playbook are left
    without their file (there the mention shows as *Pausado en la Librería*)
    and, as rule 2 of
    [How the agent chooses which file to send](#how-the-agent-chooses-which-file-to-send)
    explains, they may end up delivering another similar file: remove them or
    reconnect them with the Doctor. If the agent also had it in memory, switch
    off **memoria del agente**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Send a file to the trash (and get it back)">
    In the **acciones** column, the three-dot menu → **Enviar a la papelera**
    (*Send to trash*, in red). It asks for confirmation at the top center of the
    screen — *«¿Enviar "catalogo_verano.pdf" a la papelera?»* (*Send
    "catalogo_verano.pdf" to the trash?*), with **Confirmar** (*Confirm*) and
    **Cancelar** (*Cancel*) — so don't look for it next to the row.

    You can also tick several rows with the checkboxes: at the top,
    **«N seleccionado(s)»** (*N selected*) appears with the **Eliminar**
    (*Delete*) and **Limpiar** (*Clear*) buttons. Despite the name,
    **«Eliminar» doesn't delete: it sends to the trash.**

    As soon as it's in the trash, the agent stops sending it and, if the file
    belongs only to this funnel, it also stops using what it learned from it. If
    several funnels used the file, it only leaves the selected funnel's Library:
    the others keep it. In that case, if it had memory switched on, switch it
    off first and then send it to the trash: that way you make sure the
    selected funnel's agent stops using its content. (If the file disappears
    from the table when you switch it off, you're done: nothing else is
    needed.)

    In the **Papelera** tab each row has a **Restaurar** (*Restore*) button
    that acts instantly, without asking for confirmation. The file comes back
    with everything it had configured. The notice says *«…el agente lo está
    re-aprendiendo»* (*…the agent is re-learning it*), but if it was memory, it
    actually recovers what it had already learned instantly, without
    processing it again or consuming balance.

    <Warning>
    **The trash empties itself after 30 days.** A daily process permanently
    deletes the files that have been in the trash for 30 days, and then you can
    no longer recover them. The screen doesn't warn you about this deadline:
    don't use the trash as a historical archive.
    </Warning>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Turn an image into a sticker">
    In the three-dot menu: **Marcar como sticker** (*Mark as sticker*) /
    **Quitar de stickers** (*Remove from stickers*). It only appears on images.
    Remember that a sticker **carries no text caption**. If several funnels use
    the image, the change only applies to the selected funnel.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="View a file without leaving the dashboard">
    Press the name in the **archivo** column: a viewer opens with **Abrir en
    una pestaña nueva** (*Open in a new tab*) and **Descargar** (*Download*).
    DOCX and CSV files aren't rendered in the browser and show *«Este formato
    no se puede ver aquí»* (*This format can't be viewed here*) — the file is
    fine, it's the browser that can't display it.

    If the viewer says *«No pudimos mostrar este archivo»* (*We couldn't
    display this file*), reload the page: the link the dashboard uses to open
    each file expires after 24 hours. If it's still the same after that, your
    browser can't play that format (it happens with some MKV videos). Press
    **Descargar** and open it on your computer.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The «Ubicaciones» (Locations) tab is a different thing">
    It shares a screen with the Library but has nothing to do with files: these
    are your **business's premises** (your shop, your pickup point), which the
    agent sends as a map pin. Like file permissions, **they belong to each
    funnel**: the ones you add apply to the selected funnel.

    The button is **Agregar ubicación** (*Add location*) and it asks for
    **Nombre de la sede** (*Name of the premises*), **Dirección** (*Address*)
    and **Cuándo enviarla (opcional)** (*When to send it (optional)*). Here
    there is a **Guardar** (*Save*) button inside the window: it doesn't work
    like the rest of the screen.

    <Frame caption="The Ubicaciones (Locations) tab, still empty («Aún no hay ubicaciones», No locations yet), with the «Agregar ubicación» (Add location) button at the top.">
      <img src="/images/libreria/12-ubicaciones.png" alt="The Library's empty Ubicaciones (Locations) tab, with the Nombre, Dirección, Cuándo enviarla, Coordenadas and Acciones columns and the Agregar ubicación (Add location) button" />
    </Frame>

    When you save, the address is placed on the map by itself. If you get
    *«No pudimos ubicar esa dirección en el mapa»* (*We couldn't find that
    address on the map*), complete it with street, number, district and city.
    To remove a location, open the three-dot menu on its row → **Eliminar**
    (*Delete*): it asks for confirmation and the agent can no longer send it.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## If something is not working

| What you see | It's almost always | How to fix it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **«Aún no hay archivos»** (*There are no files yet*) and you know you uploaded them | You have another funnel selected | Switch funnels in the selector, inside **Ventas** in the menu |
| I uploaded the file and **the agent doesn't send it** | You uploaded it with Insertar and sending was born switched off | Switch on «enviar por WhatsApp»; the send trigger fills itself in |
| The text goes out, **but without the attachment** | The nickname doesn't match any sendable file of that funnel (files in the trash or with sending off don't count), or it already sent it less than 6 hours ago | Copy the nickname exactly as it appears in the «condición de envío» column and check the mention's status in the playbook |
| **The wrong file arrives** | Two files with similar names and the marker didn't match either of them | Give them different, clear nicknames |
| **The marker shows up written in the chat** | The marker is malformed | Delete it and insert it again with **Recursos** or with **@** |
| **My changes were lost** | Name, nickname and message don't save themselves | Press **Guardar N cambio(s)** before switching tab, funnel or screen |
| **«Hubo un problema»** in the memory | No balance, a scanned PDF with no text, or an empty CSV | Hover to read the reason and check [the table of reasons](#the-other-switch-memoria-del-agente-agent-memory) |
| The customer **asks for the file again and it doesn't arrive** | The 6-hour block | It's normal; after the 6 hours it goes out again |
| I uploaded an `.mp3` and **`.webm` appears** | Audio files are converted on upload | That's correct: same audio |
| **«Embudo desactivado»** (*Funnel deactivated*) and you can't touch anything | The funnel is paused and the Library is read-only | Activate it from **Inicio** (*Home* — the switch on your funnel's card) or from **Personalizar → Canales** (*Customize → Channels*) |
| The viewer says **«No pudimos mostrar este archivo»** | The file's link expired (they last 24 hours), or your browser can't play that format | Reload the page; if it's still the same, press **Descargar** and open it on your computer |
| I can't find **the search box** | This screen has no search box | Sort by the **tipo** (*type*) or **nombre** columns |

## Who can see your files

The Library keeps your files private. The dashboard opens them with temporary
links that expire after 24 hours, and within your team, the people with access
to the **Ventas** app can see it.

<Warning>
Even so, keep three things in mind:

- **While a temporary link is valid, anyone who has it can open it without
  signing in.** Don't paste the «Abrir en una pestaña nueva» link in public
  places.
- **What the agent sends on WhatsApp stays on the customer's phone.**
- **What you put in memory, the agent uses to answer your customers.**

So **don't upload internal costs, margins, other customers' data or contracts
to the Library**: neither to send nor as memory.
</Warning>

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does sending files cost me extra credits?">
    Sending a file has no charge of its own, separate from the conversation's.
    Two things that happen on their own do consume balance: processing a file
    for **memory** and **transcribing an audio**.

    The first happens when you upload a PDF, a photo (unless you mark it as
    Sticker), a DOCX or a CSV with **Insertar**, because they're born with
    memory switched on; and also when you switch memory on, when you replace
    the file or when you switch memory back on; without balance, it fails with
    *«sin créditos»* (*no credits*). If a photo is only for sending, switch off
    **memoria del agente**. The second is done for every audio, even though
    audio doesn't add memory and even if you never send it. The details are in
    [Credits and top-ups](/en/getting-started/credits-and-top-ups).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I have the same file in two funnels?">
    Yes. The file is uploaded once and each funnel has its own permissions. You
    can have the catalog sendable for one funnel and in memory for another, or
    both at once for the same funnel.

    If you later change the file itself from one of them — replace it, rename
    it, turn it into a sticker or send it to the trash — the change only
    applies to the selected funnel: the others keep their version.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens to the Library if I duplicate a funnel?">
    The new funnel uses the same files with the same permissions: they aren't
    copied, and the ones in the trash are left out. Whatever was in memory, the
    new agent learns again, and that consumes balance. How to duplicate one is
    in [Your funnels](/en/guides/your-funnels).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does the customer see my file's name?">
    On documents, yes: WhatsApp shows the name. On photos and videos, no. Give
    a presentable name to whatever you're going to send as a document.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I change the nickname without touching the playbook?">
    You can, and you don't need to touch the playbook: when you save a new send
    trigger, the system repoints the markers that named the old one by itself.
    The exception is when another sendable file of the same funnel still uses
    the old nickname; then the playbook isn't touched and you have to review it
    by hand.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How many files fit?">
    There's no limit on the number of files. The limit is 20 MB per file.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My catalog is 40 MB, what do I do?">
    Split it into two PDFs (by category, for example) or export it at a lower
    resolution. Almost any PDF program has a «reduce size» option.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The customer says the audio won't play">
    Before sending it, the system converts the audio to the WhatsApp voice-note
    format, so it also plays on iPhone. If the file can't be processed when you
    upload it, the upload fails with the notice *«No se pudo procesar el archivo
    de audio»* (*The audio file couldn't be processed*). If a customer tells you
    it won't play, or it happens to you repeatedly, write to us from **Ayuda →
    Escríbenos por WhatsApp** and tell us the file's name.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## The same thing, from your assistant

<Note>
This section is **optional and only applies if you connected an assistant**
(Claude, ChatGPT, Codex or another) to your business. If you work in the
dashboard, you're done.
</Note>

With the assistant connection you can ask for all of this in your own language.
If you connected with the dashboard link (**Personalizar → Asistentes IA →
Copiar link** — *Customize → AI assistants → Copy link*), the Library is
already included. You'd only be missing it if you use the bare address with
nothing after it, or a list of groups that doesn't include `library`:
[The connection URL](/en/reference/connection-url) explains it, with the
address ready to copy and paste. Also, it only works if your user has access to
the Ventas app.

What it can and can't do:

- **It can** upload files (up to **2.5 MB**, much less than the dashboard's
  20 MB), list them (including the ones in the trash), read a file's text,
  grant and revoke the send and memory permissions, and change the nickname,
  which repoints the playbook's markers by itself. If you also gave it
  permission to edit the playbook, it can write the markers too.
- **It can** write the message that goes with the file, but only when uploading
  it or granting the permission: to change it later it would have to revoke the
  permission and grant it again, so it's easier to do in the dashboard. **It
  can't** send files to the trash or restore them, or mark stickers. That's
  done in the dashboard.
- **It can't upload SVG**, for security reasons, even though the dashboard
  accepts it.
- **If you have many files, the list reaches it trimmed**, and it will tell
  you so. Ask for it filtered: «list the sendable files of my sales funnel».

Phrases that work:

- *"List my funnel's sendable files with their send trigger."*
- *"Upload this PDF to the library and make it sendable for my sales funnel,
  with the send trigger 'catalogo'."*
- *"Search the playbook for all the file markers and tell me which ones don't
  match any active sendable file."*
- *"Before touching the playbook, tell me the exact send triggers of this
  funnel's files."*
- *"I already uploaded the catalog to the Library: read it, make it sendable
  for my funnel and write the shared section with what it says."*

<Warning>
**If the file is over 2.5 MB, the upload fails.** Your assistant may explain it
to you, or you may only see an error. Either way the fix is the same: upload it
through the dashboard and then ask your assistant to link it.
</Warning>
