Each funnel has its playbook, its agent with its persona, its files, its catalog
and its settings. **From your funnel's card on Home you can duplicate it, share
it with another account or delete it.** And if someone shares one with you, you
review it and accept it from your Home or from the link you receive.

This guide covers those four things, and above all **what travels and what
doesn't** in each one.

The dashboard interface is in Spanish, so buttons and menus are named here in
Spanish with the English meaning in parentheses.

<Note>
**Where it is.** Go to [optimind.darkfunnels.ai](https://optimind.darkfunnels.ai)
and stay on **Inicio** ("Home"). In the section with your funnels, each funnel
has its card. At the top, next to the on/off switch, is the **⋯** button
(«Opciones del embudo», "Funnel options"). Pressing it opens a menu with, at
most, three options: **Duplicar embudo** ("Duplicate funnel"), **Compartir
embudo…** ("Share funnel…") and **Eliminar embudo…** ("Delete funnel…").
</Note>

<Frame caption="The ⋯ menu on the funnel's card: duplicate, share and, at the bottom, delete.">
  <img src="/images/embudos/01-menu-del-embudo.png" alt="Funnel card on Inicio (Home) with the menu open and the options Duplicar embudo, Compartir embudo… and Eliminar embudo…" />
</Frame>

<Tip>
**"Funnel" and "agent" aren't the same thing.** The funnel is what you
duplicate, share or delete from this menu; the agent is the AI that chats with
your customers inside each funnel.
[The apps](/en/getting-started/the-apps#app-agent-and-funnel-are-not-the-same-thing)
explains it.
</Tip>

## What you need, in one table

| You want to… | Use | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Try another version of the script without touching the funnel that's already selling | **Duplicar embudo** | A copy is born in your same account, with no WhatsApp connected. |
| Give your funnel to another business, to a partner or to another DarkFunnels account that signs in with a different email | **Compartir embudo…** | The other account receives a copy and puts its details in place of yours. |
| Make the funnel stop existing | **Eliminar embudo…** | It's deleted along with its conversations and its contacts. If it has contacts, sales or notes, we save an Excel file for you first. |
| Have it stop answering for a while, without deleting anything | The card's **switch** | The funnel is **Pausado** ("Paused") and its place among your active funnels is freed up. |

## Duplicate a funnel

**Duplicating creates a copy of the funnel inside your same account.** It's for
trying a different script, another way of charging or another persona, without
touching the funnel that's already answering.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the menu and press «Duplicar embudo»">
    On the card of the funnel you want to copy, press **⋯** and then **Duplicar
    embudo**. While it works, the option says **Duplicando…** ("Duplicating…")
    and can't be pressed again.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait without closing the page">
    A curtain appears with the notice **«Un momento, por favor no cierres esta
    página»** ("One moment, please don't close this page") and below it
    *«Estamos duplicando tu embudo con todo su contenido.»* ("We're duplicating
    your funnel with all its content.")
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the new card">
    When it finishes, the notice **«Embudo duplicado como «Ventas Moda Sol
    (copia)»»** ("Funnel duplicated as «Ventas Moda Sol (copia)»") appears and
    the new card shows up next to the others. If you duplicate it again while
    the first copy keeps that name, the next one is called **«Ventas Moda Sol
    (copia 2)»**, and so on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect a WhatsApp to it">
    **The copy is born with no WhatsApp connected.** For it to answer, connect
    a number to it in **Personalizar → Canales** ("Customize → Channels"), the
    same as the first time. [Connect WhatsApp](/en/getting-started/connect-whatsapp)
    explains it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
**The copy takes up an active funnel slot.** If you've already reached the
active funnel limit of your plan or your trial, the same window appears as when
you create a funnel (for example, **«Llegaste a tu límite de embudos
activos»**, "You've reached your active funnel limit") and the copy isn't
created. You can switch off another funnel or add a slot:
[Pricing](/en/getting-started/pricing) explains it.
</Warning>

**The copy is made in one go.** If something fails halfway, you aren't left
with half a funnel: either it's copied whole, or nothing is created.

**From that moment on, each funnel goes its own way.** Changing the copy's
playbook, persona or settings doesn't touch the original, and vice versa. There
are two exceptions, because they belong to your whole business and not to one
funnel:

- **The Library.** The copy uses the same files, with the same sending and
  memory permissions. They aren't duplicated. The files the agent had in
  memory are processed again for the copy, and that uses balance.
  [The Library](/en/guides/library) covers it.
- **The Catalog.** The copy sells the same products as the original. If you
  change a price in the Catalog, it changes for both. For a product to be sold
  by only one of them, tick it in «Embudos que lo venden» ("Funnels that sell
  it") (covered in [the catalog](/en/guides/catalog)).

<Note>
**Duplicar embudo doesn't appear for the whole team.** It's seen by the same
people who see the **Crear embudo** ("Create funnel") button on Inicio. If you
don't get it, ask whoever manages your account. Who can do what:
[Invite your team](/en/getting-started/invite-your-team).
</Note>

## Share a funnel with another account

**Sharing sends a full copy of your funnel to another DarkFunnels account.**
The other person reviews it, swaps your details for theirs and accepts it. On
accepting, the funnel is copied into their account and the dashboard takes them
to scan their WhatsApp QR. If they don't have an account yet, they create it
from the same link.

Two things worth being clear on before you start:

- **The funnel is copied as it is on the day the other person accepts**, not
  as it was when you shared it. If you change it in the meantime, they get the
  new version.
- **After accepting, the two copies are independent.** What you change in
  yours doesn't reach theirs, nor the other way round.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open «Compartir embudo…»">
    On the funnel's card, press **⋯** and then **Compartir embudo…**. The
    **«Compartir «Ventas Moda Sol»»** ("Share «Ventas Moda Sol»") window
    opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read what's copied and what isn't">
    The window says it in two lines:

    - **Se copia** ("Copied"): *«Manual, persona, archivos, catálogo,
      variables, etiquetas, agenda y ajustes.»* ("Playbook, persona, files,
      catalog, variables, tags, calendar and settings.")
    - **No se copia** ("Not copied"): *«Conversaciones, contactos, pedidos,
      conexiones ni tus números de aviso.»* ("Conversations, contacts, orders,
      connections or your notification numbers.")

    The detail is further down, in
    [What travels when you duplicate and when you share](#what-travels-when-you-duplicate-and-when-you-share).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type the other account's email">
    In **Correo de la otra cuenta** ("The other account's email"), type the
    email that person signs in (or will sign in) to DarkFunnels with. Press
    **Enviar solicitud** ("Send request").

    <Frame caption="The share window: what's copied, what isn't and the other account's email.">
      <img src="/images/embudos/02-compartir.png" alt="«Compartir» (Share) window with the Se copia and No se copia blocks, the Correo de la otra cuenta field and the Enviar solicitud button" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send them the link by WhatsApp too">
    **«Solicitud enviada a…»** ("Request sent to…") appears with the email. The
    other person gets an email with your business name in the subject (for
    example, *«Moda Sol te compartió su embudo «Ventas Moda Sol»»*, "Moda Sol
    shared their funnel «Ventas Moda Sol» with you") and the **Ver el embudo**
    ("See the funnel") button. If the email couldn't go out, the window tells
    you.

    In both cases you have the **Copiar enlace** ("Copy link") button: paste
    it into a WhatsApp chat with that person. It's the surest way for them to
    see it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
**The playbook travels as is.** The window itself warns you: *«si trae tu Yape,
tu cuenta o tus números, la otra persona los verá y deberá cambiarlos por los
suyos.»* ("if it contains your Yape, your account or your numbers, the other
person will see them and will have to change them for theirs."). Whoever
receives it has a form to replace them before accepting, but **they'll see
them anyway**. If there's something you don't want another person to read,
remove it before sharing: check the playbook, its memory, the files, the
persona, the referral program messages, the product descriptions and your store
addresses.
</Warning>

<Warning>
**The link works like a key.** Whoever opens it can accept the copy into their
own account, if they're the one who manages it, and the link is good for a
single copy. Send it only to the right person. If you sent it to the wrong
person, cancel the request (we tell you how below).
</Warning>

### Follow your request: «Compartido con» ("Shared with")

When you open **Compartir embudo…** again on that funnel, the **Compartido con**
("Shared with") list appears below: each email with its status.

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Pendiente** ("Pending") | The other person hasn't accepted or rejected it yet. Next to the status you have **Cancelar** ("Cancel"). |
| **Copiando…** ("Copying…") | The other person accepted and the funnel is being copied into their account. |
| **Aceptado** ("Accepted") | They already have their copy. |
| **Rechazado** ("Rejected") | The other person rejected it. |
| **Cancelado** ("Cancelled") | You cancelled it, or you replaced it by sharing again with the same email. |
| **Vencido** ("Expired") | 14 days went by with no answer. |

- **The request expires after 14 days.** The email the other person receives
  states the date. After that, share it again.
- **Sharing again with the same email replaces the previous request.** The one
  that was still pending is cancelled and its link stops working: the new one
  is the one that counts.
- **If you delete the funnel before the other person accepts**, their link no
  longer works.

<Note>
**Sharing belongs to whoever manages your account.** The menu shows
**Compartir embudo…** to everyone who sees the card, but if you don't manage
the account, when you press **Enviar solicitud** you get *«Solo quien
administra la cuenta puede compartir embudos.»* ("Only whoever manages the
account can share funnels.")
</Note>

## What travels when you duplicate and when you share

Duplicating and sharing copy almost the same things. **The big difference is in
your number lists and your Datos del negocio ("Business details"):** when you
duplicate, they keep your values, because the copy is still yours; when you
share, those values don't travel and whoever receives the funnel puts in
theirs.

Careful: whatever is written in the playbook or in the memory (your Yape, your
account, your numbers) does travel, unless whoever receives it changes it in
«Tus datos en este embudo» ("Your details in this funnel").

| What | When you duplicate (your account) | When you share (another account) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Playbook** | The current version, whole. | The current version, whole, with the changes the recipient makes in «Tus datos en este embudo». |
| **Persona** | Yes. | Yes. The voice and the avatar may be left out: if that happens, the form warns you. |
| **Memory** | Yes. | Yes, already processed: it doesn't need processing again. |
| **Library files** | The same files, with the same permissions. Not the ones in the trash. | The ones that funnel uses are copied to the recipient's Library, with their permissions. |
| **Catalog** | The same products. | A copy of the products that funnel sells, **without stock control** and only for the new funnel. |
| **Variables and tags** | Yes. | Yes. Tags arrive without being assigned to anyone. |
| **Calendar** (services and schedules) | Yes. The booking page arrives switched off. | Yes, without the schedule blocks. The booking page arrives switched off. |
| **Configuración** ("Settings": model, schedules, typing slips, referral program…) | Yes. | Yes. |
| **Informar de las ventas** ("Report sales"), **Reportes** ("Reports") and **Alertas de error** ("Error alerts") | The same numbers. | **Your numbers don't travel:** they go to the WhatsApp the recipient puts in. |
| **Envío de imágenes y archivos** ("Sending images and files") | The same numbers. | If you used it, it stays on, but towards the recipient's WhatsApp. |
| **Números frecuentes** ("Frequent numbers") | The same ones. | Only the contacts named by the playbook's notifications, with the number the recipient types in. |
| **Números de prueba** ("Test numbers") | The same ones. | Empty. |
| **Datos del negocio** ("Business details": Yape, account, social media…) | With your values. | The name of each detail travels; **the value is put in by the recipient**. |
| **Conversations, contacts and orders** | No. | No. |
| **WhatsApp** | The copy is born unconnected. | The copy is born unconnected. |

The sections of the settings are explained in
[Funnel settings](/en/guides/funnel-settings).

## If someone shares a funnel with you

You find out in one of these three ways:

- **An email** with the subject *«… te compartió su embudo «…»»* ("… shared
  their funnel «…» with you") and the **Ver el embudo** ("See the funnel")
  button.
- **A link** someone sends you by WhatsApp. It's the same one as in the email.
- **A notice on your Inicio**: *«… te compartió el embudo «…»»* ("… shared the
  funnel «…» with you"), with the **Revisar** ("Review") and **Rechazar**
  ("Reject") buttons. It appears if you sign in with the same email it was
  shared to and your account already existed at that moment. If you created
  your account afterwards, go in through the link.

<Info>
**Accepting a funnel belongs to whoever manages the account.** If you don't
manage it, the notice on Inicio has no buttons and says *«Pídele a quien
administra tu cuenta que lo acepte desde su Inicio.»* ("Ask whoever manages
your account to accept it from their Home."). The fastest way: pass the link to
that person so they open it with their session. Next time, ask for it to be
shared straight to their email.
</Info>

### If you don't have an account yet

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the link">
    The **«Te compartieron un embudo.»** ("A funnel was shared with you.") page
    opens: who's sharing it with you, what the funnel is called and how many
    chapters, files and products it brings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your account with that same email">
    The page's notice tells you which email it was shared to. Press **Crear mi
    cuenta** ("Create my account"): the sign-up form already has that email
    filled in. If you already have an account, press **Inicia sesión** ("Sign
    in").
  </Step>
  <Step title="You go straight back to approve it">
    When you finish signing up, the copilot doesn't open to build a funnel: you
    return to this page to review and accept the funnel. If you close the tab
    halfway, on your Inicio, from that same browser, the notice **«Tienes un
    embudo compartido por aprobar»** ("You have a shared funnel to approve") is
    waiting for you with the **Revisar** button.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Sign-up itself is covered in
[Create your account](/en/getting-started/create-your-account).

### Review and accept

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the review">
    From the notice on Inicio, press **Revisar**: the **«Te compartieron
    «…»»** ("«…» was shared with you") window opens. From the link, the same
    form appears on the page.

    At the top you have the summary: **Embudo** ("Funnel"), **Te lo comparte**
    ("Shared by") and **Incluye** ("Includes") (for example, *«4 capítulos · 6
    archivos · 12 productos»*, "4 chapters · 6 files · 12 products").
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in «Tu embudo» (Your funnel)">
    | Field | What to put |
    | --- | --- |
    | **Nombre del embudo** ("Funnel name") | The original's name comes filled in. Change it if you like: it's the name of the card on your Inicio. |
    | **Tu nombre comercial** ("Your trade name") | *«Así se presenta el embudo ante tus clientes.»* ("This is how the funnel introduces itself to your customers.") |
    | **Nombre de la vendedora** ("Salesperson's name") | The name of the original's persona comes filled in. *«Al conectar, tu perfil de WhatsApp tomará este nombre.»* ("When you connect, your WhatsApp profile will take this name.") If you change it, the copy also changes it wherever the playbook writes its full name. |
    | **Tu WhatsApp para avisos de venta y comprobantes** ("Your WhatsApp for sales notifications and receipts") | **Required**, with country code: `51 987 654 321`. That's where your sales, your reports and your customers' payment photos reach you. Your number comes filled in if it's already in your account. |
  </Step>
  <Step title="Swap their details for yours: «Tus datos en este embudo»">
    It's the list of what the other person's funnel has written in it that is
    theirs: their WhatsApp, their Yape or their account, the account holder,
    their social media, their brand. It comes in groups — **WhatsApp y
    teléfonos** ("WhatsApp and phones"), **Pagos** ("Payments") and **Nombre,
    redes y enlaces** ("Name, social media and links") — and each group counts
    how many details already have a destination (for example, *2/3*).

    Each row shows on the left the sentence where the detail appears (column
    **«En el embudo de…»**, "In …'s funnel") and on the right your field
    (**«En tu copia»**, "In your copy"). For each detail you have three paths:

    - **You type yours.** In the sentence, theirs appears crossed out and
      yours next to it: that's how it will look in your copy. The row says
      **Se cambia** ("Gets changed").
    - **You leave it as is** with the row's checkbox: **Es mío, se queda**
      ("It's mine, it stays") in payments, or **Mantener este número** ("Keep
      this number"), **Mantener este enlace** ("Keep this link"), **Mantener
      este usuario** ("Keep this username") or **Mantener este nombre** ("Keep
      this name"). The row says **Se queda** ("Stays").
    - **You leave it empty.** The row tells you what will happen with that
      detail: **Pendiente** ("Pending"; we remind you in the Manual) or **Se
      queda**, depending on the detail.

    In **Pagos** there may also be **Cambiar toda la línea** ("Change the whole
    line"): it's for rewriting an entire payment line (bank, number and
    holder) in one go. It's optional.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in who it notifies and your business details">
    If the playbook's notifications name someone (for example, «Almacén»,
    "Warehouse"), in **A quién avisa el embudo** ("Who the funnel notifies")
    you have a field **Número de «Almacén»** ("«Almacén»'s number"). Whatever
    you leave empty stays pending: that notification doesn't go out until you
    give it a number in [Números frecuentes](/en/guides/funnel-settings)
    ("Frequent numbers"). If **«Equipo de ventas»** ("Sales team") appears, it
    notifies the WhatsApp you entered above.

    If the funnel uses business details, in **Tus datos del negocio** ("Your
    business details") you have one field for each. Whatever you leave empty
    will say «(pendiente)» ("(pending)") until you fill it in under
    [Datos del negocio](/en/guides/funnel-settings) ("Business details").
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read «Revisa también» (Also check)">
    When it applies, the form leaves you notes about what can't be changed from
    here. For example: that the persona's voice or avatar belong to the account
    of whoever is sharing, that the store addresses are theirs, that the
    products arrive without stock control or that the booking page arrives
    switched off.

    At the end of «Tus datos en este embudo», just before these notes, the list
    **«Revisa estos archivos en tu Librería después de aceptar»** ("Check these
    files in your Library after accepting") may also appear. They're files
    that show the sharer's details (for example, the photo of their Yape, an
    audio with their voice or a PDF with their account). What's **inside** a
    file can't be replaced from here: you'll have to change that file.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Press «Aceptar y copiar el embudo» (Accept and copy the funnel)">
    The button changes to **Copiando el embudo…** ("Copying the funnel…") and
    then to **Abriendo tu QR…** ("Opening your QR…"). When it finishes, the
    dashboard takes you to **Personalizar → Canales** ("Customize → Channels")
    with the new funnel's QR ready to scan. Follow
    [Connect WhatsApp](/en/getting-started/connect-whatsapp).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
**You don't have to finish in one go.** What you type is saved automatically in
your account: at the bottom you read *«Se guarda solo mientras escribes»*
("Saves on its own while you type") and then *«Guardado · puedes cerrar y volver
cuando quieras»* ("Saved · you can close and come back whenever you like").
When you come back, the top says *«Seguimos donde lo dejaste: recuperamos lo
que escribiste el…»* ("Picking up where you left off: we recovered what you
typed on…") with the day and time. What you typed is only seen by your
business, and it's deleted when you accept, when you reject, or when the
request is cancelled or expires.
</Tip>

**The marker at the bottom tells you how far along you are:** *«Tu copia está al
67 %»* ("Your copy is at 67%"), and with everything complete, *«¡Todo listo! Tu
copia sale con tus datos»* ("All set! Your copy goes out with your details").
You don't need to reach 100% to accept: **your WhatsApp for notifications is
enough**. Whatever you leave pending, the Manual reminds you of it.

What you change here is replaced in the text that travels with the copy: in the
playbook, the memory, the messages that accompany the files, the persona, the
referral program messages and the product descriptions.

<Warning>
**Before copying, it's checked that you have room for one more active funnel.**
If not, the form says *«Ya tienes N embudos activos de M: apaga uno o contrata
otro cupo.»* ("You already have N active funnels out of M: switch one off or
get another slot.") with the **Ver mi plan** ("See my plan") link, and nothing
is copied. If your trial has already ended: *«Tu periodo de prueba terminó:
suscríbete para activar este embudo.»* ("Your trial period has ended:
subscribe to activate this funnel.")
</Warning>

<Note>
**If your account is in several businesses**, the funnel is copied into the
business you have active when you accept. Switch to the right one first, as
[Invite your team](/en/getting-started/invite-your-team#one-account-in-several-businesses)
explains.
</Note>

### After accepting: the banner in the Manual

If you left details pending, or if the funnel came with files containing the
sharer's details, when you open the copy's [Manual](/en/guides/sales-playbook)
("Playbook") you'll see a banner above the document:

> **Datos por revisar de la copia.** Este embudo llegó de otra cuenta y todavía
> trae datos suyos.
>
> ("Copy details to review. This funnel came from another account and still
> has its details in it.")

Below it, the list of what's pending and, depending on what's missing, these
buttons:

| Button | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **Completar en Ajustes** ("Complete in Settings") | Opens the Configuración ("Settings") screen so you can type in the numbers and business details you left empty. Check that the selector has the copy chosen. |
| **Buscar en el manual** ("Search in the playbook") | Opens the playbook's [«Datos» ("Data") panel](/en/guides/sales-playbook), which tells you where each detail appears so you can change it by hand. |
| **Ver archivos** ("See files") | Opens the [«Recursos» ("Resources") panel](/en/guides/sales-playbook) with the agent's files. From **«Gestionar en Librería →»** ("Manage in Library →") you go to the Library, where you replace the ones that show the sharer's details. |
| **Ya lo revisé** ("I've checked it") | Removes the banner once you've sorted it out. |

### Reject or leave it for later

- **Rechazar** ("Reject") is in the notice on Inicio and at the end of the
  form. The request is closed with the notice *«Solicitud rechazada»*
  ("Request rejected") and nothing is copied.
- **Ahora no** ("Not now"), in the window on Inicio, just closes it: you can go
  back to **Revisar** while the request is still valid (14 days from when it
  was shared with you).

## Delete a funnel

**Deleting is permanent:** the funnel goes with its conversations and its
contacts. If you only want it to stop answering for a while, don't delete it:
**switch it off with the switch on its card**. It stays **Pausado**, keeps its
playbook, its conversations and its contacts, and its place among your active
funnels is freed up.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open «Eliminar embudo…»">
    On the card, press **⋯** and then **Eliminar embudo…**, the last option in
    the menu. **«¿Eliminar el embudo «Ventas Moda Sol»?»** ("Delete the funnel
    «Ventas Moda Sol»?") opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read what's lost">
    First it says *«Contando lo que tiene este embudo…»* ("Counting what this
    funnel has…") and then it shows you the figures:

    - how many **contacts** it has (and how many *«también hablan con otro
      embudo y se quedan allá»*, "also talk to another funnel and stay there");
    - how many **saved details** there are and in how many **variables**;
    - how many **recorded sales**;
    - how many **notes**.

    If there's none of that, it says *«Este embudo no tiene contactos, ventas
    ni notas.»* ("This funnel has no contacts, sales or notes."). If it
    couldn't count, it says so; even so, if the funnel has contacts, sales or
    notes, the copy is saved before deleting.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Look at the copy we save for you">
    If there are contacts, sales or notes, the **«Te guardamos una copia»**
    ("We save a copy for you") block appears: *«Antes de borrar te enviaremos
    un Excel con contactos, variables, ventas y notas a…»* ("Before deleting
    we'll send you an Excel file with contacts, variables, sales and notes
    to…") and the emails. If you want it now, press **Descargar copia ahora**
    ("Download copy now").
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type the name and confirm">
    In **Escribe su nombre para confirmar** ("Type its name to confirm"), type
    the funnel's name exactly as it is. Only then does **Sí, eliminar** ("Yes,
    delete") become active.

    <Frame caption="The confirmation says what is deleted and what is kept, offers the copy and asks you to type the name.">
      <img src="/images/embudos/03-eliminar.png" alt="«¿Eliminar el embudo «Agente de Ventas»?» (Delete the funnel?) window with what is deleted and what is kept, the Te guardamos una copia block with Descargar copia ahora, the Escribe su nombre para confirmar field and the Sí, eliminar and Cancelar buttons" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Done">
    The card disappears instantly and **«Tu embudo «Ventas Moda Sol» ha sido

    plano.»* ("We're finishing deleting its conversations and files in the

### What goes and what stays

The window sums it up like this: *«Es permanente: se borran sus conversaciones,
capítulos, contactos, archivos y persona, y se cierra su sesión de WhatsApp. Se
conservan el consumo facturado y las ventas cobradas.»* ("It's permanent: its
conversations, chapters, contacts, files and persona are deleted, and its
WhatsApp session is closed. Billed usage and collected sales are kept."). In
detail:

- **It stops answering as soon as it's taken down**, after saving the copy (if
  there's a copy). The card goes instantly, but in a large funnel saving the
  copy can take a little while, and meanwhile the funnel is still active. Once
  it's taken down, its place among your active funnels is freed up. The heavy
  part (the conversations and their files) finishes being deleted in the
  background.
- **The sales the agent recorded in its chats are deleted**: they're the
  "recorded sales" in the count, and they go in the **Ventas** ("Sales") sheet
  of the Excel file. **The orders in Órdenes ("Orders") aren't deleted**, and
  the usage you were already charged for stays in your billing.
- **Its WhatsApp session is closed:** the number is no longer linked to the
  dashboard.
- **Its playbook and the playbook's version history are deleted.**
- **Its persona is deleted** if no other funnel of yours uses it.
- **The files in your Library stay there** for your other funnels: what goes is
  this funnel's permission over them. The "files" the window deletes are the
  ones from its chats: the photos, audios and documents that were sent in its
  conversations.
- **The Catalog products stay.** If a product was marked for this funnel, in its
  funnels column you'll see **«Embudo eliminado»** ("Funnel deleted"): open it
  and tick which funnels should sell it (see [the catalog](/en/guides/catalog)).
- **The same customer in another funnel isn't touched.** Each funnel has its
  own record of each customer: if that number also writes to another of your
  funnels, their record in that other funnel stays.

<Warning>
**Deleting isn't reserved for whoever manages the account.** The menu shows it
to whoever sees the card, and anyone on your team with access to Ventas
("Sales") can do it. That's why the Excel copy reaches whoever deletes **and
also the account owner**, if they're different people. Who has Ventas on your
team: [Invite your team](/en/getting-started/invite-your-team).
</Warning>

### The Excel copy

If the funnel has contacts, sales or notes, **before anything is deleted an
Excel copy is saved.** If that copy can't be saved, nothing is deleted.

- **It reaches you by email** with the subject *«Copia de tu embudo «…» antes
  de borrarlo»* ("Copy of your funnel «…» before deleting it"), to whoever
  deletes and to the account owner.
- **It has five sheets:** **Resumen** ("Summary"), **Contactos** ("Contacts";
  with their saved details, tags and whether they're still in another funnel),
  **Ventas** ("Sales"), **Notas** ("Notes") and **Variables**.
- **It doesn't include the full conversations**: for each contact only the
  text of their last message comes. If you need a whole chat, copy it from
  [Conversations](/en/guides/conversations) before deleting.
- If the funnel has no contacts, sales or notes, there's nothing to copy and no
  email is sent.

## Common problems

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I don't see «Duplicar embudo» (Duplicate funnel) in the menu">
    Your access doesn't include it: it appears for the same people who see the
    **Crear embudo** ("Create funnel") button on Inicio. Ask whoever manages
    your account to duplicate it for you. The option appears for whoever has
    **Ver saldo y facturación** ("See balance and billing") or manages the
    apps.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Duplicating shows «Llegaste a tu límite de embudos activos» (You've reached your active funnel limit)">
    The copy counts as one more funnel and you have no room left. Switch off a
    funnel you don't use with the switch on its card and duplicate again, or
    add a slot from **Saldo y facturación** ("Balance and billing"). On the
    trial, the window tells you how many active funnels it includes and offers
    you a subscription.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="«Solo quien administra la cuenta puede compartir embudos.» (Only whoever manages the account can share funnels.)">
    Sharing belongs to whoever manages the account. Ask them to share it from
    their Inicio.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="«Ese es tu propio correo: para tener otra copia en tu cuenta usa «Duplicar embudo».» (That's your own email: to have another copy in your account, use «Duplicar embudo».)">
    You typed your own email, and you can't share a funnel with yourself. To
    have another copy in the same business, use **Duplicar embudo**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The other person didn't get the email">
    Have them check their spam folder. The most direct way: open **Compartir
    embudo…** again, share it again to the same email and press **Copiar
    enlace** to send it to them by WhatsApp. The new request replaces the
    previous one.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="«Vas demasiado rápido…» (You're going too fast…) when sending the request">
    Your account can send up to 10 requests per hour. Wait for the hour to
    pass and try again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The link says it expired, or that the request was cancelled">
    The page tells you the reason: *«Este enlace venció o el embudo original ya
    no existe. Pide que te lo compartan otra vez.»* ("This link expired or the
    original funnel no longer exists. Ask for it to be shared with you
    again."), *«Quien te compartió el embudo canceló la solicitud.»* ("Whoever
    shared the funnel with you cancelled the request."), *«Esta solicitud se
    rechazó.»* ("This request was rejected.") or *«Este embudo ya se aceptó.»*
    ("This funnel has already been accepted."). In every case, ask for it to be
    shared with you again. If it says *«…ya está en tu cuenta. Solo te falta
    escanear el QR de tu WhatsApp.»* ("…is already in your account. All that's
    left is to scan your WhatsApp QR."), press **Escanear el QR** ("Scan the
    QR").
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A funnel was shared with me and I don't see the notice on my Inicio (Home)">
    The notice appears if you sign in with the same email it was shared to and
    your account already existed when it was shared. If that's not your case,
    open the link in the email or ask for it to be sent to you by WhatsApp.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="«Solo quien administra tu cuenta (…) puede aceptar un embudo.» (Only whoever manages your account (…) can accept a funnel.)">
    Accepting belongs to whoever manages the account. Pass the link to that
    person so they open it with their session.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="«Este embudo ya es de tu cuenta.» (This funnel already belongs to your account.)">
    You opened it with a session from the same business that shared it. To
    have another copy there, use **Duplicar embudo** from Inicio.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Accepting shows a notice with the name of a detail">
    For example, *«…: escribe el dato sin comillas, #, corchetes ni saltos de
    línea.»* ("…: type the detail without quotes, #, brackets or line
    breaks.") or *«…: escribe un WhatsApp válido con código de país (por
    ejemplo 51 987 654 321).»* ("…: type a valid WhatsApp number with country
    code (for example 51 987 654 321)."). The notice starts with the name of
    the detail that needs fixing: fix it in its row and accept again. What
    you've already typed isn't lost.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="«Embudo copiado. No se pudieron copiar N archivo(s)…» (Funnel copied. N file(s) couldn't be copied…)">
    The funnel was copied, but those files didn't travel (the notice says
    which ones). Ask the person who shared it with you for them and upload them
    to your [Library](/en/guides/library), with their permissions.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I deleted a funnel and the card came back">
    The deletion didn't happen. If the notice says *«No pudimos preparar la
    copia de seguridad de tu embudo, así que no borramos nada. Inténtalo de
    nuevo en unos minutos.»* ("We couldn't prepare your funnel's backup, so we
    didn't delete anything. Try again in a few minutes."), that's what
    happened: without a copy, nothing is deleted. Wait a few minutes and try
    again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="«No pudimos enviarte el correo con la copia» (We couldn't send you the email with the copy)">
    The funnel was deleted and the copy was saved, but the email didn't reach
    everyone (you, the owner, or neither of you). Press **Descargar copia**
    ("Download copy") in that same notice and save it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="«Embudo eliminado, pero no se pudo cerrar su sesión de WhatsApp» (Funnel deleted, but its WhatsApp session couldn't be closed)">
    The funnel was deleted, but the phone may still show the dashboard as a
    linked device. On that line's phone, open WhatsApp → **Linked devices**
    (*Dispositivos vinculados* on a phone set to Spanish) and close that
    session.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="If I change my funnel after sharing it, does the other person get the change?">
    It depends on when. While they haven't accepted, they get the funnel as it
    is on the day they accept, with your changes. Once it's accepted, no: the
    two copies are independent.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does the other person see my conversations, my customers or my orders?">
    No. Those don't travel. What they do see is everything that travels in
    writing: the playbook, the memory, the files, the persona, the referral
    program messages, the product descriptions and your store addresses. Check
    them before sharing.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I download a funnel's Excel file without deleting it?">
    Yes. Open **Eliminar embudo…**, press **Descargar copia ahora** and then
    **Cancelar** ("Cancel"). That copy is put together on the spot, and nothing
    is deleted until you type the name and press **Sí, eliminar**. The button
    appears when the funnel has contacts, sales or notes.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I recover a funnel I deleted?">
    Not from the dashboard: the dialog itself warns that it's permanent. What
    remains is the Excel copy with its contacts, variables, sales and notes.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does duplicating or sharing use balance?">
    When you duplicate, the Library files the agent had in memory are processed
    again for the copy, and that uses balance, as
    [The Library](/en/guides/library) explains. When you share, the memory and
    the already-processed files travel as they are, ready to use. What the AI
    does afterwards, when the copy's agent has conversations, uses balance like
    any agent: [Credits and top-ups](/en/getting-started/credits-and-top-ups).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## The same thing, from your assistant

<Note>
This section is **optional and only applies if you connected Claude, ChatGPT or
Codex** to your business. If you work in the dashboard, you're done.
</Note>

Your assistant can **duplicate** a funnel if your connection URL includes the
`agents_write` group (or `features=all`) and doesn't carry `read_only=true`;
how the URL is built is explained in
[The connection URL](/en/reference/connection-url). Just as in the dashboard,
the copy counts as one more funnel and is born without WhatsApp.

**Sharing with another account, accepting a shared funnel and deleting a funnel
are done in the dashboard.** Your assistant has no tools for that.

A sentence that works:

- *"Duplicate my sales funnel to try another version of the script."*
