**Codex is OpenAI's assistant**, the one you use in its app, in your editor or
in the terminal. Connected to DarkFunnels, it works on your business while you
chat with it: it reads your agent's playbook, the catalog, the conversations
and the customers, and suggests changes.

<Note>
**It is optional.** You don't need it to sell: your agent handles WhatsApp
just the same. It is a convenience if you already use Codex.
</Note>

The dashboard is in Spanish, so its buttons and on-screen messages are quoted
here exactly as you will see them, with the English meaning in parentheses.

## Before you start

- You need an **OpenAI account with access to Codex**.
- The Codex app, its editor extension and the terminal **share the same
  connection**. You connect it once and it works in all three.
- Have **the account you use to sign in to the DarkFunnels dashboard** at hand.
  That is the one you will authorize with.

This is the link you will paste:

```
https://mcp.darkfunnels.ai/mcp?features=all
```

Thanks to `?features=all`, you don't have to touch it again: the features we
release later reach it on their own.

<Tip>
**You also have it in the dashboard, ready to copy.** In the left-hand menu,
inside **Ventas** (*Sales*), click **Personalizar** (*Customize*) and choose
**Asistentes IA** (*AI assistants*). At the top is the link with its **Copiar
link** (*Copy link*) button. Further down, under **¿Dónde lo pego?** (*Where do
I paste it?*), the **Codex** tab has the settings path and the two terminal
commands, each with its **Copiar** (*Copy*) button.
</Tip>

## Connect it from the app or the editor extension

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the server">
    In Codex, go to **Settings → MCP servers → Add server** and choose
    **Streamable HTTP**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste the link and save">
    ```
    https://mcp.darkfunnels.ai/mcp?features=all
    ```
    Leave the token field **empty**. Authorization happens separately, with
    your account, not with a key.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize with your account">
    Your browser opens on the DarkFunnels **Autorizar acceso** (*Authorize
    access*) screen. Look at the **Autorizas como…** (*You are authorizing
    as…*) line: it has to show the email you use to sign in to the dashboard.
    If it does, click **Autorizar** (*Authorize*).

    If you weren't signed in, it first asks you to sign in. And if you had
    already authorized Codex with that account, it may go straight through,
    without showing you the button.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Connect it from the terminal

It takes two commands. The first saves the server in Codex; the second opens
your browser to authorize.

```bash
codex mcp add optimind --url "https://mcp.darkfunnels.ai/mcp?features=all"
```

```bash
codex mcp login optimind
```

- **Copy the whole command, quotes included.** Without them, the terminal may
  take the `?` in the link as a wildcard and the command fails.
- **`optimind` is the name Codex saves this connection under.** If you choose
  another one, use the same name in both commands.
- **Depending on your Codex version, the first command may already open the
  browser.** If you authorized there, you are done. The second one is for
  authorizing, or authorizing again, whenever you need to.

In the browser you see the same **Autorizar acceso** screen as above: check the
email and click **Autorizar**.

To check that it was saved, type `codex mcp list`: `optimind` has to appear.

## Try it

Open a **new** thread in Codex and ask it something, just like this:

- *"Review my conversations from yesterday and tell me where I'm losing
  sales."*
- *"Read my agent's playbook and suggest an improvement to the closing chapter;
  save it once I approve it."*
- *"Give me the customers who have gone more than 3 days without a reply, and
  what the agent would say to them to pick the conversation back up."*

**Check which account it signed in with.** Ask it *"Which DarkFunnels account
are you connected to?"*. It tells you the email and the company.

If you manage your company's apps, in the dashboard under **Personalizar →
Asistentes IA** you also see the **Conectado · última actividad** (*Connected ·
last activity*) badge, with the date, as soon as an assistant has worked in
your company.

## What it can do with this link

With `?features=all` your assistant can, among other things:

- Read and edit the agent's sales playbook, with a version history so you can
  undo.
- Review, create and update products in the catalog.
- Review conversations and customers, and tag whoever needs a follow-up.
- Warn you when your credit balance is about to run out, if your account can
  see the balance.

**That link also includes the features that have a real effect.** Some send
real WhatsApp messages, such as replying to a customer as an operator. Others
spend credits, such as optimizing the playbook with AI or asking the copilot.
And creating a new agent takes up a slot in your subscription.

**Would you rather it only read?** Connect it with this link:

```
https://mcp.darkfunnels.ai/mcp?features=all&read_only=true
```

To change the link later, remove the server and add it again with the new
link. The link's options are in [The connection URL](/en/reference/connection-url),
and each feature in the [Tool reference](/en/reference/tools).

<Note>
**It sees and does what your account can in the dashboard, nothing more.** If
you don't have access to an app in your company, your assistant can't use its
features either.

It doesn't charge your customers or transfer money: it has no features for
that. Your customers' messages reach it marked as data, not as commands. And
every action is logged: which tool it used, when, and with what result.
</Note>

## Cutting off access

There are two places, and it is best to use both.

<Steps>
  <Step title="In the dashboard: cut off access">
    Go to **Personalizar → Asistentes IA** and scroll down to the **Seguridad
    y acceso** (*Security and access*) card. Under **Asistentes con acceso a
    tu cuenta** (*Assistants with access to your account*) you see the ones you
    authorized, each with the date you did it.

    Click **Revocar** (*Revoke*) next to the one you want to cut off and
    confirm with **Revocar**. It loses access to your account within a few
    seconds. To use it again, you have to connect it again.
  </Step>
  <Step title="In Codex: remove it from the list">
    In the app or the extension: **Settings → MCP servers**, and delete it. In
    the terminal:

    ```bash
    codex mcp remove optimind
    ```
  </Step>
</Steps>

## If something goes wrong

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="It can't see your data, or sees it empty">
    The most common cause is that you authorized with **a different account**.
    Picking the wrong account doesn't give an error: Codex stays connected to
    that other account's business.

    Ask it *"Which DarkFunnels account are you connected to?"*. It tells you
    the email and the company.

    - If the account is yours but the company isn't the one you expected, see
      "It says your account changed company and won't save changes", below.
    - If both are right, see "It sees your data, but some features are
      missing".

    If the account isn't yours:

    1. In the dashboard ([optimind.darkfunnels.ai](https://optimind.darkfunnels.ai)),
       open your account menu (the circle with your initials, top right) and
       click **Cerrar sesión** (*Log out*). Sign in with the right account.
    2. Authorize Codex again. In the terminal: `codex mcp logout optimind` and
       then `codex mcp login optimind`. In the app: remove the server and add
       it again.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="It says your account changed company and won't save changes">
    This happens if your account is in **several companies**. Each connection
    is tied to the company you had selected in the dashboard the first time
    your assistant used it. If you later switch companies in the dashboard, or
    accept an invitation from another company (that also switches you to it),
    it stops saving changes until you take one of the two steps below: that
    way it doesn't write to the wrong company. What it reads, on the other
    hand, comes from the company you have selected now.

    - **To stay in the first company:** open your account menu in the
      dashboard and click that company (under its name it says **Cambiar a
      esta empresa**, *Switch to this company*).
    - **To work in the new one:** authorize Codex again. In the terminal:
      `codex mcp logout optimind` and then `codex mcp login optimind`. In the
      app: remove the server and add it again.

    If instead it says *«No se pudo confirmar en qué empresa trabaja esta
    conexión»* (*Could not confirm which company this connection works in*),
    it was a hiccup while checking and it didn't make the change. Ask it to
    try again in a few seconds.

    More on this in [Invite your team](/en/getting-started/invite-your-team),
    in the part about one account in several companies.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="It sees your data, but some features are missing">
    Your assistant works with **your permissions in that company**. If you
    don't have access to an app (for example, **Órdenes**, *Orders*), or your
    company doesn't have it switched on, it can't use its features. Ask
    whoever manages your team for access:
    [Invite your team](/en/getting-started/invite-your-team).

    If you have just been given access, wait a minute and open a new thread.

    Also check the link. If it has `read_only=true`, or if it doesn't have
    `features=all`, it connects fewer features. To change it, remove the server
    and add it with the link above; then open a new thread.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="New features don't show up">
    Start a **new thread**. Each conversation keeps the list of features it
    had when you opened it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="You authorized fine, but the tools don't show up in the app's thread">
    Some versions of the Codex desktop app have had a bug that stops the tools
    from loading in threads, even when authorization goes through
    ([openai/codex#20009](https://github.com/openai/codex/issues/20009)).

    **Update Codex** and open a new thread. If nothing changes, use Codex from
    the terminal or from the editor extension: they share the connection, so
    you don't have to connect it again.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The terminal command fails with «no matches found»">
    You copied it **without the quotes** and the terminal took the `?` in the
    link as a wildcard. Copy the whole command, with the quotes around the
    link.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="It says the session with DarkFunnels was revoked or expired">
    Someone clicked **Revocar** in the dashboard, or the authorization
    expired. Authorize again: in the terminal, `codex mcp login optimind`; in
    the app, remove the server and add it again.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The browser screen says «No se pudo autorizar» (Could not authorize)">
    The reason is written under the title. The most common one is that the
    authorization request expired or arrived incomplete: go back to Codex and
    start the authorization again, for example with
    `codex mcp login optimind`.

    If it says it could not connect to the server, check your internet
    connection and try again.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="It says «Sin créditos» (Out of credits)">
    Your balance ran out, and the AI features stay stopped until you top up.
    Top up in **Administración → Dinero → Saldo y facturación**
    (*Administration → Money → Balance and billing*). In the side menu, click
    **Administración** to expand it.

    If you don't see that entry with the block expanded, your account doesn't
    have access to the company's money: ask someone who does to top up. The
    step by step is in
    [Credits and top-ups](/en/getting-started/credits-and-top-ups).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
