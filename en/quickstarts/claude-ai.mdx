---

Works even on Claude's **free plan**, which allows one connector. The connection
lives in your account, so browser, desktop app and mobile all share it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the connector">
    In claude.ai: **Customize → Connectors → Add → Add custom connector**. On a
    team account, the organization owner adds it from the organization settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste the URL">
    ```
    https://mcp.darkfunnels.ai/mcp?features=all
    ```
    `?features=all` is the link you never have to revisit: features published
    later show up on their own.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize">
    Press **Connect** and authorize with the **same account you use to sign in
    to the DarkFunnels dashboard**. The authorization screen tells you which
    account you are signing in with — check it there.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Try it: *"Go through yesterday's conversations and tell me where I'm losing
sales."*

## Common problems

- **It can't see your data, or sees it empty** — you authorized with a different
  account. Ask it "which DarkFunnels account are you connected to?"; if it isn't
  yours, remove the connector, sign in to darkfunnels.ai with the right account
  and connect again.
- **New tools don't show up** — start a NEW chat: the tool list is frozen per
  conversation. Reconnecting is only needed to change the URL.
- **"Out of credits"** — top up in the dashboard (Billing); the agent's AI
  features stay paused until you do.
