**With Claude connected, you ask about your business from Claude's own chat**:
what happened in yesterday's conversations, where sales are dropping off, how to
improve your agent's sales playbook. Claude reads your real DarkFunnels data
and, if you ask it to, saves changes.

It is **optional**: your agent sells just the same without it. And it works
even on **Claude's free plan**, which allows one connector.

The dashboard interface is in Spanish, so its buttons and menus are named here
in Spanish with the English meaning in parentheses.

<Note>
**What you need.** A Claude account (the free one works) and the account you
use to sign in to the DarkFunnels dashboard. They are two different accounts:
on this page you link them.

Is your Claude account part of a team? The connector is added by the
organization's owner.
</Note>

## Where the link is in your dashboard

Go to the dashboard at [optimind.darkfunnels.ai](https://optimind.darkfunnels.ai).
In the left-hand menu, inside **Ventas** (*Sales*), click **Personalizar**
(*Customize*) — it is in the **Ajustes** (*Settings*) group — and choose the
**Asistentes IA** (*AI assistants*) tab. You can also get there by typing
`optimind.darkfunnels.ai/customize?panel=asistentes` in the browser's address
bar.

The first card, **Conecta tu asistente** (*Connect your assistant*), has
everything you need: the link, the **Copiar link** (*Copy link*) button, three
steps with your account's email already filled in, and the **Abrir Conectores
en Claude** (*Open Connectors in Claude*) button.

  <img src="/images/asistentes/01-conecta-tu-asistente.png" alt="Conecta tu asistente (Connect your assistant) card with the connection link, the Copiar link button, three numbered steps and the Abrir Conectores en Claude button" />
</Frame>

<Tip>
**You can also get there from Home.** While you see the **Pon tu empresa a
operar** (*Get your business running*) card on your Home (if it is folded into a
single bar, click **Ver pasos** — *View steps*), under **Cuando quieras**
(*Whenever you like*) there is the row **Conecta tu asistente de IA y
pregúntale por tus ventas** (*Connect your AI assistant and ask it about your
sales*). Its **Ver cómo →** (*See how*) button takes you to this same tab. It is
explained in [Your first look](/en/getting-started/first-look).
</Tip>

**Personalizar is part of Ventas.** If you don't see Ventas in your menu, you
can still connect: the link is the same one you have in step 1 below. That
said, your assistant will only be able to use the apps you have access to. And
the list for removing its access is also in Personalizar: without Ventas you
can't reach it. If you ever want to cut it off, ask whoever manages your company
for access to Ventas.

## Connect Claude, step by step

<Steps>
  <Step title="Copy the link">
    On the card, click **Copiar link**: the notice *«Link copiado»* (*Link
    copied*) appears. This is it:

    ```
    https://mcp.darkfunnels.ai/mcp?features=all
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Claude's connectors">
    Click **Abrir Conectores en Claude**: it takes you straight to the screen
    where you paste the link.

    If you'd rather get there by hand, in claude.ai go to **Customize →
    Connectors → Add → Add custom connector**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste the link and click Connect">
    If Claude asks you for a name for the connector, use one you'll recognize,
    for example DarkFunnels.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize with your dashboard account">
    A DarkFunnels tab opens. If you weren't signed in to the dashboard, it
    first asks you to sign in and then comes back to this screen on its own.

    The screen is titled **Autorizar acceso** (*Authorize access*) and tells you
    which assistant wants to connect to your account. Right below it is the
    line **Autorizas como** (*You are authorizing as*), with an email. **That
    email has to be the one for your dashboard account.** If it's a different
    one, click **Denegar** (*Deny*) and see the first item in
    [Common problems](#common-problems).

    If it's yours, click **Autorizar** (*Authorize*). You'll see **Acceso
    autorizado** (*Access authorized*) and you go back to Claude.

    If you had already authorized with that account before, this screen may not
    appear: you go straight back to Claude.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
**Picking the wrong account doesn't give an error.** Claude connects anyway,
but to the account you authorized with: you'll see another business, or an
empty one. That's why the screen shows you the email. Check it before you click
**Autorizar**.
</Warning>

## Try it

Open a **new chat** in Claude and paste one of these messages. They are the ones
on the dashboard's **Pruébalo con estos mensajes** (*Try it with these
messages*) card, where they appear in Spanish, each with its **Copiar**
(*Copy*) button:

- *"Review my conversations from yesterday and tell me where sales are being
  lost."*
- *"Read my agent's sales playbook and suggest an improvement to the closing
  chapter; save it once I approve it."*
- *"Give me the customers who have gone more than 3 days without a reply, and
  what the agent would say to them to pick things back up."*

Once your assistant has worked on your business, go back to **Personalizar →
Asistentes IA**. Next to the **Conecta tu asistente** title, **Conectado ·
última actividad** (*Connected · last activity*) appears, with the date. If you
connected in another tab, it updates when you return to the dashboard tab. On
Home, the **Conecta tu asistente de IA y pregúntale por tus ventas** row stops
showing **Ver cómo →**.

<Note>
**That badge, and the mark on Home, are seen by whoever manages your company's
apps** (to begin with, whoever created the account). It counts the activity of
any assistant connected to your company — yours or that of another person on
your team — and it is the date of the last time one worked, not a switch: it
doesn't turn off when you revoke an access.
</Note>

## What your assistant can do with this link

The same things the dashboard's **Qué puede hacer tu asistente** (*What your
assistant can do*) card lists:

- Read and edit your agent's sales playbook, with version history so you can
  undo.
- Review, create and update the products in your catalog.
- Review your conversations and customers, and tag the ones who need
  follow-up.
- Warn you when your balance is about to run out, if your account can see the
  balance.

**It works with your account's permissions, not more.** If in your company you
don't have access to an app — for example, **Órdenes** (*Orders*), which your
assistant calls «Pedidos» —, your assistant can't use its features. Nor those
of an app that isn't active in your company.

**You don't need to touch the link again.** The `?features=all` ending turns on all the
features, including the ones we publish later: they show up for you in a new
chat, if your account has access to the app they use.

<Warning>
**That link also turns on the features that write and send.** For example,
saving the playbook, deleting a product or sending a customer a real WhatsApp
message, as an operator. Those features are marked as writes, and Claude
usually uses that mark to ask your permission before using them. When it asks,
read what it is going to do before you accept.

Would you rather it only read? Add `&read_only=true` to the end of the link:

```
https://mcp.darkfunnels.ai/mcp?features=all&read_only=true
```

If you already had it connected, to change the link, remove the connector in
Claude and add it again with the new one.
</Warning>

Two more things:

- **It doesn't charge or transfer money.** What some features do spend is
  balance: optimizing the playbook with AI, asking the copilot, each reply from
  your agent when you test it, and reading the text in an image from the
  Library. It comes out of the same balance as your dashboard. And creating or
  duplicating a funnel takes up a slot in your plan.
- **Your customers' messages reach it marked as data, not as commands.** They
  are there for your assistant to analyze, not to obey.

All the link's options are in [The connection URL](/en/reference/connection-url),
and how your data is protected, in [Security](/en/security).

## Removing its access

In **Personalizar → Asistentes IA**, the **Seguridad y acceso** (*Security and
access*) card has the **Asistentes con acceso a tu cuenta** (*Assistants with
access to your account*) list: one row per assistant, with its name, the date
you authorized it (**Autorizado el …** — *Authorized on …*) and a **Revocar**
(*Revoke*) button.

<Frame caption="«Seguridad y acceso» (Security and access): how the connection is protected and the «Asistentes con acceso a tu cuenta» (Assistants with access to your account) list. In this screenshot it is empty; each assistant you authorize shows up there with its «Revocar» (Revoke) button.">
  <img src="/images/asistentes/03-seguridad-y-acceso.png" alt="Seguridad y acceso (Security and access) card with three bullet points about the connection and the Asistentes con acceso a tu cuenta (Assistants with access to your account) list, which reads Ningún asistente tiene acceso a tu cuenta ahora (No assistant has access to your account right now)" />
</Frame>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Revocar next to the assistant">
    In its place the notice *«… pierde el acceso a tu cuenta en unos segundos.
    Para volver a usarlo, tendrás que conectarlo de nuevo.»* (*… loses access to
    your account in a few seconds. To use it again, you'll have to connect it
    again.*) appears, with the **Revocar** and **Cancelar** (*Cancel*) buttons.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm with Revocar">
    The notice *«Acceso revocado: …»* (*Access revoked: …*) appears and the
    assistant disappears from the list. Within a few seconds it can no longer
    get into your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Remove the connector from Claude too">
    Revoking cuts off the access, but the connector stays in your Claude list.
    To remove it from there, in claude.ai go to **Customize → Connectors** and
    delete it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**The list belongs to your account.** It doesn't show the assistants that other
people on your team connected with their own accounts, and revoking yours
doesn't affect them. To use it again, connect it again from Claude and authorize
once more.

## Common problems

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="It can't see your data, or sees it empty">
    The most common cause is that you authorized with **another account**. Ask
    Claude *"which DarkFunnels account are you connected to?"*: it has a feature
    that tells it which email and which company it is connected with.

    If the email isn't yours:

    1. In claude.ai, go to **Customize → Connectors** and delete the connector.
    2. In the dashboard, open your account menu (the circle with your initials,
       top right), click **Cerrar sesión** (*Sign out*) and sign in with the
       right account.
    3. Connect again with the steps above. In **Autorizar acceso**, check that
       **Autorizas como** shows your email.

    If the email is yours but the company isn't, see the next item.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="It says your account changed company">
    It happens if your account is in **several companies** and you switch
    company in the dashboard or accept an invitation to another one (that leaves
    the new one active). Also, if your access to the connection's company was
    removed.

    Each connection stays **pinned, for saving changes, to the company you had
    active the first time your assistant used it**. If you later switch company
    — on your computer or on your phone, it makes no difference —, your
    assistant doesn't save changes: it warns you and touches nothing, so as not
    to write in the wrong company. **What it reads, on the other hand, already
    comes from the company you have active now**: if you were comparing figures,
    first go back to the connection's company.

    You have two ways out:

    - **To stay in the connection's company**, choose it again in your account
      menu, with **Cambiar a esta empresa** (*Switch to this company*).
    - **To work in the other one**, delete the connector in Claude and connect
      it again while you are in that company.

    Retrying without one of those two steps doesn't help. If the notice says
    your account no longer has access to the connection's company, only the
    second one works. How to switch company is in
    [Invite your team](/en/getting-started/invite-your-team#one-account-in-several-businesses).

    A different notice: *«No se pudo confirmar en qué empresa trabaja esta
    conexión»* (*It couldn't be confirmed which company this connection works
    in*). It's a hiccup while checking, not a change of company: your assistant
    touched nothing, and retrying in a few seconds is enough.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="It says you don't have access to an app">
    Your assistant works with **the same permissions you have in the
    dashboard**, in the connection's company. If it replies with something like
    *«Esta persona no tiene acceso a Ventas…»* (*This person doesn't have access
    to Ventas…*), ask whoever manages your company for that access: it is
    granted in **Personas y accesos** (*People and access*)
    ([Invite your team](/en/getting-started/invite-your-team)). Once you have
    it, wait a minute and open a new chat.

    If it says the app **isn't active**, it first has to be switched on in your
    company ([The apps](/en/getting-started/the-apps)). Then wait a minute and
    open a new chat.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="New features don't show up">
    Open a **new chat**: each conversation keeps the list of features that
    existed when it started. If a new chat still doesn't show them, delete the
    connector and connect it again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="It says «sin créditos» (out of credits)">
    It's the **same balance as your dashboard**, and it has run out. The AI
    features stay paused until you top up, and your WhatsApp agent also stops
    replying. The notice itself carries the link to top up.

    You top up in **Administración → Dinero → Saldo y facturación**
    (*Administration → Money → Balance and billing*). If you don't see that
    screen, ask whoever manages your company for access to the balance. All the
    details are in [Credits and top-ups](/en/getting-started/credits-and-top-ups).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The screen says «No se pudo autorizar» (Authorization failed)">
    If below it says *«La solicitud de autorización expiró o no es válida»*
    (*The authorization request expired or isn't valid*), go back to Claude and
    click **Connect** again: a new request opens.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
