With ChatGPT connected, you ask it about your business and request changes
just by chatting. It works on **your playbook, your catalog, your
conversations and your customers**, with your DarkFunnels account.

**You don't need it to sell.** Your WhatsApp agent works just the same without
it. It is a convenience if you already use ChatGPT every day.

The dashboard is in Spanish, so its buttons and on-screen messages are quoted
here exactly as you will see them, with the English meaning in parentheses.

<Warning>
**ChatGPT's free plan does not support connectors.** You need Plus, Pro,
Business (formerly Team), Enterprise or Edu. If free is all you have, connect
[claude.ai](/en/quickstarts/claude-ai): its free plan does allow one connector,
and the link is the same.
</Warning>

## The link you will paste

```
https://mcp.darkfunnels.ai/mcp?features=all
```

**It is the same for every assistant.** You don't have to touch it again: the
features we publish later show up in a new chat, without changing the link, as
long as your account has access to the app they use.

You also have it in the dashboard, under **Personalizar → Asistentes IA**
(*Customize → AI assistants*). To get there, in the left-hand menu, inside
**Ventas** (*Sales*), click **Personalizar** and choose **Asistentes IA**.
Personalizar is in the **Ajustes** (*Settings*) group of Ventas, not in the
**Ajustes** of **Administración** (*Administration*). If you prefer to go
straight there, type `optimind.darkfunnels.ai/customize?panel=asistentes` in
the browser's address bar.

There you copy it with **Copiar link** (*Copy link*), on the **Conecta tu
asistente** (*Connect your assistant*) card. That card also tells you which
email you have to authorize with.

Further down, under **¿Dónde lo pego?** (*Where do I paste it?*), there is one
tab per assistant. The **ChatGPT** one has the **Ojo** (*Heads up*) notice
about the free plan, the developer-mode path and the **Hazlo en Claude** (*Do
it in Claude*) button, in case your plan doesn't support connectors.

## Connect it, step by step

<Steps>
  <Step title="Turn on developer mode in ChatGPT">
    In ChatGPT: **Settings → Apps and connectors → Advanced → Developer
    mode**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the connector and paste the link">
    In **Settings → Apps and connectors**, create a new connector. If it asks
    for a name, use one you will recognize, for example DarkFunnels, and paste
    the link above as the server address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize with your dashboard account">
    ChatGPT opens the DarkFunnels **Autorizar acceso** (*Authorize access*)
    screen. If you don't have your dashboard session open in that browser, it
    first asks you to sign in and then comes back to this screen.

    **Before you click, look at the «Autorizas como» (*You are authorizing
    as*) line.** It has to be the email you use to sign in to the dashboard.
    If it is a different one, click **Denegar** (*Deny*) and see the "It can't
    see your data" row in [If something goes wrong](#if-something-goes-wrong).
    The screen also tells you what the assistant will be able to do and which
    address it will send you back to.

    Click **Autorizar** (*Authorize*). You will see **Acceso autorizado**
    (*Access authorized*) and you go back to ChatGPT. If you click
    **Denegar**, nothing gets connected.

    If you had already authorized before, it may not ask you again and take
    you straight back to ChatGPT.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Try it">
    Write to it in a new chat. These three messages come from the dashboard
    (on the **Pruébalo con estos mensajes** (*Try it with these messages*)
    card, each with its **Copiar** (*Copy*) button):

    - *"Review my conversations from yesterday and tell me where I'm losing
      sales."*
    - *"Read my agent's playbook and suggest an improvement to the closing
      chapter; save it once I approve it."*
    - *"Give me the customers who have gone more than 3 days without a reply,
      and what the agent would say to them to pick the conversation back
      up."*
  </Step>
</Steps>

### How you know it is connected

Go back to **Personalizar → Asistentes IA**. There are two signs:

- **Under «Seguridad y acceso» (*Security and access*)**, the **Asistentes con
  acceso a tu cuenta** (*Assistants with access to your account*) list shows
  each assistant you authorized, with **Autorizado el** (*Authorized on*) and
  the date. You see this sign even if you don't manage the company.
- **If you manage your company's apps**, you will also see, next to «Conecta
  tu asistente», the **Conectado · última actividad** (*Connected · last
  activity*) badge with a date. It appears when an assistant has already
  worked in your company, whether yours or someone else's on your team. It is
  the date of the last activity: it doesn't disappear when you revoke an
  access. If you don't manage the apps, you won't see it: go by the list.

## What it can do, and what it asks you first

With this link, ChatGPT can:

- Read and edit your agent's sales playbook, with a version history so you can
  undo.
- Review, create and update products in your catalog.
- Review conversations and customers, and tag whoever needs a follow-up.
- Warn you when your credit balance is about to run out, if your account can
  see the balance.

**The link turns on every feature, including the ones that write and send.**
Our features that change something are marked as such, and ChatGPT usually
uses that mark to ask you for confirmation before using them.

<Warning>
**Some features really do write to your customers on WhatsApp.** For example,
sending a message as an operator in a chat, or simulating a customer message
in a WhatsApp chat to test the script: the agent's reply really reaches that
number. **Read what it is going to do before you confirm.**
</Warning>

Three more things worth knowing:

- **Some features spend your company's balance**, for example optimizing the
  playbook with AI, asking the copilot or simulating a message. It is the same
  balance as in the dashboard. Creating or duplicating a funnel, on top of
  that, takes up a slot in your plan.
- **Your customers' phone numbers reach it masked** (`51•••••4321`). How to
  see them in full, in [The connection URL](/en/reference/connection-url).
- **The full list of features** is in the
  [Tool reference](/en/reference/tools).

### If you'd rather it only read

Add `&read_only=true` to the end of the link. That way ChatGPT only reads: it
doesn't get the features that change something or send messages, nor the ones
that use AI, such as asking the copilot or optimizing the playbook. **There is
one exception: reading the text of an image in your Library does spend
balance.**

```
https://mcp.darkfunnels.ai/mcp?features=all&read_only=true
```

If you already had it connected, changing the link means connecting it again:
delete the connector in ChatGPT and create it with the new link.

## Your permissions, and if you are in several companies

**ChatGPT works with your account and your permissions, nothing more.** It
can't do more than your account: it only uses what your account can use in the
dashboard, and not everything in the dashboard has a feature for it. If you
don't have access to an app, or that app isn't active in the company, its
features don't show up for it in a new chat. If it still tries to use one, it
is denied. Each person on your team who wants to use it connects with their
own account.

**If your account is in several companies**, each connection is tied to the
company you had active the first time ChatGPT used it. If you later switch
companies in the dashboard (or accept an invitation from another company,
which switches you to it), that connection stops saving changes: ChatGPT will
tell you that no change was made, so it doesn't write to the wrong company.

<Warning>
**What it reads from that moment on already comes from the company you have
active in the dashboard.** If you were comparing figures, first go back to the
connection's company.
</Warning>

You have two ways out:

- **To stay in the connection's company**: go back to it from your account
  menu, with **Cambiar a esta empresa** (*Switch to this company*).
- **To work in the other one**: delete the connector in ChatGPT and connect it
  again with that company active in the dashboard.

How to switch companies and how permissions are shared out, in
[Invite your team](/en/getting-started/invite-your-team#one-account-in-several-businesses).

## Cutting off access

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the list of assistants">
    In **Personalizar → Asistentes IA**, scroll down to **Seguridad y
    acceso**. Under **Asistentes con acceso a tu cuenta** each assistant
    appears with the name it introduced itself with when it connected (if it
    gave none, **Asistente sin nombre**, *Unnamed assistant*).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click «Revocar» (Revoke)">
    Next to the assistant you want to disconnect. The dashboard warns you of
    the consequence: that assistant *"loses access to your account within a
    few seconds. To use it again, you will have to connect it again."*
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm it">
    Click **Revocar** again (or **Cancelar**, *Cancel*, if you change your
    mind). You will see **Acceso revocado** (*Access revoked*) with the
    assistant's name, and it disappears from the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Delete it in ChatGPT too">
    Revoking cuts off access from our side. For it to stop appearing in
    ChatGPT, delete the connector in **Settings → Apps and connectors**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | The usual cause | How to fix it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **You can't find where to create the connector** | Your plan is the free one, or developer mode is off | With Plus, Pro, Business (formerly Team), Enterprise or Edu, turn it on in **Settings → Apps and connectors → Advanced**. With the free plan, use [claude.ai](/en/quickstarts/claude-ai) |
| **It can't see your data, or sees it empty** | You authorized with a different account | Ask it *"Which DarkFunnels account are you connected to?"*. If it isn't yours, delete the connector, sign in to the dashboard with the right account and connect it again |
| **Part of your business is missing**, or it tells you that you don't have access to an app | You don't have permission in that app, or the app isn't active in that company | Ask whoever manages your company (or do it yourself, if you manage it) to give you access or to turn on the app from **Apps**. Then wait a minute and open a new chat |
| **It tells you it made no change because you switched companies** | The connection is tied to another company | Go back to that company or reconnect: [Your permissions, and if you are in several companies](#your-permissions-and-if-you-are-in-several-companies) |
| **«No se pudo confirmar en qué empresa trabaja esta conexión»** (*Could not confirm which company this connection works in*) | A hiccup while checking the company, on accounts that are in several | Try again in a few seconds. No change was made |
| **New features don't show up** | The conversation kept the list of features it had when you started it | Open a new chat. If they still don't show up, delete the connector and create it again with the same link |
| **It says «Sin créditos»** (*Out of credits*) | Your balance dropped below US\$ 0.10, even if you see a remainder on screen, or your trial ended and you have no subscription | Top up in **Administración → Saldo y facturación** (*Administration → Balance and billing*) ([how to top up](/en/getting-started/credits-and-top-ups#how-to-top-up)). If you don't see that screen, ask whoever manages your company for access to the balance. If your trial ended, topping up isn't enough: activate the subscription ([how it is activated](/en/getting-started/pricing#how-it-is-activated)). Until then, the AI features stay stopped |
| **«No se pudo autorizar»** (*Could not authorize*) when connecting | The authorization request expired | Go back to ChatGPT and repeat the connection |
| **You don't see Personalizar in your menu** | Personalizar is inside Ventas, and you don't have access to that app or it isn't active in your company | Copy the link from this page: it is the same. Without Personalizar you don't see the list to revoke either: to cut off access, delete the connector in ChatGPT and, if it also has to be cut off from our side, [write to us](/en/security) |
