---
title: "ChatGPT"
description: "Connect ChatGPT (developer mode) to DarkFunnels."
---

<Warning>
ChatGPT's **free plan does not support custom connectors**. You need Plus, Pro,
Team, Enterprise or Edu, with **developer mode** enabled. If free is all you
have, go to [claude.ai](/en/quickstarts/claude-ai): its free plan does allow one
connector, and it is the same link.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enable developer mode">
    **Settings → Apps and connectors → Advanced → Developer mode**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the connector">
    Create a new custom connector.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste the URL">
    ```
    https://mcp.darkfunnels.ai/mcp?features=all
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize">
    With the **same account you use to sign in to the DarkFunnels dashboard**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Writes (saving the playbook, editing the catalog) ask for confirmation inside
ChatGPT before they run.

If the assistant can't see your business, or sees it empty, you authorized with
an account other than the dashboard's: remove the connector and connect again
with the right one.
