**If you work with an AI assistant in your editor or in the terminal, it can
also work with your business.** Cursor, VS Code, Gemini CLI and any program
that connects to remote MCP servers —MCP is the standard AI assistants use to
connect to other services— can read your conversations, your playbook and your
catalog, and save changes if you ask them to.

It is **optional**: your agent sells just the same without it.

The dashboard interface is in Spanish, so its buttons and menus are named here
in Spanish with the English meaning in parentheses.

<Note>
**What you need.** The account you use to sign in to the DarkFunnels dashboard
and a client that supports **remote MCP servers over HTTP, with sign-in
(OAuth)**. If your client only accepts servers that run on your own computer,
it won't work for this.
</Note>

## The link

It is the same for every client:

```
https://mcp.darkfunnels.ai/mcp?features=all
```

You also have it in your dashboard, ready to copy. Go to
[optimind.darkfunnels.ai](https://optimind.darkfunnels.ai). In the left-hand
menu, inside **Ventas** (*Sales*), click **Personalizar** (*Customize*) — it is
in the **Ajustes** (*Settings*) group — and choose the **Asistentes IA** (*AI
assistants*) tab. You can also get there by typing
`optimind.darkfunnels.ai/customize?panel=asistentes` in the browser's address
bar.

The first card, **Conecta tu asistente** (*Connect your assistant*), has the
link and the **Copiar link** (*Copy link*) button.

<Frame caption="The «Conecta tu asistente» (Connect your assistant) card: the connection link with its Copiar link (Copy link) button and the three steps.">
  <img src="/images/asistentes/01-conecta-tu-asistente.png" alt="Conecta tu asistente (Connect your assistant) card with the connection link, the Copiar link (Copy link) button and three numbered steps" />
</Frame>

**Personalizar is part of Ventas.** If you don't see Ventas in your menu, the
link is the one above and you can connect anyway. That said, the list for
removing an assistant's access is also in Personalizar. If you ever need it, ask
whoever manages your company for access to Ventas, or write to us from **Ayuda →
Escríbenos por WhatsApp** (*Help → Write to us on WhatsApp*).

**There is nothing to register and no key to request.** When it connects, your
client works out on its own how to ask for your permission and sends you to the
browser so you can authorize with your account.

<Warning>
**The `?features=all` ending switches on every feature**, including the ones
that write and send: saving the playbook, deleting a product or sending a real
WhatsApp message to a customer. And the ones we publish later. If you'd rather
your assistant only read, use the [Read-only](#read-only) link.
</Warning>

## Connect it, step by step

<Steps>
  <Step title="Copy the link">
    In **Personalizar → Asistentes IA** (*Customize → AI assistants*), click
    **Copiar link** (*Copy link*). The notice *«Link copiado»* (*Link copied*)
    appears.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste it into your client's configuration">
    Each client keeps it in a different file and with different field names.
    Below you have [Cursor](#cursor), [VS Code](#vs-code) and
    [Gemini CLI](#gemini-cli). Give it a name you'll recognize, for example
    DarkFunnels.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize in the browser">
    When you switch the server on, your client opens a DarkFunnels tab. If you
    weren't signed in to the dashboard, it first asks you to sign in and then
    comes back to this screen on its own.

    The screen is titled **Autorizar acceso** (*Authorize access*) and tells
    you which assistant wants to connect to your account. Below it is the line
    **Autorizas como** (*You are authorizing as*), with an email. **That email
    has to be the one for your dashboard account.**

    If it is, click **Autorizar** (*Authorize*): you'll see **Acceso
    autorizado** (*Access authorized*) and you go back to your client. If it's
    a different one, click **Denegar** (*Deny*) and see
    [If something goes wrong](#if-something-goes-wrong).

    If that account had already authorized this same assistant before, you may
    not see this screen: it goes straight to **Acceso autorizado**. Check the
    account with the next step.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check what you connected to">
    Ask your assistant: *"Which DarkFunnels account and which company are you
    connected to?"*. It has tools that tell it your email, your company and
    your permissions.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
**Picking the wrong account doesn't give an error.** Your client connects
anyway, but to the account you authorized with: you'll see another business, or
an empty one. Check the email under **Autorizas como** before you click
**Autorizar**. If that screen didn't appear, ask the question from the last
step.
</Warning>

## Cursor

Edit `~/.cursor/mcp.json` (for all your projects) or `.cursor/mcp.json` (that
project only):

```json
{
  "mcpServers": {
    "DarkFunnels": {
      "url": "https://mcp.darkfunnels.ai/mcp?features=all"
    }
  }
}
```

Cursor supports sign-in for remote servers: the first time, it sends you to the
browser to authorize.

## VS Code

Edit `.vscode/mcp.json` in your project. To have it in all your projects, open
the one in your profile with the **MCP: Open User Configuration** command.

```json
{
  "servers": {
    "DarkFunnels": {
      "type": "http",
      "url": "https://mcp.darkfunnels.ai/mcp?features=all"
    }
  }
}
```

<Warning>
VS Code is where people get it wrong most, over two details:

- The root block is called **`servers`**, not `mcpServers` as in Cursor.
- **`"type": "http"`** is required in VS Code.
</Warning>

## Gemini CLI

Edit `~/.gemini/settings.json` (or the project's `.gemini/settings.json`).
Watch the field name: here the URL goes in **`httpUrl`**, not in `url`. In
Gemini CLI, `url` is for another kind of connection (SSE) that DarkFunnels
doesn't use.

```json
{
  "mcpServers": {
    "DarkFunnels": {
      "httpUrl": "https://mcp.darkfunnels.ai/mcp?features=all"
    }
  }
}
```

Gemini CLI works out on its own how to authorize you, stores the session in
`~/.gemini/mcp-oauth-tokens.json` and renews it when it expires.

## Any other client

Three details change from one client to another. Look them up in your client's
documentation before fighting with the file:

| Client | Name of the root block | URL field | Connection type |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cursor | `mcpServers` | `url` | No need to set it |
| VS Code | `servers` | `url` | Required: `"type": "http"` |
| Gemini CLI | `mcpServers` | `httpUrl` | No need to set it |

If your client lets you choose the connection type, choose **HTTP** (sometimes
called "Streamable HTTP"). **SSE** and **stdio** don't work with DarkFunnels.

<Note>
**If your client keeps waiting after you clicked Autorizar**, write to us from
**Ayuda → Escríbenos por WhatsApp** (*Help → Write to us on WhatsApp*; the
question mark in the dashboard's top bar) and tell us which client you use. It
can happen with clients that receive the response on your own computer, because
of a limitation of our identity provider. With Claude Code it is a known case,
and [its guide](/en/quickstarts/claude-code) explains how to get around it.
</Note>

## Read-only

If you want your assistant to look things up and explain them without being
able to save anything, add `&read_only=true` to the end of the link:

```
https://mcp.darkfunnels.ai/mcp?features=all&read_only=true
```

`read_only=true` overrides any other option in the link: with it, no feature
that writes comes through.

To change the link of a connection you already had, change it in the
configuration file and connect the server again from your client. All the
link's options are in [The connection URL](/en/reference/connection-url).

## If your client runs out of room for tools

Some clients limit how many tools the model can have at once, **adding up all
your servers and the client's own tools**. VS Code, for example, allows up to
128 per request.

With `?features=all`, an account with access to every app receives **more than
60 tools** from DarkFunnels. **The exact number depends on you**: your
assistant receives only the ones for the apps you can use and that your company
has switched on (we explain it in
[Your account, your company and your permissions](#your-account-your-company-and-your-permissions)).
If you already have other servers connected, you may fall short.

You have three ways out, from the simplest to the most fine-grained:

1. **Remove the `?features=all`.** The bare link,
   `https://mcp.darkfunnels.ai/mcp`, brings the reads —playbook, catalog,
   conversations, customers and the account basics— and three minor writes:
   creating labels, changing a customer's labels and noting down a request
   your assistant couldn't fulfil. That is less than half of what
   `?features=all` brings.
2. **Ask only for the groups you are going to use**, for example
   `https://mcp.darkfunnels.ai/mcp?features=conversations,orders`. **The list
   replaces the base set, it doesn't extend it**: the account basics come with
   any list, but with that link you get neither the playbook nor the catalog.
   The groups and what each one brings are in
   [The connection URL](/en/reference/connection-url), and the list of tools,
   in the [tools reference](/en/reference/tools).
3. **Switch tools off in your client**, if it lets you. VS Code, for example,
   has a tool picker in the chat.

## Your account, your company and your permissions

**Your assistant comes in with your account and sees what your account sees in
the dashboard, nothing more.** Three consequences:

- **Your permissions rule.** Your assistant only gets the tools of the apps
  that you can use and that your company has switched on. If someone on your
  team connects their own assistant, it works with that person's permissions.
  How they are shared out, in
  [Invite your team](/en/getting-started/invite-your-team).
- **One connection, one company to save in.** If your account is in several
  companies, each connection is pinned to the company you had active the first
  time your assistant used it. If you later switch company in the dashboard,
  that connection doesn't save changes until you go back to that company or
  connect it again. That way it doesn't write in the wrong company. **Careful:
  what it reads does change.** After the switch, your assistant reads the data
  of the company you have active now.
- **What it can't do, it notes down.** If you ask it for something its tools
  don't cover, your assistant can log it for our team and give you the request
  number. With `read_only=true` it can't.

## Usage limits

So that a runaway assistant doesn't leave anyone without service, the bridge
—our MCP connection, the way your assistant comes in— sets two caps:

| Cap | How much | What you see if you go over it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Per person** | Around 60 requests per minute and 300 per hour, adding up all the assistants you connected with your account | An error that says *«rate limit; reintenta en N s»* (*rate limit; retry in N s*). Wait those seconds and carry on. |
| **Per company** | To start with, 20,000 tool calls per day, adding up everyone | *«La empresa alcanzó su cupo diario de llamadas por el puente MCP; se restablece a medianoche (hora de Lima)…»* (*The company reached its daily quota of calls through the MCP bridge; it resets at midnight, Lima time…*) |

Every time your client talks to the bridge counts as a request: using a tool,
but also asking it for the list of tools. **If you work with an agent that
chains many calls, the cap you'll notice most is the hourly one**: 300 per hour
is about 5 per minute on average.

If your business needs more daily quota, the owner can ask for it from **Ayuda →
Escríbenos por WhatsApp** (*Help → Write to us on WhatsApp*).

## Removing its access

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the list of assistants">
    In **Personalizar → Asistentes IA** (*Customize → AI assistants*), scroll
    down to the **Seguridad y acceso** (*Security and access*) card. The
    **Asistentes con acceso a tu cuenta** (*Assistants with access to your
    account*) list has one row per assistant: the name your client introduced
    itself with, the date you authorized it (**Autorizado el …**, *Authorized
    on …*) and the **Revocar** (*Revoke*) button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Revocar and confirm">
    The dashboard warns you: *«… pierde el acceso a tu cuenta en unos segundos.
    Para volver a usarlo, tendrás que conectarlo de nuevo.»* (*… loses access
    to your account within a few seconds. To use it again, you will have to
    connect it again.*) Confirm with **Revocar**. The notice *«Acceso revocado:
    …»* (*Access revoked: …*) appears and the assistant disappears from the
    list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Remove it from your client too">
    Revoking cuts off access, but the entry is still in your configuration file
    (`mcp.json` in Cursor and VS Code, `settings.json` in Gemini CLI). Delete it
    so your client stops trying.
  </Step>
</Steps>

  <img src="/images/asistentes/03-seguridad-y-acceso.png" alt="Seguridad y acceso (Security and access) card with three bullet points about the connection and the Asistentes con acceso a tu cuenta (Assistants with access to your account) list, which reads Ningún asistente tiene acceso a tu cuenta ahora (No assistant has access to your account right now)" />

**The list belongs to your account.** It doesn't show the assistants other
people on your team connected with their own accounts, and revoking yours
doesn't affect them.

## No human present (headless)

**There are no API keys today.** The connection starts with a person who
authorizes in the browser with their account. An agent that runs on its own —a
scheduled task, a worker, OpenAI's Agents SDK or Claude's Agent SDK— can't take
that step by itself.

If you need to automate something like that, write to us from **Ayuda →
Escríbenos por WhatsApp** (*Help → Write to us on WhatsApp*) and we'll look at
it with you.

## The documentation, over MCP too

If you only want your assistant to look things up in this documentation
—without touching your business—, there is a public MCP server. **It doesn't
ask for an account or authorization**, so an agent with no person present can
use it too:

```
https://docs.darkfunnels.ai/mcp
```

It searches every page of this documentation and reads them.

Both servers are in the official MCP registry:
`ai.darkfunnels/whatsapp-sales-agent` is the one for your business (with
authorization) and `ai.darkfunnels/docs` is the documentation one. If your
client installs servers from that registry, look us up as DarkFunnels.

## If something goes wrong

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The browser doesn't open to authorize">
    The first thing to check is the configuration file. Review the three
    details in [Any other client](#any-other-client): in VS Code, the `servers`
    block and `"type": "http"`; in Gemini CLI, the `httpUrl` field. And that
    the link is complete, starting with `https://`.

    If your client only supports servers that run on your computer, it can't
    connect directly to DarkFunnels.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="It sees another business, or an empty one">
    The most common cause is that you authorized with **a different account**.
    Ask your assistant *"which DarkFunnels account and which company are you
    connected to?"*. If the email isn't yours:

    1. Remove the server from your client (and its stored session, if your
       client keeps it separately).
    2. In the dashboard, open your account menu (the circle with your
       initials, top right), click **Cerrar sesión** (*Sign out*) and sign in
       with the right account.
    3. Connect again. If **Autorizar acceso** appears, check that **Autorizas
       como** shows your email. If it doesn't appear, ask your assistant the
       same question as before.

    If the email is yours but the company isn't, see the next item.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="It says your account switched company">
    It happens if your account is in **several companies** and, after
    connecting, you switched company in the dashboard or accepted an invitation
    to another company (when you accept it, it becomes the active one). Your
    assistant warns you with *«La cuenta cambió de empresa en la app de
    DarkFunnels…»* (*The account switched company in the DarkFunnels app…*)
    and doesn't save anything, so it doesn't write in the wrong company.

    You have two ways out:

    - **To stay in the connection's company**, open your account menu and
      click that company's name (underneath it says **Cambiar a esta
      empresa**, *Switch to this company*).
    - **To work in the one you have active now**, connect it with a new
      session. In **Personalizar → Asistentes IA**, click **Revocar** on that
      assistant and connect it again from your client: the new session is
      pinned to the active company. If you'd rather not revoke, remove the
      server from your client **and also its stored session**, if your client
      keeps it separately, and connect it again.

    Retrying without one of those two steps doesn't help. If the notice says
    your account **no longer has access** to the connection's company, only the
    second way out works: connecting it again. How to switch company, in
    [Invite your team](/en/getting-started/invite-your-team#one-account-in-several-businesses).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="It says you don't have access to an app, or that it isn't switched on">
    If it answers something like *«Esta persona no tiene acceso a Ventas…»*
    (*This person doesn't have access to Sales…*), ask whoever manages your
    company for that access
    ([Invite your team](/en/getting-started/invite-your-team)).

    If it says *«La app … no está activa…»* (*The … app isn't switched on…*),
    it first has to be switched on in your company from **Apps**
    ([The apps](/en/getting-started/the-apps)).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Tools you expected are missing">
    Check three things, in this order:

    1. **Your permissions and your apps.** The tools of an app you can't use,
       or that your company doesn't have switched on, don't appear.
    2. **Your link.** If you asked for individual groups with `?features=`, the
       rest don't come through. With `?features=all` you get all the ones your
       account can use, unless the link carries `read_only=true`: then none
       that writes comes through.
    3. **An old list.** New tools appear when your client asks for the list
       again: open a new chat or connect the server again from your client. If
       you were just given a permission, wait a minute first.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="It says «rate limit; reintenta en N s»">
    Your assistant went over the per-person cap (explained in **Usage limits**,
    above). Wait the seconds it indicates and carry on.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="It says the company reached its daily quota">
    Between everyone in your company, the day's calls were used up. It resets
    at midnight, Lima time. If your business needs more quota, the owner can ask
    for it from **Ayuda → Escríbenos por WhatsApp** (*Help → Write to us on
    WhatsApp*).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="It says «Sin créditos» (Out of credits)">
    It is **the same balance as in your dashboard**, and it ran out. The AI
    features stay paused until you top up.

    You top up in **Administración → Dinero → Saldo y facturación**
    (*Administration → Money → Balance and billing*). All the details, in
    [Credits and top-ups](/en/getting-started/credits-and-top-ups).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="It says the authorization was revoked, or asks you to authorize again">
    The most common cause is that someone clicked **Revocar** for that
    assistant in **Personalizar → Asistentes IA**, with your account. It also
    happens if your assistant's session no longer exists for another reason, or
    if your client lost the one it had stored. Either way, the fix is the same:
    connect it again and authorize.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The screen says «No se pudo autorizar» (Couldn't authorize)">
    If underneath it says *«La solicitud de autorización expiró o no es
    válida»* (*The authorization request expired or isn't valid*), go back to
    your client and connect again: a new request opens.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

How your data is protected along this route, in [Security](/en/security).
