**DarkFunnels answers the WhatsApp messages that reach your number for you.** It
is not a bot of loose replies: it follows your business's sales script — it
greets, works out what the customer is looking for, sends them the photo and
the price, handles the objection and closes — and it lets you know on WhatsApp
when it records a sale.

Once you are connected, your account needs three things for the agent to
reply: its funnel has to be **switched on**, you need **balance** (at least
**US\$ 0.10**) and your **trial must still be running** or you need a
**subscription**. If one of them is missing, it stops replying to new messages.
When the trial or the balance runs out, Home tells you.

On top of that, each chat has its own **«Piloto automático»** (*Autopilot*)
switch: when it is off, your agent stays quiet in that conversation and you
reply yourself. If a customer was left without a reply, go through
[My agent didn't reply to a customer](/en/reference/faq).

**What you need:**

- the WhatsApp number your customers already write to, with its phone at hand
  to scan a QR code;
- a computer to complete your agent's playbook (on your phone you can touch up
  individual details, such as prices, phone numbers and links, and test your
  agent, but the script is rewritten from the computer);
- and a little time the first time round.

**What you do NOT need:** to change number, to know how to code, to put in a
card to try it, or the WhatsApp Business API.

## The words you will read all over this site

Before anything else, eight words. With these eight you will understand almost
the whole site; the rest are in the [glossary](/en/reference/glossary).

| Word | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Funnel** (*Embudo*) | What you create in Ventas (*Sales*) to sell over WhatsApp: it has its WhatsApp number, its playbook and **an agent** that serves your customers for you. You select it, set it up, duplicate it or share it, and each active funnel takes up a slot in your plan. |
| **Agent** (*Agente*) | Your automatic salesperson: the AI that chats with your customers inside a funnel, following its playbook. It introduces itself with a name and a way of speaking, its **persona**; in the Home headline, «Personas que venden por ti» (*People who sell for you*) means your agents. Careful: the **«Personas nuevas»** (*New people*) chart on Home counts customers, not agents. |
| **Playbook** (*Manual*) | The agent's script, written in **chapters**, like the one you would give a new salesperson. It is what you will edit most. |
| **Placeholder** (*Dato por completar*) | A detail of your business still to fill in, written in capitals with underscores and between « », like `«PRODUCTO_MARCA»`. It comes in the playbook of a sales funnel created with the Ecommerce template or from scratch. While any are left, you cannot connect WhatsApp. |
| **Catalog** (*Catálogo*) | Your products with their prices and variants. The agent checks it when a customer asks about a product, to give the exact price. |
| **Library** (*Librería*) | Your business's files: the ones the agent sends to the customer (photos, PDF catalogs, audio) and the ones it only reads so it knows how to answer (warranties, shipping policies). |
| **Copilot** (*Copiloto*) | The AI that lives **inside** the dashboard, in the drawer on the right, and helps you build and fix your funnel. |
| **Credits** (*Créditos*) | The balance the AI spends: your agent when it chats, and the copilot too. If your balance drops below US\$ 0.10, your agent stops replying to new messages, even if you see some balance left on screen. |

<Note>
**"Customer"**, in this documentation, is **the person who writes to you on
WhatsApp**: the one who buys from you.

**optiMind** is the name of the DarkFunnels product. You will see it in the
dashboard, for example when you hover over the copilot button («Copiloto
optiMind»): you are in the right place. Inside the dashboard, the copilot
introduces itself as **Kipu**.
</Note>

  <Step title="Get to know the dashboard">

    menu is.
    [Your first look](/en/getting-started/first-look) ·
    [The DarkFunnels apps](/en/getting-started/the-apps)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your funnel with the copilot">
    The copilot asks you what you sell (it also works if you book appointments
    or screen job applicants). If you sell over WhatsApp, you choose between
    **«Usar la plantilla de Ecommerce»** (*Use the Ecommerce template*), a
    playbook with base content to adapt to your business, or **«Armar mi flujo
    desde 0»** (*Build my flow from scratch*), with the same chapters and the
    steps empty.
    [Your first funnel](/en/getting-started/your-first-funnel)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the playbook">
    Fill the placeholders with your business details and write whatever is
    missing, from the computer. With the playbook's **«Probar»** (*Test*)
    button you talk to your agent without WhatsApp, to see how it replies
    before you let it loose on your customers.
    [The sales playbook](/en/guides/sales-playbook)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your WhatsApp">
    In **«Personalizar → Canales»** (*Customize → Channels*) you scan a QR code
    with your number's phone, just like when you open WhatsApp Web. The day you
    connect, your 7-day trial starts and your agent begins to reply, if you
    have balance left: no new balance arrives when you connect, because you
    already had the US\$ 10 welcome balance from sign-up.

    If your funnel was born from the Ecommerce template (or from scratch) and its
    playbook is missing something, instead of the QR you will see **«Antes de
    conectar:»** (*Before connecting:*) with what is missing: placeholders,
    chapters without steps, or your payment and shipping details not yet
    confirmed.

From the moment you connect, **your agent replies to the one-to-one chats that
reach that number**: it does not reply to groups or statuses, or to the
contacts you have opted out. That is why the order matters: first the complete
playbook, then the QR. The rest — catalog, files, settings — you fine-tune with
the guides below.

## Before you connect your WhatsApp

It is exactly what everybody asks: *is it safe for an AI to answer my customers
and read my chats?* The long answer — who sees your chats, which outside
companies your data travels to, how long each thing is kept and how to delete
a customer — is in **[Security](/en/security)**.

The short answer about money: **your agent does not process payments.** It
records the sale and, when the customer decides to buy, it gives them the
payment details it has in its playbook and asks them for a photo of the
receipt. Confirming that the payment arrived is up to you or your team.

If you save your Yape or your bank account in **«Configuración → Datos del
negocio»** (*Settings → Business details*), insert them into the playbook with
**@**: the agent reads them wherever the playbook names them.
[Funnel settings](/en/guides/funnel-settings) explains how.

What is charged automatically, to the card you left saved, is your own
subscription and, if you turn it on, the automatic balance top-up.

## Optional: run it from Claude or ChatGPT

Once your agent is selling, you can connect your business to **the assistant
you already use** to ask it things and make changes by chatting: *"go through
yesterday's conversations and tell me where sales are being lost"* or
*"propose an improvement to the closing chapter and save it once I approve
it"*.

<Note>
This uses **MCP**, the standard that Claude and ChatGPT use to connect to
external tools; our connection to that standard is what these pages call **the
bridge**. **You don't need it to sell**: it is a convenience for anyone who
already uses one of those assistants. The link to connect it is in
**«Personalizar → Asistentes IA»** (*Customize → AI assistants*).

Authorize with **the same account you use to sign in to the dashboard**. Your
assistant gets in with your permissions: whatever your account can't do in the
dashboard, it can't do either. If your account is in several companies, each
connection stays tied to the company you had selected the first time your
assistant used it. If you later switch company in the dashboard, that
connection stops saving changes until you go back to the first one, and your
assistant tells you so. To stay in the first one, select it again in the
dashboard. To work in the new one, disconnect the connector and connect it
again.
</Note>
