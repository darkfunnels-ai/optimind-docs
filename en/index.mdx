**DarkFunnels answers the WhatsApp messages that reach your number for you.** It
is not a bot of loose replies: it follows a sales script that you write — it
greets, works out what the customer is looking for, sends them the photo and the
price, handles the objection and closes — and it tells you when it has sold. It
replies within some safety limits: up to 150 messages and 150 distinct customers
per day and per number, and as long as you have balance and the agent is active.

You connect the WhatsApp number you already use by scanning a QR from the
dashboard — no Meta Cloud API application, no message templates to get approved,
no per-conversation fee.

**What you need:** the WhatsApp number your customers already write to, and some
time the first time round.
**What you do NOT need:** to change number, to know how to code, or to put in a
card to try it.

## Before you connect your WhatsApp

It is exactly what everybody asks: *is it safe for an AI to answer my customers
and read my chats?* The long answer — what is stored, who sees it and what does
not happen — is in **[Security](/en/security)**.

The short answer about money: **your agent never charges your customers
anything.** It records the sale; collecting it is up to you. The only thing
charged automatically is your own subscription and, if you switch it on, the
balance top-up to the card you left stored.

## Optional: run it from Claude or ChatGPT

Once your agent is selling, you can connect your business to **the assistant you
already use** and ask it things or make changes by talking to it: *"go through
yesterday's conversations and tell me where sales are being lost"* or *"propose
an improvement to the closing chapter and save it once I approve it"*.

<Note>
This uses **MCP**, the standard that Claude and ChatGPT use to connect to
external tools; our connection to that standard is what these pages call **the
bridge**. **It is not needed in order to sell**: it is a convenience for anyone
who already uses one of those assistants.

Always authorize with **the same account you use to sign in to the dashboard** —
with any other one you would be connecting a business that is not yours.
</Note>

One URL for everything:

```
https://mcp.darkfunnels.ai/mcp?features=all
```

### What the bridge does not do

The bridge **does not move money**: none of its tools charges, transfers, issues
payment links or touches your payment provider. Sales are recorded by your
WhatsApp agent; the bridge only reads them. The details are in
[Security](/en/security).
